Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

This Privacy Policy explains how NextGrade ("we", "us") at nextgrade.in handles information when you use this website in any language (Hindi, English, Hinglish or Bengali), including the games under nextgrade.in/games/. Our apps on nextgrade.in subdomains (for example hisab.nextgrade.in) have their own privacy pages that apply in addition to this one.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account on this website and we do not collect your name, email or phone number unless you choose to email us. We have no server or database that stores your information. The Wish Card Maker works entirely in your browser: the names and message you type are not sent to us or stored by us. A name you add to a share link (for example ?from=Rahul) stays only in that link. Games save your best score and chosen language only on your own device.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and content delivery network (Cloudflare) may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the service running.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law (for example for visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), Google will show a consent message before using cookies for ads.

Children

This website is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@nextgrade.in.

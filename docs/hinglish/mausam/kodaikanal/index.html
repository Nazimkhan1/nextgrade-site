🌦️ Kodaikanal ka Mausam Aaj

Kodaikanal (Tamil Nadu) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

🧳 Ghoomne ke liye

Taare ke aakaar ki jheel, jungle aur thandi hawa — Tamil Nadu ka shaant hill station. Kis mahine kahan ghoomein →

Kodaikanal ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Kodaikanal mein sabse zyada garmi April mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 26°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 8°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish August, October aur November mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 1190 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Kodaikanal ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, February, March, April, June, July aur December.

📍 Kodaikanal kahan hai

Kodaikanal rajya ki rajdhani Chennai se lagbhag 440 km dakshin-paschim mein hai. Sabse paas ka bada sheher Bengaluru hai — lagbhag 300 km, uttar disha mein. Aaspaas ke sheher: Bodinayakkanur (30 km), Munnar (50 km), Dindigul (55 km), Valparai (60 km).

🌡️ Kodaikanal aur Bengaluru ke mausam mein farq

  • April mein Kodaikanal ka din Bengaluru se lagbhag 10°C kam garam rehta hai (26°C vs 36°C).
  • January ki raatein Kodaikanal mein 6°C zyada thandi hoti hain (8°C vs 14°C).
  • Kodaikanal mein saal bhar mein zyada baarish hoti hai — kareeb 1,190 mm, jabki Bengaluru mein 940 mm.

👕 Kodaikanal mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • October, November — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, December — Halki thand: subah-shaam sweater ya halki jacket.
  • January — Thand: garam jacket, topi, moze; buzurg aur bachchon ka khaas dhyan rakhein.

🧳 Kodaikanal ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Munnar (50 km) — Door tak faile chai ke bagaan, jharne aur baadal — baarish mein aur bhi hara-bhara.
  • Madurai (80 km) — Meenakshi Amman mandir ke rangeen gopuram aur purana sheher.
  • Kochi (140 km) — Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi ki galiyan aur Kathakali.

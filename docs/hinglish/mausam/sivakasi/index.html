🌦️ Sivakasi ka Mausam Aaj

Sivakasi (Tamil Nadu) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Sivakasi ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Sivakasi mein sabse zyada garmi April mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 38°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 20°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish May, October aur November mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 1220 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Sivakasi ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, June, July aur December.

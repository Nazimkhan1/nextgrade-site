🌦️ Ahmedabad ka Mausam Aaj

Ahmedabad (Gujarat) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Ahmedabad ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Ahmedabad mein sabse zyada garmi May mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 43°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 12°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 790 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Ahmedabad ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, November aur December.

📍 Ahmedabad kahan hai

Ahmedabad rajya ki rajdhani Gandhinagar se lagbhag 25 km dakshin-paschim mein hai. Aaspaas ke sheher: Sanand (20 km), Kalol (25 km), Dholka (35 km), Kadi (40 km).

🌡️ Ahmedabad aur Delhi ke mausam mein farq

  • May mein Ahmedabad ka din Delhi se lagbhag 1°C zyada garam rehta hai (43°C vs 42°C).
  • January ki raatein Ahmedabad mein 5°C kam thandi hoti hain (12°C vs 7°C).
  • Saal bhar ki baarish dono jagah lagbhag barabar hai (kareeb 790 mm).

👕 Ahmedabad mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • April, May, June — Bahut garmi: sooti dheele kapde, topi/gamchha, paani saath rakhein; dopahar 12–4 dhoop se bachein.
  • February, March, October — Garmi: halke sooti kapde, dhoop ka chashma, paani ki bottle.
  • July, August — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • September, November — Suhana mausam: halke kapde kaafi hain; shaam ke liye ek patli layer.
  • January, December — Halki thand: subah-shaam sweater ya halki jacket.

🧳 Ahmedabad ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Mount Abu (170 km) — Rajasthan ka iklauta hill station — Nakki jheel aur Dilwara Jain mandir.
  • Udaipur (210 km) — Jheelon ka sheher — Pichola jheel, City Palace; baarish mein jheelein bhar jaati hain.
  • Chittorgarh (290 km) — Bharat ke sabse bade kilon mein se ek — Vijay Stambh aur Rani Padmini ki kahani.

🌦️ Siwan ka Mausam Aaj

Siwan (Bihar) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Siwan ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Siwan mein sabse zyada garmi May mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 41°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 9°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 890 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Siwan ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, February, October, November aur December.

📍 Siwan kahan hai

Siwan rajya ki rajdhani Patna se lagbhag 100 km uttar-paschim mein hai. Sabse paas ka bada sheher Varanasi hai — lagbhag 170 km, dakshin-paschim disha mein. Aaspaas ke sheher: Chhapra (65 km), Deoria (65 km), Bettiah (65 km), Motihari (75 km).

🌡️ Siwan aur Patna ke mausam mein farq

  • May mein dono shehron ka din ka taapmaan lagbhag barabar rehta hai (41°C).
  • January ki raatein dono jagah lagbhag ek jaisi thandi hoti hain (9°C).
  • Saal bhar ki baarish dono jagah lagbhag barabar hai (kareeb 890 mm).

👕 Siwan mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • April, May, June — Bahut garmi: sooti dheele kapde, topi/gamchha, paani saath rakhein; dopahar 12–4 dhoop se bachein.
  • March — Garmi: halke sooti kapde, dhoop ka chashma, paani ki bottle.
  • July, August, September — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • October, November — Suhana mausam: halke kapde kaafi hain; shaam ke liye ek patli layer.
  • January, February, December — Halki thand: subah-shaam sweater ya halki jacket.

🧳 Siwan ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Varanasi (170 km) — Ganga ke ghaat, shaam ki Ganga aarti aur Kashi Vishwanath mandir.
  • Bodh Gaya (180 km) — Mahabodhi mandir aur Bodhi vriksh — jahan Buddh ko gyaan mila.
  • Ayodhya (220 km) — Shri Ram Janmabhoomi mandir, Saryu ke ghaat aur Hanumangarhi.

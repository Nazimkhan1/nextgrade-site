🌦️ Surat ka Mausam Aaj

Surat (Gujarat) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Surat ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Surat mein sabse zyada garmi April mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 41°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 16°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 1610 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Surat ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, October aur December.

📍 Surat kahan hai

Surat rajya ki rajdhani Gandhinagar se lagbhag 230 km dakshin mein hai. Aaspaas ke sheher: Bilimora (50 km), Ankleshwar (50 km), Bharuch (60 km), Valsad (65 km).

🌡️ Surat aur Delhi ke mausam mein farq

  • April mein Surat ka din Delhi se lagbhag 2°C zyada garam rehta hai (41°C vs 39°C).
  • January ki raatein Surat mein 9°C kam thandi hoti hain (16°C vs 7°C).
  • Surat mein saal bhar mein zyada baarish hoti hai — kareeb 1,610 mm, jabki Delhi mein 780 mm.

👕 Surat mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • March, April, May — Bahut garmi: sooti dheele kapde, topi/gamchha, paani saath rakhein; dopahar 12–4 dhoop se bachein.
  • February, November — Garmi: halke sooti kapde, dhoop ka chashma, paani ki bottle.
  • June, July, August, September — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • January, October, December — Suhana mausam: halke kapde kaafi hain; shaam ke liye ek patli layer.

🧳 Surat ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Shirdi (230 km) — Sai Baba ka Samadhi mandir.
  • Lonavala (280 km) — Mumbai-Pune ke beech — baarish mein jharne, kile aur hari pahaadiyan; chikki mashhoor.
  • Alibag (280 km) — Mumbai se naav ya sadak se — beach aur Kolaba kila.

🌦️ Amritsar ka Mausam Aaj

Amritsar (Punjab) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

🧳 Ghoomne ke liye

Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib), Jallianwala Bagh aur Attari-Wagah border. Kis mahine kahan ghoomein →

Amritsar ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Amritsar mein sabse zyada garmi June mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 42°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 6°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 900 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Amritsar ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, February, March, October, November aur December.

📍 Amritsar kahan hai

Amritsar rajya ki rajdhani Chandigarh se lagbhag 210 km uttar-paschim mein hai. Sabse paas ka bada sheher Dehradun hai — lagbhag 330 km, dakshin-poorv disha mein. Aaspaas ke sheher: Batala (35 km), Kapurthala (55 km), Gurdaspur (70 km), Jalandhar (75 km).

🌡️ Amritsar aur Chandigarh ke mausam mein farq

  • June mein Amritsar ka din Chandigarh se lagbhag 2°C zyada garam rehta hai (42°C vs 40°C).
  • January ki raatein Amritsar mein 3°C zyada thandi hoti hain (6°C vs 9°C).
  • Amritsar mein saal bhar mein kam baarish hoti hai — kareeb 900 mm, jabki Chandigarh mein 1,250 mm.

👕 Amritsar mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • May, June — Bahut garmi: sooti dheele kapde, topi/gamchha, paani saath rakhein; dopahar 12–4 dhoop se bachein.
  • April, September — Garmi: halke sooti kapde, dhoop ka chashma, paani ki bottle.
  • July, August — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • March, October — Suhana mausam: halke kapde kaafi hain; shaam ke liye ek patli layer.
  • February, November, December — Halki thand: subah-shaam sweater ya halki jacket.
  • January — Thand: garam jacket, topi, moze; buzurg aur bachchon ka khaas dhyan rakhein.

🧳 Amritsar ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Dharamshala (150 km) — McLeod Ganj, Tibetan monastery, Dhauladhar ke pahaad aur Triund trek.
  • Katra (150 km) — Mata Vaishno Devi yatra ka shuruaati padaav (lagbhag 12 km ki chadhaai).
  • Kasauli (210 km) — Chandigarh ke paas chhota, shaant hill station — purani imaaratein aur cheed ke ped.

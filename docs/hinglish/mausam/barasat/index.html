🌦️ Barasat ka Mausam Aaj

Barasat (West Bengal) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Barasat ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Barasat mein sabse zyada garmi April mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 39°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 12°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 2090 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Barasat ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, February, November aur December.

📍 Barasat kahan hai

Barasat rajya ki rajdhani Kolkata se lagbhag 20 km uttar-poorv mein hai. Sabse paas ka bada sheher Ranchi hai — lagbhag 330 km, paschim disha mein. Aaspaas ke sheher: Serampore (15 km), Bhatpara (20 km), Bhadreswar (20 km), Habra (20 km).

🌡️ Barasat aur Kolkata ke mausam mein farq

  • April mein dono shehron ka din ka taapmaan lagbhag barabar rehta hai (39°C).
  • January ki raatein Barasat mein 1°C kam thandi hoti hain (12°C vs 11°C).
  • Barasat mein saal bhar mein zyada baarish hoti hai — kareeb 2,090 mm, jabki Kolkata mein 1,700 mm.

👕 Barasat mein kis mahine kya pehnein

  • April — Bahut garmi: sooti dheele kapde, topi/gamchha, paani saath rakhein; dopahar 12–4 dhoop se bachein.
  • March — Garmi: halke sooti kapde, dhoop ka chashma, paani ki bottle.
  • May, June, July, August, September, October — Baarish: chhata ya raincoat, jaldi sookhne wale kapde, grip wale joote.
  • February, November — Suhana mausam: halke kapde kaafi hain; shaam ke liye ek patli layer.
  • January, December — Halki thand: subah-shaam sweater ya halki jacket.

🧳 Barasat ke paas ghoomne ki jagah

  • Kolkata (20 km) — Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, College Street aur mithaiyan.
  • Shantiniketan (130 km) — Rabindranath Tagore ka Vishva Bharati, Sonajhuri haat aur Poush Mela.
  • Digha (160 km) — Kolkata se nazdeek ka popular beach — parivaar ke saath chhoti chhutti ke liye.

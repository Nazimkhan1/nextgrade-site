🌦️ Thanesar ka Mausam Aaj

Thanesar (Haryana) ka abhi ka mausam, aaj aur kal ka haal, aur agle 10 din ka forecast — har ghante update.

Thanesar ka mausam kaisa rehta hai?

Thanesar mein sabse zyada garmi June mein padti hai — tab din ka average temperature lagbhag 41°C rehta hai. Sabse thanda mahina January hai, jab raat mein paara average 7°C tak gir jaata hai. Zyaadatar baarish July, August aur September mein hoti hai; poore saal mein lagbhag 1090 mm baarish hoti hai.

Ghoomne ka achha samay

Thanesar ghoomne ke liye sabse aaraamdaayak mausam: January, February, March, October, November aur December.

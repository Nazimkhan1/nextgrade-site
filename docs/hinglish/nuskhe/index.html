🌿 Nani-Dadi ke Gharelu Nuskhe

Har mausam ke saath ghar mein kuch purane, aazmaaye hue upaay yaad aate hain. Yahan wahi nuskhe hain — aasaan, rasoi ki cheezon se, aur har ek ke saath saavdhaani.

❄️

Sardi ke Gharelu Nuskhe — Nani-Dadi ki baatein

Sardi mein khansi-zukaam, gale ki kharash, band naak, fate honth, fati ediyan aur rukhi twacha ke aasaan gharelu nuskhe — kise nahi lene chahiye, doctor ko kab dikhayein, aur sardi mein ghar ki suraksha.

Garmi aur baarish ke nuskhe jald aa rahe hain.

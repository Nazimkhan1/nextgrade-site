🇮🇳 26 January (Republic Day) par bhashan aur nibandh

School ke bachchon ke liye Gantantra Diwas par aasaan bhashan — chhota, medium aur lamba — saath mein nibandh aur 10 lines. "Sunao" dabakar abhyaas karo ya print nikaal lo.

Din ki poori jaankari

Bhashan ki shuruaat aise karo

Aadarniya pradhanacharya ji, sammanit shikshakgan aur mere pyaare saathiyon, aap sabko Gantantra Diwas ki hardik shubhkaamnayein.

Aaj ke is paavan avsar par mujhe do shabd kehne ka mauka dene ke liye main aap sabka dhanyavaad karta/karti hoon.

Chhota bhashan (1 minute) — class 1 se 4

Aadarniya pradhanacharya ji, shikshakgan aur mere pyaare doston, aap sabko Gantantra Diwas ki badhaai.

Aaj 26 January hai. 26 January 1950 ko hamare desh ka Samvidhan laagu hua tha. Samvidhan mein desh ko chalaane ke niyam likhe hain. Isi din se Bharat ek gantantra bana.

Is din hum tiranga phehraate hain, rashtragaan gaate hain aur desh ke veeron ko yaad karte hain.

Aaiye, hum vaada karein ki hum man lagaakar padhenge, sabse pyaar karenge aur apne desh ka naam roshan karenge. Jai Hind! Jai Bharat!

Medium bhashan (2–3 minute) — class 5 se 8

Aadarniya pradhanacharya ji, sammanit shikshakgan aur mere priya saathiyon, aap sabhi ko 78ve Gantantra Diwas ki hardik shubhkaamnayein.

Aazaadi humein 15 August 1947 ko mili, lekin desh kaise chalega, iske niyam us samay taiyaar nahi the. Iske liye Samvidhan Sabha bani, jisne lagbhag teen saal mehnat karke hamara Samvidhan taiyaar kiya. Samvidhan ki Praroop Samiti ke adhyaksh Dr Bhimrao Ambedkar the. Samvidhan 26 November 1949 ko apnaya gaya aur 26 January 1950 ko laagu hua. Usi din Dr Rajendra Prasad desh ke pehle Rashtrapati bane.

26 January ki tareekh isliye chuni gayi, kyunki 26 January 1930 ko desh ne "Poorna Swaraj" ka sankalp liya tha.

Hamara Samvidhan humein baraabari, aazaadi aur apni baat kehne ka adhikaar deta hai. Saath hi humein kartavya bhi deta hai — kaanoon maanna, sabka samman karna aur desh ki sampatti ki raksha karna.

Is din Nayi Dilli ke Kartavya Path par bhavya parade hoti hai, jismein sena ke jawaan, rajyon ki jhaankiyan aur flypast hote hain.

Aaiye, aaj hum sankalp lein ki hum achhe naagrik banenge, saaf-safaai rakhenge aur desh ki tarakki mein apna hissa denge. Jai Hind!

Nibandh: Gantantra Diwas (lagbhag 250 shabd)

Gantantra Diwas Bharat ka rashtriya parv hai, jo har saal 26 January ko manaya jaata hai. Isi din 1950 mein hamara Samvidhan laagu hua tha aur Bharat ek gantantra bana tha.

Bharat 15 August 1947 ko aazaad hua, lekin desh ko chalaane ke liye apna Samvidhan chahiye tha. Samvidhan Sabha ne Dr Bhimrao Ambedkar ki adhyakshta wali Praroop Samiti ke saath milkar Samvidhan taiyaar kiya, jise 26 November 1949 ko apnaya gaya. 26 January 1950 ko yeh laagu hua aur Dr Rajendra Prasad desh ke pehle Rashtrapati bane. 26 January isliye chuni gayi kyunki 1930 mein isi din Poorna Swaraj ka sankalp liya gaya tha.

Is din Nayi Dilli mein Rashtrapati tiranga phehraate hain aur Kartavya Path par bhavya parade hoti hai. Ismein thal sena, nau sena aur vaayu sena ki tukdiyan, alag-alag rajyon ki jhaankiyan aur bachchon ke rangaarang kaaryakram hote hain. 29 January ko "Beating Retreat" ke saath samaaroh poora hota hai.

Gantantra Diwas humein yaad dilaata hai ki Samvidhan ne humein adhikaar diye hain, to hamare kuch kartavya bhi hain. Humein Samvidhan ka samman karna chahiye, aapas mein bhaichaara rakhna chahiye aur desh ki pragati mein yogdaan dena chahiye.

Gantantra Diwas par 10 lines

  1. Gantantra Diwas har saal 26 January ko manaya jaata hai.
  2. 26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua tha.
  3. Isi din Bharat ek gantantra bana.
  4. Dr Rajendra Prasad Bharat ke pehle Rashtrapati the.
  5. Samvidhan ki Praroop Samiti ke adhyaksh Dr Bhimrao Ambedkar the.
  6. Yeh hamara rashtriya parv hai.
  7. Is din Nayi Dilli ke Kartavya Path par bhavya parade hoti hai.
  8. School mein jhanda phehraaya jaata hai aur rashtragaan gaaya jaata hai.
  9. 29 January ko Beating Retreat ke saath samaaroh poora hota hai.
  10. Yeh din humein apne adhikaar aur kartavya yaad dilaata hai.

Naare aur ant ki lines

  • Jai Hind, Jai Bharat!
  • Samvidhan ka karein samman, yahi hai Bharat ki shaan.
  • Adhikaar bhi, kartavya bhi — yahi hai sachcha gantantra.
  • Ek desh, ek tiranga, ek sankalp — aage badhta Bharat.

Bhashan dene ke aasaan tips

  • Bhashan ko 3–4 baar zor se padhkar abhyaas karo — "Sunao" button se sunkar bhi seekh sakte ho.
  • Dheere aur saaf bolo, beech-beech mein logon ki taraf dekho.
  • Tareekhein aur naam (26 January 1950, Dr Ambedkar, Dr Rajendra Prasad) achhe se yaad kar lo.
  • Ant mein "Jai Hind" zor se aur josh ke saath bolo.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Gantantra Diwas 26 January ko hi kyun manaate hain?

Kyunki 26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua tha. Yeh tareekh isliye chuni gayi kyunki 26 January 1930 ko desh ne Poorna Swaraj ka sankalp liya tha.

2027 mein kaun sa Gantantra Diwas hai?

26 January 2027 ko Bharat 78va Gantantra Diwas manayega.

Chhote bachchon ke liye kaun sa bhashan achha hai?

Class 1 se 4 ke liye upar diya chhota (1 minute ka) bhashan sabse achha hai.

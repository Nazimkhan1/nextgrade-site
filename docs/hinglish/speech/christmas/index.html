🎄 Christmas par Bhashan

School ke Christmas programme ke liye aasaan bhashan — chhota aur medium — saath mein 10 lines aur bolne ke tips. "Sunao" dabakar abhyaas karo ya print nikaal lo.

Din ki poori jaankari

Bhashan ki shuruaat aise karein

Aadarniya Pradhanacharya ji, sammanit shikshakgan aur mere pyaare saathiyo, aap sabko Merry Christmas!

Aaj Christmas ke is khushiyon bhare mauke par mujhe do shabd kehne ka avsar dene ke liye main aap sabka dhanyavaad karta/karti hoon.

Chhota bhashan (1 minute) — Class 1 se 4

Aadarniya Pradhanacharya ji, shikshakgan aur mere pyaare dosto, aap sabko Merry Christmas!

Christmas har saal 25 December ko aata hai. Is din Isa Masih ka janmdin manaya jata hai. Hum Christmas tree sajaate hain, us par lights aur chamakta taara lagate hain.

Santa Claus laal kapdon mein bachchon ke liye tohfe laate hain. Hum cake khaate hain aur carol gaate hain.

Christmas humein sikhata hai ki hum sabse pyaar karein aur apni cheezein doosron ke saath baantein. Dhanyavaad! Merry Christmas!

Medium bhashan (2–3 minute) — Class 5 se 12

Aadarniya Pradhanacharya ji, sammanit shikshakgan aur mere priya saathiyo, aap sabhi ko Christmas ki haardik shubhkamnayein.

Har saal 25 December ko duniya bhar mein Christmas manaya jata hai. Bharat mein ise pyaar se "Bada Din" bhi kehte hain. Isai manyata ke anusaar, isi din Bethlehem mein Mata Mariyam ke ghar Isa Masih ka janm hua tha. Unka janm ek saadharan goshaale mein hua, lekin unka sandesh poori duniya tak pahuncha — prem, daya, seva aur maaf karne ka sandesh.

Christmas se pehle gharon aur churchon ko roshni, sitaaron aur ghantiyon se sajaya jata hai. 24 December ki raat, Christmas Eve par, church mein prarthana hoti hai aur 25 December ko log ek-doosre ko badhaai dete hain, cake baantte hain aur carol gaate hain. Bachchon ke pyaare Santa Claus humein yaad dilate hain ki bina kuch maange doosron ko khushi dena hi sabse bada tohfa hai.

Dosto, hamara Bharat alag-alag dharmon ka sundar bageecha hai. Jaise hum Diwali, Eid aur Gurpurab milkar manate hain, waise hi Christmas bhi hum sabka tyohaar hai. Aaiye, is Christmas hum kisi zarooratmand ki madad karein — kisi ko garam kapda dein, kisi ke chehre par muskaan laayein. Yahi Christmas ki sachchi khushi hai.

Inhi shabdon ke saath main apni baat samaapt karta/karti hoon. Dhanyavaad! Merry Christmas!

Nibandh: Christmas (lagbhag 150 shabd)

Christmas Isai dharm ka sabse bada tyohaar hai, jo har saal 25 December ko manaya jata hai. Is din Isa Masih ka janmdin manaya jata hai, jinhone duniya ko prem aur seva ka sandesh diya.

Tyohaar se pehle log ghar sajaate hain, Christmas tree lagate hain aur us par taara, ghantiyan aur roshni lagate hain. Christmas Eve ki raat church mein prarthana hoti hai. 25 December ko log cake kaatte hain, carol gaate hain aur tohfe dete hain. Santa Claus bachchon ke sabse pyaare mehmaan hote hain.

Bharat mein Goa, Kerala, poorvottar aur bade shehron mein Christmas ki khaas raunak rehti hai. Yeh tyohaar humein sikhata hai ki khushiyan baantne se badhti hain aur humein hamesha doosron ki madad karni chahiye.

Christmas par 10 lines

  1. Christmas ka tyohaar har saal 25 December ko aata hai.
  2. Yeh Isa Masih ke janm ki khushi mein manaya jata hai.
  3. Isa Masih ka janm Bethlehem mein hua tha.
  4. Unki mata ka naam Mariyam tha.
  5. Christmas tree ko rangeen lights aur taare se sajaya jata hai.
  6. Christmas ke geeton ko carol kehte hain.
  7. Santa Claus ko "Father Christmas" bhi kehte hain.
  8. Is din log church jaakar prarthana karte hain.
  9. Cake aur cookies Christmas ki khaas mithaiyan hain.
  10. Christmas prem, shaanti aur bhaichaare ka sandesh deta hai.

Ant ki lines

  • Khushiyan baanto, pyaar phailao — yahi hai Christmas!
  • Jiske dil mein daya hai, uske ghar roz Christmas hai.
  • Merry Christmas aur naye saal ki dheron shubhkamnayein!

Bhashan dene ke aasaan tips

  • Shuruaat aur ant mein muskurakar "Merry Christmas!" zaroor bolein.
  • Chahein to Santa ki topi pehenkar ya chhota-sa taara haath mein lekar bolein — bachchon ko bahut pasand aata hai.
  • Taareekhein yaad rakhein: Christmas Eve 24 December, Christmas 25 December.
  • Dheere aur saaf bolein; chhote bachche sirf chhota bhashan ya 10 lines bol sakte hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Christmas kab manaya jata hai?

Har saal 25 December ko. 24 December ki shaam Christmas Eve hoti hai.

Christmas par bhashan kitna lamba hona chahiye?

Class 1–4 ke liye 1 minute ka chhota bhashan aur Class 5–12 ke liye 2–3 minute ka medium bhashan theek rehta hai.

Christmas ko Bada Din kyon kehte hain?

Bharat mein Christmas ko pyaar se "Bada Din" kehte hain, kyonki yeh Isai dharm ka sabse bada tyohaar hai.

Hisab Kitab: video dekhiye, app chalaiye

Roz ka kharcha, aamdani aur udhar-len-den ek jagah. Is 80 second ke video mein dekhiye ki app kaise chalta hai, phir apnon ko bhejiye.

📒 App kholiye

Video aur app share kariye

Pehle button se video file hi WhatsApp, Instagram ya kisi bhi app mein chali jaati hai, saath mein app ka link bhi. Computer par ya purane phone par link share hota hai.

App kaise chalayein

  1. Hisab Kitab kholiye: hisab.nextgrade.in. Chahein to "Install" dabakar phone par app ki tarah rakh lijiye.
  2. Kharcha likhne ke liye "Kharcha" chuniye, rakam daaliye, shreni (khana-peena, bill, safar…) chuniye aur "Entry jodiye" dabaiye.
  3. Paise aaye to "Aamdani" chuniye. Kisi ko udhar diya ya liya to "Udhar diya" / "Udhar liya" chun kar uska naam likhiye.
  4. Likhna nahi hai? "Bolkar likhein" dabakar boliye, jaise "sabzi 50 rupaye, doodh 60 rupaye". App poori list bana deta hai; check karke "Entry jodiye" dabaiye.
  5. "Roz ka hisab" mein mahine ka kharcha-aamdani, "Len-den" mein kisse kitna lena-dena hai, aur "Statement" mein poore mahine ka hisab dekhiye aur Excel (CSV) ya PDF mein save kariye.

Hisab Kitab kyon

Chhoti dukaan ho, ghar ka budget ho ya doston ka udhar, bahut-se log ab bhi copy mein hisab likhte hain, aur copy kho jaaye to sab chala jaata hai. Hisab Kitab wahi copy hai, aapke phone mein, aur zyada aasaan.

Aapka data aapke paas rehta hai: bina login ke entries isi phone par save hoti hain, aur Google se login karne par aapki apni Google Drive mein. Hamare server par aapka hisab nahi jaata.

App Hindi, English, Hinglish, Bangla aur Urdu mein chalta hai, internet na ho tab bhi khulta hai, aur poori tarah free hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kya Hisab Kitab free hai?

Haan, poori tarah free hai. Koi subscription ya chhupa charge nahi hai.

Kya login zaroori hai?

Nahi. Bina login ke bhi sab chalta hai. Google se login karne par hisab aapki Google Drive mein save rehta hai aur phone badalne par bhi mil jaata hai.

Bolkar likhna kaise kaam karta hai?

Phone ka browser aapki baat ko likhe hue shabdon mein badalta hai, phir app usme se cheezein aur rakam nikaalta hai. Save karne se pehle aap naam ya rakam badal sakte hain.

Purane pate wala hisab kahan gaya?

Hisab Kitab ka naya pata hisab.nextgrade.in hai. Purane pate par is phone mein jo hisab tha, wo apne aap naye pate par aa jaata hai. Bas app dobara install kar lijiye.

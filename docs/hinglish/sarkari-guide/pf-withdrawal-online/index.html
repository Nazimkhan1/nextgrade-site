🏦 PF online kaise nikale

Aakhri baar jaancha gaya: 26 November 2026

Ek nazar mein

KahanEPFO member portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) — UAN aur password se
ZarooriChalu UAN + Aadhaar, PAN aur Aadhaar se juda bank khata (KYC)
FeesKoi fees nahi — EPFO ki online seva free hai
Helpline14470 / 1800-118-005 (EPFO)

Official website kholein ↗

📄 Kya-kya chahiye

  • UAN (Universal Account Number) aur password
  • Aadhaar se juda mobile — OTP ke liye
  • PAN — UAN mein juda ho
  • Bank khata aur IFSC — aapke naam par, Aadhaar se seed (linked) ho
  • Poori nikasi ke liye: pichhli company ne naukri chhodne ki tareekh (Date of Exit) darj ki ho

1UAN se member portal mein login karein

unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in kholein, UAN, password aur captcha daalein. UAN activate nahi hai to portal ke anusaar ab yeh UMANG app mein Aadhaar face authentication se hota hai. Password bhool gaye hon to "Forgot Password" se Aadhaar wale mobile par OTP lekar naya banayein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • UAN
  • Password
  • Captcha

Official website kholein ↗

2KYC poora hai, yeh check karein

Manage → KYC mein dekhein ki Aadhaar, PAN aur bank khata "Approved/Verified" dikh rahe hon. EPFO aur Aadhaar mein naam, janmtithi aur gender ek jaise hone chahiye — farq ho to pehle use theek karayein, warna claim atak sakta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Aadhaar — Verified
  • PAN — Verified
  • Bank khata + IFSC — Verified

Official website kholein ↗

3Online Services → Claim kholein

Upar ke menu mein Online Services → Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D) chunein (naam thoda alag dikh sakta hai). Screen par aapka bank khata dikhega — uske aakhiri ank daalkar Verify karein aur sharten maankar aage badhein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Bank khate ke aakhiri ank
  • Verify
  • Sharten sweekar karein

Official website kholein ↗

4Claim ka type, kaaran aur rakam chunein

Naukri chal rahi hai aur kuch hissa chahiye to PF Advance (Form 31) — phir kaaran ka group chunein aur rakam daalein; portal khud batata hai ki kitni rakam mil sakti hai. Naukri chhod chuke hain aur poora paisa chahiye to Form 19 (PF) aur pension wale hisse ke liye Form 10C — kab se milega, yeh portal par dikhi sharton ke anusaar. Pata bharein; kuch maamlon mein document upload ko kaha ja sakta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Form 31 / 19 / 10C
  • Nikasi ka kaaran
  • Rakam
  • Poora pata

Official website kholein ↗

5Aadhaar OTP se submit karein aur status dekhein

"Get Aadhaar OTP" dabayein, Aadhaar wale mobile par aaya OTP daalkar claim submit karein. Mila claim/reference number sambhalein. Phir Online Services → Track Claim Status mein status dekhein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Aadhaar OTP
  • Claim number sambhalein
  • Track Claim Status

Official website kholein ↗

🔍 Status kaise dekhein

Member portal mein Online Services → Track Claim Status mein claim ki sthiti dikhti hai; passbook.epfindia.gov.in aur UMANG app mein bhi dekh sakte hain, aur registered mobile par SMS aate hain. EPFO ke anusaar jo claim saari jaanch mein pass hote hain woh apne-aap (auto mode) 3 din se kam mein nipat sakte hain; baaki mein zyada samay lagta hai. Atak jaaye to epfigms.gov.in par shikayat ya 14470 par call karein.

❌ In wajahon se aavedan atakta/reject hota hai

  • Bank khata Aadhaar se seed nahi, ya khate ka naam EPFO record se alag — pehle KYC theek karayein
  • Aadhaar aur EPFO mein naam/janmtithi alag — profile sudhaar ke baad dobara claim karein
  • Poori nikasi maangi par pichhli company ne Date of Exit nahi daali — company se dalwayein
  • Partial nikasi ke liye service 12 mahine se kam, ya portal par dikhi seema se zyada rakam
  • Kisi agent/cyber cafe ko UAN password ya OTP de diya — khate se chhedchhad ka khatra; password turant badlein
⚠️ Dhokhe se bachein
  • Sirf .gov.in wali (ya upar di gayi) official site use karein.
  • OTP, Aadhaar ki poori jaankari ya password kisi ko na batayein — phone par bhi nahi.
  • "Pakka kaam" ke naam par zyada paise maangne wale agents se bachein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

PF online nikalne mein kitne din lagte hain?

EPFO ke anusaar jaanch mein pass claim auto mode mein 3 din se kam mein nipat sakte hain; baaki claim mein zyada samay lag sakta hai. Sthiti Track Claim Status mein dekhte rahein.

Naukri ke dauraan PF se kitna paisa nikal sakte hain?

EPFO ne partial nikasi ke kaaran teen groups mein baante hain — zaroori zarooratein (bimari, padhai, shaadi), ghar, aur vishesh paristhitiyan. Kam se kam 12 mahine ki service chahiye aur contribution ka ek hissa minimum balance ke roop mein khate mein rakhna hota hai (kitna, yeh claim form mein dikhta hai — niyam haal mein badle hain). Aapko kitna milega, yeh claim form mein portal khud dikhata hai.

Naukri chhodne ke baad poora PF kab nikal sakte hain?

Berozgari ke baad poori nikasi ki avadhi ko EPFO ne 2025-26 mein badla hai aur is par khabrein alag-alag hain, isliye hum koi tay sankhya nahi likh rahe — claim form mein jo shart dikhe wahi maanya hai. 58 saal par retirement ke baad poori nikasi ho sakti hai.

Kya PF nikalne ke liye agent ko paise dene chahiye?

Nahi. EPFO ki online sevayein free hain aur EPFO ne khud logon ko bina adhikar wale agent aur cyber cafe se bachne ko kaha hai. Sirf epfindia.gov.in / .gov.in wali site use karein aur UAN password ya OTP kisi ko na batayein — EPFO kabhi phone par paise ya OTP nahi maangta.

Claim reject ho gaya to kya karein?

Track Claim Status mein reject ka kaaran likha hota hai. Woh kami (jaise KYC, bank ya naam ka farq) theek karein aur phir se claim daalein. Samajh na aaye to epfigms.gov.in par shikayat karein ya 14470 par call karein.

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. Sarkari websites apne page, button aur fees samay-samay par badalti rehti hain — aap jab karenge tab kuch naam alag dikh sakte hain; tab official site par di jaankari hi maanein. Hum is guide ko update karte rahenge.

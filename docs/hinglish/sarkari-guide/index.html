🏛️ Sarkari Guide — ghar baithe, step by step

Aadhaar, PAN, Ayushman jaise roz ke sarkari kaam kaise karein — har step ke saath sahi (.gov.in) website ka link aur bataya hai ki kaun sa khaana kya bharna hai.

🏠

Aadhaar mein address kaise badlein (online)

Kahan: myAadhaar portal (uidai.gov.in ka) · Zaroori: Aadhaar se juda mobile number (OTP aayega)

🔗

PAN ko Aadhaar se link kaise karein

Kahan: Income Tax ka e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in) · Fees: ₹1,000 (late linking fees) — pehle bharni hoti hai

🪪

Naya PAN card kaise banwayein

Sabse aasaan: Aadhaar se turant e-PAN — incometax.gov.in par, free · Zaroori: Aadhaar + usse juda mobile

🏥

Ayushman card kaise banayein (PM-JAY)

Kahan: beneficiary.nha.gov.in ya Ayushman App · Fees: Card banana free

📥

e-Aadhaar (Aadhaar PDF) download kaise karein

Kahan: myAadhaar portal (uidai.gov.in ka) · Fees: Free

🗳️

Naya voter ID kaise banwayein (Form 6)

Kahan: voters.eci.gov.in ya Voter Helpline app · Fees: Free

🛂

Passport kaise banwayein (online)

Kahan: Passport Seva portal (passportindia.gov.in) / mPassport Seva app · Fees (36 page): Normal ₹2,500 · Tatkaal ₹5,000 (1 July 2026 se)

🗂️

DigiLocker kaise use karein

Kahan: digilocker.gov.in ya DigiLocker app · Fees: Free

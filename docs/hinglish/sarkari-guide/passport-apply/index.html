🛂 Passport kaise banwayein (online)

Aakhri baar jaancha gaya: 26 November 2026

Ek nazar mein

KahanPassport Seva portal (passportindia.gov.in) / mPassport Seva app
Fees (36 page)Normal ₹2,500 · Tatkaal ₹5,000 (1 July 2026 se)
60 pageNormal ₹3,500 · Tatkaal ₹6,000
18 saal se kam36 page normal ₹1,750

Official website kholein ↗

📄 Kya-kya chahiye

  • Pate ka praman — jaise Aadhaar, bijli/paani bill, bank passbook (photo wali)
  • Janmtithi ka praman — jaise birth certificate, 10th marksheet, PAN, Aadhaar
  • Original documents PSK par saath le jaayein

1Portal par register karein

passportindia.gov.in → "Register Now".

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Passport office (aapke pate ke hisaab se)
  • Naam, janmtithi, email, login ID

Official website kholein ↗

2Aavedan form bharein

Login → "Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue".

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Fresh, Normal/Tatkaal, 36/60 page
  • Vyaktigat, parivaar, pata, emergency sampark
  • Pichhle passport (agar ho)

Official website kholein ↗

3Fees bharein aur appointment lein

"Pay and Schedule Appointment" — online fees bharein aur nazdeeki PSK/POPSK par tareekh chunein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • PSK/POPSK
  • Tareekh aur samay
  • Payment

Official website kholein ↗

4PSK par jaayein

Appointment raseed aur original documents lekar tay samay par jaayein — photo aur fingerprint wahin honge.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Appointment raseed
  • Original documents + photocopy

Official website kholein ↗

5Police verification

Police aapke pate par jaanch karegi; uske baad passport daak se aata hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Ghar par maujood rahein
  • Pehchaan ke documents taiyaar rakhein

Official website kholein ↗

🔍 Status kaise dekhein

passportindia.gov.in → "Track Application Status" mein file number (raseed par) aur janmtithi daalein. SMS se bhi update aate hain.

❌ In wajahon se aavedan atakta/reject hota hai

  • Form aur documents mein naam/janmtithi alag
  • Pate ke praman mein pata adhoora
  • Appointment par der se pahunchna ya original documents bhoolna
⚠️ Dhokhe se bachein
  • Sirf .gov.in wali (ya upar di gayi) official site use karein.
  • OTP, Aadhaar ki poori jaankari ya password kisi ko na batayein — phone par bhi nahi.
  • "Pakka kaam" ke naam par zyada paise maangne wale agents se bachein.

🧰 Form ke liye kaam ke tools

Photo aur signature ko sahi size/KB mein karein Photo ko 20/50/100 KB mein karein PDF chhota karein Photo se PDF banayein

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Passport ki fees kitni hai?

1 July 2026 se 36 page ka normal passport ₹2,500 aur Tatkaal ₹5,000; 60 page ₹3,500/₹6,000. Bharne se pehle portal par taaza fees dekhein.

Passport kitne din mein aata hai?

Normal mein aamtaur par kuch hafte (police verification ke hisaab se); Tatkaal mein jaldi.

Kya agent zaroori hai?

Nahi, poora aavedan aap khud online kar sakte hain.

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. Sarkari websites apne page, button aur fees samay-samay par badalti rehti hain — aap jab karenge tab kuch naam alag dikh sakte hain; tab official site par di jaankari hi maanein. Hum is guide ko update karte rahenge.

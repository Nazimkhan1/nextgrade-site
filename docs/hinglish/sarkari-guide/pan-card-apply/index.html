🪪 Naya PAN card kaise banwayein

Aakhri baar jaancha gaya: 26 November 2026

Ek nazar mein

Sabse aasaanAadhaar se turant e-PAN — incometax.gov.in par, free
ZarooriAadhaar + usse juda mobile
Physical cardProtean (NSDL) ya UTIITSL se — fees portal par
Samaye-PAN kuch minute se kuch ghante; card daak se kuch hafte

Official website kholein ↗

📄 Kya-kya chahiye

  • Aadhaar (usi se pehchaan, pata, janmtithi)
  • Aadhaar se juda mobile number
  • Physical card ke aavedan mein photo aur signature — size portal batayega (hamare form tool se theek kar sakte hain)

1e-PAN: "Instant e-PAN" kholein

incometax.gov.in home page → "Instant e-PAN" → "Get New e-PAN".

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Aadhaar number
  • Sahmati wale checkbox

Official website kholein ↗

2OTP aur jaankari ki pushti

Aadhaar se jude mobile par OTP aayega; Aadhaar ki jaankari dikhegi — jaanch kar submit karein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • OTP
  • Email (chahein to) — e-PAN email par bhi aata hai

Official website kholein ↗

3Physical PAN: Protean (NSDL) se

Protean ki site par "New PAN — Indian Citizen (Form 49A)" chunein; Aadhaar e-KYC se form bharein aur fees dein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Application type: New PAN – Indian Citizen (Form 49A)
  • Naam, janmtithi, email, mobile
  • KYC ka tarika — Aadhaar e-KYC
  • Fees (portal par dikhegi)

Official website kholein ↗

4Ya UTIITSL se

UTIITSL ki site par "Apply PAN Card" se bhi aavedan hota hai; tarika milta-julta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Vyaktigat jaankari
  • Document/e-KYC
  • Fees

Official website kholein ↗

🔍 Status kaise dekhein

e-PAN: incometax.gov.in → Instant e-PAN → "Check Status / Download PAN". Protean/UTIITSL: unki site par acknowledgement number se "Track PAN status".

❌ In wajahon se aavedan atakta/reject hota hai

  • Aadhaar mein naam/janmtithi galat ho to pehle Aadhaar sudhaarein
  • Pehle se PAN hai to doosra na banwayein — do PAN rakhna mana hai
  • Photo/signature galat size mein
⚠️ Dhokhe se bachein
  • Sirf .gov.in wali (ya upar di gayi) official site use karein.
  • OTP, Aadhaar ki poori jaankari ya password kisi ko na batayein — phone par bhi nahi.
  • "Pakka kaam" ke naam par zyada paise maangne wale agents se bachein.

🧰 Form ke liye kaam ke tools

Photo aur signature ko sahi size/KB mein karein Photo ko 20/50/100 KB mein karein PDF chhota karein Photo se PDF banayein

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kya PAN card free mein banta hai?

Aadhaar se "Instant e-PAN" incometax.gov.in par free hai; chhapa hua card chahiye to Protean/UTIITSL par fees lagti hai.

e-PAN maanya hai kya?

Haan, e-PAN bhi maanya PAN hai.

Ek se zyada PAN ho sakte hain?

Nahi, ek vyakti ka ek hi PAN hona chahiye; doosra PAN rakhna niyam ke khilaaf hai.

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. Sarkari websites apne page, button aur fees samay-samay par badalti rehti hain — aap jab karenge tab kuch naam alag dikh sakte hain; tab official site par di jaankari hi maanein. Hum is guide ko update karte rahenge.

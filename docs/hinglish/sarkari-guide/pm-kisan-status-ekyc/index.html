🌾 PM Kisan status aur eKYC kaise karein

Aakhri baar jaancha gaya: 26 November 2026

Ek nazar mein

Kahanpmkisan.gov.in → Farmers Corner, ya PM-KISAN mobile app
Paisa₹6,000 saal mein — ₹2,000 ki teen kistein, Aadhaar se jude bank khaate mein
ZaroorieKYC poora + bank khaata Aadhaar se seed + zameen ka record portal par
Agli kistTareekh pmkisan.gov.in par announce hoti hai — andaaze wali khabron par bharosa na karein

Official website kholein ↗

📄 Kya-kya chahiye

  • Aadhaar number
  • Aadhaar se juda mobile — OTP ke liye
  • Registration number (na pata ho to portal par mobile/Aadhaar se nikalta hai)
  • Bank khaata jo Aadhaar se seed ho (DBT ke liye)
  • Naya registration: zameen ke kagaz (khasra/khatauni aadi) aur kai rajyon mein Farmer ID

1"Know Your Status" kholein

pmkisan.gov.in par Farmers Corner mein "Know Your Status" chunein. Registration number aur captcha bharein, phir mobile par aaya OTP daalein. Registration number yaad na ho to usi page par "Know your registration no." se mobile ya Aadhaar daal kar nikaal lein. (Button ka naam thoda alag dikh sakta hai.)

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Registration number
  • Captcha
  • OTP

Official website kholein ↗

2Status ki lines samjhein

Page par har kist ka byora aur kuch zaroori lines dikhti hain — jaise eKYC, Aadhaar-bank seeding aur zameen ka record (land seeding). Jis line mein "No" ya "Pending" ho, wahi theek karna hai; baaki steps usi hisaab se karein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • eKYC status
  • Aadhaar-bank seeding
  • Land seeding

Official website kholein ↗

3eKYC — portal par OTP se

Farmers Corner mein "e-KYC" chunein, Aadhaar number daalein aur Aadhaar se jude mobile par aaya OTP bharein. Yeh sabse aasaan tarika hai, par Aadhaar mein aapka mobile number juda hona chahiye.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Aadhaar number
  • Aadhaar wale mobile ka OTP

Official website kholein ↗

4eKYC — app mein chehre se ya CSC par angoothe se

Aadhaar mein mobile nahi juda to Play Store se sarkari PM-KISAN app lein aur face authentication se eKYC karein (app chehra scan karne ke liye UIDAI ka ek aur app maang sakta hai). Ya paas ke Common Service Centre (CSC) par biometric se karaayein — wahan ki fees centre par poochhein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • PM-KISAN app (sarkari)
  • Chehra scan
  • Ya CSC par angootha

Official website kholein ↗

5Bank khaata Aadhaar se seed karaayein

Paisa DBT se sirf usi khaate mein aata hai jo Aadhaar se seed (NPCI mapping) ho. Apni bank branch mein Aadhaar dekar DBT ke liye seeding karaayein, ya post office mein India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) ka khaata khulwaayein. Naam Aadhaar aur bank mein ek jaisa ho.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Bank mein Aadhaar seeding form
  • Ya IPPB khaata
  • Naam ki spelling milaayein

Official website kholein ↗

6Naya registration aur Farmer ID

Abhi tak naam nahi juda to Farmers Corner mein "New Farmer Registration" karein ya CSC jaayein. Sarkar ne Lok Sabha (August 2025) mein bataya ki 14 rajyon mein naye registration ke liye Farmer ID zaroori hai — aapke rajya mein kya niyam hai, portal ya krishi vibhag se poochhein. Rajya zameen ke record jaanch kar manzoori deta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Aadhaar aur mobile
  • Rajya/zila/gaon
  • Zameen ka byora
  • Farmer ID (agar rajya mein laagu)

Official website kholein ↗

🔍 Status kaise dekhein

Kist ka paisa aur har line ka haal "Know Your Status" mein dikhta hai; PM-KISAN app mein bhi. Sawaal ho to portal ka Kisan-eMitra chatbot (Hindi, Bangla samet 11 bhashayein) poochhein. Shikayat portal ke Help Desk ya pgportal.gov.in (CPGRAMS) par karein. Helpline number pmkisan.gov.in par hi dekhein.

❌ In wajahon se aavedan atakta/reject hota hai

  • eKYC baaki — OTP, app mein chehre ya CSC se poora karein
  • Bank khaata Aadhaar se seed nahi — bank ya IPPB mein karaayein
  • Aadhaar, bank aur record mein naam alag — ek jaisa karaayein
  • Zameen ka record rajya ne abhi verify nahi kiya — tehsil/krishi vibhag se poochhein
  • Income tax dene wale, sarkari karmchari jaise apaatra varg — inhein paisa nahi milta
  • Kisi "agent" ko OTP de diya — OTP kisi ko na batayein, sirf .gov.in site use karein
⚠️ Dhokhe se bachein
  • Sirf .gov.in wali (ya upar di gayi) official site use karein.
  • OTP, Aadhaar ki poori jaankari ya password kisi ko na batayein — phone par bhi nahi.
  • "Pakka kaam" ke naam par zyada paise maangne wale agents se bachein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

PM Kisan ki agli kist kab aayegi?

Kisi kist ki pakki tareekh sarkar pmkisan.gov.in aur PIB par announce karti hai. Social media ki "tareekh" aksar andaaza hoti hai. Tab tak eKYC aur bank seeding poori rakhein taaki kist atke nahi.

22vi kist kab aayi thi?

PIB ke anusaar 22vi kist 13 March 2026 ko Guwahati se jaari hui, kareeb 9.32 crore kisanon ko. Uske baad ki kiston ki jaankari portal par dekhein.

eKYC ke bina kist milegi?

Nahi, portal ke anusaar registered kisanon ke liye eKYC anivarya hai. OTP, PM-KISAN app mein chehre ya CSC par biometric — kisi ek se kar lein.

Status mein sab "Yes" hai phir bhi paisa nahi aaya?

Bank mein poochhein ki Aadhaar DBT ke liye kis khaate se juda hai — paisa usi mein jaata hai. Phir bhi na aaye to portal ke Help Desk par shikayat karein.

Kya yeh sarkari website hai?

Nahi, NextGrade sarkari site nahi hai; hum sirf tarika samjhaate hain. Asli kaam pmkisan.gov.in ya PM-KISAN app par hi karein.

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. Sarkari websites apne page, button aur fees samay-samay par badalti rehti hain — aap jab karenge tab kuch naam alag dikh sakte hain; tab official site par di jaankari hi maanein. Hum is guide ko update karte rahenge.

🎬 Creator Guide — video/blog se kamai kaise shuru karein

Bahut log video bana kar kamaana chahte hain, par pata nahi hota ki kahan se shuru karein, kaun sa form kaise bharein aur kamai kab shuru hoti hai. Yahan har platform ke liye step by step guide hai.

▶️

YouTube channel bana kar kamai kaise shuru karein

Neeche har step ke saath sahi (official) link hai aur bataya hai ki wahan kaun sa khaana kya bharna hai. Phone se bhi ho jaata hai.

📸

Instagram par creator ban kar kamai kaise karein

Instagram par kamai zyaadatar brand deal aur Instagram ke apne features (jahan available hon) se hoti hai. Har step ke saath official link hai.

📘

Facebook par video se kamai kaise shuru karein

Facebook par kamai ke tools (ads, Stars, subscription) page ya professional mode wali profile ko, sharten poori hone par milte hain. Har step ke saath official link hai.

✍️

Blog bana kar kamai kaise shuru karein

Jo likhna aata hai — recipe, padhai, kheti, tech — usi par blog banayein. Har step ke saath official link hai.

💰

YouTube kamai calculator

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. YouTube, Instagram aur Google apne page aur niyam samay-samay par badalte rehte hain — aap jab yeh karenge tab button ya naam thode alag dikh sakte hain. Aisa ho to upar diye official link par taaza jaankari dekhein. Hum bhi is guide ko update karte rahenge.

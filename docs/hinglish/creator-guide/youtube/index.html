▶️ YouTube channel bana kar kamai kaise shuru karein

Neeche har step ke saath sahi (official) link hai aur bataya hai ki wahan kaun sa khaana kya bharna hai. Phone se bhi ho jaata hai.

1Google account banayein (agar nahi hai)

YouTube chalane ke liye Google (Gmail) account chahiye. Channel ke liye alag Gmail banana achha rehta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Naam
  • Janm tithi aur ling
  • Naya Gmail pata
  • Mazboot password
  • Phone number (account surakshit rakhne ke liye)

Official page kholein ↗

2YouTube channel banayein

youtube.com par login karein → upar daayein apni photo → "Create a channel".

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Channel ka naam — chhota, yaad rehne wala
  • @handle — jaise @apnarasoi (baad mein badal sakte hain)
  • Profile photo — chehra ya logo

Official page kholein ↗

3Channel ko sajaayein (YouTube Studio → Customisation)

studio.youtube.com kholein → baayein "Customisation".

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Banner image (2560×1440 px)
  • Description — channel kis baare mein hai, kab naye video aate hain
  • Links — Instagram/website
  • Contact info — business email

Official page kholein ↗

4Pehla video upload karein

Studio mein upar "Create → Upload videos" → video chunein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Title — log kya search karenge, wahi shabd (100 akshar tak)
  • Description — 2–3 line mein video ka saar
  • Thumbnail — saaf chehra/bade akshar (phone verify karne par)
  • Audience — "Kya yeh bachchon ke liye bana hai?" sahi chunna zaroori hai
  • Visibility — Public / Unlisted / Schedule

Official page kholein ↗

5YouTube Partner Program (kamai) mein apply

Studio → "Earn". Sharten poori hote hi Apply ka button aata hai; AdSense account jodna hota hai aur channel ki jaanch hoti hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Sharten (subscribers, watch-time ya Shorts views) — official page par taaza dekhein
  • AdSense account — naam aur pata bilkul bank/ID jaisa
  • Tax jaankari (Studio → AdSense mein)

Official page kholein ↗

6Payment

Kamai AdSense mein jama hoti hai. Tay nyuntam rakam hone par bank account mein aati hai; pehle pate par PIN verification hota hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Bank account ka naam, number, IFSC
  • PIN (daak se aata hai) daal kar pata verify karein

Official page kholein ↗

Kamai ke aur raaste

  • Channel membership, Super Chat, Super Thanks (YPP ke baad)
  • Brand ke saath sponsored video — video mein "Paid promotion" ka nishaan lagayein
  • Apni website/blog — wahan bhi AdSense

⚠️ Yeh galtiyan kabhi na karein

  • Doosron ke video, gaane ya film ke hisse bina anumati na lagayein — copyright strike se channel band ho sakta hai
  • Views/subscribers khareedein nahi, "sub4sub" na karein
  • Apne hi ads par click na karein, na karwayein
  • Bhramak thumbnail ya khatarnak stunt wale video na daalein

Apni andaazan kamai dekhein: YouTube kamai calculator →

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. YouTube, Instagram aur Google apne page aur niyam samay-samay par badalte rehte hain — aap jab yeh karenge tab button ya naam thode alag dikh sakte hain. Aisa ho to upar diye official link par taaza jaankari dekhein. Hum bhi is guide ko update karte rahenge.

📘 Facebook par video se kamai kaise shuru karein

Facebook par kamai ke tools (ads, Stars, subscription) page ya professional mode wali profile ko, sharten poori hone par milte hain. Har step ke saath official link hai.

1Facebook page banayein (ya profile mein professional mode on karein)

facebook.com/pages/create kholein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Page name — channel jaisa naam
  • Category — jaise "Video creator", "Education"
  • Bio — ek line mein aap kya banate hain

Official page kholein ↗

2Page poora karein

Profile aur cover photo, contact jaankari aur username jodein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Profile picture aur Cover photo
  • Username — facebook.com/aapkanaam
  • Contact — email/website

Official page kholein ↗

3Video aur Reels daalein

Page se "Reel" ya "Video" chunein.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Title/Caption — pehli line mein saaf baat
  • Thumbnail
  • Apna banaya video — doosron ka dobara upload nahi

Official page kholein ↗

4Kamai: Professional dashboard

Professional dashboard → "Monetisation" — kaun sa tool milega aur uski sharten kya hain, wahin dikhta hai.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Sharten (followers, dekhne ke minute) — wahin taaza dekhein
  • Payment account — bank ki jaankari, tax jaankari

Official page kholein ↗

5Niyam padhein

Meta ke creator aur monetization niyam — kaun sa content kamai ke laayak nahi.

Yahan kya bharein:

  • Original content
  • Bhramak title ya "like/share karo" wali zabardasti nahi

Official page kholein ↗

Kamai ke raaste

  • Video par ads (jahan available)
  • Stars — viewers seedha support karte hain
  • Brand deal — "Paid partnership" label ke saath

⚠️ Yeh galtiyan na karein

  • Doosron ke video dobara upload (reupload) na karein
  • Likes/followers na khareedein
  • Clickbait aur bhramak thumbnail se bachein

Andaazan kamai dekhein: kamai calculator mein "Facebook video" chunein →

📌 Dhyaan dein: yeh steps aaj ke hisaab se likhe hain. YouTube, Instagram aur Google apne page aur niyam samay-samay par badalte rehte hain — aap jab yeh karenge tab button ya naam thode alag dikh sakte hain. Aisa ho to upar diye official link par taaza jaankari dekhein. Hum bhi is guide ko update karte rahenge.

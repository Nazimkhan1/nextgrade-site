🌙 Aaj ki Islami tareekh

Hijri calendar chaand pe chalta hai. Neeche aaj ki Hijri tareekh, is mahine ka matlab aur saare 12 mahinon ki jaankari hai.

Aaj —

Is mahine ka matlab:

Ye tareekh Umm-ul-Qura calendar se nikaali gayi hai. Chaand dikhne ke hisaab se Bharat mein tareekh aksar ek din baad hoti hai — sahi tareekh apni masjid ya local committee se zaroor poochhein.

Islami calendar ki kuch purani aur dilchasp baatein

  1. Chaand pe chalne wala saal lagbhag 354-355 din ka hota hai — solar saal se kareeb 11 din chhota. Isiliye Ramzan aur Eid har saal kareeb 11 din pehle aate hain, aur lagbhag 33 saal mein Ramzan har mausam se guzar jaata hai.
  2. Hijri calendar doosre Khalifa Hazrat Umar (RA) ke zamaane mein, lagbhag 638 CE mein tay hua. Saal 1 woh saal hai jab Paigambar ﷺ Makkah se Madinah hijrat kar gaye (622 CE).
  3. Mahinon ke naam mausamon pe hain (bahaar, sookha, garmi), kyunki pehle beech-beech mein ek mahina jod kar saal ko mausam se milaaya jaata tha (nasi). Islam ne ye tareeka khatm kiya (Surah Tauba 9:36-37), isliye ab mahine har mausam mein ghoomte hain.
  4. Chaar hurmat wale (pavitra) mahine hain: Muharram, Rajab, Zil-Qad aur Zil-Hijja.
  5. Rajab ko purane zamaane mein "al-Asamm" yaani "behra" kaha jaata tha, kyunki isme hathiyaaron ki awaaz nahi sunaayi deti thi.
  6. Islami tareekh Maghrib (sooraj doobne) se badalti hai. Isliye Shab-e-Barat ya Shab-e-Qadr ki "raat" tareekh se pehle wali shaam se shuru hoti hai.
  7. Jumme ka purana Arabic naam "Yawm al-Arubah" tha. "Jumma" shabd "jama hone" se bana hai.
  8. Kuch log Safar ko manhoos maante hain, par hadees mein Paigambar ﷺ ne farmaaya "La Safar" — yaani Safar mein koi nahoosat nahi.

Aane wali Islami tareekhein (lagbhag)

Rajab shuru (Rajab Mubarak)~10-11 December 2026
Shab-e-Miraj (27 Rajab ki raat)~4-6 January 2027
Shab-e-Barat (15 Shaban ki raat)~22-24 January 2027
Ramzan shuru~8-9 February 2027
Eid ul-Fitr~9-10 March 2027
Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid)~16-17 May 2027
Islami naya saal 1449~6-7 June 2027
Ashura (10 Muharram)~15-16 June 2027

Saari tareekhein andaazan hain aur chaand dikhne pe 1-2 din aage-peeche ho sakti hain.

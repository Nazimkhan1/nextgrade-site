    Games

    🛶 Puzzle game

    Nadi Paar

    Purani dimaagi paheli ab game mein: kisan, sher, bakri aur ghaas ko bina kuch khaaye-piye paar karao — phir Papa aur do bachche, aur 3 sipahi-3 chor. Hint, kam chaal par zyada star, WhatsApp challenge.

     🗼 Puzzle game

    Brahma ki Meenar

    Sone ki thaaliyon ka minaar ek khambe se doosre par le jaao — ek baar mein ek, badi kabhi chhoti ke upar nahi. 3 se 7 thaali, hint aur star.

     🏺 Puzzle game

    Do Matke

    3 aur 5 litre ke matkon se theek 4 litre naapo — bharo, khaali karo, udelo. Phir 4 aur 9 se 6 litre, aur 8-5-3 se 4+4 baanto.

     🔦 Puzzle game

    Raat ka Pul

    Andheri raat, kamzor pul aur ek torch — 4 logon ko 17 minute mein paar karao. Pul par ek baar mein 2, dheeme ki raftaar se. Teen level.

     🪔 Puzzle game

    Diye Jalao

    Ek diya chhuo — woh aur uske padosi badal jaate hain. 3×3, 4×4 aur 5×5 board par saare diye jalao. Hint aur star.

     ⚪ Puzzle game

    Gotiyan

    Brainvita wali purani paheli: goti ke upar se koodo, aakhir mein sirf ek goti bachao — poore 32 goti wale board tak. Wapas aur hint.

     ✨ Puzzle game

    Jaadui Varg

    Khaane bharo taaki har line, column aur tirchhi line ka jod barabar ho — 3×3 se Khajuraho mandir ke 1000 saal purane 4×4 varg tak.

     🧩 Puzzle game

    Khiskao Tasveer

    Tukde khaali jagah mein khiskaao aur dhalte suraj wale mahal ki tasveer poori karo — 3×3 aur 4×4. Hint aur star.

     🇮🇳 Daily GK game

    Bharat Quiz

    Roz 10 sawaal, har ek ke liye 10 second: rajya-rajdhani, rashtriya prateek, itihas, bhugol, vigyan, khel aur sanskriti. Har jawab ke baad ek rochak tathya, vishay-wise practice aur WhatsApp par score challenge.

     🔤 Daily game

    Aaj ka Shabd

    Roz ek naya 5 akshar ka shabd — 6 koshish mein pehchano. Hindi shabd (Roman mein, matlab ke saath) ya English words. Rang batate hain kaunsa akshar sahi hai; result WhatsApp par bhejo aur streak banao.

     🪁 Makar Sankranti game

    Patang Udao

    Apni patang ghumao, doosron ki dor kaato — "Wo kaata!" — apni dor bachao, til-gud laddoo pakdo aur pakshiyon se bacho. 60 second, combo aur WhatsApp challenge.

     💃 Fitness game

    Ghar Baithe Zumba

    Mahilaon ke liye ghar ka dance workout — music ke saath 8 mazedaar moves, cartoon coach jo har move ke saath fit hoti jaati hai, awaaz mein coach, roz ki 🔥 streak aur saheliyon ko challenge.

     🌅 Chhath Puja game

    Chhath Ghat

    Bhor mein nadi mein dupki lagao, ugte suraj ko sahi pal pe arghya do, phir soop mein thekua-kela-ganna pakad ke vrat kholo. Teen stage, ghanti aur paani ki awaaz, WhatsApp challenge.

     💪 Daily game

    Subah ka 2-Minute Challenge

    Cartoon coach ke saath 8 aasaan kasratein — timer, roz ki 🔥 streak, badges aur dosto ko WhatsApp pe challenge. Sirf 2 minute (har kasrat 15 second), bina camera, bina login.

     🎆 New Year game

    Aatishbaazi Pop

    Neeche se uthte rockets tap karke aasmaan mein phodo — sabse upar phodo to Perfect, jaldi-jaldi phodo to combo. Saath mein naye saal ka live countdown.

     🎅 Christmas game

    Santa ke Gift Pakdo

    Santa ki sleigh chalao, girte gifts pakdo, koyle se bacho aur doston ko WhatsApp pe score ka challenge bhejo. Hindi, Hinglish, English aur Bangla mein.

     🎇 New Year game

    Saal ka Safar 2026 → 2027

    Saal bhar ki yaadein chhoo kar daudo, 10…1 countdown ke baad 2027 ke gate se niklo — aatishbaazi aur dhol pe naach-gaane ke saath.

     🐦 Bachpan game

    Chidiya Udd

    Bachpan wala khel: tota udd, kabootar udd… bhains udd? 😂 Sirf udne wali cheez pe tap karo. 48 cheezein, 3 jaan, har baar thoda tez.

     🐍 Board game game

    Saanp-Seedhi

    Bachpan wala board game: computer se ya ek hi phone pe 2–4 doston ke saath khelo. Har seedhi ek achhi aadat, har saanp ek buri.

     🇮🇳 Republic Day game

    Parade ki Taal

    Dhol ki taal pe tap karo, parade kadam se kadam milake chale: fauj, BSF oont, NCC band aur tiranga flypast.

     🌙 Ramzan game

    Chaand Dhundo

    Doorbeen se shaam ke aasmaan mein patla sa naya chaand dhundo, chhupne se pehle — sitara, jahaz aur patang dhoka denge! 10 shaamein.

     🙏 Gurpurab game

    Langar Seva

    Gurpurab par langar ki seva: pangat mein baithi sangat ko prashada, dal, kheer aur jal parosiye. 60 second, bina music, Guru Nanak Dev Ji ki seekhon ke saath.

     🌼 Vasant Panchami game

    Basant ki Sargam

    Maa Saraswati ki veena jaisi sargam: phool ki saat pankhudiyan Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni bajati hain — dhun suno aur dohrao, har round ek swar lambi. Vasant Panchami game.

     🎨 Holi game

    Rang Barsao (Holi)

    Braj ki gali mein jhaankte doston pe sookha gulal udaao — pasandida rang pe double points. Jo mana kare unpe rang nahi, jaanwaron pe bilkul nahi! 60 second.

     🔵 Bachpan game

    Kanche

    Bachpan wale kanche: apna kancha peeche kheench kar chhodo, ghere se saare kanche bahar nikaalo ya goli gucchi mein daalo. 6 level, star — koi daav nahi, sirf nishana.

     🏏 Bachpan game

    Gilli-Danda

    Bachpan wala gilli-danda: pehle tap se gilli uchhalo, doosre se hawa mein zor se maaro. Oonchi shot ya neechi — gaon ke bachche lapakne ko taiyaar hain. Score dandon mein.

     🪨 Bachpan game

    Pitthu (Lagori)

    Bachpan wala Pitthu: gend se saat pattharon ki dheri giraao, phir bade se chhote tak jaldi jamao — saamne wale gend maarenge, jhuk ke bacho. Dheri poori to "Pitthu!"

     🥭 Bachpan game

    Aam Todo — Gulel se

    Bachpan wali gulel: patthar peeche kheencho aur ped se pake peele aam todo — kachche hare aam chhod do, hawa ka dhyan rakho aur madhumakkhi ke chhatte se bacho. 6 level, star.

     🐯 Bachpan game

    Bagh-Bakri

    Purana desi khel: 4 baagh aur 20 bakriyan. Bakri banke baaghon ko gher lo, ya baagh banke 5 bakriyan khao. Computer (aasaan, medium, mushkil) ya ek phone par dost ke saath.

     ❌ Bachpan game

    Kattam-Katti (Zero-Kaata)

    Slate par chalk wala Zero-Kaata: computer ke saath (aasaan, medium, kabhi na haarne wala mushkil) ya ek phone par dost ke saath. Teen ek line mein milao, 5 game ka match.

     🌰 Bachpan game

    Pallanguzhi (Gutte ka khel)

    Nani-dadi wala lakdi ka board: 14 gadde, imli ke beej. Beej bono aur khaali ghar ke baad wala ghar jeeto. Computer (aasaan, medium, mushkil) ya ek phone par dost ke saath.

     🔴 Bachpan game

    Sholo Guti (16 Goti)

    Gaon wala 16 goti ka khel: lakeer ke saath chalo, kood kar saamne wale ki goti maaro, lagaataar kood bhi! Computer (aasaan, medium, mushkil) ya ek phone par dost ke saath.

     🐚 Bachpan game

    Chaupar / Pachisi

    Kapde wali cross bisaat aur chhe kaudiyan: 10 aur 25 pe daan, kile wale khaane, goti maaro aur sabse pehle chaaron goti char-koni mein pahunchao. Computer se ya ek hi phone pe 2–4 log, 2 vs 2 jodi bhi. Sirf mazze ke liye.

     ⚪ Bachpan game

    Nau Gutti

    Mandir ke farsh par khuda purana khel: 9-9 gotiyan, teen chaukor. Teen ek line mein milao aur saamne wale ki goti uthao. Computer (aasaan, medium, mushkil) ya ek phone par dost ke saath.

     👑 Bachpan game

    Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

    Chitthi wala purana khel, ab ek phone par: 4 dost phone ghuma kar apni chitthi dekhein, ya computer ke saath khelo. Mantri ji, chor kaun hai?

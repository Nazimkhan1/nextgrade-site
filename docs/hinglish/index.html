Games

💪 Daily game

Subah ka 2-Minute Challenge

Cartoon coach ke saath 8 aasaan kasratein — timer, roz ki 🔥 streak, badges aur dosto ko WhatsApp pe challenge. Sirf 2 minute (har kasrat 15 second), bina camera, bina login.

 🎆 New Year game

Aatishbaazi Pop

Neeche se uthte rockets tap karke aasmaan mein phodo — sabse upar phodo to Perfect, jaldi-jaldi phodo to combo. Saath mein naye saal ka live countdown.

 🎅 Christmas game

Santa ke Gift Pakdo

Santa ki sleigh chalao, girte gifts pakdo, koyle se bacho aur doston ko WhatsApp pe score ka challenge bhejo. Hindi, Hinglish, English aur Bangla mein.

💍 Shaadi Card Maker

Dulha-dulhan ka naam, date aur jagah bharo, apni pasand ka design aur colour choose karo — aur ek sundar Shubh Vivah invitation card turant banao. Free, phone mein hi banta hai, download karo ya WhatsApp pe bhejo.

Design (template) 👇

Shaadi card kaise banayein

  1. Dulha aur dulhan ka naam, shaadi ki date, din, time aur jagah bharo.
  2. Niche diye design (template) mein se ek choose karo aur apni pasand ka colour/combination set karo.
  3. “Download” dabake card save karo, phir WhatsApp pe bhejo ya print nikaalo.
  4. Poora card aapke device mein hi banta hai — koi jaankari kahin upload nahi hoti.

Shubh Vivah invitation card — ghar baithe, free

Shaadi ke card ke liye aksar ₹100 se ₹900 tak dene padte hain. Yahan aap wahi kaam bilkul free kar sakte ho — naam, date aur jagah bharo, design aur colour choose karo, aur sundar Shubh Vivah card banake seedha WhatsApp pe bhej do.

Yahan bahut ready designs hain — Royal Red, Maroon Gold, Rose Gold, Royal Blue, Green Gold aur bhi. Har design mein colour badal sakte ho, ya do colours ka apna combination bhi choose kar sakte ho. Card HD mein banta hai, print ke liye bhi saaf.

Yeh tool poori tarah aapke phone/computer mein chalta hai — koi naam, photo ya detail kahin upload ya save nahi hoti.

FAQ

Kya shaadi card sach mein free hai?

Haan, bilkul free. Koi hidden charge nahi, koi watermark nahi. Jitne chaho utne card banao.

Card download ya WhatsApp pe kaise bhejein?

“Download” dabane par card PNG image ke roop mein phone mein save ho jata hai, jise WhatsApp/Instagram pe share kar sakte ho. “WhatsApp pe bhejo” button seedha share bhi karta hai.

Kya colour aur design badal sakte hain?

Haan. Bahut templates hain aur har ek mein colour/combination badal sakte ho — red-gold, maroon, royal blue, green, purple, ya apna colour.

Kya ise print kara sakte hain?

Haan, card HD (1080×1500) mein banta hai jo print ke liye accha hai. “Print” button se seedha print nikaal sakte ho.

Kya meri jaankari safe hai?

Haan. Poora card browser mein banta hai — koi detail kahin bheji ya save nahi hoti.

% Percentage Calculator

Kisi number ka kitne percent hota hai turant nikaalein — jaise "500 ka 10% = 50".

Percentage kya hai

Percentage ka matlab hai "sau mein se kitna". Jaise 10% ka matlab hai har 100 mein 10. Yeh calculator kisi number ka diya hua percent turant nikaal deta hai.

Formula

X% of Y = (X × Y) ÷ 100. Yani pehle percent aur number ko multiply karein, phir 100 se divide karein.

Udaharan

500 ka 10% nikaalna hai, to: (10 × 500) ÷ 100 = 5000 ÷ 100 = 50. Yani 500 ka 10% hota hai 50.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

500 ka 10% kitna hota hai?

50. Formula: (10 × 500) ÷ 100 = 50.

Percentage kaise nikaalte hain?

Number ko percent se multiply karke 100 se divide karein: (X × Y) ÷ 100.

Kya yeh calculator free hai?

Haan, bilkul free hai aur aapke phone mein hi chalta hai, koi data kahin nahi jaata.

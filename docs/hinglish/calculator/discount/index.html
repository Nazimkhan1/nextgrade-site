🏷️ Discount (Chhoot) Calculator

Asli price aur discount ka percentage daalein aur turant jaanein kitni bachat hui aur ab price kitni hai.

Discount kaise nikaalein

Sale mein "40% off" likha hota hai, par asli chukani wali price kitni hogi? Yeh calculator price aur discount percentage se bachat aur final price turant nikaal deta hai.

Formula

Bachat = price × discount% ÷ 100. Final price = price − bachat.

Udaharan

₹2,000 ki cheez par 25% chhoot: bachat = 2000 × 25 ÷ 100 = ₹500, final price = 2000 − 500 = ₹1,500.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

₹2000 par 25% chhoot ke baad price kitni hogi?

₹1,500. Aapki bachat ₹500 hogi.

Discount kaise nikaalte hain?

Bachat = price × discount% ÷ 100, aur final price = price − bachat.

Kya isme GST bhi judta hai?

Nahi, yeh sirf discount nikaalta hai. GST ke liye hamara GST calculator istemal karein.

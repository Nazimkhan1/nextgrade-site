📅 Dinon ka Farq Calculator

Do tarikhein chunein aur jaanein unke beech kitne din, hafte, mahine aur saal ka farq hai.

Kis kaam aata hai

Do tarikhon ke beech kitne din hain — yeh jaanna kai baar zaroori hota hai: kisi event mein kitne din bache, kitne din ki chhutti li, ya kisi kaam ko kitne din hue. Yeh calculator yeh farq din, hafte, mahine aur saal mein bata deta hai.

Kaise gina jata hai

Farq = doosri tarikh − pehli tarikh. Ise kul dinon mein, aur phir saal-mahine-din mein baantkar dikhaya jata hai. Leap year ka bhi dhyan rakha jata hai.

Udaharan

1 January 2026 aur 20 November 2026 ke beech ka farq hai 323 din, yani lagbhag 10 mahine aur 19 din, ya 46 hafte aur 1 din.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Do tarikhon ke beech din kaise ginein?

Dono tarikhein chunein; calculator beech ka farq din, hafte, mahine aur saal mein bata dega.

Kya leap year ka dhyan rakha jata hai?

Haan, February 29 wale leap year ko ginti mein shaamil kiya jata hai, isliye nateeja sahi aata hai.

Kya tarikhon ka kram maayne rakhta hai?

Nahi. Aap kisi bhi kram mein tarikhein daalein, calculator hamesha sahi (positive) farq dikhata hai.

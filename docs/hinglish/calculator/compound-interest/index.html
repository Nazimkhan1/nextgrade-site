📊 Compound Interest Calculator (Chakravriddhi Byaj)

Mooldhan, rate, samay aur byaj kitni baar judta hai — daalein aur jaanein chakravriddhi byaj aur total rashi.

Chakravriddhi byaj kya hai

Chakravriddhi byaj (Compound Interest) mein har avdhi ka byaj mooldhan mein jud jata hai, aur agli baar byaj is badi rakam par lagta hai — yani "byaj par bhi byaj". Lambe samay mein yeh sadharan byaj se bahut zyada ho jata hai, isiliye bachat aur nivesh mein yeh bahut taaqatwar hai.

Formula

Total A = P × (1 + r ÷ (100 × n))^(n × t). Chakravriddhi byaj = A − P. Yahan P = mooldhan, r = saalana rate, t = saal, n = saal mein byaj kitni baar judta hai.

Udaharan

₹10,000 par 8% saalana, 3 saal, saalana chakra: A = 10000 × (1.08)³ ≈ ₹12,597. Yani chakravriddhi byaj ≈ ₹2,597 (sadharan byaj ₹2,400 se thoda zyada).

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Chakravriddhi byaj kaise nikaalte hain?

A = P × (1 + r/(100n))^(nt), aur CI = A − P. Yeh calculator yahi ganana apne aap kar deta hai.

"Timahi compounding" ka kya matlab hai?

Matlab byaj har 3 mahine (saal mein 4 baar) judta hai. Bank FD aksar timahi compounding par hoti hain.

Chakravriddhi byaj sadharan byaj se zyada kyon hota hai?

Kyonki isme pehle ka byaj bhi aage byaj kamata hai, isliye samay ke saath rakam tezi se badhti hai.

💰 YouTube Kamai Calculator — 1000 views par kitna paisa?

Mahine ke views, channel ka topic aur viewers kahan se hain — chunein. Hum batayenge andaazan kitni kamai ho sakti hai. Yeh pakki rakam nahi, ek andaaze ki range hai.

RPM aur CPM kya hai

CPM = advertiser har 1000 ad dikhne par kitna deta hai. RPM = har 1000 views par aapko (YouTube ka hissa katne ke baad) kitna milta hai. Apni kamai ka andaaza RPM se lagaiye.

India ke viewers par RPM kam hota hai, videsh (US, UK) ke viewers par kai guna zyada. Paisa, tech aur padhai wale topic par ads mehnge hote hain; bachchon wale aur Shorts par sabse kam.

YouTube par kamai shuru karne ke liye YouTube Partner Program mein judna hota hai — sharten (subscribers, watch-time) samay-samay par badalti hain, taaza sharten YouTube ki official site par dekhein.

Formula

Kamai = (mahine ke views ÷ 1000) × RPM.

RPM ki range topic aur viewers ke hisaab se badalti hai — yahan aam taur par dikhne wali range li gayi hai.

Udaharan

India ke viewers, padhai wala channel, mahine ke 1,00,000 views (lambe video): lagbhag ₹3,250 se ₹11,700 tak mahina.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

YouTube 1000 views par kitna paisa deta hai?

India ke viewers par lambe video ka RPM aam taur par ₹25–₹90 ke aaspaas rehta hai; paisa/tech topic par zyada, manoranjan aur bachchon wale par kam. Shorts par 1000 views ke kuch hi rupaye bante hain.

10 lakh (1 million) views par kitni kamai?

India ke viewers aur aam topic par lambe video se lagbhag ₹25,000–₹90,000. Upar calculator mein apna topic chunkar dekhein.

Kya views khareedne ya apne ad par click karne se kamai badhti hai?

Nahi — aisa karne par channel ya AdSense account band ho sakta hai. Asli viewers aur apna banaya content hi tikaau kamai deta hai.

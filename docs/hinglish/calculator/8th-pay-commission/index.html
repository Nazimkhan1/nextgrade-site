🏛️ 8th Pay Commission Salary Calculator

Apni 7th Pay Commission wali basic pay daalein, fitment factor chunein aur dekhein ki 8th Pay Commission mein nayi basic pay lagbhag kitni ho sakti hai.

8th Pay Commission kya hai

Kendra sarkar har kuch saalon mein vetan aayog banati hai, jo kendriya karmchariyon aur pensionbhogiyon ki salary aur pension ki samiksha karta hai. 8th Pay Commission ki ghoshna January 2025 mein hui. Uski sifarishein aane aur lagu hone ke baad hi asli nayi salary tay hogi.

Tab tak log "fitment factor" se andaza lagate hain — yaani abhi ki basic pay ko kis gunak se guna karke nayi basic pay banegi. 7th Pay Commission mein yeh 2.57 tha. 8th ke liye alag-alag anumaan (lagbhag 1.92 se 2.86) charcha mein hain; koi bhi official nahi hai.

Kaise gina jata hai

Nayi basic pay ≈ abhi ki basic pay × fitment factor (₹100 tak gol kiya gaya).

Nayi salary shuru hote samay DA aam taur par shoonya se shuru hota hai, kyonki purana DA nayi basic mein jud jata hai. Isliye tulna "abhi ki basic + DA" se ki gayi hai.

Udaharan

Basic pay ₹18,000 (Level 1), DA 58%, fitment factor 2.57: nayi basic ≈ 18,000 × 2.57 = ₹46,260 → ₹46,300. Abhi basic + DA = 18,000 + 10,440 = ₹28,440, yaani basic + DA se lagbhag ₹17,860 zyada.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

8th Pay Commission ka fitment factor kitna hoga?

Abhi tay nahi hai. Charcha mein lagbhag 1.92 se 2.86 tak ke anumaan hain. Official factor aayog ki report aur sarkar ke faisle ke baad hi pata chalega.

8th Pay Commission kab lagu hoga?

Iski ghoshna January 2025 mein hui. Lagu hone ki tareekh sarkar ke faisle par nirbhar hai — taaza jaankari ke liye sarkari notification dekhein.

Kya yeh calculator HRA aur doosre bhatte bhi jodta hai?

Nahi, yeh sirf basic pay ka andaza deta hai. HRA, TA jaise bhatton ki nayi dar aayog ki sifarishon par nirbhar karegi.

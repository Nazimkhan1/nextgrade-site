📈 SIP Calculator

Har mahine kitna jama karenge, kitne saal aur kitne % return — daalein aur dekhein aapka paisa kitna banega.

SIP kya hai

SIP yani "Systematic Investment Plan" — mutual fund mein har mahine ek tay rakam jama karna. Lambe samay mein compounding ke karan chhoti rakam bhi badi ban jati hai. Yeh calculator anumaanit return dikhata hai; asli return market par depend karta hai.

Formula

Total = M × [((1+i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1+i). Yahan M = har mahine ki rakam, i = mahine ki return rate (saalana ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = mahinon ki sankhya.

Udaharan

Har mahine ₹5,000, 12% saalana expected return par, 10 saal ke liye. Jama kiya ₹6,00,000, aur total lagbhag ₹11.6 lakh — yani lagbhag ₹5.6 lakh ka anumaanit laabh.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

SIP calculator kaise kaam karta hai?

Yeh har mahine ki jama rashi par compounding lagakar batata hai ki chune gaye saalon mein kul kitna paisa ban sakta hai. Return rate aapka anumaan hai.

Kya yeh return pakka hai?

Nahi. Mutual fund market se jude hote hain, isliye asli return kam ya zyada ho sakta hai. Yeh sirf anumaan hai.

12% par 10 saal mein ₹5000 ki SIP kitni banegi?

Lagbhag ₹11.6 lakh, jisme ₹6 lakh aapka jama aur baaki anumaanit laabh hai.

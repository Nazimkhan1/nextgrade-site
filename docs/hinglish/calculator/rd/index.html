🪙 RD (Recurring Deposit) Calculator

Har mahine kitna jama karenge, kitne mahine aur kitni byaj rate — daalein aur jaanein maturity par total kitna milega.

RD kya hai

RD yani Recurring Deposit — bank ya post office mein har mahine ek tay rakam jama karna, jis par tay byaj milta hai. Yeh un logon ke liye achha hai jo thoda-thoda bachakar ek badi rakam jodna chahte hain. Byaj aam taur par quarterly compound hota hai.

Kaise gina jata hai

Har mahine ki jama rashi alag-alag samay tak byaj kamati hai — pehli kist sabse zyada mahine, aakhri sabse kam. Har kist par quarterly compound byaj jodkar kul maturity nikaali jati hai. Total byaj = maturity − kul jama.

Udaharan

Har mahine ₹2,000, 7% saalana dar par, 12 mahine ke liye: kul jama ₹24,000, aur maturity lagbhag ₹24,900 — yani lagbhag ₹900 ka byaj.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

RD ki maturity kaise nikalti hai?

Har mahine ki kist par uske bache mahinon ke hisaab se quarterly compound byaj joda jata hai, phir sab jodkar maturity nikalti hai. Yeh calculator yahi karta hai.

RD aur FD mein kya farq hai?

FD mein ek baar ekmusht paisa jama hota hai, jabki RD mein har mahine thoda-thoda. Bachat ki aadat daalne ke liye RD achha hai.

Kya RD ka byaj tax yogya hota hai?

Haan, RD ka byaj bhi tax yogya hota hai. Yeh calculator tax se pehle ki rashi dikhata hai.

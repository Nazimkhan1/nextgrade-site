📈 DA (Mehngai Bhatta) Calculator

Apni basic pay aur DA ka pratishat daalein. DA badha hai to nayi dar bhi daalein — dekhein har mahine aur poore saal mein kitna zyada milega.

DA (Mehngai Bhatta) kya hai

Mehngai bhatta (Dearness Allowance) sarkar apne karmchariyon ko badhti mehngai se raahat dene ke liye basic pay ke pratishat ke roop mein deti hai. Pensionbhogiyon ko isi tarah DR (Mehngai Raahat) milti hai.

Kendra sarkar aam taur par saal mein do baar — January aur July se lagu — DA ki dar badalti hai. Nayi dar ka elaan aksar kuch mahine baad hota hai, isliye beech ke mahinon ka bakaya (arrear) bhi milta hai. Taaza dar sarkari notification se jaanch lein.

Formula

DA = basic pay × DA% ÷ 100.

DA badhne par har mahine zyada = basic pay × (naya DA% − purana DA%) ÷ 100.

Udaharan

Basic pay ₹25,500, DA 55% se 58%: pehle DA = ₹14,025, ab DA = ₹14,790. Har mahine ₹765 zyada, saal mein ₹9,180 zyada.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

DA kaise nikaalte hain?

DA = basic pay × DA% ÷ 100. Jaise ₹30,000 basic par 58% DA = ₹17,400.

DA saal mein kitni baar badhta hai?

Kendra sarkar aam taur par saal mein do baar DA badalti hai, jo 1 January aur 1 July se lagu hota hai.

Kya DA par tax lagta hai?

Haan, DA poori tarah taxable income hai aur salary ke saath judkar tax mein gina jata hai.

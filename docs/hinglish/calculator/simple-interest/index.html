💰 Simple Interest Calculator (Sadharan Byaj)

Mooldhan, byaj rate aur samay daalein aur jaanein kitna sadharan byaj banega aur total rashi kitni hogi.

Sadharan byaj kya hai

Sadharan byaj (Simple Interest) wah byaj hai jo hamesha sirf mooldhan par lagta hai — byaj par byaj nahi judta. Yeh school ke maths aur kai loan-bachat yojnaon mein kaam aata hai.

Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (Mooldhan × Rate × Samay) ÷ 100. Total rashi = Mooldhan + byaj. Yahan rate saalana percent mein aur samay saalon mein hai.

Udaharan

₹10,000 par 8% saalana rate se 3 saal ka sadharan byaj = (10000 × 8 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹2,400. Total rashi = 10000 + 2400 = ₹12,400.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Sadharan byaj kaise nikaalte hain?

SI = (Mooldhan × Rate × Samay) ÷ 100. Yeh calculator yahi ganana apne aap kar deta hai.

Sadharan aur chakravriddhi byaj mein kya farq hai?

Sadharan byaj sirf mooldhan par lagta hai, jabki chakravriddhi byaj mein har avdhi ka byaj bhi mooldhan mein judkar agli baar byaj kamata hai.

₹10000 par 8% se 3 saal ka byaj kitna hoga?

₹2,400, aur total rashi ₹12,400.

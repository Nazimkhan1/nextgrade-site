✅ Loan Eligibility Calculator

Har mahine jitni EMI aap aaram se bhar sakte hain, rate aur tenure daalein aur jaanein utni EMI mein kitna loan mil sakta hai.

Yeh kaise kaam karta hai

Loan lete samay pehla sawaal hota hai — mujhe kitna loan mil sakta hai? Yeh is par depend karta hai ki aap har mahine kitni EMI bhar sakte hain. Yeh calculator EMI se ulta chalkar batata hai ki utni kist mein kitna loan sambhav hai.

Formula

Loan P = EMI × [(1+i)ⁿ − 1] ÷ [i × (1+i)ⁿ]. Yahan i = mahine ki byaj rate (saalana ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = mahinon ki sankhya. Yeh EMI formule ka ulta hai.

Udaharan

Agar aap har mahine ₹15,000 EMI bhar sakte hain, byaj rate 9% saalana aur tenure 20 saal hai, to aapko lagbhag ₹16.67 lakh tak ka loan mil sakta hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Mujhe kitna loan mil sakta hai?

Yeh is par depend karta hai ki aap har mahine kitni EMI bhar sakte hain. Aam taur par bank aapki aamdani ka 40–50% tak EMI maante hain. Yahan apni EMI daalkar andaza lagayein.

Yeh asli approval se alag kyon ho sakta hai?

Asli loan approval aamdani, credit score, pehle se chal rahe loan aur bank ki policy par bhi depend karti hai. Yeh sirf EMI-based anumaan hai.

EMI aamdani ka kitna hissa honi chahiye?

Salah di jati hai ki saari EMI milakar aapki monthly aamdani ke 40–50% se zyada na ho, taki baaki kharch aaram se chalein.

Yahan har countdown aapke phone ya computer ki ghadi se Indian time (IST) ke hisaab se chalta hai. Tyohaar ke liye ginti us din ki aadhi raat (raat 12 baje) tak hai, aur exam ke liye pehle paper ke shuru hone ke samay tak.

Tyohaaron ki tareekhein wahi hain jo hamare festival pages par hain — kai panchang dekhkar jaanchi gayi. Tithi wale tyohaar (Holi, Shivratri, Janmashtami) kabhi-kabhi panchang ke hisaab se ek din aage-peeche hote hain, isliye apne shehar ka panchang bhi dekh lein. Eid aur Ramzan chaand dikhne par tay hote hain — isliye un par "anumaanit" likha hai.

Kisi bhi card par tap karke us tyohaar ki poori jaankari, kahani aur wishes ya exam ki date sheet dekh sakte hain.

Aksar puchhe jaane wale sawal

Christmas mein kitne din baaki hain?

Christmas 25 December 2026 (Shukravaar) ko hai — aaj se 27 din baaki.

Countdown kis samay tak ginta hai?

Tyohaar ke din ki shuruaat (raat 12 baje, IST) tak; exam ke liye pehle paper ke samay tak.

Eid ki tareekh "anumaanit" kyon hai?

Eid chaand dikhne par tay hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.

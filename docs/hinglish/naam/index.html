Bacche ka naam kaise chunein?

Achha naam chhota, bolne-likhne mein aasaan aur acche matlab wala hota hai. Yahan har naam ke saath uska matlab diya hai — pehle akshar se apni pasand ka chuno.

Kya ye naam rashi ke hisaab se hain?

Yahan naam pehle akshar (A–Z) se diye hain. Bahut families janm ke nakshatra/rashi ke akshar se naam chunte hain — us akshar par click karke us se shuru hone wale naam dekho.

Kya ye modern naam hain?

Haan, isme aaj ke popular modern aur traditional dono naam hain, sab ke matlab ke saath.

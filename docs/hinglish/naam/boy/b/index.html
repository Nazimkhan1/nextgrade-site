B se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka B Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: ब (बद्रीनाथ, बलदेव, बलराज); भा (भानु, भार्गव, भास्कर); भ (भगीरथ, भरत, भव्य); भू (भूपेंद्र, भूपेश, भूषण); बा (बादल); भै (भैरव); भी (भीम); भु (भुवन); बि (बिपिन); बृ (बृजेश). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Badrinath (Lord Vishnu), Baldev (Lord Balram, god of strength), Balram (Elder brother of Lord Krishna), Bhairav (A form of Lord Shiva), Bhavesh (Lord of the world (Shiva)), Brijesh (Lord of Braj (Krishna))
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Badal (Cloud), Basant (Spring season), Bhagirath (King who brought the Ganga to earth), Bhanu (The sun, ray of light), Bhaskar (The sun), Bhupesh (Lord of the earth, king)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Balraj (King of strength), Balwant (Strong, powerful), Bharat (India; an ancient king), Bhargav (Descendant of sage Bhrigu; Lord Parshuram), Bhavya (Grand, magnificent), Bhim (Mighty; one of the Pandavas), Bhupendra (King of kings), Bhushan (Ornament), Bhuvan (The world), Bipin (Forest)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Bhim. Lambe aur traditional naam: Badrinath, Bhagirath, Bhupendra — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

B se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par B se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Badal, Badrinath, Baldev, Balraj, Balram.

B se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Badrinath, Baldev, Balram, Bhairav, Bhavesh, Brijesh.

B se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Bhim.

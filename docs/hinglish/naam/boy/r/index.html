R se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka R Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: रा (राघव, राहुल, राजीव); र (रजत, रमन, रमेश); ऋ (ऋषभ, ऋषि, ऋत्विक); रे (रेहान, रेयांश); रो (रोहन, रोहित); रौ (रौनक); रु (रुद्र); रू (रूपेश). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Raghav (Lord Rama), Ram (Lord Ram), Ramesh (Husband of Lakshmi; Lord Vishnu), Reyansh (Ray of light; Lord Vishnu), Rudra (Lord Shiva)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Rajeev (Lotus), Rakesh (Full moon, lord of the night), Ravi (The sun), Rohit (Red; the rising sun)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Rahul (Efficient; son of Gautam Buddha), Rajat (Silver), Rajesh (King of kings), Raman (Pleasing, beloved), Ranjit (Victorious in battle), Ranveer (Hero of the battle), Rehan (Fragrant herb, sweet basil), Rishabh (Superior, excellent), Rishi (Sage), Ritvik (A priest who performs rituals), Rohan (Ascending, growing), Ronak (Brightness, glow), Rupesh (Lord of beauty)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Ram, Ravi.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

R se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par R se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Raghav, Rahul, Rajat, Rajeev, Rajesh.

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Raghav, Ram, Ramesh, Reyansh, Rudra.

R se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

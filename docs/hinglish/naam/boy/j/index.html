J se ladke ke naam

J se shuru hone wale ladkon ke naam, matlab ke saath. · 22 naam

  • जगन्नाथ JagannathLord of the universe; Lord Vishnu
  • जगत JagatThe world
  • जगदीश JagdishLord of the world
  • जगमोहन JagmohanOne who charms the world; Lord Krishna
  • जयदेव JaidevGod of victory; poet Jayadeva
  • जलज JalajLotus
  • जनक JanakFather; King Janak
  • जनार्दन JanardanLord Vishnu
  • जसकरण JaskaranOne who does praiseworthy deeds
  • जसमीत JasmeetFriend of glory
  • जसवंत JaswantFamous, glorious
  • जतिन JatinOne with matted hair (Shiva)
  • जावेद JavedEternal

  • जुगल JugalPair; Radha and Krishna

J se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka J Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: ज (जगन्नाथ, जगत, जगदीश); जि (जिग्नेश, जिष्णु, जितेंद्र); जी (जीत, जीवन); जा (जावेद); जु (जुगल). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Jagannath (Lord of the universe; Lord Vishnu), Jagmohan (One who charms the world; Lord Krishna), Jaidev (God of victory; poet Jayadeva), Janardan (Lord Vishnu), Jatin (One with matted hair (Shiva)), Jugal (Pair; Radha and Krishna)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Jalaj (Lotus), Jawahar (Jewel)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Jagat (The world), Jagdish (Lord of the world), Janak (Father; King Janak), Jaskaran (One who does praiseworthy deeds), Jasmeet (Friend of glory), Jaswant (Famous, glorious), Javed (Eternal), Jay (Victory), Jayesh (Lord of victory), Jeet (Victory), Jeevan (Life), Jignesh (Curiosity), Jishnu (Victorious; Arjun), Jitendra (Conqueror of the senses)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Jay, Jeet. Lambe aur traditional naam: Jagannath, Jagmohan, Janardan, Jaskaran, Jitendra — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

J se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par J se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Jagannath, Jagat, Jagdish, Jagmohan, Jaidev.

J se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Jagannath, Jagmohan, Jaidev, Janardan, Jatin, Jugal.

J se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Jay, Jeet.

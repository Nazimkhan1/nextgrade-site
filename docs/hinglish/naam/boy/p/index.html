P se ladke ke naam

P se shuru hone wale ladkon ke naam, matlab ke saath. · 22 naam

P se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka P Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: प् (प्रभात, प्रदीप, प्रकाश); पु (पुलकित, पुनीत, पुरुषोत्तम); प (पराग, पवन); पा (पारस, पार्थ); पं (पंकज); पी (पीयूष); पृ (पृथ्वीराज). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Purushottam (Supreme being; Lord Vishnu)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Pankaj (Lotus), Pawan (Wind, breeze), Piyush (Nectar), Prabhat (Morning, dawn), Pradeep (Lamp, light), Prakash (Light), Prithviraj (King of the earth), Pushkar (Lotus; a holy lake)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Parag (Pollen), Paras (Touchstone that turns iron to gold), Parth (Arjun (the warrior)), Pranav (The sacred syllable Om), Pranay (Love), Prashant (Calm, tranquil), Pratap (Glory, valour), Pratham (First, foremost), Pratik (Symbol), Prem (Love), Pritam (Beloved), Pulkit (Thrilled, happy), Puneet (Pure, holy)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Prem. Lambe aur traditional naam: Prashant, Prithviraj, Purushottam — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

P se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par P se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Pankaj, Parag, Paras, Parth, Pawan.

P se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Purushottam.

P se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Prem.

C se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka C Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: चं (चंदन, चंद्र, चंद्रगुप्त); च (चक्रधर, चक्रपाणि, चक्षु); चि (चिदानंद, चिन्मय, चिराग); चै (चैतन्य); चा (चाणक्य); चे (चेतन); चिं (चिंतन). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Chakradhar (Holder of the discus; Lord Vishnu), Chakrapani (Discus in hand; Lord Vishnu), Chandrashekhar (Moon-crested; Lord Shiva), Charan (Feet (of the divine)), Chaturbhuj (Four-armed; Lord Vishnu), Chidanand (Consciousness and bliss; Lord Shiva)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Chaman (Garden), Chandra (The moon), Chandragupt (Protected by the moon; Maurya emperor), Chandrakant (Beloved of the moon, moonstone), Chandramohan (Charming like the moon), Chandresh (Lord of the moon), Chirag (Lamp, light)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Chaitanya (Consciousness), Chakshu (Eye), Chanakya (Great scholar, Kautilya), Chandan (Sandalwood), Chetan (Aware, full of life), Chinmay (Full of knowledge), Chintan (Thought, reflection), Chiranjeev (Long-lived), Chirantan (Eternal)

Lambe aur traditional naam: Chaitanya, Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chanakya, Chandragupt, Chandrakant — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

C se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par C se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Chaitanya, Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chakshu, Chaman.

C se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chandrashekhar, Charan, Chaturbhuj, Chidanand.

C se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Is akshar ke naam zyada lambe hain; pukaarne ke liye inka pehla hissa chhote naam ki tarah chal sakta hai.

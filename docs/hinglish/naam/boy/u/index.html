U se ladke ke naam

U se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka U Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: उ (उचित, उदय, उद्भव); ऊ (ऊर्जित). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Uddhav (Friend of Lord Krishna), Umakant (Husband of Uma; Lord Shiva), Umashankar (Uma and Shankar; Lord Shiva), Umesh (Lord of Uma; Lord Shiva), Upendra (Lord Vishnu)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Uday (Rising, dawn), Urvish (Lord of the earth), Utpal (Blue lotus)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Uchit (Right, proper), Udbhav (Origin, creation), Udit (Risen, awakened), Ugrasen (King of Mathura), Ujjwal (Bright), Ulhas (Joy, delight), Umaid (Hope), Umang (Enthusiasm), Unmesh (Revelation, blossoming), Upamanyu (Devoted disciple; name of a sage), Urjit (Energetic, powerful), Utkarsh (Progress, prosperity), Utsav (Festival, celebration), Uttam (The best)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Uday, Udit. Lambe aur traditional naam: Umashankar, Upamanyu — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

U se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par U se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Uchit, Uday, Udbhav, Uddhav, Udit.

U se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Uddhav, Umakant, Umashankar, Umesh, Upendra.

U se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Uday, Udit.

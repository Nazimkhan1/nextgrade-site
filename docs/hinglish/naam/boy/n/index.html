N se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka N Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: न (नचिकेत, नकुल, नमन); नि (निहाल, निखिल, निमिष); नी (नील, नीरज, नीलेश); नं (नंदन, नंदकिशोर); ना (नारायण). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Nandkishore (Son of Nand; Lord Krishna), Narayan (Lord Vishnu), Nilesh (Blue lord; Lord Krishna), Niranjan (Spotless; Lord Shiva)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Neeraj (Lotus), Nishant (Dawn, end of night)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Nachiket (Boy who learned wisdom from Yama), Nakul (One of the Pandavas), Naman (Salutation), Nandan (Son; one who gives joy), Narendra (King of men), Naresh (King), Navdeep (New lamp), Naveen (New, fresh), Navneet (Fresh butter), Neel (The colour blue), Nihal (Happy, content), Nikhil (Whole, complete), Nimish (Blink of an eye, moment), Nirmal (Pure, clean), Nirvaan (Liberation, bliss), Nitin (Master of the right path)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Neel. Lambe aur traditional naam: Nachiket, Nandkishore, Narendra, Niranjan — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

N se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par N se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Nachiket, Nakul, Naman, Nandan, Nandkishore.

N se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Nandkishore, Narayan, Nilesh, Niranjan.

N se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Neel.

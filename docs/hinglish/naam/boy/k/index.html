K se ladkon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka K Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: क (कबीर, कमल, कमलेश); कु (कुबेर, कुलदीप, कुणाल); कौ (कौशल, कौशिक, कौस्तुभ); के (केदार, केशव, केतन); कि (कियान, किशोर); कै (कैलाश); का (कार्तिक); कृ (कृष्ण); क् (क्षितिज). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Kailash (Abode of Lord Shiva), Kamlesh (Lord of the lotus; Lord Vishnu), Kartik (Son of Shiva; a month), Kaustubh (Gem of Lord Vishnu), Kedar (Lord Shiva), Keshav (Lord Krishna), Kishore (Youthful; Lord Krishna), Krishna (Lord Krishna), Kuber (God of wealth), Kush (Son of Lord Rama)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Kamal (Lotus), Kunal (Lotus; son of Emperor Ashoka)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Kabir (Great; the saint Kabir), Kanishk (Famous Kushan emperor), Kapil (Name of a great sage), Karan (Generous warrior Karna; skilful), Kaushal (Skill), Kaushik (Sage Vishwamitra), Ketan (Home, flag), Kiaan (King, grace), Kshitij (Horizon), Kuldeep (Lamp of the family)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Kush. Lambe aur traditional naam: Kaustubh — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

K se ladkon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par K se shuru hone wale ladkon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Kabir, Kailash, Kamal, Kamlesh, Kanishk.

K se ladkon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Kailash, Kamlesh, Kartik, Kaustubh, Kedar, Keshav, Kishore, Krishna.

K se ladkon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Kush.

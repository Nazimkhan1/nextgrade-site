H se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka H Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: ह (हरिणी, हरिप्रिया, हरलीन); हि (हिना, हिबा, हिमजा); हे (हेमा, हेमांगी, हेमवती); हं (हंसा, हंसिका); ही (हीर, हीरा); हु (हुमा). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Haripriya (Beloved of Vishnu; Goddess Lakshmi), Harleen (Absorbed in God), Hemavati (Goddess Parvati), Himaja (Daughter of the Himalaya; Goddess Parvati), Himani (Snow; Goddess Parvati), Hiranmayi (Golden; Goddess Lakshmi)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Huma (Mythical bird of good fortune)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Hansa (Swan), Hansika (Swan; sweet), Harini (Doe, deer), Harshada (Giver of joy), Harshita (Joyful), Hasina (Beautiful), Heena (Henna), Heer (Diamond), Heera (Diamond), Hema (Golden), Hemangi (Golden-bodied), Hemlata (Golden creeper), Hetal (Friendly, affectionate), Hiba (Gift), Hiya (Heart)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Heer, Hema, Hiba, Hiya, Huma. Lambe aur traditional naam: Haripriya, Harshada, Harshita, Hemavati, Hiranmayi — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

H se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par H se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Hansa, Hansika, Harini, Haripriya, Harleen.

H se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Haripriya, Harleen, Hemavati, Himaja, Himani, Hiranmayi.

H se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Heer, Hema, Hiba, Hiya, Huma.

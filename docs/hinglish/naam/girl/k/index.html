K se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka K Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: क (कल्पना, कल्याणी, कमला); का (काजल, कामाक्षी, काश्वी); को (कोकिला, कोमल); कृ (कृपा, कृति); कौ (कौशिकी); के (केतकी); खु (खुशी); कि (कियारा); की (कीर्ति); कु (कुसुम). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Kalyani (Auspicious; Goddess Parvati), Kamakshi (A form of Goddess Parvati), Kamala (Lotus; Goddess Lakshmi), Kaushiki (Goddess Durga)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Kaveri (Holy river Kaveri), Ketaki (A fragrant flower), Kiara (Bright, light), Kusum (Flower)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Kajal (Kohl), Kalpana (Imagination), Kanika (Small particle), Karishma (Miracle), Kashvi (Shining, radiant), Kasturi (Musk, fragrance), Kavita (Poem), Kavya (Poetry), Khushi (Happiness, joy), Kirti (Fame, glory), Kokila (Cuckoo), Komal (Soft, gentle), Kripa (Grace, kindness), Kriti (Creation)

Lambe aur traditional naam: Kamakshi, Karishma, Kaushiki — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

K se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par K se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Kajal, Kalpana, Kalyani, Kamakshi, Kamala.

K se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Kalyani, Kamakshi, Kamala, Kaushiki.

K se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Is akshar ke naam zyada lambe hain; pukaarne ke liye inka pehla hissa chhote naam ki tarah chal sakta hai.

Y se ladki ke naam

Y se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke naam, matlab ke saath. · 15 naam

  • यामिनी YaminiNight
  • यमुना YamunaThe river Yamuna
  • यशस्विनी YashasviniFamous, successful
  • याशिका YashikaFame, success
  • यशोदा YashodaMother of Krishna
  • यशोधरा YashodharaWife of Gautama Buddha
  • यशवी YashviSuccessful, famous
  • यासमीन YasminJasmine flower
  • योगेश्वरी YogeshwariGoddess Durga
  • योगिनी YoginiFemale yogi; form of the goddess
  • योगमाया YogmayaGoddess Yogmaya
  • युक्ति YuktiIdea, skill
  • युसरा YusraEase, comfort
  • यूथिका YuthikaA kind of jasmine
  • युविका YuvikaYoung woman

Y se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka Y Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: य (यमुना, यशस्विनी, यशोदा); या (यामिनी, याशिका, यासमीन); यो (योगेश्वरी, योगिनी, योगमाया); यु (युक्ति, युसरा, युविका); यू (यूथिका). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Yashoda (Mother of Krishna), Yogeshwari (Goddess Durga), Yogini (Female yogi; form of the goddess), Yogmaya (Goddess Yogmaya)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Yamuna (The river Yamuna), Yasmin (Jasmine flower)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Yamini (Night), Yashasvini (Famous, successful), Yashika (Fame, success), Yashodhara (Wife of Gautama Buddha), Yashvi (Successful, famous), Yukti (Idea, skill), Yusra (Ease, comfort), Yuthika (A kind of jasmine), Yuvika (Young woman)

Lambe aur traditional naam: Yashasvini, Yashodhara, Yogeshwari — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Y se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par Y se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 15 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Yamini, Yamuna, Yashasvini, Yashika, Yashoda.

Y se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Yashoda, Yogeshwari, Yogini, Yogmaya.

Y se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Is akshar ke naam zyada lambe hain; pukaarne ke liye inka pehla hissa chhote naam ki tarah chal sakta hai.

R se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka R Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: र (रचना, रजनी, रम्या); रे (रेखा, रेणु, रेवती); रा (राधा, रागिनी, रानी); ऋ (ऋद्धि, ऋतु); रो (रोहिणी, रोशनी); रु (रुचि, रुक्मिणी); रू (रूही, रूपाली); रि (रिया). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Radha (Krishna's beloved), Rukmini (Wife of Lord Krishna)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Rashmi (Ray of light), Revati (A star; wife of Balarama), Rewa (River Narmada), Rohini (A star; mother of Balarama), Roshni (Light)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Rachna (Creation), Ragini (Melody), Rajni (Night), Ramya (Beautiful, pleasing), Rani (Queen), Rasika (Connoisseur of art), Rati (Wife of Kamadeva), Rekha (Line), Renu (Particle; pollen), Riddhi (Prosperity), Ritu (Season), Riya (Singer; graceful), Ruchi (Interest, taste), Ruhi (Soul, spiritual), Rupali (Beautiful)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Rani, Rati, Renu, Rewa, Ritu, Riya, Ruhi.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

R se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par R se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Rachna, Radha, Ragini, Rajni, Ramya.

R se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Radha, Rukmini.

R se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Rani, Rati, Renu, Rewa, Ritu, Riya.

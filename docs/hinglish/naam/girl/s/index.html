S se ladki ke naam

S se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke naam, matlab ke saath. · 22 naam

  • सान्वी SaanviGoddess Lakshmi
  • साक्षी SakshiWitness
  • सना SanaPraise; brilliance
  • सरस्वती SaraswatiGoddess Saraswati of learning
  • सावित्री SavitriWife of Satyavan; a goddess
  • शालिनी ShaliniModest, graceful
  • शनाया ShanayaFirst ray of sun; eminent
  • शिल्पा ShilpaSculpture, craft
  • श्रेया ShreyaAuspicious, best

S se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka S Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: स् (स्मिता, स्नेहा, स्वरा); सु (सुधा, सुहानी, सुनीता); सा (सान्वी, साक्षी, सावित्री); स (सना, सरस्वती); श् (श्रेया, श्रुति); सि (सिमरन, सिया); शा (शालिनी); श (शनाया); शि (शिल्पा); सी (सीता); सो (सोनाली). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Saanvi (Goddess Lakshmi), Saraswati (Goddess Saraswati of learning), Savitri (Wife of Satyavan; a goddess), Simran (Remembrance of God), Sita (Goddess Sita, wife of Rama), Siya (Goddess Sita)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Shanaya (First ray of sun; eminent), Sudha (Nectar), Swati (A star)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Sakshi (Witness), Sana (Praise; brilliance), Shalini (Modest, graceful), Shilpa (Sculpture, craft), Shreya (Auspicious, best), Shruti (The Vedas; hearing), Smita (Smile), Sneha (Love, affection), Sonali (Golden), Suhani (Pleasant, lovely), Sunita (Well-behaved, of good conduct), Sushma (Beauty, grace), Swara (A musical note)

Chhote aur pukaarne mein aasaan naam: Sana, Sita, Siya. Lambe aur traditional naam: Saraswati — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

S se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par S se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Saanvi, Sakshi, Sana, Saraswati, Savitri.

S se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Saanvi, Saraswati, Savitri, Simran, Sita, Siya.

S se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Chhote aur bolne mein aasaan naam: Sana, Sita, Siya.

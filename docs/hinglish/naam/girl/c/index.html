C se ladkiyon ke naamon ke baare mein

English ka C Hindi mein kai aawaazon ke liye aata hai. Is list ke naam Hindi mein aise shuru hote hain: चं (चंपा, चंदा, चंदना); चि (चिन्मयी, चित्रा, चित्रलेखा); चा (चारु, चारुलता, चार्वी); च (चकोरी, चमेली); चै (चैताली); चाँ (चाँदनी); चे (चेतना); छ (छवि); छा (छाया). Naam rakhte waqt Hindi spelling bhi tay kar lo, taaki birth certificate, Aadhaar aur school mein ek jaisi likhi jaaye.

Matlab ke hisaab se naam

  • Bhagwan aur devi-devtaon se jude: Chhaya (Shade; wife of Sun god)
  • Prakriti se jude (suraj, chaand, phool, nadi…): Chakori (Bird enamoured of the moon), Chameli (Jasmine flower), Champa (Champak flower), Chanda (Moon), Chandrakala (Phase of the moon), Chandralekha (Crescent of the moon), Chandramukhi (Moon-faced), Chandrima (Moon, moonlight)
  • Gun aur bhaavna wale: Chaitali (Born in the month of Chaitra), Chandana (Sandalwood), Chandni (Moonlight), Chandrika (Moonlight), Charu (Beautiful), Charulata (Beautiful creeper), Charvi (A beautiful woman), Chetna (Consciousness, awareness), Chhavi (Image, beauty), Chinmayi (Full of consciousness, blissful), Chitra (Picture), Chitralekha (Picture; a celestial nymph), Chitrangada (Wife of Arjuna)

Lambe aur traditional naam: Chaitali, Chandana, Chandrakala, Chandralekha, Chandramukhi, Chandrika — inse pyaar ka chhota naam bhi aasaani se ban jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

C se ladkiyon ke kitne naam yahan hain?

Is page par C se shuru hone wale ladkiyon ke 22 naam hain, har naam ke matlab ke saath. Kuch naam: Chaitali, Chakori, Chameli, Champa, Chanda.

C se ladkiyon ke bhagwan se jude naam kaun se hain?

Is list mein ye naam bhagwan ya devi-devtaon se jude hain: Chhaya.

C se ladkiyon ka chhota naam kaun sa accha hai?

Is akshar ke naam zyada lambe hain; pukaarne ke liye inka pehla hissa chhote naam ki tarah chal sakta hai.

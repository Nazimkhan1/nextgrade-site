Sampark karein

Sujhav, kisi page mein galti, ya kisi app se juda sawaal — hamein email karo.

Email: contact@nextgrade.in

Hum aam taur pe 2-3 working days mein jawaab dete hain. Email mein password, OTP ya bank ki jaankari kabhi mat bhejna.

Kin baaton ke liye likho

  • Galti ki soochna — kisi tyohar ki tareekh, kahani ya jaankari mein galti ho to page ka link aur sahi jaankari bhejo.
  • Sujhav — koi tyohar, kahani, wish ya game jo aap yahan dekhna chahte ho.
  • App ya game mein dikkat — phone ka naam, browser (Chrome/Safari) aur kya hua, ye likho.
  • Copyright ya content se judi shikayat — page ka link aur apni baat saaf-saaf likho, hum jaldi action lenge.
  • Partnership ya business ki baat — school, sanstha ya brand ke saath kaam ke liye.

Hamare baare mein

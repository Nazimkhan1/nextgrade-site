NextGrade ke baare mein

NextGrade (nextgrade.in) Bharat mein bani ek chhoti, independent website hai.

Hum kya karte hain

Hum teen tarah ki cheezein banate hain: tyoharon ki simple aur bharosemand jaankari — kahani, mahatva, parampara, pakwan aur wishes; tyoharon ke chhote games; aur free web apps, jaise kharche aur udhaar ka hisaab rakhne wala Hisab Kitab aur Wish Card Maker. Site Hindi, English, Hinglish aur Bangla mein hai.

Hamari jaankari

Tyoharon ke pages aam taur pe maani jaane wali paramparaon, dharmik granthon ki mashhoor kathaon aur public sources pe based hain. Jin tyoharon ki tareekh tithi se tay hoti hai, unke liye hum mahina aur tithi likhte hain, kyunki English tareekh har saal badalti hai. Koi galti dikhe to hamein batao — hum jaldi theek karenge.

Hamare apps aur aapka data

Hum kisi server ya database mein aapki jaankari nahi rakhte. Apps aapka data aapke hi device pe rakhte hain, aur Wish Card Maker aapka naam ya message kahin nahi bhejta — card aapke browser mein hi banta hai.

Yahan aapko kya milega

  • Tyoharon ki guide — Christmas se Diwali tak har bade tyohar ki kahani, mahatva, reeti-riwaaz, pakwan aur aksar puchhe jaane wale sawaal.
  • Wishes — Good Morning, birthday, anniversary, Jumma Mubarak jaise roz ke mauko ke liye hamare apne likhe message, copy aur WhatsApp button ke saath.
  • Kahaniyan — Panchatantra, Akbar-Birbal, Tenali Raman jaisi purani kahaniyan, hamare apne shabdon mein, awaaz mein sunne aur night mode ke saath.
  • Islamic calendar, tyoharon ke games, naam wale animated wish aur Wish Card Maker.

Hum kaise likhte hain

Har page hum khud likhte hain — kisi doosri website ya kitaab se copy nahi karte. Likhne se pehle hum kai public sources, panchang aur dharmik paramparaon ki jaani-maani baatein mila kar dekhte hain. Jahan alag-alag ilaakon mein alag maanyataayein hain, wahan hum ye saaf likhte hain. Dharmik vishayon pe hum izzat ke saath aur bina kisi paksh ke likhte hain, aur tareekhon ke liye hamesha local panchang, Gurdwara, masjid ya church se confirm karne ki salaah dete hain.

Ads aur kharcha

NextGrade poori tarah free hai. Site chalane ka kharcha Google AdSense ke ads se nikalta hai. Ads kabhi game ya card ke upar nahi aate, aur hum kisi se bhi ads pe click karne ko nahi kehte. Zyada jaankari ke liye Privacy Policy dekho.

Sudhaar aur sujhav

Hum har saal tyoharon ki tareekhein aur jaankari dobara check karte hain. Agar aapko koi galti, purani jaankari ya kisi nayi kahani/tyohar ka sujhav dena hai, to contact page se hamein likho — har email padha jaata hai.

🕌 Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) · ईद-उल-अज़हा (बक़रीद)

📅 Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 2027 kab hai?

17 May 2027, Somvaar (anumaanit) · 172 din baaki

Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 17 May 2027, Somvaar ko hone ki ummeed hai. Eid chaand dikhne par hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Eid ul-Adha, jise Bakrid bhi kehte hain, tyaag aur Allah pe bharose ki Eid hai. Yeh Hazrat Ibrahim (alaihissalam) ki us qurbani ki yaad dilaati hai, jab woh Allah ke hukm pe apni sabse pyaari cheez qurbaan karne ko taiyaar ho gaye the. Yahi samay Hajj ka bhi hota hai.

KabIslami (Hijri) calendar ke 12vein mahine Zilhijja ki 10 tareekh — English tareekh har saal lagbhag 10-11 din pehle aati hai (chaand ke elaan pe tay)
KahanPoore Bharat aur duniya bhar mein

Qurbani ki kahani

Islami parampara ke mutabiq Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) ne khwaab mein dekha ki woh apne bete Hazrat Ismail (AS) ko Allah ki raah mein qurbaan kar rahe hain. Baap aur beta dono Allah ke hukm ke liye taiyaar ho gaye. Tab Allah ne Ismail ki jagah ek mendha bhej diya.

Yeh kahani sikhaati hai ki Allah pe poora bharosa rakho aur uski raah mein apni pyaari cheez dene se bhi peeche mat hato.

Eid kaise manayi jaati hai

Subah naha kar, naye ya saaf kapde pehen kar log Eidgah ya masjid mein Eid ul-Adha ki namaaz padhte hain aur ek-doosre ko gale laga kar "Eid Mubarak" kehte hain.

Jin pe qurbani wajib hoti hai, woh jaanwar ki qurbani karte hain. Gosht teen hisson mein baanta jaata hai — ek apne parivaar ke liye, ek rishtedaaron-doston ke liye aur ek zaroorat-mandon ke liye.

Qurbani karte waqt saaf-safaai aur local kanoonon ka dhyaan rakhna aur padosiyon ki bhavnaon ki izzat karna bhi Eid ki seekh ka hissa hai.

Hajj aur Zilhijja

Zilhijja Islami saal ka aakhri mahina hai. Iski 8 se 12 tareekh ke beech Makka mein Hajj hota hai, aur 9 tareekh ko Arafat ka din hota hai. Eid ul-Adha Hajj ke inhi dinon mein aati hai.

Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 2027 kab hai?

Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 17 May 2027, Somvaar ko hone ki ummeed hai. Eid chaand dikhne par hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.

Eid-ul-Adha (Bakrid) kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Yeh tyohar Hazrat Ibrahim (A.S.) ki us qurbani ki yaad mein manaya jaata hai, jab woh Allah ke hukm par apni sabse pyaari cheez — apne bete — ko qurbaan karne ko taiyaar ho gaye the aur Allah ne bete ki jagah ek mendha bhej diya. Yeh Hajj ke dinon mein, Zil-Hijja ki 10 tareekh ko aata hai. Qurbani ka gosht teen hisson mein baanta jaata hai — parivaar, rishtedaar aur gareebon ke liye.

Bakrid kab manayi jaati hai?

Islami calendar ke Zilhijja mahine ki 10 tareekh ko, jo Eid ul-Fitr ke lagbhag do mahine das din baad aati hai. Sahi tareekh chaand dikhne ke elaan se tay hoti hai.

Eid ul-Fitr aur Eid ul-Adha mein kya farq hai?

Eid ul-Fitr Ramzan ke rozon ke baad aati hai (Meethi Eid). Eid ul-Adha Hajj ke dinon mein qurbani ki Eid hai.

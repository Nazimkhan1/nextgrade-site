🌅 Chhath Puja · छठ पूजा

📅 Chhath Puja 2027 kab hai?

4 November 2027, Guruvaar · 345 din baaki

Chhath Puja 4 November 2027, Guruvaar ko hai. Nahay-Khay 2 Nov, Kharna 3 Nov, Sandhya Arghya 4 Nov, Usha Arghya 5 Nov. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Chhath Puja Surya Dev aur Chhathi Maiya ki upasana ka chaar din ka mahaparv hai. Isme vrati lagbhag 36 ghante ka nirjala vrat rakh ke nadi ya taalaab mein khade ho kar doobte aur phir ugte suraj ko arghya dete hain. Ye prakriti, saaf-safaai aur anushaasan ka tyohar maana jaata hai — isme na koi murti zaroori hai, na purohit.

KahanBihar, Jharkhand, poorvi UP aur Nepal ke Terai mein sabse bada; Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata aur videsh mein bhi

Chaar din ki puja

Pehla din — Nahay-Khay: vrati nadi ya taalaab mein snaan karte hain aur ghar saaf karke saattvik khaana — kaddu-bhaat aur chane ki daal — ek baar khaate hain.

Doosra din — Kharna: din bhar upvaas ke baad shaam ko gud ki kheer (rasiya) aur roti ka prasad banta hai. Prasad khaane ke baad se lagbhag 36 ghante ka nirjala vrat shuru hota hai.

Teesra din — Sandhya Arghya: shaam ko parivaar thekua, phal aur ganne se saje baans ke soop aur daura le ke ghat pe jaata hai. Vrati paani mein khade ho kar doobte suraj ko arghya dete hain.

Chautha din — Usha Arghya: bhor se pehle phir ghat pe pahunch ke ugte suraj ko arghya diya jaata hai. Iske baad prasad kha ke vrat khola jaata hai, jise parana kehte hain.

Thekua, soop aur kosi

Thekua Chhath ka sabse khaas prasad hai — gehun ke aate, gud aur ghee se bana, saanche pe daba ke tala hua. Iske saath kela, nariyal, ganna, singhaada, daabh nimbu aur mausami phal soop mein rakhe jaate hain.

Kai gharon mein Sandhya Arghya ki raat aangan ya ghat pe kosi bhari jaati hai — ganne ke gatthar se chhatra jaisa bana ke uske neeche mitti ke diye aur prasad rakhe jaate hain. "Kelwa ke paat par ugelan Suraj Mal" aur "Kaanch hi baans ke bahangiya" jaise paaramparik Chhath geet poori raat gaaye jaate hain.

Chhath kyun khaas hai

Chhath mein doobte suraj ki bhi puja hoti hai — ye sikhaata hai ki dhalte waqt ka bhi samman karo. Ghaaton ki safaai poora mohalla mil ke karta hai, aur ameer-gareeb sab ek hi ghat pe ek jaisa arghya dete hain.

Vrat bahut kathin hota hai, isliye buzurgon, beemaaron aur garbhwati mahilaon ko doctor aur ghar ke badon ki salaah se hi vrat rakhna chahiye. Ghat pe bachchon ko gehre paani se door rakhein.

Chhath Puja 2027 kab hai?

Chhath Puja 4 November 2027, Guruvaar ko hai. Nahay-Khay 2 Nov, Kharna 3 Nov, Sandhya Arghya 4 Nov, Usha Arghya 5 Nov.

Chhath Puja kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Chhath Puja Surya Dev aur Chhathi Maiya ko dhanyavaad dene ka parv hai — jeevan, oorja, achhi fasal aur parivaar ki sukh-samriddhi ke liye. Vrati chaar din niyam se upvaas rakhte hain aur nadi ya taalaab mein khade ho kar doobte aur ugte suraj ko arghya dete hain.

Chhath Puja 2026 mein kab hai?

Nahay-Khay 13 November, Kharna 14 November, Sandhya Arghya 15 November (Raviwar) aur Usha Arghya 16 November (Somwar) ko hai. Arghya ka samay apne shehar ke sooryast-sooryoday ke hisaab se local panchang se dekhein.

Kharna kya hota hai?

Chhath ka doosra din, jab vrati din bhar upvaas ke baad shaam ko gud ki kheer aur roti ka prasad khaate hain. Iske baad se nirjala vrat shuru hota hai.

Thekua kaise banta hai?

Gehun ka aata, gud ka ghol, ghee aur thodi saunf/nariyal mila ke sakht aata goondhte hain, saanche pe daba ke aakaar dete hain aur dheemi aanch pe ghee ya tel mein sunehra hone tak talte hain.

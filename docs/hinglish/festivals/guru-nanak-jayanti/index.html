🙏 Guru Nanak Jayanti · गुरु नानक जयंती

📅 Guru Nanak Jayanti 2027 kab hai?

14 November 2027, Ravivaar · 353 din baaki

Guru Nanak Jayanti 14 November 2027, Ravivaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Guru Nanak Jayanti Sikh dharm ke sansthaapak aur pehle Guru, Guru Nanak Dev Ji ke prakash (janm) ka parv hai. Ise Gurpurab ya Prakash Parv bhi kaha jaata hai aur ye Sikh samudaay ka sabse bada tyohar maana jaata hai.

KabKartik Purnima (aam taur pe November); ise Gurpurab ya Prakash Parv bhi kehte hain
KahanPoore Bharat aur duniya bhar ke Sikh samudaay mein; Punjab mein sabse bada utsav

Guru Nanak Dev Ji

Guru Nanak Dev Ji ka janm 1469 mein Rai Bhoi di Talwandi mein hua tha, jo aaj Pakistan mein Nankana Sahib ke naam se jaana jaata hai. Sikh parampara mein unka Prakash Parv Kartik Purnima ko manaya jaata hai.

Unhone "Ik Onkar" — yaani Ishwar ek hai — ka sandesh diya. Unki shikshaon ke teen mool siddhant maane jaate hain: Naam Japna (Ishwar ka smaran), Kirat Karni (imaandaari se mehnat ki kamaai) aur Vand Chhakna (apni kamaai zaroorat-mandon ke saath baantna). Unhone jaati-bhed aur oonch-neech ka virodh kiya aur sabko baraabar maana.

Gurpurab kaise manate hain

Parv se do din pehle gurudwaron mein Akhand Path shuru hota hai — Guru Granth Sahib ka lagbhag 48 ghante tak bina ruke poora paath.

Ek din pehle Nagar Kirtan nikalta hai, jiski aguvaai Panj Pyare karte hain. Palki mein Guru Granth Sahib ko le jaaya jaata hai, sangat shabad-kirtan gaati hai aur gatka (Sikh martial art) ka pradarshan hota hai.

Tyohar se pehle ke dinon mein subah-subah Prabhat Pheriyan nikalti hain. Parv ke din gurudwaron mein kirtan, katha aur ardaas hoti hai, aur langar mein har dharm aur jaati ke log ek saath baith kar bhojan karte hain.

Langar aur seva

Langar (saamudaayik rasoi) ki parampara Guru Nanak Dev Ji ne shuru ki thi. Yahan har koi zameen pe pangat mein baith kar ek jaisa bhojan karta hai — ameer-gareeb, kisi bhi dharm ka. Gurpurab pe bahut se log gurudware mein seva karte hain — rasoi mein madad, bartan dhona ya joote sambhaalna.

  • Ik Onkar — Guru Ji ka sandesh aapke jeevan mein sachchai, seva aur prem bhare. Prakash Parv ki shubhkamnayein!
  • Naam japo, kirat karo, vand chhako — Guru Nanak Jayanti ki haardik badhaai!
  • Waheguru aapko chardi kala mein rakhein. Guru Nanak Dev Ji ke Prakash Parv ki dher saari shubhkamnayein!

Guru Nanak Jayanti 2027 kab hai?

Guru Nanak Jayanti 14 November 2027, Ravivaar ko hai.

Guru Nanak Jayanti kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Guru Nanak Jayanti (Prakash Purab) Sikhon ke pehle Guru, Guru Nanak Dev Ji ke janm ki yaad mein manaayi jaati hai. Unhone ek Ishwar, sabki barabari, imaandaari ki kamaai aur mil-baant kar khaane (langar) ki seekh di. Yeh Kartik Purnima ko manaayi jaati hai.

Guru Nanak Jayanti kab manayi jaati hai?

Kartik maas ki Purnima ko, jo aam taur pe November mein padti hai. English tareekh har saal badalti hai.

Akhand Path kya hai?

Guru Granth Sahib ka shuru se ant tak lagbhag 48 ghante mein bina ruke kiya jaane wala paath, jo Gurpurab se do din pehle shuru hota hai.

Gurpurab pe kya keh kar badhaai dete hain?

"Gurpurab diyan lakh-lakh vadhaiyan" ya "Happy Gurpurab" — dono aam taur pe kahe jaate hain.

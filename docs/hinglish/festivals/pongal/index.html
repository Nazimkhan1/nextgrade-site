🍚 Pongal · पोंगल

📅 Pongal 2027 kab hai?

15 January 2027, Shukravaar · 50 din baaki

Pongal 15 January 2027, Shukravaar ko hai. Bhogi 14 January ko; Thai Pongal 15 January ko. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Pongal Tamil Nadu ka fasal ka tyohar hai, jo Surya, dharti, baarish aur kheton mein saath dene wale pashuon ko shukriya kehne ke liye manaya jaata hai. Uttar Bharat mein isi mausam mein Makar Sankranti aur Lohri aati hai.

KabAam taur pe 14 ya 15 January se chaar din — Tamil mahine "Thai" ki shuruaat pe
KahanTamil Nadu, Puducherry aur duniya bhar mein rehne wale Tamil parivaar

"Pongal" ka matlab

Tamil mein "pongal" ka arth hai ubal kar uphanna. Naye mitti ke bartan mein naye chawal, doodh aur gud pakaaye jaate hain, aur jab doodh uphan kar bahar aata hai to sab milkar "Pongalo Pongal!" bolte hain — yaani ghar mein khushhaali bhar-bhar ke aaye.

Meethe sakkarai pongal ke alawa namkeen ven pongal bhi banta hai. Ghar ke aangan mein chawal ke aate se sundar kolam (rangoli) banayi jaati hai aur ganna zaroor hota hai.

Pongal ke chaar din

Bhogi: purani, bekaar cheezein hata kar ghar ki safaai aur nayi shuruaat.

Thai Pongal: mukhya din, Surya Dev ko pongal ka bhog lagaya jaata hai.

Mattu Pongal: kheti mein saath dene wali gaay-bailon ko nehla kar sajaya jaata hai aur unki pooja hoti hai.

Kaanum Pongal: rishtedaaron se milna, ghoomne jaana aur badon ka aashirvaad lena.

  • Nayi fasal, nayi mithaas aur nayi shuruaat — aapko aur aapke parivaar ko Happy Pongal!
  • Surya Dev ki kirpa se aapka saal dhan-dhaanya aur sehat se bhara rahe. Pongal mubarak!
  • Kolam ke rang aur ganne ki mithaas aapki zindagi mein bani rahe. Happy Pongal!

Pongal 2027 kab hai?

Pongal 15 January 2027, Shukravaar ko hai. Bhogi 14 January ko; Thai Pongal 15 January ko.

Pongal kyun manaya jaata hai?

Pongal Tamil Nadu ka fasal ka tyohar hai, jismein Surya Dev, dharti aur kheti mein madad karne wale pashuon (gaay-bail) ko dhanyavaad diya jaata hai. Naye chaawal, doodh aur gud se "pongal" pakaya jaata hai; bartan se ufan kar bahar aana ghar mein khushhaali ka shubh sanket maana jaata hai. Yeh chaar din chalta hai.

Pongal ka kya matlab hai?

Tamil mein "pongal" ka matlab hai ubal kar uphanna. Naye bartan mein chawal-doodh-gud ka uphanna ghar mein khushhaali aane ka prateek maana jaata hai.

Mattu Pongal kya hai?

Pongal ka teesra din, jab kheti mein madad karne wali gaayon aur bailon ko nehla kar, seeng rang kar aur maala pehna kar unki pooja ki jaati hai.

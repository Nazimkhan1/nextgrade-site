🎄 Christmas · क्रिसमस

📅 Christmas 2026 kab hai?

25 December 2026, Shukravaar · 27 din baaki

Christmas 25 December 2026, Shukravaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Christmas Yeshu (Jesus Christ) ke janm ki khushi mein manaya jaane wala tyohar hai. Aaj ye sirf Isai samudaay ka nahi, balki har us insaan ka tyohar ban gaya hai jo pyaar, baantne aur naye saal ki umeed mein vishwas rakhta hai.

Christmas kyun manate hain

Isai manyata ke anusaar Yeshu ka janm Bethlehem mein ek saadharan gaushala mein hua tha. Unka sandesh tha — ek-doosre se pyaar karo, zaroorat-mand ki madad karo aur maaf karna seekho. Christmas isi sandesh ko yaad karne ka din hai.

25 December ki tareekh chauthi sadi se Rome mein manayi jaane lagi aur dheere-dheere poori duniya mein yahi tareekh apna li gayi.

Kaise manate hain

24 December ki raat "Christmas Eve" hoti hai. Churchon mein aadhi raat ki prarthana (midnight mass) hoti hai aur carols gaaye jaate hain.

Gharon mein Christmas tree sajaya jaata hai — us par taare, ghantiyan, rang-birangi lights aur chhote tohfe latkaye jaate hain. Bachchon ke liye Santa Claus tohfe laate hain, ye kahani har bachche ki pasandeeda hai.

Bharat mein plum cake, cookies aur Goa-Kerala mein khaas pakwan bante hain. Log ek-doosre ke ghar mithai aur cake bhejte hain.

Is Christmas kuch achha karo

Kisi anaathaalay ya vriddhashram mein thoda samay bitao, purane garam kapde zaroorat-mandon ko do, ya kisi akele padosi ko cake de aao. Christmas ka asli matlab yahi hai.

Christmas 2026 kab hai?

Christmas kis tareekh ko aata hai?

Christmas har saal 25 December ko manaya jaata hai. 24 December ki shaam "Christmas Eve" kehlaati hai.

Christmas tree kyun sajate hain?

Sadabahar ped sardiyon mein bhi hara rehta hai, isliye ise jeevan aur umeed ka prateek maana jaata hai. Saja hua ped ghar mein khushi aur raunak laata hai.

Bharat mein Christmas kahan sabse zyada manaya jaata hai?

Goa, Kerala, Mizoram, Nagaland, Meghalaya aur Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru jaise shehron mein.

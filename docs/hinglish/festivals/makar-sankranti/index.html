🪁 Makar Sankranti · मकर संक्रांति

📅 Makar Sankranti 2027 kab hai?

15 January 2027, Shukravaar · 55 din baaki

Makar Sankranti 15 January 2027, Shukravaar ko hai. Zyadatar panchang 15 January batate hain; kuch panchang aur kai rajyon ki chhutti list mein 14 January hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Makar Sankranti woh din hai jab Surya Dhanu rashi se Makar rashi mein pravesh karta hai. Ye un gine-chune Hindu tyoharon mein se hai jo Surya ki chaal se tay hote hain, isliye iski tareekh lagbhag har saal ek jaisi rehti hai.

Uttarayan aur iska mahatva

Is din se Surya ki uttar disha ki yatra (Uttarayan) shuru maani jaati hai aur din dheere-dheere bade hone lagte hain. Ise shubh samay maana jaata hai — log Ganga ya pavitra nadiyon mein snaan karte hain aur til, gud, khichdi, kambal waghera ka daan karte hain.

Ek tyohar, kai naam

Gujarat mein ise Uttarayan kehte hain aur poora aasmaan patangon se bhar jaata hai. Tamil Nadu mein yahi samay Pongal ka hai, Assam mein Magh Bihu (Bhogali Bihu), UP aur Bihar mein Khichdi, aur Maharashtra mein log tilgul baantte hue kehte hain — "Tilgul ghya, god god bola" (til-gud lo aur meetha-meetha bolo).

Til-gud kyun

Sardiyon mein til aur gud shareer ko garmi aur taakat dete hain. Til-gud baantna rishton mein mithaas banaye rakhne ka prateek bhi hai.

Patang udaate samay Chinese manjhe se bacho — ye pakshiyon aur raahgeeron ke liye jaanleva hota hai, aur kai rajyon mein is pe rok hai.

  • Surya Dev aapke jeevan ko roshni aur oorja se bhar dein. Happy Makar Sankranti!
  • Tilgul ghya, god god bola! Aapko aur aapke parivaar ko Sankranti ki badhaai.
  • Aasmaan mein udti patang ki tarah aapke sapne bhi oonche udein. Happy Uttarayan!

Makar Sankranti 2027 kab hai?

Makar Sankranti 15 January 2027, Shukravaar ko hai. Zyadatar panchang 15 January batate hain; kuch panchang aur kai rajyon ki chhutti list mein 14 January hai.

Makar Sankranti ki tareekh lagbhag ek jaisi kyun rehti hai?

Kyunki ye chandrama se nahi, Surya ke Makar rashi mein pravesh se tay hoti hai. Isliye ye zyadatar 14 January ko aur kabhi-kabhi 15 January ko padti hai.

Sankranti pe kya daan karte hain?

Til, gud, khichdi (chawal-daal), kambal aur garam kapde daan karne ki parampara hai.

🇮🇳 Republic Day · गणतंत्र दिवस

📅 Republic Day 2027 kab hai?

26 January 2027, Mangalvaar · 63 din baaki

Republic Day 26 January 2027, Mangalvaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan lagoo hua aur Bharat ek samprabhu, loktantrik ganrajya bana. Gantantra Diwas isi aitihasik din ki yaad mein manaya jaata hai.

KabHar saal 26 January
KahanPoora Bharat — rashtriya parv

Samvidhan ki kahani

Samvidhan Sabha ne lagbhag teen saal ki mehnat ke baad 26 November 1949 ko Samvidhan apnaya (is din ko ab Samvidhan Diwas kehte hain). Drafting committee ke adhyaksh Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar the.

26 January ki tareekh isliye chuni gayi kyunki 26 January 1930 ko Congress ne "Poorna Swaraj" ka sankalp liya tha.

Kaise manate hain

Nayi Dilli mein Kartavya Path pe bhavya parade hoti hai, jisme sena ki tukdiyan, rajyon ki jhaankiyan aur saanskritik karyakram dikhaye jaate hain. Is din Rashtrapati tiranga fehraate hain.

Schools aur offices mein jhanda fehraya jaata hai, rashtragaan gaaya jaata hai aur deshbhakti ke karyakram hote hain.

Tirange ka samman

Jhanda fehraate samay kesariya rang upar rahe, jhanda zameen ko na chhuye aur kata-phata na ho. Karyakram ke baad kaagaz ke jhande sadak pe na phenkein, unhe samman se sambhaal kar rakhein.

Republic Day 2027 kab hai?

Republic Day 26 January 2027, Mangalvaar ko hai.

Gantantra Diwas kyun manaya jaata hai?

Kyunki 26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua aur Bharat ek samprabhu, loktantrik ganrajya bana. Yeh tareekh isliye chuni gayi kyunki 26 January 1930 ko Poorna Swaraj ka sankalp liya gaya tha.

Republic Day aur Independence Day mein kya farq hai?

Independence Day (15 August) 1947 mein angrezi shaasan se azaadi ka din hai. Republic Day (26 January) 1950 mein Samvidhan lagoo hone ka din hai.

26 January ko jhanda kaun fehraata hai?

Gantantra Diwas pe Rashtrapati tiranga fehraate hain; Swatantrata Diwas pe Pradhan Mantri Laal Qile se jhanda fehraate hain.

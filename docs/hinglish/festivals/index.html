Bharat ke tyohar

Har tyohar ka apna page — kyun manate hain, kaise manate hain, kya pakwan bante hain, aur apno ko bhejne ke liye wishes. List aane wale tyoharon ke order mein hai.

📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

🙏

Guru Nanak Jayanti · गुरु नानक जयंती

Kartik Purnima (aam taur pe November); ise Gurpurab ya Prakash Parv bhi kehte hain

 🌅

Chhath Puja · छठ पूजा

Kartik Shukla Chaturthi se Saptami tak, Diwali ke chhah din baad (2026 mein 13–16 November)

 🎄

Christmas · क्रिसमस

Har saal 25 December

 🎆

New Year · नया साल

Har saal 1 January (31 December ki raat se jashn)

 🔥

Lohri · लोहड़ी

Aam taur pe 13 January (Makar Sankranti se ek din pehle)

 🪁

Makar Sankranti · मकर संक्रांति

Aam taur pe 14 January (kuch saalon mein 15 January)

 🇮🇳

Republic Day · गणतंत्र दिवस

Har saal 26 January

 🌼

Vasant Panchami · वसंत पंचमी

Magh maas ke shukla paksh ki Panchami (aam taur pe January ke aakhir ya February mein)

 🔱

Maha Shivratri · महाशिवरात्रि

Phalgun maas ke krishna paksh ki Chaturdashi (aam taur pe February ya March)

 🎨

Holi · होली

Phalgun Purnima pe Holika Dahan, agle din rangon wali Holi (aam taur pe March)

 🌙

Eid ul-Fitr · ईद-उल-फ़ित्र

Shawwal mahine ki pehli tareekh — Ramzan ke baad chaand dikhne pe (English tareekh har saal lagbhag 10-11 din pehle aati hai)

 🏹

Ram Navami · राम नवमी

Chaitra maas ke shukla paksh ki Navami (aam taur pe March ya April)

 🪢

Raksha Bandhan · रक्षा बंधन

Shravan maas ki Purnima (aam taur pe August)

 🪔

Diwali · दिवाली

Kartik maas ki Amavasya (aam taur pe October ya November); Dhanteras se Bhai Dooj tak paanch din

Jin tyoharon ki tareekh chandra calendar (tithi) se tay hoti hai, unki sahi tareekh har saal badalti hai — puja ka muhurat apne local panchang se zaroor dekhein.

🎨 Holi · होली

📅 Holi 2027 kab hai?

22 March 2027, Somvaar · 115 din baaki

Holi 22 March 2027, Somvaar ko hai. Holika Dahan ek din pehle, 21 March ki shaam. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Holi rangon, masti aur bhaichaare ka tyohar hai. Ye burai pe achhai ki jeet aur vasant ke swagat ka parv hai. Is din log purane gile-shikwe bhool kar ek-doosre ko rang lagaate aur gale milte hain.

KabPhalgun Purnima pe Holika Dahan, agle din rangon wali Holi (aam taur pe March)
KahanPoore Bharat aur Nepal mein; Braj (Mathura-Vrindavan-Barsana) ki Holi sabse mashhoor

Holika Dahan aur Prahlad ki katha

Raja Hiranyakashipu chahta tha ki sab uski puja karein, lekin uska beta Prahlad Bhagwan Vishnu ka bhakt tha. Raja ki behen Holika ko aag mein na jalne ka vardaan tha. Woh Prahlad ko god mein le kar aag mein baithi, par Bhagwan ki kripa se Prahlad bach gaye aur Holika jal gayi.

Isi yaad mein Holi se ek raat pehle Holika Dahan kiya jaata hai — lakdi aur uplon ka dher jala kar uski parikrama ki jaati hai.

Rangon wali Holi

Agle din (Dhulandi) subah se rang, gulaal aur paani se Holi kheli jaati hai. Braj mein Holi kai dinon tak chalti hai — Barsana ki Lathmar Holi, Vrindavan ki phoolon wali Holi aur Mathura ki Holi duniya bhar mein mashhoor hain.

Pakwan

Gujiya, dahi-bhalle, malpua, papdi, namakpare aur thandai Holi ki pehchaan hain.

Surakshit Holi

Natural ya herbal rang use karo, aankhon aur munh mein rang jaane se bacho, jaanwaron pe rang mat daalo aur paani ki barbaadi kam karo. Jo rang nahi lagwana chahta, uski marzi ka samman karo.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Holi 2027 kab hai?

Holi 22 March 2027, Somvaar ko hai. Holika Dahan ek din pehle, 21 March ki shaam.

Holi kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Holi burai par achhai ki jeet aur basant ke aane ki khushi ka tyohar hai. Holika Dahan ki katha mein bhakt Prahlad aag se bach gaye aur Holika jal gayi. Rang khelne ki parampara Radha-Krishna ki Braj ki Holi se judi maani jaati hai.

Holika Dahan kab hota hai?

Phalgun Purnima ki raat, rangon wali Holi se ek din pehle. Sahi muhurat panchang ke anusaar dekha jaata hai.

Lathmar Holi kahan hoti hai?

Uttar Pradesh ke Barsana aur Nandgaon mein, jahan mahilayein hansi-mazaak mein purushon pe laathiyan barsaati hain aur purush dhaal se bachte hain.

Skin se Holi ka rang kaise chhudaayein?

Khelne se pehle chehre aur baalon pe nariyal ya sarson ka tel laga lo. Baad mein halke saabun aur besan-dahi se dho, zor se mat ragdo.

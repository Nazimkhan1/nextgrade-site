🌙 Eid ul-Fitr · ईद-उल-फ़ित्र

📅 Eid ul-Fitr 2027 kab hai?

10 March 2027, Budhvaar (anumaanit) · 106 din baaki

Eid ul-Fitr 10 March 2027, Budhvaar ko hone ki ummeed hai. Eid chaand dikhne par hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Eid ul-Fitr Ramzan ke ek mahine ke rozon ke baad aane wali khushi ki Eid hai. Ise "Meethi Eid" bhi kehte hain, kyunki is din har ghar mein sewaiyan aur mithaiyan banti hain.

KabShawwal mahine ki pehli tareekh — Ramzan ke baad chaand dikhne pe (English tareekh har saal lagbhag 10-11 din pehle aati hai)
KahanPoori duniya ke Muslim samudaay mein; Bharat mein har jagah

Ramzan aur Eid

Islami (Hijri) calendar ke nauvein mahine Ramzan mein Musalmaan subah se shaam tak roza rakhte hain, zyada ibaadat karte hain aur zaroorat-mandon ki madad karte hain. Ramzan khatm hone pe Shawwal ka chaand dikhte hi agle din Eid manayi jaati hai.

Hijri calendar chaand pe chalta hai, isliye Eid ki English tareekh har saal lagbhag 10-11 din pehle khisak jaati hai.

Eid ka din

Subah log naha-dho kar naye kapde pehente hain, kuch meetha khaate hain aur Eidgah ya masjid mein Eid ki namaz padhte hain. Namaz se pehle "fitra" (Zakat-ul-Fitr) dena zaroori hai, taaki gareeb parivaar bhi Eid mana sakein.

Namaz ke baad log gale mil kar "Eid Mubarak" kehte hain. Bade bachchon ko "Eidi" dete hain aur doston-rishtedaaron ke ghar aana-jaana hota hai.

Pakwan

Sheer khurma, kimami sewaiyan, doodh wali sewaiyan, biryani aur kebab Eid ki daawat ki shaan hain.

Eid ul-Fitr 2027 kab hai?

Eid ul-Fitr 10 March 2027, Budhvaar ko hone ki ummeed hai. Eid chaand dikhne par hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.

Eid-ul-Fitr kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Eid-ul-Fitr Ramzan ke poore mahine ke roze poore hone ki khushi aur Allah ka shukr ada karne ka din hai. Yeh Shawwal mahine ki pehli tareekh ko chaand dikhne par manaayi jaati hai. Namaz se pehle zaroorat-mandon ko fitra (daan) diya jaata hai, taaki sab Eid mana sakein.

Eid ul-Fitr aur Eid ul-Adha mein kya farq hai?

Eid ul-Fitr Ramzan ke baad manayi jaati hai (Meethi Eid). Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) lagbhag do mahine baad, Zilhijja mahine mein Hajj ke samay manayi jaati hai.

Fitra kya hai?

Eid ki namaz se pehle har saksham Musalmaan ki taraf se gareebon ko diya jaane wala daan, taaki woh bhi Eid mana sakein.

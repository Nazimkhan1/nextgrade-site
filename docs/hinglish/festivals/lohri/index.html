🔥 Lohri · लोहड़ी

📅 Lohri 2027 kab hai?

13 January 2027, Budhvaar · 46 din baaki

Lohri 13 January 2027, Budhvaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Lohri sardiyon ki vidaai aur nayi fasal ki khushi ka tyohar hai. Is raat log aag (alaav) ke chaaron taraf ikattha ho kar geet gaate hain, bhangra-giddha karte hain aur til, gud, rewri, moongphali aur popcorn aag mein arpit karte hain.

Lohri ka mahatva

Lohri ke aas-paas sabse lambi sard raatein khatm hone lagti hain aur din bade hone lagte hain. Kisaanon ke liye ye rabi ki fasal (khaas kar ganne) ki khushi ka samay hai.

Jis ghar mein nayi shaadi hui ho ya bachche ka janm hua ho, wahan pehli Lohri bahut dhoom-dhaam se manayi jaati hai.

Dulla Bhatti ki kahani

Lohri ke geeton mein "Sundar mundriye ho, tera kaun vichara, Dulla Bhatti wala" zaroor gaaya jaata hai. Lok-katha ke anusaar Dulla Bhatti Mughal kaal ka ek bahadur Punjabi tha, jisne gareeb ladkiyon ko bachaya aur unki shaadi karwayi. Isliye use Lohri pe yaad kiya jaata hai.

Lohri pe kya khaate hain

Til aur gud ki rewri, gajak, moongphali, bhune makke ke daane (popcorn), aur khaane mein sarson ka saag aur makki di roti.

Lohri 2027 kab hai?

Lohri 13 January 2027, Budhvaar ko hai.

Lohri kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Lohri kadaake ki sardi ke jaane aur rabi ki fasal (khaas kar ganne) ke pakne ki khushi mein manaayi jaati hai. Log alaav jala kar agni ko dhanyavaad dete hain aur lok-nayak Dulla Bhatti ke geet gaate hain, jinhone gareeb ladkiyon ki madad ki thi.

Lohri kab manayi jaati hai?

Lohri Makar Sankranti se ek raat pehle manayi jaati hai, jo aam taur pe 13 January hoti hai.

Lohri mein aag mein kya daalte hain?

Til, gud, rewri, moongphali aur popcorn agni ko arpit kiye jaate hain aur phir prasad ke roop mein baante jaate hain.

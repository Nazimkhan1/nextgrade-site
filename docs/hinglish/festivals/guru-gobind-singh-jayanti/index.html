🙏 Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti · गुरु गोबिंद सिंह जयंती

📅 Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti 2027 kab hai?

15 January 2027, Shukravaar · 50 din baaki

Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti 15 January 2027, Shukravaar ko hai. Yeh panchang (Paush Shukla Saptami) ki tareekh hai; Nanakshahi calendar ke hisaab se 5 January. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Guru Gobind Singh Ji Sikhon ke dasvein Guru the. Unke janamdin ko Prakash Purab ya Gurpurab kehte hain. Is din gurudwaron mein kirtan, Akhand Path, Nagar Kirtan aur langar hota hai, aur sangat unki seekh — himmat, barabari aur seva — ko yaad karti hai.

KabPaush mahine ke shukla paksh ki saptami — aam taur pe December ke aakhir ya January mein (sahi tareekh gurudwara ya calendar mein dekhein)
KahanPoore Bharat aur duniya ke gurudwaron mein; khaas kar Patna Sahib, Anandpur Sahib aur Hazur Sahib (Nanded)

Guru Gobind Singh Ji ka jeevan

Unka janm 1666 mein Patna Sahib (Bihar) mein hua. Unke pita Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji nauvein Guru the. 1675 mein pita ki shahadat ke baad sirf nau saal ki umar mein unhone guru gaddi sambhaali.

Woh yoddha bhi the aur kavi bhi; Punjabi, Braj, Farsi aur Sanskrit jaante the. Unke chaaron bete — chaar Sahibzade — dharam ki raksha mein shaheed hue; chhote Sahibzadon ki yaad mein 26 December ko "Veer Baal Diwas" manaya jaata hai.

Khalsa Panth ki sthapna

1699 ki Baisakhi par Anandpur Sahib mein Guru Ji ne Khalsa Panth ki sthapna ki. Unhone paanch Sikhon — Panj Pyaron — ko amrit chhakaya aur phir khud bhi unse amrit liya, taaki Guru aur Sikh ki barabari dikhe.

Khalsa ke paanch kakaar hain: kesh, kangha, kada, kachhera aur kirpan. Har Sikh purush ke naam ke saath "Singh" aur mahila ke saath "Kaur" juda.

Guru Granth Sahib — sada ke Guru

1708 mein Hazur Sahib (Nanded, Maharashtra) mein Guru Ji ne kaha ki unke baad koi dehdhaari guru nahi hoga; Guru Granth Sahib hi hamesha ke liye Guru hain. Isiliye gurudware mein Guru Granth Sahib ko Guru ki tarah samman diya jaata hai.

Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Yeh Sikhon ke dasven Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji ke Prakash Purab (janm diwas) ke roop mein manaayi jaati hai. Panj Pyare kaun the?

1699 mein amrit chhakne wale pehle paanch Sikh: Bhai Daya Singh, Bhai Dharam Singh, Bhai Himmat Singh, Bhai Mohkam Singh aur Bhai Sahib Singh.

🪢 Raksha Bandhan · रक्षा बंधन

📅 Raksha Bandhan 2027 kab hai?

17 August 2027, Mangalvaar · 265 din baaki

Raksha Bandhan 17 August 2027, Mangalvaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Raksha Bandhan bhai-behen ke pyaar aur bharose ka tyohar hai. Behen bhai ki kalaai pe rakhi baandhti hai aur uski lambi umar ki dua karti hai; bhai hamesha saath nibhaane ka vaada karta hai.

KabShravan maas ki Purnima (aam taur pe August)
KahanPoore Bharat aur Nepal mein

Rakhi ki parampara

Subah behen puja ki thaali sajaati hai — roli, akshat (chawal), diya, mithai aur rakhi. Bhai ke maathe pe tilak laga kar, aarti karke rakhi baandhi jaati hai aur mithai khilaayi jaati hai. Bhai behen ko tohfa deta hai.

Rakhi baandhte samay panchang mein "Bhadra kaal" dekha jaata hai, kyunki us samay rakhi baandhna shubh nahi maana jaata.

Kahaniyan

Mahabharat ki katha mein Shishupal vadh ke samay Shri Krishna ki ungli kat gayi thi, tab Draupadi ne apni saadi ka tukda baandha. Shri Krishna ne cheerharan ke samay unki laaj bacha kar ye vachan nibhaya.

Aaj Raksha Bandhan sirf sage bhai-behen tak seemit nahi hai — log cousins, doston, sainikon aur pedon ko bhi rakhi baandhte hain.

Raksha Bandhan ki wishes

  • Rakhi ka ye dhaaga hamare rishte ko aur mazboot kare. Raksha Bandhan ki haardik shubhkamnayein!
  • Bachpan ki ladaiyan aur dher saara pyaar — mere pyaare bhai/behen ko Happy Raksha Bandhan!
  • Door ho ya paas, tumhara saath hamesha mere saath hai. Rakhi mubarak!
  • Bhai-behen ka ye pyaar sada bana rahe. Raksha Bandhan ki badhaai!

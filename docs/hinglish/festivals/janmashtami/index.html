🦚 Janmashtami · जन्माष्टमी

📅 Janmashtami 2027 kab hai?

24 August 2027, Mangalvaar · 270 din baaki

Janmashtami 24 August 2027, Mangalvaar ko hai. Smarta parampara mein 24 August; ISKCON/Vaishnav parampara mein 25 August. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Janmashtami Bhagwan Shri Krishna ke janm ka utsav hai. Kaha jaata hai ki unka janm aadhi raat ko Mathura mein Kans ke kaaragaar mein hua, isliye is din log vrat rakhte hain aur raat 12 baje Kanha ka janmotsav manaate hain.

KabBhadrapad mahine ke krishna paksh ki ashtami — aam taur pe August ya September mein (kuch jagah Smarta aur Vaishnav tithi ek din alag hoti hai; apna panchang dekhein)
KahanPoore Bharat mein; Mathura, Vrindavan, Dwarka aur ISKCON mandiron mein khaas dhoom

Krishna janm ki katha

Mathura ka raja Kans apni behen Devaki ke aathve putra se darta tha, isliye usne Devaki aur Vasudev ko jail mein band kar diya. Aathve putra Krishna ke janm ki raat pehredaar so gaye, taale khul gaye aur Vasudev tez baarish mein Yamuna paar karke shishu ko Gokul mein Nand aur Yashoda ke ghar chhod aaye.

Gokul aur Vrindavan mein Krishna ka bachpan — makhan chori, gopiyon ke saath raas, Govardhan parvat uthaana — aaj bhi geeton aur jhaankiyon mein zinda hai.

Kaise manaate hain

Log din bhar vrat rakhte hain, mandiron aur gharon mein jhaanki sajaate hain, Laddu Gopal ko naye kapde pehnaate hain aur jhoola jhulaate hain. Aadhi raat ko shankh-ghantiyon ke saath janm ki aarti hoti hai aur phir panjiri, makhan-mishri aur panchamrit ka prasad bantta hai.

Maharashtra mein agle din "Dahi Handi" hoti hai — oonchaai pe tangi matki ko insaani piramid bana kar phoda jaata hai, jo Krishna ki makhan chori ki yaad hai.

Janmashtami 2027 kab hai?

Janmashtami 24 August 2027, Mangalvaar ko hai. Smarta parampara mein 24 August; ISKCON/Vaishnav parampara mein 25 August.

Janmashtami kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Janmashtami Bhagwan Shri Krishna ke janm ki khushi mein manaayi jaati hai. Maanyata hai ki unka janm Bhadrapad mahine ke krishna paksh ki ashtami ko aadhi raat mein Mathura ki jail mein hua tha, jahan Kans ne unke maata-pita Devaki aur Vasudev ko qaid kar rakha tha. Isliye bhakt vrat rakh kar aadhi raat ko janmotsav manaate hain.

Janmashtami kab manayi jaati hai?

Bhadrapad mahine ke krishna paksh ki ashtami ko, jo aam taur pe August ya September mein aati hai. Kabhi-kabhi do din manayi jaati hai, isliye apna panchang dekhein.

Dahi Handi kya hai?

Janmashtami ke agle din oonchaai pe bandhi dahi-makhan ki matki ko insaani piramid bana kar phodne ki parampara, jo Krishna ki makhan chori ki yaad dilaati hai.

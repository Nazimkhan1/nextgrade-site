🏹 Ram Navami · राम नवमी

📅 Ram Navami 2027 kab hai?

15 April 2027, Guruvaar · 146 din baaki

Ram Navami 15 April 2027, Guruvaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Ram Navami Bhagwan Shri Ram ke janm ka utsav hai. Maanyata ke anusaar Shri Ram ka janm Ayodhya mein Raja Dashrath aur Mata Kaushalya ke ghar Chaitra Shukla Navami ko dopahar ke samay hua tha. Isi din Chaitra Navratri ka samaapan bhi hota hai.

KabChaitra maas ke shukla paksh ki Navami (aam taur pe March ya April)
KahanPoore Bharat mein; Ayodhya mein sabse bada utsav

Maryada Purushottam

Shri Ram ko "Maryada Purushottam" kaha jaata hai — yaani aisa aadarsh insaan jisne har rishte aur kartavya ko poori imaandaari se nibhaya. Pita ke vachan ke liye 14 saal ka vanvaas sweekar karna unke charitra ki sabse badi misaal hai.

Kaise manate hain

Mandiron mein dopahar ke samay Ram-janm ki aarti hoti hai, Ramcharitmanas ka paath aur bhajan-kirtan hote hain. Kai jagah shobha yatrayein nikalti hain. Bhakt upvaas rakhte hain aur panjiri, panchamrit aur phal ka prasad baantte hain.

Jo log Chaitra Navratri rakhte hain, woh is din kanya pujan karke vrat kholte hain.

Ram Navami kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Ram Navami Bhagwan Shri Ram ke janm ki khushi mein manaayi jaati hai. Maanyata hai ki unka janm Chaitra mahine ke shukla paksh ki navami ko Ayodhya mein Raja Dashrath aur Rani Kaushalya ke ghar hua tha. Isi din Chaitra Navratri bhi poori hoti hai.

Ram Navami kis tithi ko hoti hai?

Chaitra maas ke shukla paksh ki Navami tithi ko, jo aam taur pe March ya April mein padti hai.

Ram Navami pe puja kis samay hoti hai?

Maanyata hai ki Shri Ram ka janm madhyaahn (dopahar) mein hua tha, isliye mukhya puja aur aarti dopahar ke samay hoti hai.

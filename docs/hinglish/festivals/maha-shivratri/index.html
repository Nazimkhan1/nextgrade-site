🔱 Maha Shivratri · महाशिवरात्रि

📅 Maha Shivratri 2027 kab hai?

6 March 2027, Shanivaar · 98 din baaki

Maha Shivratri 6 March 2027, Shanivaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Maha Shivratri Bhagwan Shiv ki aaradhana ki sabse badi raat hai. Maanyata hai ki isi raat Shiv aur Parvati ka vivah hua tha. Bhakt is din upvaas rakhte hain aur raat bhar jaag kar Shiv ka dhyaan karte hain.

KabPhalgun maas ke krishna paksh ki Chaturdashi (aam taur pe February ya March)
KahanPoore Bharat aur Nepal mein

Puja kaise karte hain

Shivling pe jal, doodh, dahi, shahad aur Gangajal se abhishek kiya jaata hai. Belpatra, dhatura, aak ke phool aur bhaang arpit kiye jaate hain. "Om Namah Shivaya" mantra ka jaap kiya jaata hai.

Raat ko chaar prahar ki puja hoti hai aur kai mandiron mein poori raat bhajan-kirtan chalta hai.

Upvaas

Kai log nirjala vrat rakhte hain, to kai phal, doodh aur sabudana-kuttu jaise vrat ke khaane lete hain. Buzurg, beemar ya garbhvati mahilayein apni sehat ke hisaab se hi vrat rakhein.

Prasiddh sthaan

Kashi Vishwanath (Varanasi), Ujjain ke Mahakaleshwar, Somnath, Nepal ke Pashupatinath aur desh ke baarah jyotirlingon pe is din bhaari bheed hoti hai.

Maha Shivratri 2027 kab hai?

Maha Shivratri 6 March 2027, Shanivaar ko hai.

Mahashivratri kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Mahashivratri Bhagwan Shiv ki aaradhana ki raat hai. Maanyata hai ki isi raat Shiv aur Parvati ka vivah hua tha, aur yeh bhi ki Shiv ne isi raat jyotirling ke roop mein darshan diye the. Bhakt vrat rakhte hain, Shivling par jal-belpatra chadhaate hain aur raat bhar jaagran karte hain.

Maha Shivratri aur masik Shivratri mein kya farq hai?

Har mahine krishna paksh ki Chaturdashi ko masik Shivratri hoti hai. Phalgun maas wali Shivratri sabse badi maani jaati hai, isliye use Maha Shivratri kehte hain.

Shivling pe kya chadhate hain?

Jal, doodh, belpatra, dhatura, aak ke phool, chandan aur bhasm. Tulsi aam taur pe Shivling pe nahi chadhayi jaati.

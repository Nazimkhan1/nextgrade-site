🌾 Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) · बैसाखी

📅 Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 2027 kab hai?

14 April 2027, Budhvaar · 140 din baaki

Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 14 April 2027, Budhvaar ko hai. 📅 2027 ke saare tyohar aur tareekhein

Baisakhi rabi ki fasal — khaas kar gehun — pakne ki khushi ka tyohar hai. Punjab mein dhol ki thaap pe bhangra-giddha hota hai, mele lagte hain, aur Sikh dharam mein yeh din Khalsa Panth ki sthapna ki yaad ke roop mein bahut khaas hai.

KabAam taur pe 13 April (kuch saalon mein 14 April)
KahanPunjab, Haryana aur poore Uttar Bharat mein; isi din desh ke kai hisson mein naya saal bhi manaya jaata hai

Fasal ki khushi

Baisakhi tak gehun ki sunehri fasal katne ko taiyaar hoti hai. Kisaan mehnat ka phal milne pe Bhagwan ka shukriya karte hain, naye kapde pehente hain aur gaon-gaon mein mele lagte hain.

Khalsa Panth ki sthapna (1699)

1699 ki Baisakhi pe Guru Gobind Singh Ji ne Anandpur Sahib mein Khalsa Panth ki sthapna ki aur Panj Pyaron ko amrit chhakaya. Isliye gurudwaron mein is din kirtan, ardaas, Nagar Kirtan aur langar hota hai.

Ek din, kai naam

Isi samay Bengal mein Poila Boishakh (Bangla naya saal), Assam mein Bohag Bihu (Rongali Bihu), Tamil Nadu mein Puthandu (Tamil naya saal) aur Kerala mein Vishu manaya jaata hai.

13 April 1919 ko Baisakhi ke din hi Amritsar ke Jallianwala Bagh mein angrezi hukoomat ne nihatthe logon pe goliyan chalayi thi. Is din un shaheedon ko bhi yaad kiya jaata hai.

Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 2027 kab hai?

Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 14 April 2027, Budhvaar ko hai.

Baisakhi kyun manaayi jaati hai?

Baisakhi Punjab aur uttar Bharat mein rabi ki fasal (gehun) ki katai ki khushi ka tyohar hai aur saur nav-varsh ki shuruaat bhi. Sikhon ke liye yeh isliye khaas hai kyunki 1699 mein isi din Guru Gobind Singh Ji ne Anandpur Sahib mein Khalsa Panth ki sthaapna ki thi.

Baisakhi kab manayi jaati hai?

Aam taur pe 13 April ko, aur kuch saalon mein 14 April ko. Yeh saur calendar se tay hoti hai, isliye tareekh lagbhag wahi rehti hai.

Baisakhi ka Khalsa se kya sambandh hai?

1699 ki Baisakhi pe Guru Gobind Singh Ji ne Anandpur Sahib mein Khalsa Panth ki sthapna ki thi, isliye Sikhon ke liye yeh Khalsa Sajna Diwas bhi hai.

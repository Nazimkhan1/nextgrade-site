🎆 New Year · नया साल

Naya saal Gregorian calendar ke pehle din, 1 January ko manaya jaata hai. Ye beete saal ko thank you kehne aur aane wale saal ke liye naye iraade banane ka din hai.

Naye saal ka jashn

31 December ki raat log parivaar aur doston ke saath ikattha hote hain aur theek aadhi raat ko countdown ke saath naye saal ka swagat karte hain. Aatishbaazi, music aur "Happy New Year" ke messages is raat ki pehchaan hain.

Bharat mein kai log saal ke pehle din mandir, gurudwara, masjid ya church jaa kar naye saal ki shuruaat karte hain.

Naye saal ke sankalp (Resolutions)

Naya saal apne liye chhote-chhote goals tay karne ka achha mauka hai — roz tehelna, har mahine kuch paise bachana, koi nayi bhasha ya hunar seekhna. Sankalp chhote aur saaf rakho, to nibhaana aasaan hota hai.

Kharche pe nazar rakhna bhi ek achha sankalp hai — iske liye hamara free app Hisab Kitab use kar sakte ho.

Bharat ke doosre nav varsh

Bharat mein kai kshetriya nav varsh bhi manaye jaate hain — jaise Chaitra Shukla Pratipada (Hindu Nav Samvatsar), Gudi Padwa, Ugadi, Baisakhi, Pohela Boishakh, Puthandu aur Vishu. Ye zyadatar March-April mein aate hain.

New Year ki wishes

  • Naya saal aapke liye nayi umeedein, nayi kaamyaabi aur dher saari khushiyan le kar aaye. Happy New Year!
  • Beete saal ki yaadein meethi rahein aur aane wala saal usse bhi behtar ho. Naya saal mubarak!
  • Har din ek naya mauka hai, aur ye saal aise 365 mauke le kar aaya hai. Happy New Year!
  • Aapke ghar mein sehat, sukoon aur samriddhi bani rahe. Naye saal ki bahut-bahut badhaai!
  • Purane gile-shikwe bhool kar naye saal mein nayi shuruaat karein. Happy New Year!

New Year wishes in English

  • Here is to new beginnings, kind people and dreams that come true. Happy New Year!
  • Thank you for being part of my year. Wishing you a wonderful one ahead!
  • New year, new chances. May every one of them work out for you. Happy New Year!

Naya saal 1 January ko hi kyun manate hain?

Aaj duniya bhar mein Gregorian calendar chalta hai, jiska pehla din 1 January hai. Isliye international naya saal isi din manaya jaata hai.

Hindu nav varsh kab hota hai?

Hindu Nav Samvatsar Chaitra maas ke shukla paksh ki pratipada ko shuru hota hai, jo aam taur pe March ya April mein padti hai.

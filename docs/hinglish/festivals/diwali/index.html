🪔 Diwali · दिवाली

Diwali (Deepawali) roshni ka tyohar hai — andhere pe ujaale aur burai pe achhai ki jeet ka parv. Is din ghar-ghar diye jalaaye jaate hain aur Maa Lakshmi aur Bhagwan Ganesh ki puja hoti hai.

KabKartik maas ki Amavasya (aam taur pe October ya November); Dhanteras se Bhai Dooj tak paanch din
KahanPoore Bharat aur duniya bhar ke Bharatiyon mein

Diwali kyun manate hain

Maanyata hai ki Ravan pe vijay aur 14 saal ke vanvaas ke baad Shri Ram, Mata Sita aur Lakshman isi din Ayodhya laute the. Ayodhyavaasiyon ne diye jala kar unka swagat kiya tha.

Jain dharm mein ye Bhagwan Mahavir ke nirvaan ka din hai, aur Sikh dharm mein isi samay Bandi Chhor Diwas manaya jaata hai.

Paanch din ka utsav

Dhanteras (bartan, sona-chaandi khareedne ka din), Narak Chaturdashi ya Chhoti Diwali, mukhya Diwali (Lakshmi-Ganesh puja), Govardhan Puja aur Bhai Dooj — ye paanch din mil kar Deepotsav banate hain.

Taiyaari aur pakwan

Diwali se pehle ghar ki safaai aur sajaavat hoti hai, rangoli banayi jaati hai. Laddoo, kaju katli, shakkarpare, chakli aur gujiya jaise pakwan bante hain aur rishtedaaron mein mithai baanti jaati hai.

Patakhe kam chalao aur apne shehar ke niyam maano — hawa saaf rahegi aur jaanwar bhi darenge nahi.

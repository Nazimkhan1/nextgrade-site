🌼 Vasant Panchami · वसंत पंचमी

Vasant Panchami vasant ritu ke aagman aur gyaan ki devi Maa Saraswati ki puja ka din hai. Is din peela rang pehenne aur peele pakwan banane ki parampara hai.

KabMagh maas ke shukla paksh ki Panchami (aam taur pe January ke aakhir ya February mein)
KahanUttar aur Poorvi Bharat mein khaas; Bengal mein Saraswati Puja ke roop mein

Maa Saraswati ki puja

Maa Saraswati vidya, sangeet aur kala ki devi hain. Is din vidyarthi apni kitaabein, kalam aur vaadya yantra Maa ke saamne rakh kar puja karte hain. Bengal, Bihar aur Odisha mein school-college mein Saraswati Puja bade utsaah se hoti hai.

Kai parivaron mein chhote bachchon ka "vidyarambh" (pehli baar akshar likhwaana) isi din karwaya jaata hai.

Peela rang kyun

Vasant mein sarson ke khet peele phoolon se bhar jaate hain. Peela rang oorja, ujaale aur gyaan ka prateek maana jaata hai, isliye log peele kapde pehente hain aur kesariya bhaat, boondi ya peele chawal banate hain.

Patang aur vasant utsav

Punjab aur aas-paas ke ilaakon mein Vasant Panchami pe bhi patangbaazi hoti hai. Kai jagah sangeet aur kavita ke karyakram rakhe jaate hain.

Vasant Panchami ki wishes

  • Maa Saraswati aapko gyaan, buddhi aur safalta ka aashirwaad dein. Vasant Panchami ki haardik shubhkamnayein!
  • Peele phoolon ki tarah aapki zindagi bhi khilkhilaati rahe. Happy Vasant Panchami!
  • Vidya ki devi aapki har pariksha aasaan karein. Saraswati Puja ki shubhkamnayein!
  • Vasant ki taazgi aur Maa Saraswati ka aashirwaad sada aapke saath rahe.

Vasant Panchami wishes in English

  • Bright as yellow mustard fields — wishing you a joyful spring. Happy Basant Panchami!
  • Happy Saraswati Puja! May learning always light your way.

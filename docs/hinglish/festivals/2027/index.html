📅 2027 ke Tyohar aur Tareekhein

  • Makar Sankranti: Zyadatar panchang 15 January batate hain; kuch panchang aur kai rajyon ki chhutti list mein 14 January hai.
  • Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti: Yeh panchang (Paush Shukla Saptami) ki tareekh hai; Nanakshahi calendar ke hisaab se 5 January.
  • Pongal: Bhogi 14 January ko; Thai Pongal 15 January ko.
  • Eid ul-Fitr: Eid chaand dikhne par hoti hai, isliye tareekh ek din aage-peeche ho sakti hai.
  • Holi: Holika Dahan ek din pehle, 21 March ki shaam.
  • Chhath Puja: Nahay-Khay 2 Nov, Kharna 3 Nov, Sandhya Arghya 4 Nov, Usha Arghya 5 Nov.

Tithi wale tyohaaron mein apne shehar ka panchang bhi dekh lein.

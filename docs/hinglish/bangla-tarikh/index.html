📅 Aaj Bangla tarikh kya hai?

Aaj: Shaniwar, 28 November 2026

West Bengal (panjika): 12 Agrahayan 1433Hemonto

Bangladesh: 13 Agrahayan 1433Hemonto

🔁 Tarikh badlo

Dono Bengal mein tarikh alag kyon?

  • West Bengal mein panjika ke hisaab se suraj jab agli raashi mein jaata hai (sankranti), uske agle din naya mahina shuru hota hai. Isliye mahina 29 se 32 din ka hota hai aur Pohela Boishakh 14 ya 15 April ko padta hai.
  • Bangladesh mein 2019 (1426) se fixed calendar hai: Pohela Boishakh hamesha 14 April; Boishakh se Ashwin 31 din, Kartik se Magh aur Chaitra 30 din, Falgun 29 din (leap year mein 30).
  • Isliye ek hi din dono jagah Bangla tarikh 1–2 din alag ho sakti hai. Bangabda = English saal − 593 (Pohela Boishakh ke baad), usse pehle − 594.
  • Hamara hisaab astronomy ke formula se hai (Lahiri ayanamsa); kuch chhapi panjika (jaise Gupta Press) se kabhi-kabhi ek din ka farq ho sakta hai.

Bangla calendar 1433 Bangla calendar 1434

Sawal-jawab

Abhi Bangla kaun sa saal hai?

Abhi 1433 Bangabda chal raha hai; 1434 Pohela Boishakh ko shuru hoga — West Bengal mein 15 April 2027, Bangladesh mein 14 April 2027.

Pohela Boishakh 2027 kab hai?

West Bengal mein guruwar 15 April 2027 (Mesh sankranti 14 April); Bangladesh mein budhwar 14 April 2027.

Bangla mahine kaun-kaun se hain?

Boishakh, Joishtho, Asharh, Shrabon, Bhadro, Ashwin, Kartik, Agrahayan, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra — do mahine milkar ek ritu, kul chhe ritu.

Bangla tarikh kab badalti hai?

Parampara mein suryoday par; yeh page suvidha ke liye raat 12 baje (IST) badalta hai.

Hisaab: Lahiri ayanamsa, Meeus · Aakhri baar jaancha: 26 Nov 2026

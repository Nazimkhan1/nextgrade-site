🌳 Pedon ka Mahatva par Nibandh

Ped hamare sachche mitra hain. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Ped humein saans lene ke liye oxygen dete hain.
  2. Wo hawa se carbon dioxide lekar use saaf karte hain.
  3. Ped humein phal, phool, lakdi aur dawaiyan dete hain.
  4. Garmi mein ped ki chhaya bahut thandak deti hai.
  5. Pedon ki jadein mitti ko behne se rokti hain.
  6. Zyada ped hone se achhi barish hoti hai.
  7. Ped pakshiyon aur jaanwaron ka ghar hote hain.
  8. Ped kaatna prakriti aur hamari sehat dono ke liye haanikarak hai.
  9. Humein har saal naye ped lagane chahiye — ise vriksharopan kehte hain.
  10. "Ek ped, sau sukh" — isliye pedon ko bachana hum sabka kartavya hai.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Ped hamare sabse achhe mitra hain. Wo humein saans lene ke liye oxygen dete hain aur hawa ko saaf rakhte hain. Pedon se humein phal, phool, chhaya, lakdi aur dawaiyan milti hain. Garmi mein ped ki chhaya mein baithna bahut achha lagta hai. Ped pakshiyon aur jaanwaron ka ghar bhi hote hain. Jahan zyada ped hote hain, wahan achhi barish hoti hai aur hawa thandi rehti hai. Humein ped nahi kaatne chahiye, balki har saal naye ped lagane chahiye. Ped lagao, dharti bachao.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Prakriti ne humein bahut kuch diya hai, aur usme ped sabse bada uphaar hain. Ped chupchaap khade rehkar jeevan bhar doosron ko dete rehte hain aur badle mein kuch nahi maangte. Sach kaha gaya hai ki ped hamare sabse sachche aur nihswarth mitra hain.

Ped humein kya dete hain

Ped humein saans lene ke liye oxygen dete hain aur hawa se zehreeli carbon dioxide sokh lete hain. Inse humein phal, sabziyan, phool, lakdi, rubber, kaagaz aur kai dawaiyan milti hain. Garmi mein inki chhaya raahat deti hai. Pedon ki jadein mitti ko pakad kar rakhti hain, jisse baadh aur bhoomi-katav rukta hai.

Ped aur vatavaran

Ped dharti ka taapmaan santulit rakhte hain aur baadlon ko bulakar achhi barish karane mein madad karte hain. Jahan ped zyada hote hain, wahan ki hawa saaf aur thandi rehti hai. Ped pradushan, dhool aur shor ko bhi kam karte hain. Isiliye junglon ko "dharti ke phephde" kaha jata hai.

Jeev-jantuon ka ghar

Ped asankhya pakshiyon, gilahriyon, bandaron aur keedon ka ghar hote hain. Jungle hazaron jaanwaron aur jadi-bootiyon ko aashray dete hain. Agar ped na rahein, to yeh poora sansar ujad jaayega aur prakriti ka santulan bigad jaayega.

Pedon ki kataai — ek khatra

Aaj shahar basane, karkhane aur sadkein banane ke liye andhadhundh ped kaate ja rahe hain. Isse barish kam ho rahi hai, garmi badh rahi hai, hawa zehreeli ho rahi hai aur kai jaanwar beghar ho rahe hain. Yeh laalach aage chalkar poori maanav jaati ke liye khatarnak saabit hoga.

Hamara kartavya

Humein pedon ko bachana chahiye aur har saal naye ped lagane chahiye — isi ko vriksharopan kehte hain. Apne janamdin ya kisi khaas din par ek ped zaroor lagayein aur uski dekhbhaal karein. Doosron ko bhi ped na kaatne ke liye samjhayein. Yahi dharti ke liye sabse badi seva hai.

Upsanhaar

Pedon ke bina jeevan ki kalpana bhi nahi ki ja sakti. Wo humein saans, bhojan, chhaya aur sundarta dete hain. Agar hum aaj ped lagayenge aur bachayenge, to aane wali peedhiyon ko saaf hawa aur hari-bhari dharti milegi. Isliye aao, ek ped zaroor lagayein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pedon ka mahatva nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, ped humein kya dete hain, ped aur vatavaran, jeev-jantuon ka ghar, kataai ka khatra, hamara kartavya aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Pedon ko dharti ke phephde kyon kehte hain?

Kyonki ped hawa se carbon dioxide sokhkar humein oxygen dete hain aur hawa saaf rakhte hain, theek waise hi jaise phephde shareer ke liye karte hain.

Vriksharopan ka kya arth hai?

Vriksharopan ka arth hai naye ped lagana aur unki dekhbhaal karna, taki paryavaran hara-bhara aur swachh bana rahe.

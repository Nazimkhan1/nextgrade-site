❄️ Sardi ka Mausam par Nibandh

Sardi ka mausam (sheet ritu) sabse pyaare mausamon mein se ek hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Bharat mein sardi ka mausam lagbhag November se February tak rehta hai.
  2. December aur January sabse thande mahine hote hain.
  3. Is mausam mein din chhote aur raatein lambi ho jaati hain.
  4. Subah kohra chha jaata hai aur ghaas par os ki boondein chamakti hain.
  5. Hum sweater, topi, moze aur shawl pehen kar thand se bachte hain.
  6. Log dhoop sekna aur alaav ke paas baithna pasand karte hain.
  7. Is mausam mein gaajar, matar, palak, mooli aur santre khoob milte hain.
  8. Gaajar ka halwa, moongphali, gud aur til ke laddoo sardi ka mazaa badha dete hain.
  9. Christmas, Naya Saal, Lohri aur Makar Sankranti isi mausam mein aate hain.
  10. Sardi humein mehnat karne ki taakat deti hai, isliye mujhe yeh mausam bahut achha lagta hai.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Bharat mein chhah ritu hoti hain. Unmein se ek hai sardi ka mausam, jise sheet ritu bhi kehte hain. Yeh November se February tak rehta hai. Is mausam mein thandi hawa chalti hai, subah kohra hota hai aur dhoop bahut pyaari lagti hai. Hum garam kapde pehente hain aur raat ko razaai odh kar sote hain. Bazaar mein taazi hari sabziyan aur phal aate hain. Maa gaajar ka halwa banati hain. Christmas, Naya Saal aur Makar Sankranti jaise tyohar bhi isi mausam mein aate hain. Sardi mein khaana jaldi pachta hai aur shareer mazboot hota hai. Mujhe sardi ka mausam bahut pasand hai.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Prakriti ne humein alag-alag ritu ka uphaar diya hai — garmi, barsaat, sardi aur basant. Har ritu ki apni sundarta hai, lekin sardi ka mausam sehat aur swaad dono ke liye sabse achha maana jaata hai. Barsaat ke baad jab hawa mein halki thandak aane lagti hai, to samajh lijiye ki sardi dastak de rahi hai.

Sardi kab aur kyun aati hai

Bharat mein sardi aam taur par November se February tak rehti hai. December aur January sabse thande hote hain. In mahinon mein suraj ki kirnein uttari Bharat par tirchhi padti hain aur din chhote hote hain, isliye dharti kam garam hoti hai. Uttar Bharat mein thand zyada padti hai, pahadon par barf girti hai, jabki dakshin aur samudra ke paas ke ilaakon mein mausam halka thanda rehta hai.

Sardi ki subah aur prakriti

Sardi ki subah badi niraali hoti hai. Chaaron taraf ghana kohra chhaya rehta hai, kabhi-kabhi kuch kadam aage bhi saaf nahi dikhta. Ghaas aur patton par os ki boondein motiyon jaisi chamakti hain. Jaise hi suraj nikalta hai, sab log chhat ya aangan mein dhoop sekne baith jaate hain. Kheton mein sarson ke peele phool aur gehun ki hari fasal man moh leti hai. Kai pakshi door thande deshon se ud kar Bharat ki jheelon mein aate hain.

Khaan-paan aur pehnaava

Sardi ko khaane-peene ka mausam bhi kaha jaata hai. Is samay gaajar, matar, gobhi, palak, methi, mooli aur shakarkand jaisi sabziyan aur santra, amrood, aamla jaise phal bharpoor milte hain. Gaajar ka halwa, sarson ka saag, makke ki roti, gud, moongphali aur til ke laddoo sardi ke khaas pakwaan hain. Garam chai, soup aur doodh peena achha lagta hai. Thand se bachne ke liye hum oonee sweater, jacket, topi, dastaane aur moze pehente hain.

Sardi ke tyohar

Is mausam mein kai tyohar aate hain — 25 December ko Christmas, 1 January ko Naya Saal, phir Lohri, Makar Sankranti, Pongal aur Basant Panchami. 26 January ko Gantantra Diwas bhi isi mausam mein manaya jaata hai. School mein sardi ki chhuttiyan hoti hain, jinmein bachche ghoomne jaate hain ya parivaar ke saath samay bitaate hain.

Sardi ki pareshaniyan

Sardi jitni suhaani hai, kuch logon ke liye utni hi kathin bhi. Jinke paas garam kapde aur pakka ghar nahi hai, unke liye thandi raatein bahut mushkil hoti hain. Ghane kohre ke kaaran sadak par durghatnayein hoti hain aur trains-flights der se chalti hain. Sardi-zukaam aur khaansi bhi jaldi ho jaate hain. Humein zaroorat-mandon ko purane kambal aur kapde de kar unki madad karni chahiye.

Upsanhaar

Sardi ka mausam humein taazgi, achhi sehat aur dher saare swaad deta hai. Is mausam mein vyayaam karna aur padhai karna aasaan lagta hai. Agar hum thand se bachne ka dhyaan rakhein aur doosron ki madad karein, to yeh mausam sabke liye anand bhara ban sakta hai. Isiliye sardi mera sabse priya mausam hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Bharat mein sardi ka mausam kab se kab tak rehta hai?

Aam taur par November se February tak. December aur January sabse thande mahine hote hain.

Sardi mein din chhote kyun hote hain?

In mahinon mein dharti ka uttari hissa suraj se thoda door jhuka rehta hai, isliye suraj der se nikalta hai, jaldi doobta hai aur kirnein tirchhi padti hain.

Sardi par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, sardi kab aati hai, subah aur prakriti, khaan-paan aur kapde, tyohar, pareshaniyan aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

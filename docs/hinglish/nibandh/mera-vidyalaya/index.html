🏫 Mera Vidyalaya par Nibandh

"Mera Vidyalaya" lagbhag har class ke exam mein poocha jaata hai. Neeche 10 lines, chhota nibandh aur headings wala lamba nibandh hai — apne school ka naam aur baatein jod kar likhein.

10 lines

  1. Mere vidyalaya ka naam ______ hai. (yahan apne school ka naam likhein)
  2. Mera vidyalaya mere ghar ke paas hai aur bahut sundar hai.
  3. Iski imaarat badi hai, jismein kai hawaadaar kakshaayein hain.
  4. Vidyalaya mein ek pustakalaya, ek prayogshaala aur ek computer room hai.
  5. Hamare vidyalaya mein khelne ke liye ek bada maidaan hai.
  6. Har din subah prarthana sabha se hamari padhai shuru hoti hai.
  7. Hamare shikshak bahut pyaar aur mehnat se padhaate hain.
  8. Yahan 15 August, 26 January aur Annual Day dhoom-dhaam se manaaye jaate hain.
  9. Vidyalaya humein anushaasan, safaai aur mil-jul kar rehna sikhata hai.
  10. Mujhe apne vidyalaya par garv hai aur main ise bahut pyaar karta/karti hoon.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Mere vidyalaya ka naam ______ hai. Yeh mere ghar se thodi door hai. Mera vidyalaya bahut bada aur saaf-suthra hai. Ismein bahut saari kakshaayein, ek pustakalaya aur ek bada khel ka maidaan hai. Har subah hum prarthana karte hain aur phir apni class mein jaate hain. Hamare shikshak bahut achhe hain. Woh humein padhai ke saath achhi baatein bhi sikhaate hain. Aadhi chhutti mein hum doston ke saath khaana khaate hain aur khelte hain. Hamare vidyalaya mein har saal khel-kood pratiyogita aur varshik utsav hota hai. Mujhe apna vidyalaya bahut achha lagta hai.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Ghar ke baad agar koi jagah humein sabse zyada sikhati hai, to woh hai hamara vidyalaya. Vidyalaya ko gyaan ka mandir kaha jaata hai, kyunki yahan hum sirf kitaabein hi nahi padhte, balki achha insaan banna bhi seekhte hain. Mere vidyalaya ka naam ______ hai aur mujhe iska vidyarthi hone par garv hai.

Vidyalaya ka bhavan

Mera vidyalaya ek khuli aur shaant jagah par bana hai. Iski imaarat do-teen manzila hai aur chaaron taraf hare ped aur phoolon ki kyaariyan hain. Kakshaayein badi, roshni wali aur hawaadaar hain. Har class mein board, pankhe aur baithne ke liye achhe desk hain. Vidyalaya mein peene ke saaf paani aur alag-alag shauchalayon ki bhi vyavastha hai.

Pustakalaya, prayogshaala aur computer room

Hamare vidyalaya ka pustakalaya meri sabse pasandeeda jagah hai. Yahan kahaniyon, vigyaan, itihaas aur samanya gyaan ki saikdon kitaabein aur akhbaar hain. Vigyaan ki prayogshaala mein hum khud prayog karke seekhte hain. Computer room mein humein typing, internet ka sahi upyog aur chhote-chhote program banana sikhaya jaata hai.

Hamare shikshak

Kisi bhi vidyalaya ki asli taakat uske shikshak hote hain. Hamare pradhanacharya ji bahut anushaasan-priya aur dayalu hain. Hamare shikshak har vishay ko aasaan udaharanon se samjhaate hain aur kamzor bachchon par khaas dhyaan dete hain. Woh humein sach bolna, badon ka aadar karna aur mehnat karna bhi sikhaate hain.

Khel-kood aur doosri gatividhiyan

Hamare vidyalaya mein padhai ke saath khel-kood ko bhi barabar mahatva diya jaata hai. Bade maidaan mein hum cricket, football, kho-kho aur kabaddi khelte hain. Har saal Sports Day hota hai. Iske alawa bhashan, kavita-paath, chitrakala, nibandh lekhan aur vigyaan pradarshani jaisi pratiyogitayein bhi hoti hain. 15 August, 26 January, Shikshak Diwas aur Bal Diwas par rangaarang karyakram hote hain.

Anushaasan

Mere vidyalaya mein anushaasan ka bahut dhyaan rakha jaata hai. Sabhi vidyarthi saaf uniform pehen kar samay par aate hain. Prarthana sabha mein hum line mein khade hote hain aur Rashtragaan gaate hain. Class mein shor nahi karte aur vidyalaya ko saaf rakhte hain. Yahi aadatein aage jeevan mein bhi kaam aati hain.

Upsanhaar

Mera vidyalaya mere liye doosre ghar jaisa hai. Yahan mujhe achhe shikshak, sachche dost aur dher saara gyaan mila hai. Main chahta/chahti hoon ki main khoob padh-likh kar ek achha naagrik banoon aur apne vidyalaya ka naam roshan karoon.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Mera Vidyalaya nibandh mein kaun-kaun si headings likhein?

Prastavana, vidyalaya ka bhavan, pustakalaya aur prayogshaala, shikshak, khel-kood aur gatividhiyan, anushaasan aur upsanhaar.

Vidyalaya ko gyaan ka mandir kyun kehte hain?

Kyunki yahan padhai ke saath achhe sanskaar, anushaasan aur mil-jul kar rehna sikhaya jaata hai — jaise mandir mein man shaant hota hai, waise hi vidyalaya mein man gyaan se bharta hai.

Kya main yeh nibandh apne school ke hisaab se badal sakta hoon?

Haan, zaroor. ______ ki jagah apne school ka naam likhein aur bhavan, khel ya gatividhiyon mein apne school ki asli baatein jodein.

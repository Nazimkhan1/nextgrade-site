✍️ Nibandh

School ke bachchon ke liye aasaan nibandh: har nibandh mein 10 lines, chhoti classes ke liye chhota nibandh aur badi classes ke liye headings wala lamba nibandh — hamare apne shabdon mein.

❄️

Sardi ka Mausam par Nibandh

Sardi ka mausam (sheet ritu) sabse pyaare mausamon mein se ek hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🏫

Mera Vidyalaya par Nibandh

"Mera Vidyalaya" lagbhag har class ke exam mein poocha jaata hai. Neeche 10 lines, chhota nibandh aur headings wala lamba nibandh hai — apne school ka naam aur baatein jod kar likhein.

🐄

Gaay par Nibandh

Gaay par nibandh chhoti classes mein sabse pehle likhwaye jaane wale nibandhon mein se hai. Neeche 10 lines, chhota nibandh aur badi classes ke liye headings wala lamba nibandh hai.

👩

Meri Maa par Nibandh

"Maa" duniya ka sabse pyaara shabd hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🌳

Pedon ka Mahatva par Nibandh

Ped hamare sachche mitra hain. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

💧

Jal hi Jeevan hai par Nibandh

Paani ke bina jeevan sambhav nahi. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🪔

Diwali par Nibandh

Diwali roshni aur khushiyon ka tyohaar hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Mera Priya Mitra par Nibandh

Sachcha dost zindagi ka sabse bada khazana hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🧘

Yoga par Nibandh

Yoga sehat ka khazana hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🏭

Pradushan par Nibandh

Pradushan aaj ki sabse badi samasyaon mein se ek hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🎄

Christmas par Nibandh

Christmas pyaar, daya aur khushiyan baantne ka tyohaar hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

🇮🇳

Gantantra Diwas par Nibandh

Gantantra Diwas hamare Samvidhan aur loktantra ka rashtriya parv hai, jo har saal 26 January ko manaya jata hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur lagbhag 150 shabdon ka chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lagbhag 500 shabdon ka lamba nibandh.

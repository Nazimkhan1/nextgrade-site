🇮🇳 Gantantra Diwas par Nibandh

Gantantra Diwas hamare Samvidhan aur loktantra ka rashtriya parv hai, jo har saal 26 January ko manaya jata hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur lagbhag 150 shabdon ka chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lagbhag 500 shabdon ka lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Gantantra Diwas har saal 26 January ko manaya jata hai.
  2. 26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua aur Bharat ek gantantra bana.
  3. Samvidhan 26 November 1949 ko apnaya gaya tha, isliye 26 November ko Samvidhan Diwas manate hain.
  4. Samvidhan ki Drafting Committee (praroop samiti) ke adhyaksh Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar the.
  5. Dr. Rajendra Prasad Bharat ke pehle Rashtrapati bane.
  6. Nai Dilli ke Kartavya Path par ek shaandaar parade nikalti hai.
  7. Parade mein Rashtrapati salaami lete hain aur rajyon ki sundar jhaankiyan nikalti hain.
  8. Schoolon mein jhanda fehraya jata hai, rashtragaan gaaya jata hai aur mithai baanti jaati hai.
  9. 29 January ko Beating Retreat samaroh ke saath Gantantra Diwas ke karyakram poore hote hain.
  10. Yeh din humein Samvidhan ka samman karna aur achha nagrik banna sikhata hai.

Chhota nibandh — lagbhag 150 shabd (Class 1 se 4)

Gantantra Diwas hamara rashtriya parv hai, jo har saal 26 January ko manaya jata hai. San 1950 mein isi din Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua tha aur Bharat ek gantantra bana. Gantantra ka matlab hai aisa desh, jahan janta apne pratinidhi khud chunti hai aur desh ka mukhiya raja nahin, balki chuna hua Rashtrapati hota hai. Hamare Samvidhan ko banane mein Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar ka bada yogdaan tha. Dr. Rajendra Prasad desh ke pehle Rashtrapati bane. Is din Nai Dilli ke Kartavya Path par shaandaar parade hoti hai, jismein sena ke jawan, rang-birangi jhaankiyan aur school ke bachche hissa lete hain. Rashtrapati parade ki salaami lete hain. Hamare school mein bhi tiranga fehraya jata hai, rashtragaan gaaya jata hai, deshbhakti ke geet aur bhashan hote hain aur ant mein mithai baanti jaati hai. Gantantra Diwas humein apne desh se pyaar karna, niyamon ka paalan karna aur sabke saath mil-julkar rehna sikhata hai. Mujhe yeh din bahut achha lagta hai.

Lamba nibandh — lagbhag 500 shabd (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Bharat mein teen rashtriya parv manaye jaate hain — Swatantrata Diwas, Gandhi Jayanti aur Gantantra Diwas. Inmein Gantantra Diwas ka apna khaas sthaan hai, kyonki yeh hamare Samvidhan ke laagu hone ki yaad dilata hai. Har saal 26 January ko poora desh tirange ke rang mein rang jata hai. 26 January 2027 ko desh apna 78va Gantantra Diwas manayega.

Gantantra Diwas ka itihaas

15 August 1947 ko Bharat azaad to ho gaya, lekin us samay desh chalane ke liye apna Samvidhan nahin tha. Iske liye Samvidhan Sabha banayi gayi. Samvidhan ki Drafting Committee ke adhyaksh Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar the, jinhone bahut mehnat se Samvidhan ka masauda taiyaar karwaya. Lagbhag teen saal ki charcha ke baad 26 November 1949 ko Samvidhan apnaya gaya. Isi yaad mein ab 26 November ko Samvidhan Diwas manaya jata hai. Phir 26 January 1950 ko Samvidhan poore desh mein laagu hua aur Bharat ek gantantra ban gaya. Dr. Rajendra Prasad desh ke pehle Rashtrapati bane.

26 January hi kyon

Samvidhan laagu karne ke liye 26 January ki tareekh soch-samajhkar chuni gayi. 26 January 1930 ko swatantrata senaniyon ne Purna Swaraj, yaani poori azaadi, ka sankalp ghoshit kiya tha. Us din ki yaad ko samman dene ke liye Samvidhan bhi isi tareekh ko laagu kiya gaya.

Hamara Samvidhan aur Prastavana

Samvidhan ki Prastavana (Preamble) batati hai ki Bharat ek samprabhu, samajwadi, panthnirpeksh, loktantrik ganrajya hai. Iska matlab hai ki Bharat apne faisle khud leta hai, sab dharmon ko barabar samman deta hai aur janta apni sarkaar khud chunti hai. Prastavana har nagrik ko nyay, swatantrata, samanta aur bandhutva ka bharosa deti hai. Samvidhan humein adhikaar deta hai, to saath mein hamare kartavya bhi batata hai.

Kartavya Path ki parade

Gantantra Diwas ka sabse bada samaroh Nai Dilli ke Kartavya Path par hota hai. Rashtrapati tiranga fehrate hain, rashtragaan hota hai aur woh parade ki salaami lete hain. Thal sena, nausena aur vayusena ki tukdiyan kadam se kadam milakar chalti hain. Alag-alag rajyon ki jhaankiyan hamari sanskriti ki jhalak dikhati hain. Bahadur bachchon aur lok kalakaron ke karyakram hote hain aur aasmaan mein vimaanon ka kartab sabka mann moh leta hai. Aksar kisi doosre desh ke neta mukhya atithi bankar aate hain.

Schoolon aur desh bhar mein utsav

Desh ke har kone mein, har school, daftar aur gali-mohalle mein jhanda fehraya jata hai. Schoolon mein bachche deshbhakti ke geet gaate hain, bhashan dete hain, natak aur kavayad karte hain, aur ant mein mithai baanti jaati hai. Rajyon ki rajdhaniyon mein bhi parade hoti hai. 29 January ko Beating Retreat samaroh hota hai, jismein sena ke band madhur dhunein bajate hain, aur isi ke saath Gantantra Diwas ke utsav poore hote hain.

Upsanhaar

Gantantra Diwas sirf parade dekhne ka din nahin hai. Yeh humein yaad dilata hai ki desh ki asli taakat uski janta hai. Humein Samvidhan ka samman karna chahiye, niyamon ka paalan karna chahiye, bade hokar vote zaroor dena chahiye aur har insaan se barabari ka vyavhaar karna chahiye. Agar hum sab apne kartavya imaandari se nibhayein, to hamara gantantra aur bhi mazboot banega.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Gantantra Diwas par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, Gantantra Diwas ka itihaas, 26 January hi kyon, Samvidhan aur Prastavana, Kartavya Path ki parade, schoolon mein utsav aur upsanhaar. Chhoti classes ke liye upar di gayi 10 lines ya 150 shabdon ka nibandh kaafi hai.

Gantantra Diwas 26 January ko hi kyon manaya jata hai?

Kyonki 26 January 1950 ko Bharat ka Samvidhan laagu hua tha. Yeh tareekh isliye chuni gayi kyonki 26 January 1930 ko Purna Swaraj ka sankalp ghoshit kiya gaya tha.

2027 mein kaun-sa Gantantra Diwas hai?

26 January 2027 ko Bharat apna 78va Gantantra Diwas manayega.

Samvidhan Diwas aur Gantantra Diwas mein kya antar hai?

26 November 1949 ko Samvidhan apnaya gaya tha, isliye 26 November Samvidhan Diwas hai. 26 January 1950 ko Samvidhan laagu hua, isliye 26 January Gantantra Diwas hai.

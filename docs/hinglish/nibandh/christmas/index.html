🎄 Christmas par Nibandh

Christmas pyaar, daya aur khushiyan baantne ka tyohaar hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Christmas har saal 25 December ko manaya jata hai.
  2. Is din Isa Masih (Yeshu) ka janmdin manaya jata hai.
  3. Bharat mein Christmas ko "Bada Din" bhi kehte hain.
  4. Log apne gharon mein Christmas tree sajaate hain aur us par taara lagate hain.
  5. 24 December ki raat ko "Christmas Eve" kehte hain.
  6. Church mein prarthana hoti hai aur carol (Christmas ke geet) gaaye jaate hain.
  7. Bachchon ko Santa Claus ka intezaar rehta hai, jo tohfe laate hain.
  8. Log cake kaatte hain aur ek-doosre ko "Merry Christmas" kehte hain.
  9. Is din zarooratmandon ki madad karna bhi achha mana jata hai.
  10. Christmas humein pyaar, daya aur maaf karna sikhata hai.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Christmas ek pyaara tyohaar hai, jo har saal 25 December ko aata hai. Is din Isa Masih ka janmdin manaya jata hai. Bharat mein ise Bada Din bhi kehte hain. Log gharon mein Christmas tree sajaate hain aur us par rang-birangi lights, ghantiyan aur sabse upar ek chamakta taara lagate hain. Church mein prarthana hoti hai aur meethe carol gaaye jaate hain. Bachchon ko Santa Claus bahut pasand hain, kyonki woh laal kapdon mein tohfe lekar aate hain. Sab milkar cake khaate hain aur ek-doosre ko "Merry Christmas" kehte hain. Christmas humein sabse pyaar karna aur apni khushiyan baantna sikhata hai. Mujhe Christmas bahut achha lagta hai.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Bharat alag-alag dharmon aur tyohaaron ka desh hai. Yahan har tyohaar mil-julkar manaya jata hai. Christmas Isai dharm ka sabse bada tyohaar hai, lekin aaj har dharm ke log iski raunak ka anand lete hain. Yeh har saal 25 December ko manaya jata hai aur Bharat mein ise "Bada Din" bhi kaha jata hai.

Christmas kyon manaya jata hai

Isai manyata ke anusaar, lagbhag do hazaar saal pehle Bethlehem nagar mein Mata Mariyam aur Yusuf ke ghar Isa Masih ka janm hua tha. Kaha jata hai ki unka janm ek saadharan goshaale mein hua aur aasmaan mein ek chamkeela taara dikhai diya, jiske sahare log unse milne pahunche. Isa Masih ne logon ko pyaar, daya, seva aur maaf karne ka sandesh diya. Unke janmdin ki yaad mein hi Christmas manaya jata hai.

Taiyariyan aur sajavat

Christmas se kai din pehle taiyariyan shuru ho jaati hain. Gharon, churchon aur bazaaron ko rangeen lights, sitaaron aur ghantiyon se sajaya jata hai. Log Christmas tree laakar use khilaunon, ribbon aur chamkeeli gendon se sajaate hain aur sabse upar taara lagate hain. Kai gharon mein Isa Masih ke janm ki jhaanki (crib) bhi banai jati hai.

Christmas ka din

24 December ki raat, yaani Christmas Eve par, kai churchon mein aadhi raat ki vishesh prarthana hoti hai. 25 December ko log naye kapde pehenkar church jaate hain, carol gaate hain aur ek-doosre ko badhaai dete hain. Gharon mein cake, cookies aur tarah-tarah ke pakwaan bante hain. Bachchon ke liye Santa Claus sabse bada aakarshan hote hain — safed daadhi aur laal kapdon wale Santa, jinhe tohfe baantne wala mana jata hai.

Bharat mein Christmas

Bharat mein Goa, Kerala, poorvottar ke rajya jaise Nagaland, Mizoram aur Meghalaya, aur Kolkata, Mumbai jaise shehron mein Christmas ki khaas dhoom rehti hai. Schoolon mein Christmas se pehle karyakram hote hain, jinmein bachche Santa bante hain aur natak va carol prastut karte hain. 25 December ko poore desh mein chhutti rehti hai.

Christmas ka sandesh

Christmas humein sikhata hai ki sachchi khushi doosron ko khushi dene mein hai. Is din bahut se log anathalayon, aspatalon aur gareeb bastiyon mein jaakar kapde, kambal aur khaana baantte hain. Santa Claus ki kahani bhi yahi batati hai ki bina kisi swarth ke doosron ki madad karni chahiye.

Upsanhaar

Christmas pyaar, bhaichaare aur seva ka tyohaar hai. Yeh humein yaad dilata hai ki sabhi insaan ek parivaar ki tarah hain. Humein Christmas ki tarah poore saal doosron se pyaar karna, maaf karna aur zarooratmandon ki madad karni chahiye.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Christmas par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, Christmas kyon manaya jata hai, taiyariyan aur sajavat, Christmas ka din, Bharat mein Christmas, Christmas ka sandesh aur upsanhaar. Chhoti classes ke liye upar di gayi 10 lines kaafi hain.

Christmas ko Bada Din kyon kehte hain?

Bharat mein Christmas ko pyaar se "Bada Din" kaha jata hai, kyonki yeh Isai dharm ka sabse bada tyohaar hai aur poore desh mein dhoomdhaam se manaya jata hai.

Christmas Eve kya hai?

24 December ki shaam aur raat ko Christmas Eve kehte hain. Is raat kai churchon mein aadhi raat ki prarthana hoti hai aur log Christmas ka swagat karte hain.

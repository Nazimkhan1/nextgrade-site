💧 Jal hi Jeevan hai par Nibandh

Paani ke bina jeevan sambhav nahi. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Jal hi jeevan hai — paani ke bina koi jeev nahi reh sakta.
  2. Hum peene, nahane, khana banane aur safai mein paani ka upyog karte hain.
  3. Ped-paudhe aur jaanwar bhi jeene ke liye paani par nirbhar hain.
  4. Dharti ka teen-chauthai hissa paani se dhaka hai, par peene layak paani bahut kam hai.
  5. Saaf paani humein nadiyon, kuon, talabon aur barish se milta hai.
  6. Ganda paani peene se kai bimariyan ho jati hain.
  7. Humein nal khula chhodkar paani barbaad nahi karna chahiye.
  8. Barish ke paani ko bachana jal sanrakshan kehlata hai.
  9. "Jal hai to kal hai" — paani bachana hum sabka kartavya hai.
  10. Ek-ek boond keemti hai, isliye paani soch-samajhkar istemal karein.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Jal hi jeevan hai. Paani ke bina koi bhi jeev zinda nahi reh sakta. Hum paani peete hain, nahate hain, khana banate hain aur safai karte hain. Ped-paudhon aur jaanwaron ko bhi paani chahiye. Saaf paani humein nadi, kuan, talab aur barish se milta hai. Ganda paani peene se bimari hoti hai, isliye hamesha saaf paani peena chahiye. Humein paani barbaad nahi karna chahiye — brush karte ya nahate samay nal band rakhna chahiye. Paani bachao, jeevan bachao.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

"Jal hi jeevan hai" — yeh sirf ek kahawat nahi, balki sachchai hai. Hawa ke baad jeene ke liye sabse zaroori cheez paani hai. Manushya kai din bhookha reh sakta hai, par paani ke bina kuch hi din mein uska jeevan kathin ho jata hai.

Jal ka mahatva

Hamare shareer ka lagbhag do-tihai hissa paani se bana hai. Hum paani peene, nahane, khana banane, kapde dhone aur safai mein iska upyog karte hain. Kheti, karkhanon aur bijli banane mein bhi bahut paani lagta hai. Ped-paudhe, pashu-pakshi aur har jeev jeene ke liye paani par nirbhar hain.

Paani kahan se milta hai

Dharti ka lagbhag teen-chauthai hissa paani se dhaka hai, par isme se adhiktar samudra ka khara paani hai, jo peene layak nahi. Peene layak saaf paani nadiyon, jheelon, kuon, talabon, zameen ke neeche aur barish se milta hai, aur yeh bahut kam hai. Isiliye har boond keemti hai.

Paani ka sankat

Aaj aabadi badhne, barbadi aur pradushan ke karan kai jagah paani ka sankat gehra gaya hai. Garmiyon mein kuan aur talab sookh jate hain aur logon ko door-door se paani lana padta hai. Karkhanon aur gandagi se nadiyan pradushit ho rahi hain. Ganda paani peene se haija, peeliya jaisi bimariyan failti hain.

Jal sanrakshan kaise karein

Humein paani bachane ki aadat daalni chahiye. Brush karte aur nahate samay nal band rakhein, tapakte nalon ko theek karayein aur baalti se nahayein. Barish ke paani ko chhat aur gaddhon mein ikattha karna (varsha jal sanchayan) bahut achha upaay hai. Nadiyon aur talabon mein kooda na phenkein aur zyada ped lagayein, kyonki ped barish aur bhoojal badhate hain.

Hamara kartavya

Paani bachana kisi ek ka nahi, balki hum sabka kartavya hai. Humein khud paani bachana chahiye aur doosron ko bhi iske liye jagaruk karna chahiye. Agar aaj hum laparwahi karenge, to aane wali peedhiyon ko peene ke paani ke liye tarasna padega.

Upsanhaar

Paani prakriti ka anmol uphaar hai, jise na to hum bana sakte hain aur na khareed sakte hain. "Jal hai to kal hai." Agar hum aaj se paani ki ek-ek boond bachana shuru karein, to dharti par jeevan hamesha hara-bhara aur sukhi rahega.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Jal hi jeevan hai nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, jal ka mahatva, paani kahan se milta hai, paani ka sankat, jal sanrakshan ke upaay, hamara kartavya aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Jal sanrakshan ke aasaan tareeke kya hain?

Nal band rakhna, tapakte nal theek karana, baalti se nahana, barish ka paani ikattha karna (varsha jal sanchayan) aur nadiyon-talabon ko saaf rakhna.

Dharti par itna paani hone par bhi peene ka paani kam kyon hai?

Kyonki dharti ka adhikansh paani samudra ka khara paani hai, jo peene layak nahi. Saaf meetha paani bahut kam hai, isliye use bachana zaroori hai.

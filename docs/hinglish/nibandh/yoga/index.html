🧘 Yoga par Nibandh

Yoga sehat ka khazana hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Yoga hamare shareer aur mann dono ko swasth rakhta hai.
  2. Yeh Bharat ki hazaron saal purani den hai.
  3. Yoga mein aasan, pranayam aur dhyan shaamil hain.
  4. Roz subah khali pet yoga karna sabse achha hota hai.
  5. Surya Namaskar sabse achhe aur poorn yoga abhyason mein se ek hai.
  6. Pranayam yani saans ke abhyas se mann shaant hota hai.
  7. Yoga se shareer lachila banta hai aur bimariyan door rehti hain.
  8. Har saal 21 June ko Antarrashtriya Yoga Diwas manaya jata hai.
  9. Yoga padhai mein dhyan lagane aur tanav kam karne mein madad karta hai.
  10. Humein roz thoda yoga zaroor karna chahiye.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Yoga hamari sehat ke liye bahut achha hai. Yeh Bharat ki bahut purani den hai. Yoga mein tarah-tarah ke aasan, saans ke abhyas (pranayam) aur dhyan hote hain. Roz subah yoga karne se shareer mazboot aur mann shaant rehta hai. Surya Namaskar ek bahut achha yoga hai. Yoga karne se bimariyan door rehti hain aur padhai mein bhi mann lagta hai. Har saal 21 June ko Yoga Diwas manaya jata hai. Humein roz thoda yoga zaroor karna chahiye.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Aaj ki bhaagdaud bhari zindagi mein swasth rehna ek chunauti ban gaya hai. Aise mein yoga ek aisa khazana hai jo bina dawa aur bina kharch ke humein tan aur mann dono se swasth rakhta hai. Yoga Bharat ki hazaron saal purani den hai, jise aaj poori duniya apna rahi hai.

Yoga kya hai

Yoga ka arth hai judna — shareer, mann aur saans ka aapas mein mel. Isme mukhya roop se teen cheezein aati hain: aasan (shareer ke vyayam), pranayam (saans ke abhyas) aur dhyan (mann ko ekagra karna). Ye teeno milkar insaan ko poori tarah swasth banate hain.

Yoga ke fayde

Niyamit yoga se shareer lachila aur mazboot banta hai, pachan achha hota hai aur rogon se ladne ki taaqat badhti hai. Pranayam se mann shaant hota hai, gussa aur tanav kam hote hain. Dhyan se ekagrata badhti hai, jisse vidyarthiyon ka padhai mein mann lagta hai aur yaaddasht tez hoti hai.

Yoga kab aur kaise karein

Yoga ke liye subah ka samay sabse achha maana jata hai, jab hawa saaf aur mann taza hota hai. Ise hamesha khali pet, khuli aur shaant jagah par karna chahiye. Shuruaat aasan aasanon aur Surya Namaskar se karein, aur kisi jaankar ya shikshak ki dekhrekh mein seekhein taki koi chot na lage.

Antarrashtriya Yoga Diwas

Yoga ke mahatva ko dekhte hue Sanyukt Rashtra ne 21 June ko "Antarrashtriya Yoga Diwas" ghoshit kiya hai. Pehli baar yeh 2015 mein manaya gaya. Is din duniya bhar mein laakhon log ek saath yoga karte hain, jisse Bharat ki yeh den poori duniya mein phail rahi hai.

Upsanhaar

Yoga sirf ek vyayam nahi, balki jeene ka ek achha tarika hai. Yeh humein swasth shareer, shaant mann aur achhi aadatein deta hai. Agar hum roz thoda samay yoga ko dein, to hum jeevan bhar swasth aur khush reh sakte hain. Isliye aao, yoga ko apni dincharya ka hissa banayein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Yoga par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, yoga kya hai, yoga ke fayde, yoga kab aur kaise karein, Antarrashtriya Yoga Diwas aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Antarrashtriya Yoga Diwas kab manaya jata hai?

Har saal 21 June ko, pehli baar 2015 mein manaya gaya tha.

Yoga ke mukhya ang kaun-se hain?

Aasan (shareer ke vyayam), pranayam (saans ke abhyas) aur dhyan — ye yoga ke teen mukhya ang hain.

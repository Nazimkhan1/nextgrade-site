🏭 Pradushan par Nibandh

Pradushan aaj ki sabse badi samasyaon mein se ek hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Hamare vatavaran ke gande hone ko pradushan kehte hain.
  2. Mukhya roop se vayu, jal, mitti aur dhwani pradushan hote hain.
  3. Gaadiyon aur karkhanon ka dhuaan hawa ko ganda karta hai.
  4. Karkhanon aur koode se nadiyon ka paani zehreela ho jata hai.
  5. Plastic aur rasaayanik khaad mitti ko kharab karte hain.
  6. Tez horn aur loudspeaker se dhwani pradushan hota hai.
  7. Pradushan se saans, aankh aur twacha ki bimariyan hoti hain.
  8. Ped kaatne aur paraali jalane se pradushan aur badhta hai.
  9. Zyada ped lagakar aur plastic kam karke hum ise rok sakte hain.
  10. Paryavaran ko bachana hum sabka kartavya hai.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Hamare vatavaran ke gande hone ko pradushan kehte hain. Yeh chaar tarah ka hota hai — vayu, jal, mitti aur dhwani pradushan. Gaadiyon aur karkhanon ka dhuaan hawa ko ganda karta hai, aur kooda-kachra nadiyon ke paani ko zehreela bana deta hai. Pradushan se humein khaansi, saans aur aankhon ki bimariyan hoti hain. Humein ped lagane chahiye, plastic kam karna chahiye aur kooda sahi jagah phenkna chahiye. Saaf hawa aur paani hum sabke liye zaroori hain.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Prakriti ne humein saaf hawa, nirmal paani aur hari-bhari dharti di hai. Lekin insaan ne apni suvidha aur laalach ke karan ise ganda kar diya hai. Vatavaran ke isi gande hone ko pradushan kehte hain, aur aaj yeh poori duniya ki sabse badi samasyaon mein se ek ban gaya hai.

Pradushan ke prakar

Pradushan mukhya roop se chaar tarah ka hota hai. Vayu pradushan — gaadiyon aur karkhanon ke dhuen se. Jal pradushan — karkhanon ke gande paani aur koode se nadiyon mein. Mrida (mitti) pradushan — plastic aur rasaayanik khaad se. Aur dhwani pradushan — tez horn, loudspeaker aur machinon ke shor se.

Pradushan ke kaaran

Badhti aabadi, karkhane, laakhon gaadiyan, pedon ki andhadhundh kataai, plastic ka badhta istemal aur kheton mein paraali jalana — ye pradushan ke mukhya kaaran hain. Hum suvidha ke peeche bhaagte hue prakriti ka nuksan karte ja rahe hain.

Pradushan ke nuksan

Pradushan hamari sehat ke liye bahut haanikarak hai. Gandi hawa se saans aur phephdon ki bimariyan, gande paani se pet ki bimariyan aur shor se bahrapan va tanav hota hai. Isse pashu-pakshi bhi marte hain, mausam bigadta hai aur dharti ka taapmaan badhta ja raha hai, jise global warming kehte hain.

Pradushan rokne ke upay

Pradushan rokne ke liye humein zyada se zyada ped lagane chahiye aur ped katne se rokne chahiye. Gaadi ki jagah paidal, cycle ya bus ka istemal karein. Plastic ki jagah kapde aur kaagaz ke thaile lein, kooda sahi jagah phenkein aur karkhanon ka ganda paani nadiyon mein jaane se rokein.

Upsanhaar

Pradushan kisi ek desh ya insaan ki nahi, poori maanav jaati ki samasya hai. Agar aaj humne dhyan nahi diya, to aane wali peedhiyon ko saaf hawa aur paani tak nahi milega. Isliye hum sabko milkar paryavaran ko saaf aur hara-bhara rakhna chahiye — yahi dharti aur hamari apni bhalai hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pradushan par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, pradushan ke prakar, kaaran, nuksan, rokne ke upay aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Pradushan kitne prakar ka hota hai?

Mukhya roop se chaar — vayu, jal, mrida (mitti) aur dhwani pradushan.

Pradushan rokne ke aasaan upay kya hain?

Zyada ped lagana, plastic kam karna, kooda sahi jagah phenkna, gaadi ki jagah paidal/cycle/bus ka istemal aur karkhanon ke gande paani ko rokna.

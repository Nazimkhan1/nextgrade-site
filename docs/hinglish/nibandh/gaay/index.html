🐄 Gaay par Nibandh

Gaay par nibandh chhoti classes mein sabse pehle likhwaye jaane wale nibandhon mein se hai. Neeche 10 lines, chhota nibandh aur badi classes ke liye headings wala lamba nibandh hai.

10 lines

  1. Gaay ek paaltu aur bahut seedha-saada jaanwar hai.
  2. Gaay ke chaar pair, do seeng, do kaan, do aankhein aur ek lambi poonchh hoti hai.
  3. Gaay safed, kaali, bhoori ya chitkabri rang ki hoti hai.
  4. Gaay ghaas, bhoosa, chaara, khali aur hari pattiyan khaati hai.
  5. Gaay humein doodh deti hai, jo bahut paushtik hota hai.
  6. Doodh se dahi, makkhan, ghee, paneer aur mithaiyan banti hain.
  7. Gaay ke gobar se khaad aur uple bante hain.
  8. Gaay ke bachche ko bachhda ya bachhiya kehte hain.
  9. Bharat mein bahut se log gaay ko samman se "Gau Mata" kehte hain.
  10. Humein gaay ki achhi dekhbhaal karni chahiye aur use plastic nahi khaane dena chahiye.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Gaay ek paaltu jaanwar hai. Yeh bahut seedhi aur shaant hoti hai. Gaay ke chaar pair, do seeng, do kaan aur ek lambi poonchh hoti hai. Poonchh se woh makkhiyan udaati hai. Gaay ghaas, bhoosa aur hara chaara khaati hai. Gaay humein meetha aur paushtik doodh deti hai. Doodh pee kar bachche swasth aur taakatwar bante hain. Doodh se dahi, makkhan, ghee aur paneer banta hai. Gaay ka gobar kheton mein khaad ke kaam aata hai. Bharat ke gaon mein lagbhag har ghar mein gaay paali jaati hai. Humein gaay ko pyaar karna chahiye aur uska dhyaan rakhna chahiye.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Bharat ek krishi pradhan desh hai aur yahan ke kisaanon ke jeevan mein pashuon ka bahut bada sthaan hai. In pashuon mein gaay sabse upyogi aur priya maani jaati hai. Sadiyon se gaay Bharatiya parivaaron ka hissa rahi hai, isiliye bahut se log use aadar se "Gau Mata" kehte hain.

Gaay ka shareer

Gaay ek chaupaya jaanwar hai. Uske chaar mazboot pair hote hain jinke neeche khur hote hain. Sir par do seeng, do bade kaan aur do chamakdaar aankhein hoti hain. Uski lambi poonchh ke aakhir mein baalon ka guchha hota hai, jisse woh makkhi-machchhar bhagaati hai. Gaay alag-alag rangon ki hoti hai — safed, kaali, bhoori, laal ya chitkabri.

Bhojan aur swabhaav

Gaay shakahaari hai. Woh hari ghaas, bhoosa, chaara, khali, anaaj aur sabziyon ke chhilke khaati hai. Gaay jugaali karne wala pashu hai — woh pehle jaldi-jaldi khaana nigal leti hai aur phir aaraam se baith kar use dobaara chabaati hai. Gaay ka swabhaav bahut shaant hota hai. Woh apne paalne wale ko pehchaanti hai aur bulaane par paas aa jaati hai.

Gaay ki upyogita

Gaay ka doodh bahut paushtik hota hai. Ismein calcium aur protein hota hai, jo haddiyon aur shareer ko mazboot banata hai. Doctor bhi bachchon ko doodh peene ki salaah dete hain. Doodh se dahi, chhaachh, makkhan, ghee, paneer, kheer aur kai mithaiyan banti hain. Gaay ka gobar kheton ke liye achhi jaivik khaad hai. Gaon mein isse uple aur biogas bhi banaayi jaati hai. Gaay ke bachhde bade ho kar bail bante hain, jo khet jotne aur bailgaadi kheenchne mein kisaanon ki madad karte hain.

Bharat mein gaay ki nasl

Bharat mein gaay ki kai desi nasl paayi jaati hain, jaise Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar, Laal Sindhi aur Rathi. Gir gaay Gujarat ki aur Sahiwal Punjab ki maani jaati hai. Yeh nasl garmi sehne aur achha doodh dene ke liye jaani jaati hain.

Hamara kartavya

Aaj shehron mein kai gaayein sadkon par ghoomti dikhti hain aur kooday mein pada plastic kha leti hain, jisse woh beemaar ho jaati hain. Humein kabhi bhi plastic ki thailiyon mein khaana nahi phenkna chahiye. Gaay ko saaf paani, achha chaara, chhaayadaar jagah aur samay par ilaaj milna chahiye. Pashuon ke saath daya ka vyavhaar karna hum sabka kartavya hai.

Upsanhaar

Gaay sachmuch manushya ki sachchi mitra hai. Woh humse bahut kam leti hai aur badle mein bahut kuch deti hai. Humein gaay aur sabhi pashuon ki dekhbhaal karni chahiye, tabhi hamara paryavaran aur samaaj dono swasth rahenge.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Gaay ko jugaali karne wala pashu kyun kehte hain?

Kyunki gaay pehle khaana jaldi nigal leti hai aur baad mein aaraam se baith kar use wapas munh mein la kar dobaara chabaati hai. Ise jugaali karna kehte hain.

Bharat ki prasiddh desi gaayein kaun si hain?

Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar, Laal Sindhi aur Rathi Bharat ki prasiddh desi nasl hain.

Gaay par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, gaay ka shareer, bhojan aur swabhaav, upyogita, nasl, hamara kartavya aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

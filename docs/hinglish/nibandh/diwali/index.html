🪔 Diwali par Nibandh

Diwali roshni aur khushiyon ka tyohaar hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Diwali Bharat ka sabse bada aur sabse pyaara tyohaar hai.
  2. Ise Deepawali bhi kehte hain, jiska arth hai "deepon ki pankti".
  3. Yeh tyohaar October ya November mein aata hai.
  4. Kehte hain isi din Bhagwan Ram chaudah saal ka vanvaas poora karke Ayodhya laute the.
  5. Log gharon ki safai karte hain aur rangoli banate hain.
  6. Shaam ko diye, mombattiyan aur bijli ki jhaalrein jalai jati hain.
  7. Is din Maa Lakshmi aur Bhagwan Ganesh ki pooja hoti hai.
  8. Log naye kapde pehente hain aur mithaiyan baantte hain.
  9. Humein patakhe kam chalane chahiye taki hawa saaf rahe.
  10. Diwali humein andhkaar par prakash aur burai par achhai ki jeet sikhati hai.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Diwali hamara sabse pyaara tyohaar hai. Ise deepon ka tyohaar kehte hain. Yeh tyohaar October ya November mein aata hai. Kehte hain isi din Bhagwan Ram vanvaas se Ayodhya laute the. Isliye log ghar saaf karte hain, rangoli banate hain aur shaam ko diye jalate hain. Is din Maa Lakshmi aur Ganesh ji ki pooja hoti hai. Hum naye kapde pehente hain aur mithaiyan baantte hain. Humein patakhe kam chalane chahiye taki hawa saaf rahe. Diwali humein khushiyan baantna sikhati hai. Mujhe Diwali bahut pasand hai.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Bharat tyohaaron ka desh hai, aur inme Diwali sabse bada aur sabse chamkeela tyohaar hai. Ise Deepawali bhi kehte hain, jiska arth hai "deepon ki pankti". Yeh tyohaar andhkaar par prakash aur burai par achhai ki jeet ka prateek hai.

Diwali kyon manai jati hai

Manyata hai ki isi din Bhagwan Ram, Ravan ko harakar aur chaudah varsh ka vanvaas poora karke Mata Sita aur bhai Lakshman ke saath Ayodhya laute the. Unke swagat mein nagarvasiyon ne ghee ke diye jalakar poori nagari ko roshan kar diya tha. Tabhi se Diwali deep jalakar manai jati hai. Kai log is din dhan ki devi Maa Lakshmi ke aagman ka utsav bhi manate hain.

Taiyariyan

Diwali se kai din pehle taiyariyan shuru ho jati hain. Log apne gharon ki saaf-safai aur rangai-putai karte hain. Bazaar diyon, mithaiyon, khilaunon aur sajawat ke saaman se saj jate hain. Ghar ke aangan aur darwaze par rang-birangi rangoli banai jati hai aur gharon ko diyon tatha bijli ki jhaalron se sajaya jata hai.

Paanch din ka tyohaar

Diwali keval ek din nahi, balki paanch dinon ka tyohaar hai — Dhanteras, Narak Chaturdashi (Chhoti Diwali), mukhya Diwali (Lakshmi Pooja), Govardhan Pooja aur Bhai Dooj. Mukhya Diwali ki shaam ko log naye kapde pehenkar Maa Lakshmi aur Bhagwan Ganesh ki pooja karte hain, ghar ko diyon se sajate hain aur ek-doosre ko mithaiyan tatha uphaar dete hain.

Diwali ka sandesh

Diwali humein sikhati hai ki sachchai aur achhai ki hamesha jeet hoti hai. Yeh tyohaar aapsi prem, bhaichara aur khushiyan baantne ka sandesh deta hai. Is din humein gareebon aur zarooratmandon ko bhi mithai aur uphaar dekar unki khushi mein shaamil hona chahiye.

Savdhaaniyan

Patakhon se hawa aur dhwani pradushan hota hai aur kai baar durghatnaayein bhi ho jati hain. Isliye humein patakhe kam chalane chahiye ya bilkul nahi, aur diye jalakar "green Diwali" manani chahiye. Chhote bachchon ko badon ke saath hi patakhon ke paas jana chahiye.

Upsanhaar

Diwali keval roshni ka nahi, balki mann ki safai aur achhai ka bhi tyohaar hai. Humein ise pyaar, suraksha aur swachhta ke saath manana chahiye. Diyon ki tarah humein bhi apne jeevan aur doosron ke jeevan mein ujaala failana chahiye. Isiliye Diwali mera sabse priya tyohaar hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Diwali par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, Diwali kyon manai jati hai, taiyariyan, paanch din ka tyohaar, Diwali ka sandesh, savdhaaniyan aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Diwali kyon manai jati hai?

Manyata hai ki isi din Bhagwan Ram chaudah varsh ka vanvaas poora kar aur Ravan ko harakar Ayodhya laute the, aur logon ne diye jalakar unka swagat kiya tha.

Diwali ko deepon ka tyohaar kyon kehte hain?

Kyonki is din log apne gharon ko dher saare diyon, mombattiyon aur roshni se sajate hain, isliye ise deepon ka tyohaar ya Deepawali kehte hain.

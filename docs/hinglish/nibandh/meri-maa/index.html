👩 Meri Maa par Nibandh

"Maa" duniya ka sabse pyaara shabd hai. Neeche chhote bachchon ke liye 10 lines aur chhota nibandh hai, aur badi classes ke liye headings ke saath lamba nibandh.

10 lines

  1. Maa duniya ka sabse pyaara aur pehla shabd hai.
  2. Meri maa ka naam … hai aur wo hamare ghar ki jaan hain.
  3. Wo subah sabse pehle uthti hain aur raat ko sabse baad mein soti hain.
  4. Maa hamare liye khana banati hain, kapde dhoti hain aur ghar sambhalti hain.
  5. Bimar hone par maa raat-raat bhar jaagkar meri dekhbhaal karti hain.
  6. Wo mujhe padhati hain, achhi baatein sikhati hain aur sahi-galat samjhati hain.
  7. Maa kabhi thakti nahi aur badle mein kuch nahi maangti.
  8. Unki god duniya ki sabse surakshit jagah lagti hai.
  9. Maa ka pyaar sachcha, nihswarth aur bina shart hota hai.
  10. Main apni maa se bahut pyaar karta/karti hoon aur unka kehna maanta/maanti hoon.

Chhota nibandh (Class 1 se 4)

Meri maa duniya ki sabse achhi maa hain. Wo poore ghar ka dhyan rakhti hain. Subah jaldi uthkar mere liye nashta aur tiffin banati hain, mujhe taiyar karke school bhejti hain. Jab main ghar aata hoon to muskura kar mera swagat karti hain. Maa mujhe padhati hain aur achhi aadatein sikhati hain — sach bolna, badon ka aadar karna aur mehnat karna. Jab main bimar hota hoon to wo mere paas baithi rehti hain. Maa kabhi apne baare mein nahi sochti, hamesha parivar ke liye jeeti hain. Main apni maa se bahut pyaar karta hoon.

Lamba nibandh (Class 5 se 10)

Prastavana

Is sansar mein maa se badhkar koi nahi. Kehte hain ki Bhagwan har jagah nahi pahunch sakte, isliye unhone maa ko banaya. Maa ka pyaar aisa jharna hai jo bina ruke, bina shart behta rehta hai. Mere liye meri maa meri pehli guru, sabse achhi dost aur sabse badi taaqat hain.

Maa ka din

Meri maa ghar mein sabse pehle uthti hain aur sabse baad mein soti hain. Wo khana banati hain, ghar saaf rakhti hain, kapde dhoti hain aur sabki zarooraton ka dhyan rakhti hain. Mere liye garam tiffin taiyar karti hain aur school jaate samay pyaar se vida karti hain. Itna kaam karne ke baad bhi unke chehre par thakan nahi, bas muskaan rehti hai.

Meri pehli shikshak

Maa hi hamari sabse pehli shikshak hoti hain. Pehla shabd, pehla kadam aur pehli seekh — sab maa se milti hai. Wo humein sach bolna, badon ka aadar karna, chhoton se pyaar karna aur mehnat se kaam karna sikhati hain. Jab mujhse koi galti hoti hai, to wo daantti nahi, balki pyaar se samjhati hain ki sahi kya hai.

Maa ka tyag

Maa apne sukh se pehle hamara sukh dekhti hain. Achhi cheez wo apne bachchon ke liye rakh deti hain. Bimar hone par wo khud dard mein rehkar bhi hamari seva karti hain aur raat-raat bhar jaagti hain. Wo apne liye kam aur parivar ke liye zyada sochti hain. Itna tyag shayad hi koi aur kar sake.

Maa ka mahatva

Maa parivar ki reedh hoti hain; unke bina ghar adhoora lagta hai. Wo na sirf shareer ka, balki mann ka bhi dhyan rakhti hain — jab main udaas hota hoon to unki baatein mera dil halka kar deti hain. Maa ki seekh aur sanskar jeevan bhar hamare saath rehte hain aur humein achha insaan banate hain.

Hamara kartavya

Maa hamare liye itna karti hain, to hamara bhi farz banta hai ki hum unka aadar karein, unki baat maanein aur unhe kabhi dukhi na karein. Bade hokar humein unki waise hi seva karni chahiye jaise unhone bachpan mein hamari ki. Maa ka dil dukhana sabse bada paap hai.

Upsanhaar

Maa ishwar ka diya sabse anmol uphaar hai. Unke pyaar ka karz hum kabhi nahi chuka sakte. Main Bhagwan se yahi prarthna karta hoon ki meri maa hamesha swasth aur khush rahein. Mujhe apni maa par garv hai aur main unse duniya mein sabse zyada pyaar karta hoon.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Meri Maa par nibandh mein kya-kya likhein?

Prastavana, maa ka din, pehli shikshak, maa ka tyag, mahatva, hamara kartavya aur upsanhaar — upar ke lambe nibandh mein yahi headings hain.

Maa ko pehli guru kyon kehte hain?

Kyonki bachcha sabse pehli seekh — pehla shabd, chalna, achhi aadatein aur sanskar — apni maa se hi paata hai, school jaane se bhi pehle.

Chhoti classes ke liye Maa par 10 lines kaise likhein?

Upar di gayi 10 aasaan lines istemal karein — maa ka naam, unka kaam, unka pyaar aur tyag, aur yeh ki aap unse kitna pyaar karte hain.

🏡 Lok-kathaayein aur Dadi-Naani ki kahaniyan

Peedhiyon se sunaayi ja rahi lok-kathaayein aur Dadi-Naani ki kahaniyan, sone se pehle sunaane ke liye.

Lok-kathaayein woh kahaniyan hain jo kisi kitaab se nahi, balki gaon-gharon mein sunaate-sunaate peedhi-dar-peedhi aage badhin. Isliye ek hi kahani ke alag-alag ilaakon mein alag-alag roop milte hain. Humne ise apne shabdon mein bachchon ke liye phir se likha hai.

Yeh Dadi-Naani ki kahani hai — aisi kahaniyan jo raat ko sone se pehle ghar ke bade bachchon ko sunaate aaye hain. Inmein koi ek lekhak nahi, balki har parivaar apna thoda rang jodta hai. Humne ise apne shabdon mein phir se likha hai.

🐦 Lok-katha · 2 minute

Pyaasa Kauwa

Garmi ki ek dopahar thi.

 🐕 Lok-katha · 2 minute

Laalchi Kutta

Ek gaon mein ek kutta rehta tha.

 🧢 Lok-katha · 3 minute

Topiwala aur Bandar

Ek topiwala gaanv-gaanv ghoom ke rang-birangi topiyan bechta tha.

 🐤 Dadi-Nani ki kahani · 3 minute

Chidiya ka Daana

Ek nanhi chidiya ko chane ka ek daana mila.

 🫏 Lok-katha · 3 minute

Baap, Beta aur Gadha

Ek kisaan apne bete ke saath apna gadha bechne baazaar ja raha tha.

🌟 Seekh

  • Pyaasa Kauwa — Mushkil mein ghabraane ke bajaye soch-samajh kar upaay dhoondho. Dheere-dheere ki gayi koshish bhi bada kaam kar deti hai.
  • Laalchi Kutta — Laalach buri bala hai. Jo hamare paas hai, usme khush rehna seekho.
  • Topiwala aur Bandar — Museebat mein gussa karne ke bajaye dhyaan se dekho aur socho. Hal aksar saamne hi hota hai.
  • Chidiya ka Daana — Chhota hone se koi kamzor nahi hota. Himmat na haaro — ek chhoti-si madad bhi bada kaam kar deti hai.
  • Baap, Beta aur Gadha — Sabko khush karna naamumkin hai. Logon ki baat suno, par faisla apni samajh se karo.

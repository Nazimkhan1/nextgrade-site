Vikram-Betaal · 3 minute

🌙 Vikram-Betaal: Sabse Komal Rani Kaun?

Raja Vikramaditya ek baar phir ped se Betaal ko utaar ke kandhe pe laade chal pade. Betaal hansa, “Rajan, raasta lamba hai. Ek kahani suno. Par yaad rakhna — jawaab jaante hue bhi chup rahe, to tumhare sir ke tukde ho jaayenge, aur bole, to main waapas ped pe ud jaaunga.”

Betaal ne kahani shuru ki: ek raja ki teen raaniyan thin, aur teeno bahut komal thin. Ek din baagh mein pehli rani ke haath pe kamal ki ek pankhudi giri — aur uske haath pe nishaan pad gaya, woh dard se behosh ho gayi.

Usi raat doosri rani chhat pe baithi thi. Chaand ki thandi chaandni uske badan pe padi, to uski tvacha pe chhaale pad gaye.

Teesri rani mahal ke andar thi. Door kisi ghar mein koi moosal se dhaan koot raha tha. Us “dham-dham” ki awaaz sunte hi rani ke haathon mein dard hone laga aur hatheliyon pe chhaale ubhar aaye.

Betaal ne poochha, “Batao Vikram, teeno mein sabse komal kaun thi?” Vikram bole, “Teesri rani. Pehli ko pankhudi chhui, doosri ko chaandni — par teesri ko to kuch chhua bhi nahi, sirf awaaz se hi chhaale pad gaye.” “Sahi jawaab!” Betaal khilkhilaaya — “par tum bol pade!” Aur woh ud ke phir ped pe ja latka.

🌟 Seekh

Dhyaan se suno aur socho — sahi jawaab aksar chhoti-si baat mein chhupa hota hai. Aur Vikram ki tarah, sach kehne se kabhi mat daro.

Vikram-Betaal ke baare mein

Vikram-Betaal ki kahaniyan "Betaal Pachisi" se hain — pachchees kahaniyon ka purana sangrah. Raja Vikram ped par latke Betaal ko kandhe par laadkar le jaate hain, aur Betaal raaste mein ek kahani sunaakar uske ant mein ek paheli poochhta hai. Raja sahi jawaab dete hain to Betaal udkar wapas ped par chala jaata hai.

🏠 Chidiya ka Ghar, Kauwe ka Ghar

Ek ped par ek chidiya aur ek kauwa padosi the. Barsaat aane waali thi, to dono ne ghar banaane ki sochi. Chidiya din-raat mehnat se tinke, dhaage aur geeli mitti jod kar mazboot ghonsla banaane lagi.

Kauwe ko yeh sab bahut dheema laga. Usne paas pade namak ke dhele uthaaye, jhatpat jod kar ghar khada kar diya aur chidiya par hansa, "Dekho, mera ghar to ek din mein ban gaya! Tum abhi tak tinke hi dho rahi ho."

Chidiya ne kuch nahi kaha, bas apna kaam karti rahi. Phir ek raat zor ki baarish aayi. Chidiya ka ghonsla mazbooti se tika raha, par kauwe ka namak ka ghar paani mein ghul kar beh gaya.

Bheega, kaanpta kauwa chidiya ke darwaaze par aaya, "Chidiya behan, mujhe thodi jagah de do, main thand se mar jaaunga." Chidiya ko kauwe ki hansi yaad aayi, par uska dil naram tha. Usne darwaaza khol diya aur garam daliya bhi khilaaya.

Kauwe ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye. "Mujhe maaf karna, maine tumhari mehnat ka mazaak udaaya." Baarish ke baad dono ne milkar kauwe ke liye bhi mazboot ghar banaaya, aur phir hamesha achhe dost bankar rahe.

🌟 Seekh

Jaldbaazi se nahi, mehnat aur dheeraj se bana kaam hi tikta hai. Aur jisne hamaara mazaak udaaya ho, museebat mein uski bhi madad karo.

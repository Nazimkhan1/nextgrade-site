👑 Akbar-Birbal ki kahaniyan

Birbal ki haazirjawaabi aur samajhdaari ke mazedaar kisse, bachchon ke liye aasaan bhasha mein.

Birbal Mughal baadshah Akbar ke darbaar mein the aur apni haazirjawaabi aur samajhdaari ke liye mashhoor the. Akbar-Birbal ki kahaniyan lok-kathaon ki tarah peedhi-dar-peedhi chali aa rahi hain — inmein se kai sachchi ghatnaayein nahi, balki Birbal ki chaturaai dikhaane wali mazedaar kahaniyan hain.

📏 Akbar-Birbal · 2 minute

Akbar-Birbal: Chhoti Lakeer

Ek din Baadshah Akbar ke darbaar mein sab baithe the.

 🐦‍⬛ Akbar-Birbal · 2 minute

Shehar mein Kitne Kauwe?

Ek subah Baadshah Akbar Birbal ke saath baagh mein tehal rahe the.

 🍲 Akbar-Birbal · 3 minute

Birbal ki Khichdi

Kadaake ki thand ka mausam tha.

 👀 Akbar-Birbal · 3 minute

Birbal: Andhon ki Ginti

Ek din darbaar mein Baadshah Akbar ne poochha, "Birbal, hamaari saltanat mein andhe zyada hain ya dekhne waale?" Birbal muskuraaye, "Jahaanpanah, andhe zyada hain." Akbar hans pade, "Saabit karke dikhao!"

🌟 Seekh

  • Akbar-Birbal: Chhoti Lakeer — Doosron ko neecha dikhaane ke bajaye khud ko behtar banao.
  • Shehar mein Kitne Kauwe? — Mushkil sawaal pe ghabrao mat. Shaant dimaag aur thodi hansi se har uljhan ka jawaab nikal aata hai.
  • Birbal ki Khichdi — Kisi ki mehnat ka haq mat chheeno. Galti samajh aaye to use maan ke sudhaarna hi sachchi badaai hai.
  • Birbal: Andhon ki Ginti — Sirf aankhein hona kaafi nahi, dhyaan se dekhna bhi zaroori hai. Sawaal poochhne se pehle ek baar khud dekho aur socho.

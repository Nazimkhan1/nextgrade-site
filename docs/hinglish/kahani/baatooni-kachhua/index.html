🐢 Baatooni Kachhua

Ek taalaab mein ek kachhua rehta tha. Uske do pakke dost the — do hans. Kachhua bahut achha tha, bas ek kami thi: woh baat-baat pe bolta tha aur chup rehna use aata hi nahi tha.

Ek saal baarish nahi hui aur taalaab sookhne laga. Hanson ne kaha, “Hum door ki ek badi jheel mein ja rahe hain. Tumhe bhi le chalenge. Hum ek lakdi ke dono sire chonch mein pakdenge, tum beech se use daanton se pakad lena.”

“Par yaad rakhna,” hans bole, “raaste bhar munh mat kholna, chahe kuch bhi ho.” Kachhue ne haami bhari. Hans lakdi le ke ud chale aur kachhua beech mein latka raha.

Jab woh ek gaanv ke upar se guzre, neeche bachche chillaaye, “Dekho, dekho! Hans kachhue ko uda le ja rahe hain!” Kachhue ko gussa aaya. Woh kehna chahta tha, “Tumhe kya matlab?”

Jaise hi usne bolne ke liye munh khola, lakdi chhoot gayi aur woh neeche gir pada. Ganeemat thi ki woh ek ghaas ke dher pe gira aur bach gaya, par use lamba aur mushkil raasta paidal tay karna pada. Tab se usne seekha ki kab bolna hai aur kab chup rehna hai.

🌟 Seekh

Har baat pe bolna zaroori nahi. Sahi samay pe chup rehna bhi badi samajhdaari hai.

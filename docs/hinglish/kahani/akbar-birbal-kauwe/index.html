Akbar-Birbal · 2 minute

🐦‍⬛ Shehar mein Kitne Kauwe?

Ek subah Baadshah Akbar Birbal ke saath baagh mein tehal rahe the. Pedon pe bahut se kauwe kaanv-kaanv kar rahe the. Akbar ko ek mazedaar sawaal soojha.

Unhone poochha, “Birbal, batao to, hamare shehar mein kul kitne kauwe hain?” Darbaari soch mein pad gaye — bhala kauwe koi gin sakta hai?

Birbal ne thoda socha aur muskura ke bole, “Jahanpanah, hamare shehar mein theek nabbe hazaar nau sau nabbe kauwe hain.”

Akbar hanse, “Aur agar ginne pe kam nikle to?” Birbal bole, “To samjhiye kuch kauwe apne rishtedaaron se milne doosre shehar gaye hain.” “Aur agar zyada nikle?” “To doosre shehron ke kauwe yahan mehmaan ban ke aaye hain, huzoor!”

Ye jawaab sun ke Akbar thahaaka maar ke hans pade aur poora darbaar bhi. Baadshah ne Birbal ki haazirjawaabi pe unhe inaam diya.

Mushkil sawaal pe ghabrao mat. Shaant dimaag aur thodi hansi se har uljhan ka jawaab nikal aata hai.

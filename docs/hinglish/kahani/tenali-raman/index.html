🧠 Tenali Raman ki kahaniyan

Vijayanagar ke kavi Tenali Raman ki chaturaai aur hasya ke kisse, bachchon ke liye.

Tenali Raman (Tenali Ramakrishna) Vijayanagar ke Raja Krishnadevaraya ke darbaar mein kavi the. Woh apni chaturaai aur mazaakiya andaaz ke liye jaane jaate hain. Dakshin Bharat se nikli unki kahaniyan aaj poore desh mein bachchon ko sunaayi jaati hain.

🪣 Tenali Raman · 3 minute

Tenali Raman aur Chor

Vijayanagar mein us saal baarish kam hui thi aur kuein ka paani bahut neeche chala gaya tha.

 🐈 Tenali Raman · 3 minute

Tenali Raman ki Billi

Vijayanagar mein choohe bahut badh gaye the.

 🐴 Tenali Raman · 3 minute

Tenali Raman ka Ghoda

Raja Krishnadevaraya ne apni ghudsena badhaane ke liye har darbaari ko ek ghoda diya aur saath mein uske daane-paani ke liye har mahine paise bhi.

 🪣 Tenali Raman · 3 minute

Tenali Raman aur Kuen ka Khazaana

Ek garmi mein Tenali Raman ke baagh ke paudhe sookhne lage.

🌟 Seekh

  • Tenali Raman aur Chor — Hoshiyaari se badi-badi mushkilein aasaan ho jaati hain. Aur galat raaste ki mehnat kabhi kaam nahi aati.
  • Tenali Raman ki Billi — Kisi cheez ko uske asli kaam se parkho, sirf dikhaawe se nahi.
  • Tenali Raman ka Ghoda — Jo cheez kisi aur ke liye di gayi ho, woh usi ko milni chahiye. Chaalaaki se thodi der bach sakte ho, par imaandaari hi sahi raasta hai.
  • Tenali Raman aur Kuen ka Khazaana — Ghabraane ki jagah dimaag lagao — buddhi se mushkil ko bhi faayde mein badla ja sakta hai.

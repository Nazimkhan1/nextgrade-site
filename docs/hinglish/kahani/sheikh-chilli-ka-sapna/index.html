Sheikh Chilli · 3 minute

🥛 Sheikh Chilli ka Sapna

Ek gaanv mein Sheikh Chilli naam ka ek bhola-bhaala ladka rehta tha. Woh mehnat kam aur sapne zyada dekhta tha. Ek din ek doodhwale ne usse kaha, “Ye doodh ka matka shehar tak pahuncha do, badle mein ek aana dunga.”

Sheikh Chilli ne matka sir pe rakha aur chal pada. Chalte-chalte woh sochne laga, “Ek aane se main murgi ke ande khareedunga. Andon se chooze niklenge, chooze murgiyan banenge, aur phir dher saare ande!”

“Ande bech ke main bakri loonga, phir gaay, phir bhains. Main ameer ho jaaunga, bada ghar banega, meri shaadi hogi aur mere bachche honge.”

“Jab bachche khaane ke liye awaaz denge, to main raub se sir hila ke kahunga — ‘Nahi, abhi nahi!’” Aur sapne mein khoye Sheikh Chilli ne sach mein zor se sir hila diya.

Dhadaam! Matka sir se gira, phoot gaya aur saara doodh sadak pe beh gaya. Na ek aana mila, na ande, na ghar. Sheikh Chilli bas toote matke ko dekhta reh gaya.

🌟 Seekh

Sapne dekhna achha hai, par pehle haath ka kaam dhyaan se poora karo. Khayaali pulao se pet nahi bharta.

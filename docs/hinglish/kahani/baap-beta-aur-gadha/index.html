🫏 Baap, Beta aur Gadha

Ek kisaan apne bete ke saath apna gadha bechne baazaar ja raha tha. Dono gadhe ke saath paidal chal rahe the. Raaste mein kuch log hanse, “Kaise bewakoof hain! Gadha saath hai, phir bhi paidal chal rahe hain.”

Ye sun ke kisaan ne bete ko gadhe pe bitha diya. Thodi door kuch buzurg bole, “Dekho, aajkal ke bachche! Jawaan ladka aaraam se baitha hai aur boodha baap paidal chal raha hai.” Beta sharminda ho ke utar gaya aur pita gadhe pe baith gaye.

Aage kuch auratein bolin, “Kitna kathor pita hai! Khud aaraam se baitha hai aur nanha beta dhoop mein thak ke chal raha hai.” To kisaan ne bete ko bhi apne saath gadhe pe bitha liya.

Ab kuch aur log bole, “Bechaara gadha! Do-do log is chhote se jaanwar pe lade hain.” Pareshaan kisaan aur bete ne gadhe ke pair ek dande se baandhe aur use kandhon pe utha ke chal pade. Log ye dekh ke zor-zor se hansne lage.

Ek pul pe itna shor sun ke gadha ghabra gaya, chhatpataaya aur nadi mein ja gira. Kisaan ne sir pakad liya, “Sabko khush karne ke chakkar mein maine apna gadha bhi kho diya.”

Panchatantra · 3 minute

🐺 Neela Siyaar

Ek jungle mein Chandarav naam ka ek siyaar rehta tha. Ek raat bhookh se pareshaan hokar woh khaane ki talaash mein paas ke gaanv mein ghus gaya. Gaanv ke kutton ne use dekha to bhaunkte hue uske peechhe daud pade.

Jaan bachaane ke liye siyaar bhaaga aur ek rangrez ke ghar mein ja ghusa. Andhere mein woh seedha neel se bhare ek bade tub mein ja gira. Jab bahar nikla, to sir se poonchh tak gehra neela ho chuka tha.

Subah jungle mein lauta to jaanwar use dekh kar dar gaye — aisa ajeeb neela jaanwar kisi ne pehle nahi dekha tha. Chaalaak siyaar ne mauka dekha aur bola, "Daro mat! Bhagwan ne mujhe tumhara raja bana kar bheja hai."

Sher, baagh, haathi — sab uski seva karne lage. Sabse achha khaana use milta. Par usne apne hi siyaar bhaiyon ko jungle se nikalwa diya, taaki koi use pehchaan na le.

Ek chaandni raat door se siyaaron ka jhund "huaan-huaan" karne laga. Aadat se majboor neela siyaar bhi gala utha kar "huaan-huaan" chilla pada. Sabko sach pata chal gaya, aur sab jaanwaron ne milkar us dhokhebaaz ko jungle se bhaga diya.

Panchatantra ke baare mein

Panchatantra Bharat ka bahut purana katha-sangrah hai, jise lagbhag do hazaar saal purana maana jaata hai aur jiska shrey Pandit Vishnu Sharma ko diya jaata hai. Kaha jaata hai ki unhone rajkumaron ko neeti aur samajhdaari sikhaane ke liye jaanwaron ki yeh kahaniyan sunaayin. Iske paanch bhaag (tantra) hain, isliye iska naam Panchatantra pada. "Neela Siyaar" kahani kahan se hai?

Yeh kahani Panchatantra se li gayi hai aur bachchon ke liye aasaan shabdon mein phir se likhi gayi hai.

"Neela Siyaar" padhne mein kitna samay lagta hai, aur yeh kis umar ke bachchon ke liye hai?

Lagbhag 3 minute. Yeh 3 se 10 saal ke bachchon ko sone se pehle sunaane ke liye achhi hai. "Sunao" button dabao to phone khud kahani padh ke suna deta hai, aur night mode aur sleep timer bhi hai.

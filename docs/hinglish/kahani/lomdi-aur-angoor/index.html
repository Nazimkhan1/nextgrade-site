🍇 Lomdi aur Khatte Angoor

Ek dopahar ek bhookhi lomdi jungle mein khaana dhoondh rahi thi. Chalte-chalte woh ek baagh mein pahunchi, jahan oonchi bel pe pake hue, raseele angooron ke guchchhe latak rahe the.

Angoor dekh kar lomdi ke munh mein paani aa gaya. Usne socha, “Waah! Aaj to daawat hogi.” Woh peechhe hati, daudi aur zor se uchhli — par angoor bahut upar the.

Usne phir koshish ki, phir ek baar aur. Har baar uska munh angooron se thoda neeche reh jaata. Aakhir mein woh haanfne lagi aur uski taangein thak gayin.

Lomdi ne idhar-udhar dekha ki koi dekh to nahi raha. Phir naak upar karke boli, “Hunh! Ye angoor to khatte hain. Aise khatte angoor kaun khaaye!”

Aur woh akad ke chali gayi, jaise use angoor chahiye hi nahi the. Ped pe baithi ek chidiya ye sab dekh rahi thi aur mann hi mann muskura rahi thi.

🌟 Seekh

Jo cheez hamein nahi milti, use bura batana aasaan hai. Sach maan lena aur phir se koshish karna behtar hai.

Maa ke liye chhoti madad
🔊 Sunao — phone khud kahani padhta hai. 🌙 Night mode — andheri screen, bade akshar, taaki bachche ki aankh pe roshni na pade. ⏲️ Sleep timer — 5/10/15 minute baad awaaz apne aap band aur screen andheri.

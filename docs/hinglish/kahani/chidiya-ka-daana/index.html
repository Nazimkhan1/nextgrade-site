🐤 Chidiya ka Daana

Ek nanhi chidiya ko chane ka ek daana mila. Woh use badhai ke aangan mein pade ek lakdi ke khoonte pe rakh ke khaane hi waali thi ki daana phisal ke khoonte ki daraar mein atak gaya. Chidiya ne bahut chonch maari, par daana nahi nikla.

Woh badhai ke paas gayi, “Badhai bhaiya, khoonta cheero, mera daana nikaalo.” Badhai bola, “Ek daane ke liye main khoonta cheeroon? Ja, bhaag!” Chidiya raja ke paas gayi, “Raja ji, badhai ko daanto, woh khoonta nahi cheerta.” Raja ne bhi hans ke taal diya.

Chidiya rani ke paas gayi — rani ne nahi suna. Saanp ke paas gayi, “Saanp bhai, rani ko darao.” Saanp ne munh pher liya. Laathi ke paas gayi, “Laathi behen, saanp ko maaro.” Laathi bhi chup rahi. Aag ke paas gayi, “Aag, laathi ko jalao.” Aag ne bhi mana kar diya.

Aakhir mein chidiya samundar ke paas gayi, “Samundar bhaiya, aag bujhao.” Samundar bhi nahi maana. Tab woh ek haathi ke paas gayi, “Haathi dada, samundar ko sokh lo.” Haathi hansa. Par ek nanhi cheenti ne chidiya ki baat sun li. Cheenti boli, “Main haathi ki soond mein ghus jaaungi!”

Bas, phir kya tha! Haathi dar ke bola, “Main samundar sokhunga!” Samundar bola, “Main aag bujhaaunga!” Aag boli, “Main laathi jalaaungi!” Laathi, saanp, rani, raja — sab ek ke baad ek maan gaye. Raja ne badhai ko bulaya, badhai ne khoonta cheera, aur chidiya ne apna daana nikaal ke khushi-khushi khaaya aur “cheen-cheen” karti ud gayi.

🌟 Seekh

Chhota hone se koi kamzor nahi hota. Himmat na haaro — ek chhoti-si madad bhi bada kaam kar deti hai.

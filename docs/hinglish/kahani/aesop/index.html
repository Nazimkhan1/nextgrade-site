🦊 Aesop ki kahaniyan

Aesop ki chhoti-chhoti kahaniyan, jinke ant mein saaf seekh hoti hai.

Aesop ki kahaniyan praacheen Yunaan (Greece) ke kathakaar Aesop ke naam se jaani jaati hain, jinke baare mein maana jaata hai ki woh lagbhag dhaai hazaar saal pehle hue. Yeh chhoti-chhoti kahaniyan zyadatar jaanwaron ki hoti hain aur har kahani ke ant mein ek saaf seekh hoti hai. Aaj yeh duniya bhar ke bachchon ko sunaayi jaati hain.

🐢 Aesop ki kahani · 3 minute

Kachhua aur Khargosh

Ek jungle mein ek khargosh rehta tha.

 🦁 Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Sher aur Chooha

Ek ghane jungle mein ek sher ped ke neeche so raha tha.

 🥚 Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Sone ka Anda Dene Wali Murgi

Ek gaon mein ek kisaan aur uski patni rehte the.

 🍇 Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Lomdi aur Khatte Angoor

Ek dopahar ek bhookhi lomdi jungle mein khaana dhoondh rahi thi.

 🦊 Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Chaalaak Lomdi aur Kauwa

Ek kauwe ko kahin se roti ka ek bada tukda mil gaya.

 🐺 Aesop ki kahani · 3 minute

Bhediya Aaya!

Ek pahaadi gaanv mein ek ladka roz gaanv ki bhedein charaane le jaata tha.

 🐜 Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Cheenti aur Kabootar

Ek garam din ek pyaasi cheenti paani peene nadi ke kinaare gayi.

 🌾 Aesop ki kahani · 3 minute

Kisaan ka Khazaana

Ek gaanv mein ek mehnati kisaan rehta tha.

 🌬️ Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

Hawa aur Suraj

Ek din Hawa aur Suraj mein bahas chhid gayi ki dono mein zyada taaqatwar kaun hai.

 🐭 Aesop ki kahani · 3 minute

Shehar ka Chooha, Gaanv ka Chooha

Gaanv ke ek khet ke kinaare ek chooha rehta tha.

🌟 Seekh

  • Kachhua aur Khargosh — Ghamand hamein hara deta hai. Dheere-dheere par lagaataar mehnat karne wala hi jeet-ta hai.
  • Sher aur Chooha — Kisi ko chhota mat samjho. Daya aur bhalaai ka badla ek din zaroor milta hai.
  • Sone ka Anda Dene Wali Murgi — Sabr ka phal meetha hota hai. Ek saath sab kuch paane ka laalach, jo hai use bhi chheen leta hai.
  • Lomdi aur Khatte Angoor — Jo cheez hamein nahi milti, use bura batana aasaan hai. Sach maan lena aur phir se koshish karna behtar hai.
  • Chaalaak Lomdi aur Kauwa — Jhoothi taareef karne waalon se saavdhaan raho. Jo bahut meetha bole, uska matlab bhi samjho.
  • Bhediya Aaya! — Jhooth bolne se bharosa toot jaata hai. Ek baar toota bharosa phir aasaani se nahi judta.
  • Cheenti aur Kabootar — Bhalaai kabhi bekaar nahi jaati. Koi kitna bhi chhota ho, waqt aane pe badi madad kar sakta hai.
  • Kisaan ka Khazaana — Mehnat hi asli khazaana hai. Jo mehnat karta hai, dharti use kabhi khaali haath nahi lautaati.
  • Hawa aur Suraj — Pyaar aur narmi zor-zabardasti se zyada kaam karti hai. Gusse se nahi, achhe bartaav se logon ka mann jeeto.
  • Shehar ka Chooha, Gaanv ka Chooha — Darr ke saath mile pakwaan se chain ki sookhi roti achhi hai. Jo hamaare paas hai, uski kadar karna seekho.

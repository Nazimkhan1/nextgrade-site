🐺 Bhediya Aaya!

Ek pahaadi gaanv mein ek ladka roz gaanv ki bhedein charaane le jaata tha. Pahaadi pe din bhar akele baithe-baithe woh oob jaata tha.

Ek din use shaitaani soojhi. Woh zor se chillaya, “Bachao! Bhediya aaya! Bhediya aaya!” Gaanv waale laathiyan le ke daude-daude aaye — par wahan koi bhediya nahi tha. Ladka pet pakad ke hansne laga.

Kuch din baad usne phir yahi kiya. Log phir bhaag ke aaye, aur phir thage gaye. Is baar woh naaraz ho ke bole, “Jhooth bolna achhi baat nahi hai!”

Phir ek shaam sach mein ek bhediya jhaadiyon se nikla aur bhedon ki taraf badha. Ladka dar ke chillaya, “Bhediya aaya! Sach mein aaya! Bachao!” Par is baar gaanv waalon ne socha ki woh phir mazaak kar raha hai, aur koi nahi aaya.

Bhediya kai bhedon ko bhaga le gaya. Ladka rota hua gaanv lauta. Ek buzurg ne pyaar se samjhaya, “Beta, jo baar-baar jhooth bolta hai, uski sachchi baat pe bhi koi bharosa nahi karta.”

🌟 Seekh

Jhooth bolne se bharosa toot jaata hai. Ek baar toota bharosa phir aasaani se nahi judta.

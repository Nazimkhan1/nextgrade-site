🦴 Sher ko Zinda Karne Waale

Ek gaanv mein chaar dost rehte the. Teen ne barson padhai karke badi-badi vidyaayein seekh li thin, par unmein samajh kam thi. Chauthe ne kitaabein kam padhi thin, par woh bahut samajhdaar tha.

Chaaron dhan kamaane pardes chale. Jungle se guzarte hue unhe ek mare hue sher ki haddiyan padi milin. Pehla bola, “Aao, apni vidya aazmaayein.” Usne mantra se saari haddiyan jod ke dhaancha bana diya.

Doosre ne dhaanche pe maans, khaal aur baal chadha diye. Ab saamne ek poora sher pada tha, bas usmein jaan nahi thi. Teesra bola, “Ab main ismein praan daalunga!”

Chautha ghabra ke bola, “Ruko! Ye sher hai. Zinda hote hi hum sabko kha jaayega.” Teeno hanse, “Tum darpok ho, hamari vidya se jalte ho.” Chauthe ne kaha, “Theek hai, par pehle mujhe is ped pe chadh jaane do.” Aur woh oonchi daal pe ja baitha.

Teesre ne mantra padha — sher ne aankhein kholin, zor se dahaada aur teeno vidwaanon pe toot pada. Ped pe baitha samajhdaar dost hi bach paaya. Sher ke jaane ke baad woh dukhi mann se ghar laut aaya.

🌟 Seekh

Gyaan ke saath samajh bhi zaroori hai. Koi kaam karne se pehle socho ki uska nateeja kya hoga.

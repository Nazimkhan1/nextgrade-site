🦊 Chaalaak Lomdi aur Kauwa

Ek kauwe ko kahin se roti ka ek bada tukda mil gaya. Woh khushi-khushi ud ke ek oonche ped ki daal pe ja baitha, taaki aaraam se kha sake.

Tabhi neeche se ek lomdi guzri. Roti ki khushboo se uski bhookh jaag uthi. Usne socha, “Ye roti to mujhe chahiye — par kauwa upar hai, main wahan nahi pahunch sakti.”

Lomdi meethi awaaz mein boli, “Kauwe bhai, aap kitne sundar ho! Kitne chamkeele kaale pankh! Maine suna hai aapki awaaz to aur bhi sureeli hai. Ek gaana suna do na!”

Apni taareef sun ke kauwa phool gaya. Usne gaane ke liye jaise hi chonch kholi — “Kaanv!” — roti ka tukda seedha neeche gir pada.

Lomdi ne jhat se roti uthayi aur boli, “Thank you, kauwe bhai! Agli baar kisi ki meethi baaton pe aankh band karke bharosa mat karna.” Aur woh roti le ke chali gayi.

🌟 Seekh

Jhoothi taareef karne waalon se saavdhaan raho. Jo bahut meetha bole, uska matlab bhi samjho.

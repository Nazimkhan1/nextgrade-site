Tenali Raman · 3 minute

🐴 Tenali Raman ka Ghoda

Raja Krishnadevaraya ne apni ghudsena badhaane ke liye har darbaari ko ek ghoda diya aur saath mein uske daane-paani ke liye har mahine paise bhi. Shart thi — teen mahine baad ghode tandurust dikhne chahiye.

Tenali Raman ne ghode ko ek chhoti-si kothri mein baandha, jiski deewar mein bas ek chhoti khidki thi. Woh usi khidki se roz thodi-si ghaas daal deta. Ghode ke paison se woh apne ghar ka kharcha chalaata raha.

Teen mahine baad sab apne ghode le ke aaye. Tenali khaali haath aaye aur bole, “Maharaj, mera ghoda itna tagda aur gussail ho gaya hai ki main use la hi nahi sakta. Kisi ko bhejiye, woh khud dekh le.” Raja ne apne ghodon ke afsar ko bheja, jiski lambi, ghani daadhi thi.

Afsar ne jaise hi khidki se jhaanka, bhookhe ghode ne uski daadhi ko ghaas samajh ke daanton se pakad liya! Afsar chillaya, par ghoda chhode hi nahi. Logon ko kothri ki deewar tod ke use chhudaana pada.

Raja ne dekha ki ghoda to dubla-patla hai! Unhone Tenali se kaaran poochha. Tenali muskuraaye, “Maharaj, jo ghoda bhookh mein daadhi tak pakad le, uski taakat to aapne dekh li. Par sach ye hai ki mujhse galti hui — ghode ka hissa mujhe ghode ko hi dena chahiye tha.” Raja hanse, par unhone Tenali ko agle teen mahine ghode ko theek se khilaane ka hukm diya.

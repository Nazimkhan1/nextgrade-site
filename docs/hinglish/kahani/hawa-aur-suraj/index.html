Aesop ki kahani · 2 minute

🌬️ Hawa aur Suraj

Ek din Hawa aur Suraj mein bahas chhid gayi ki dono mein zyada taaqatwar kaun hai. Hawa ghamand se boli, "Main to ped ukhaad sakti hoon, samudra mein lehrein utha sakti hoon!"

Tabhi neeche sadak par ek musaafir garam chaadar odhe chalta dikha. Suraj ne kaha, "Chalo, dekhte hain kaun is aadmi ki chaadar utarwa paata hai. Wahi zyada taaqatwar."

Pehle Hawa ki baari aayi. Woh zor-zor se chalne lagi — sarr-sarr, dhool udi, patte ude. Par jitna tez Hawa chalti, musaafir utna hi kas kar chaadar ko apne chaaron or lapet leta. Aakhir Hawa thak kar ruk gayi.

Ab Suraj ki baari thi. Woh baadalon ke peechhe se nikla aur dheere-dheere, pyaar se chamakne laga. Musaafir ko garmi lagne lagi. Usne pehle chaadar dheeli ki, aur thodi der baad utaar kar kandhe par rakh li.

Hawa samajh gayi ki woh haar gayi hai. Suraj muskuraya, "Dekha? Jo kaam zor-zabardasti se nahi hota, woh narmi se aasaani se ho jaata hai."

🌟 Seekh

Pyaar aur narmi zor-zabardasti se zyada kaam karti hai. Gusse se nahi, achhe bartaav se logon ka mann jeeto.

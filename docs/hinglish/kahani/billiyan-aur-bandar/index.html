🐈 Do Billiyan aur Bandar

Ek gaanv mein do billiyan rehti thin, dono pakki saheliyan. Ek din ghoomte-ghoomte unhe raaste mein ek badi-si roti padi mili. Dono ne ek saath us par jhapatta maara.

Pehli boli, "Maine pehle dekhi, bada hissa mera!" Doosri boli, "Nahi, barabar baantenge!" Dono mein jhagda badh gaya. Tabhi ped se ek chaalaak bandar utra aur bola, "Laao, main insaaf se barabar baant deta hoon."

Bandar ek taraazu le aaya. Usne roti ke do tukde karke dono paldon mein rakhe. Ek palda bhaari nikla, to usne us tukde se thoda-sa kaat kar khud kha liya.

Ab doosra palda bhaari ho gaya. Bandar ne usme se bhi thoda kha liya. Yahi chalta raha — kabhi idhar se, kabhi udhar se — aur roti chhoti hoti gayi. Billiyan boli, "Bas karo, hum khud baant lengi!"

Bandar bola, "Bacha hua to meri mehnat ki mazdoori hai!" aur aakhri dono tukde munh mein daal kar ped par chadh gaya. Dono billiyan khaali haath ek-doosre ka munh dekhti reh gayin.

🌟 Seekh

Aapas mein ladoge to faayda koi teesra utha lega. Mil-julkar, thoda jhukkar baat suljhao.

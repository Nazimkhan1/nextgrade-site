🦁 Chatur Khargosh aur Sher

Ek jungle mein Bhasuraka naam ka ek ghamandi sher rehta tha. Woh roz bina wajah kai jaanwaron ka shikaar kar leta tha. Dare hue jaanwaron ne mil ke usse kaha, “Maharaj, aap gufa mein aaraam karein. Hum roz baari-baari se ek jaanwar aapke paas bhej denge.” Sher maan gaya.

Ek din baari aayi ek chhote-se khargosh ki. Woh dara to tha, par samajhdaar bhi tha. Usne jaan-boojh ke raaste mein der lagayi aur sochta raha ki sher se kaise bacha jaaye.

Jab woh shaam ko pahuncha, sher gusse se dahaada, “Itni der kyun hui?” Khargosh ne kaanpte hue kaha, “Maharaj, raaste mein ek doosra sher mila. Woh kehta hai ki is jungle ka asli raja wahi hai, aap nahi!”

Sher aag-baboola ho gaya, “Kahan hai woh? Mujhe abhi dikhao!” Khargosh use ek gehre kuein ke paas le gaya aur bola, “Woh isi kile ke andar chhupa hai.” Sher ne kuein mein jhaanka to use paani mein apni hi parchhaai dikhi.

Sher zor se dahaada — kuein se uski dahaad ki goonj lauti. Use laga doosra sher lalkaar raha hai. Woh gusse mein kuein mein kood pada aur phir kabhi baahar nahi nikal paaya. Saare jaanwar khargosh ki buddhi ki taareef karte hue khushi se naachne lage.

🦁 Panchatantra ki kahaniyan

Bachchon ke liye Panchatantra ki chhoti kahaniyan, har kahani ke saath seekh — sunaane ke liye "Sunao" button bhi.

Panchatantra Bharat ka bahut purana katha-sangrah hai, jise lagbhag do hazaar saal purana maana jaata hai aur jiska shrey Pandit Vishnu Sharma ko diya jaata hai. Kaha jaata hai ki unhone rajkumaron ko neeti aur samajhdaari sikhaane ke liye jaanwaron ki yeh kahaniyan sunaayin. Iske paanch bhaag (tantra) hain, isliye iska naam Panchatantra pada. Iski kahaniyan duniya ki kai bhashaon mein pahunch chuki hain.

🐒 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Bandar aur Magarmachh

Nadi kinaare ek jamun ka ped tha, jis pe ek bandar rehta tha.

 🕊️ Panchatantra · 3 minute

Kabootar aur Shikaari ka Jaal

Ek jungle mein kabootaron ka ek bada jhund rehta tha.

 🦁 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Chatur Khargosh aur Sher

Ek jungle mein Bhasuraka naam ka ek ghamandi sher rehta tha.

 🦴 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Sher ko Zinda Karne Waale

Ek gaanv mein chaar dost rehte the.

 🐘 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Nanhi Chidiya aur Ghamandi Haathi

Ek ped pe ek chidiya ka ghonsla tha, jismein uske ande the.

 🐢 Panchatantra · 2 minute

Baatooni Kachhua

Ek taalaab mein ek kachhua rehta tha.

 🐺 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Neela Siyaar

Ek jungle mein Chandarav naam ka ek siyaar rehta tha.

 🦀 Panchatantra · 3 minute

Bagula aur Kekda

Ek taalaab ke kinaare ek boodha bagula rehta tha.

🌟 Seekh

  • Bandar aur Magarmachh — Museebat mein ghabraane ke bajaye buddhi se kaam lo. Aur dosti mein kabhi dhokha mat do.
  • Kabootar aur Shikaari ka Jaal — Ekta mein badi taakat hai. Aur achha mukhiya pehle apne saathiyon ki fikr karta hai.
  • Chatur Khargosh aur Sher — Buddhi taakat se badi hoti hai. Ghamand aur gussa sochne ki shakti chheen lete hain.
  • Sher ko Zinda Karne Waale — Gyaan ke saath samajh bhi zaroori hai. Koi kaam karne se pehle socho ki uska nateeja kya hoga.
  • Nanhi Chidiya aur Ghamandi Haathi — Ekta mein badi taakat hai. Chhote-chhote dost mil jaayein to bade se bade ghamandi ko bhi hara sakte hain.
  • Baatooni Kachhua — Har baat pe bolna zaroori nahi. Sahi samay pe chup rehna bhi badi samajhdaari hai.
  • Neela Siyaar — Jo hum nahi hain, uska dikhawa zyada din nahi chalta. Apne asli roop aur apne logon se kabhi sharm mat karo.
  • Bagula aur Kekda — Meethi baatein sunkar aankh moond kar bharosa mat karo. Darr ke samay bhi soch-samajh kar faisla karo, aur himmat se sach ka saamna karo.

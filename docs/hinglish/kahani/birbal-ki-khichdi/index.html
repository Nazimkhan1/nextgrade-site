Akbar-Birbal · 3 minute

🍲 Birbal ki Khichdi

Kadaake ki thand ka mausam tha. Baadshah Akbar ne ghoshna ki, “Jo aadmi poori raat mahal ke thande taalaab mein khada rahega, use inaam milega.” Ek gareeb dhobi ne himmat ki aur poori raat kaanpte hue paani mein khada raha.

Subah woh inaam lene aaya. Akbar ne poochha, “Itni thand mein tum raat bhar kaise tike?” Dhobi bola, “Huzoor, door mahal ki chhat pe ek diya jal raha tha. Main usi ko dekhta raha.” Ek darbaari turant bola, “To isne diye ki garmi li hai, inaam nahi banta!” Akbar ne bhi inaam dene se mana kar diya.

Birbal ko ye baat buri lagi. Agle din woh darbaar nahi aaye. Akbar ne bulwaaya to jawaab aaya, “Birbal khichdi paka rahe hain, pakte hi aayenge.” Ghante beet gaye, Birbal nahi aaye.

Aakhir Akbar khud Birbal ke ghar pahunche. Dekha — zameen pe thodi-si aag jal rahi hai, aur khichdi ki haandi bahut oonche baans pe tangi hai! Akbar hanse, “Birbal, itni door aag se khichdi kaise pakegi?”

Birbal ne namrata se kaha, “Jahanpanah, jab door chhat ke diye se dhobi ko garmi mil sakti hai, to is aag se meri khichdi bhi pak jaayegi.” Akbar ko apni galti samajh aa gayi. Unhone usi din dhobi ko bula ke poora inaam diya.

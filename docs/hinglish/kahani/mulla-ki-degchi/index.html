Mulla Nasruddin · 2 minute

🍲 Mulla Nasruddin ki Degchi

Ek din Mulla Nasruddin ne apne padosi se khaana pakaane ke liye ek badi degchi udhaar maangi. Agle din woh degchi lautaane gaye, to uske andar ek chhoti-si katori bhi rakhi thi.

Padosi ne hairaan hokar poochha, "Mulla, yeh katori kiski hai?" Mulla bole, "Arre bhai, khushkhabri hai! Raat ko tumhari degchi ne bachcha diya hai. Ab maa-bachcha dono tumhare."

Padosi mann hi mann hansa — "Mulla bewakoof hai, par mera kya jaata hai, muft ki katori mil gayi!" Usne dono bartan khushi-khushi rakh liye.

Kuch din baad Mulla phir wahi badi degchi maangne aaye. Padosi ne jhat se de di. Par is baar kai din beet gaye, degchi wapas nahi aayi. Aakhir padosi khud poochhne pahuncha.

Mulla ne udaas chehra banaya, "Bhai, bada dukh hai — tumhari degchi mar gayi." Padosi chillaaya, "Degchi bhi kahin marti hai?" Mulla bole, "Jab degchi bachcha de sakti hai, to mar bhi sakti hai!"

🌟 Seekh

Laalach mein jo galat baat maan lo, wahi baad mein bhaari padti hai. Faayda dikhe tab bhi sach aur samajhdaari ka saath mat chhodo.

  3. कि देगची मर गई।

🌾 Kisaan ka Khazaana

Ek gaanv mein ek mehnati kisaan rehta tha. Uske teen bete the, jo bahut aalsi the. Woh din bhar sote ya gapshap karte, khet ki taraf jhaankte bhi nahi the.

Kisaan boodha aur beemaar ho gaya. Usne teeno beton ko paas bula ke kaha, “Beta, hamare khet mein ek khazaana gada hai. Mere jaane ke baad use khod ke nikaal lena.” Kuch din baad kisaan chal basa.

Khazaane ke laalach mein teeno bhaiyon ne phaawde uthaaye aur poora khet khod daala — kona-kona, gehraai tak. Par unhe koi khazaana nahi mila. Woh bahut niraash hue.

Tabhi baarish ka mausam aa gaya. Maa ne kaha, “Itna khuda hua khet bekaar kyun chhodte ho? Ismein beej bo do.” Beton ne beej bo diye. Khudi hui naram mitti mein fasal itni achhi ugi ki poore gaanv mein kisi ke khet mein aisi fasal nahi hui.

Fasal bech ke beton ko dher saare paise mile. Tab bade bete ko samajh aaya, “Pitaji ne jis khazaane ki baat kahi thi, woh hamari mehnat hi thi!” Us din se teeno bhai mann laga ke kheti karne lage.

🌟 Seekh

Mehnat hi asli khazaana hai. Jo mehnat karta hai, dharti use kabhi khaali haath nahi lautaati.

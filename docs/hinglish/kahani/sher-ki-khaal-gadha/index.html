Jataka katha · 2 minute

🦁 Sher ki Khaal mein Gadha

Ek dhobi ka gadha din bhar kapdon ka bojh dhota aur raat ko bhookha-pyaasa reh jaata. Ek din jungle ke kinaare use ek purani sher ki khaal padi mili.

Gadhe ke mann mein ek vichaar aaya. Usne woh khaal odh li aur kheton ki or chal pada. Use dekhte hi kisaan aur jaanwar dar kar bhaag khade hue — "Bhaago, sher aaya!"

Ab gadha roz sher ki khaal odh kar hare-bhare kheton mein jaata aur maze se fasal charta. Koi uske paas aane ki himmat nahi karta tha. Gadha apni chaalaaki par bahut khush tha.

Ek shaam door se kisi gadhi ke renkne ki awaaz aayi — "Dhenchu-dhenchu!" Gadha apne aap ko rok nahi paaya aur zor se renk utha, "Dhenchu-dhenchu!"

Kisaanon ne awaaz pehchaan li. "Arre, yeh sher nahi, gadha hai!" Sab laathiyan lekar daude, aur gadha khaal chhod kar jaan bachaata hua bhaag gaya.

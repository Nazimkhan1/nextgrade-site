Tenali Raman · 3 minute

🪣 Tenali Raman aur Kuen ka Khazaana

Ek garmi mein Tenali Raman ke baagh ke paudhe sookhne lage. Kuaan gehra tha aur paani kheenchne wala koi nahi tha. Unhi dino shehar mein choriyan bhi badh gayi thin.

Ek raat Tenali ne dekha ki kuch chor unke ghar ke paas jhaadiyon mein chhupe hain. Tenali muskuraaye aur oonchi awaaz mein apni patni se bole, "Suno, choron ka darr hai. Apne saare gehne ek bakse mein bhar kar kuen mein daal dete hain — wahan koi nahi dhoondhega!"

Phir unhone ek bakse mein patthar bhare aur "chhapaak!" se kuen mein gira diya. Choron ne sab sun liya. Aadhi raat ko woh aaye aur gehne nikaalne ke liye baalti-baalti paani kheenchne lage.

Tenali ne pehle se hi kuen se baagh tak naali bana rakhi thi. Chor poori raat paani kheenchte rahe, aur saara paani beh kar baagh ke paudhon tak pahunchta raha. Bhor hote-hote unhe baksa mila — usme sirf patthar the!

Tabhi Tenali khidki se bole, "Dhanyavaad bhaiyon! Itni mehnat se mera baagh seenchne ke liye. Ab jaldi bhaago, sipaahi aate honge!" Sharminda chor bhaag gaye, aur Tenali ka baagh phir se hara-bhara ho gaya.

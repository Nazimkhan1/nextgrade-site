Lok-katha · 3 minute

🧢 Topiwala aur Bandar

Ek topiwala gaanv-gaanv ghoom ke rang-birangi topiyan bechta tha. Ek garam dopahar woh thak ke ek bade ped ke neeche ruka, topiyon ki tokri paas rakhi, apni topi sir pe rakhi aur so gaya.

Us ped pe bahut se bandar rehte the. Unhone tokri dekhi, neeche utre, ek-ek topi uthayi, pehni aur phir ped pe chadh gaye.

Topiwale ki neend khuli to tokri khaali thi! Usne upar dekha — har bandar ke sir pe uski ek topi thi. Usne haath hilaya, “Meri topiyan lautao!” Bandaron ne bhi haath hila diya. Usne munh banaya, bandaron ne bhi munh bana diya.

Tab topiwale ko samajh aaya — bandar to wahi karte hain jo woh karta hai! Usne ek tarkeeb sochi. Usne apni topi sir se utaari aur zameen pe phenk di.

Bas, phir kya tha! Saare bandaron ne bhi apni-apni topi utaar ke neeche phenk di. Topiwale ne jhatpat saari topiyan tokri mein bharin, bandaron ko muskura ke “thank you” kaha aur apni raah chal pada.

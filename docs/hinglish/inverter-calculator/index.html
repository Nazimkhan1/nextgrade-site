🔋 Inverter calculator — kitne VA ka inverter chahiye?

Bijli jaane par jo cheezein chalani hain, unki ginti daaliye. Hum batayenge kitna load hai, kitne VA ka inverter aur kitni battery lagegi.

Kaise gina jaata hai

  • Inverter VA = kul watt ÷ 0.8 (power factor), phir usse bada milne wala size.
  • Battery (Ah) = watt × ghante ÷ (voltage × 0.8). 12V 150Ah battery mein lagbhag 1,800 Wh hote hain.
  • 1100 VA tak aam taur par 12V (1 battery), 2500 VA tak 24V (2 battery), usse bada 48V.
  • Fridge aur motor shuru hote samay 2–3 guna current kheenchte hain — isliye thoda bada inverter lein. AC aur geyser aam inverter par na chalayein.

⚠️ Yeh sirf andaaza hai — asli backup battery ki haalat, brand aur taapmaan par nirbhar karta hai. Khareedne se pehle electrician se poochhein.

👉 Solar calculator — kitne kW ka solar chahiye?

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

800 VA inverter par kya-kya chalega?

Lagbhag 640 watt tak: 4 pankhe, 6 bulb, ek TV aur router aaraam se.

150Ah battery kitne ghante chalegi?

300 watt load par lagbhag 4–5 ghante; 500 watt par lagbhag 3 ghante.

Kya solar panel inverter se jod sakte hain?

Haan, solar (hybrid/PCU) inverter se. Subsidy wale on-grid system mein battery nahi hoti.

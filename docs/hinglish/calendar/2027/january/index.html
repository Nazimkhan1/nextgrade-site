Is mahine ke baare mein

January saal ka sabse thanda mahina hai — uttar Bharat mein kohra aur sheetlehar, pahadon pe barf. Kheton mein gehun, sarson aur chane ki fasal badh rahi hoti hai. Makar Sankranti, Lohri, Pongal aur Bihu ke saath fasal ka tyohaar manaya jaata hai, aur 26 January ko poora desh Gantantra Diwas manata hai. Schoolon mein board exam ki taiyaari zoron par rehti hai.

Tithi wale tyohar alag shehron mein ek din aage-peeche ho sakte hain — apna local panchang bhi dekh lo.

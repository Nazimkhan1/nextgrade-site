Is mahine ke tyohar aur chhuttiyan

  • 🔱 6 March 2027 (Shanivaar) — Maha Shivratri
  • 🌙 10 March 2027 (Budhvaar) — Eid ul-Fitr (anumaanit) · Chhutti
  • 🎨 22 March 2027 (Somvaar) — Holi · Chhutti

Hijri (Islami) mahina

  • 🌙 9 March 2027 (Mangalvaar) — Shawwal shuru (lagbhag)

Hijri tareekhein chaand dikhne par nirbhar hain; Bharat mein mahina ek din baad bhi shuru ho sakta hai.

Is mahine ke khaas din

  • 8 March 2027 — International Women’s Day
  • 15 March 2027 — World Consumer Rights Day
  • 22 March 2027 — World Water Day
  • 23 March 2027 — Shaheed Diwas (Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev)
  • 31 March 2027 — Financial year ka aakhri din

Is mahine ke baare mein

March mein garmi ki shuruaat hoti hai aur din bade hone lagte hain; 20–21 March ke aaspaas din aur raat lagbhag barabar hote hain. Uttar Bharat mein gehun ki fasal pakne lagti hai. Holi rangon ke saath basant ka swagat karti hai. 31 March financial year ka aakhri din hai — tax bachane wale nivesh, bill aur hisaab isse pehle poore karne hote hain.

Tithi wale tyohar alag shehron mein ek din aage-peeche ho sakte hain — apna local panchang bhi dekh lo.

Is mahine ke tyohar aur chhuttiyan

  • 🕊️ 2 October 2027 (Shanivaar) — Gandhi Jayanti · Chhutti
  • 🏹 9 October 2027 (Shanivaar) — Dussehra (Vijayadashami) · Chhutti
  • 🪔 29 October 2027 (Shukravaar) — Diwali · Chhutti

Hijri (Islami) mahina

  • 🌙 1 October 2027 (Shukravaar) — Jamadi-ul-Awwal shuru (lagbhag)
  • 🌙 31 October 2027 (Ravivaar) — Jamadi-ul-Akhir (Jamadi-us-Sani) shuru (lagbhag)

Hijri tareekhein chaand dikhne par nirbhar hain; Bharat mein mahina ek din baad bhi shuru ho sakta hai.

Is mahine ke khaas din

  • 1 October 2027 — International Day of Older Persons
  • 2 October 2027 — Gandhi Jayanti aur Lal Bahadur Shastri Jayanti
  • 16 October 2027 — World Food Day
  • 31 October 2027 — Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (Sardar Patel Jayanti)

Is mahine ke baare mein

October mein mausam suhana hota hai — baarish khatam, halki thandak shuru. Dhaan ki katai aur gehun-sarson ki buvaai ki taiyaari hoti hai. Navratri, Durga Puja aur Dussehra ke saath tyohaaron ka bada mausam shuru hota hai, aur kai baar Diwali bhi isi mahine padti hai. Ghoomne ke liye yeh sabse achhe mahinon mein se ek hai.

Tithi wale tyohar alag shehron mein ek din aage-peeche ho sakte hain — apna local panchang bhi dekh lo.

🧘 Pranayam — screen ke saath saans lo

Pranayam yaani saans ko sahi dhang se lena-chhodna. Yahan har pranayam ke saath ek guide hai jo batata hai kab saans leni hai, kab chhodni hai — bas uske saath chaliye.

Kaun sa pranayam karein?

👃

Anulom-Vilom

Ek naak se saans lena, doosri se chhodna — bas yahi anulom-vilom hai. Neeche ka gola aur "baayein/daayein naak" ka ishaara dekhte hue saath-saath karo.

💨

Kapalbhati

Kapalbhati mein saans chhodna zor se hota hai aur lena apne aap. Har jhatke par gola sikudega aur "tak" ki awaaz aayegi — usi taal mein karo.

🐝

Bhramari

Bhramari mein saans chhodte hue bhanvre jaisi "hmmm" ki goonj karte hain. Saans chhodte samay yahan bhi halki goonj bajegi — uske saath apni awaaz milao.

🌬️

Deergh Shwas (gehri saans)

Sabse aasaan abhyaas: 4 ginti mein saans lo, 2 roko, 6 mein dheere-dheere chhodo. Padhai se pehle, office ke beech ya sone se pehle — kahin bhi.

☀️ Subah ki 10 minute ki routine

  1. ☀️Surya Namaskar — 3 round~4 minute
  2. 👃Anulom-Vilom3 minute
  3. 🐝Bhramari2 minute
  4. 🌬️Gehri saans — shaant hokar uthein1 minute

Roz ek hi samay par karo — har page par aapka 🔥 streak gina jaata hai.

Yeh jaankari aam abhyaas ke liye hai, ilaaj nahi. Koi bimaari ho, dawa chal rahi ho ya pregnancy ho to pehle doctor ya trained yoga teacher se salaah lein.

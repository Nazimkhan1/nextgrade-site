🐝 Bhramari — goonj ke saath karo

Bhramari mein saans chhodte hue bhanvre jaisi "hmmm" ki goonj karte hain. Saans chhodte samay yahan bhi halki goonj bajegi — uske saath apni awaaz milao.

Kaise karein

  1. Seedhe baithein, aankhein band.
  2. Angoothon se kaan ke aage ka chhota hissa halka dabayein (kaan mein ungli na daalein).
  3. Tarjani maathe par, baaki ungliyan aankhon ke paas halki rakhein.
  4. Naak se saans lein.
  5. Munh band rakh kar "hmmm" ki dheemi goonj ke saath dheere-dheere saans chhodein.
  6. 5–7 baar karein, phir kuch der shaant baithein.
Kitni der: Shuruaat mein 5–7 baar (2–3 minute).

Kya mehsoos hoga

  • Sir aur chehre mein halki jhanjhanaahat/kampan
  • Mann shaant lagta hai

Aam galtiyan

  • Kaan mein ungli daalna
  • Gale par zor daal kar tez awaaz nikaalna
  • Aankhon par dabaav daalna
⚠️ Saavdhaani
  • Kaan mein dard ya infection ho to na karein
  • Let kar na karein

☀️ Subah ki 10 minute ki routine

  1. ☀️Surya Namaskar — 3 round~4 minute
  2. 👃Anulom-Vilom3 minute
  3. 🐝Bhramari2 minute
  4. 🌬️Gehri saans — shaant hokar uthein1 minute

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Bhramari kitni baar karein?

Shuruaat mein 5–7 baar; aaraam se ho to 10 baar tak.

Bhramari kab karein?

Subah ya shaam, shaant jagah par. Sone se pehle bhi kar sakte hain.

Goonj kitni tez honi chahiye?

Dheemi aur ek-si — bas itni ki aapko sir mein kampan mehsoos ho.

Doosre pranayam

👃 Anulom-Vilom 💨 Kapalbhati 🌬️ Deergh Shwas (gehri saans) ☀️ Surya Namaskar

⚖️ 1 Kos kitne Kilometer?

lagbhag 1 Kos = 3.2 Kilometer

Kos doori ki purani unit hai — alag-alag ilaakon mein 2 se 3.2 km tak maana gaya. Yahan aam hisaab 1 kos ≈ 3.2 km liya hai.

☀️ 10 kW solar system: kharcha, subsidy aur bachat

10 kW solar: subsidy ₹78,000, subsidy ke baad lagbhag ₹4,72,000 – ₹6,22,000

CheezKitna
Bijli (mahina)~1,200 unit (1,050–1,500)
Chhat par jagah~1,000 sq ft
Kul kharcha₹5,50,000 – ₹7,00,000
Subsidy (saamanya rajya)₹78,000
Subsidy (vishesh rajya)₹85,800
Subsidy ke baad₹4,72,000 – ₹6,22,000
Bachat (₹7/unit, mahina)~₹8,400
Kiske liye sahiBada ghar ya dukaan, ~1,000+ unit

🧮 Solar calculator — kitne kW ka solar chahiye?

Kaise gina jaata hai

  • Subsidy (PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, ghar ki chhat): pehle 2 kW par ₹30,000 per kW, teesre kW par ₹18,000 — yaani 1 kW = ₹30,000, 2 kW = ₹60,000, 3 kW ya zyada = ₹78,000 (isse zyada nahi).
  • Vishesh rajya (North-East, Uttarakhand, Himachal, Jammu-Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman-Nicobar, Lakshadweep): ₹33,000 per kW (2 kW tak) + teesre kW par ₹19,800 = maximum ₹85,800.
  • Bharat mein 1 kW solar ausatan roz lagbhag 4 unit, yaani mahine mein ~120 unit banata hai (dhoop aur mausam ke hisaab se 3.5–5 unit roz).
  • 1 kW ke liye lagbhag 100 sq ft chhaanv-rahit chhat chahiye.
  • Lagwane ka kharcha (on-grid, net meter ke saath) aam taur par ₹55,000–70,000 per kW (November 2026 ka andaaza) — company aur panel ke hisaab se badalta hai.
  • Portal ke hisaab se: mahine mein 0–150 unit → 1–2 kW, 150–300 unit → 2–3 kW, 300 se zyada → 3 kW se upar.

Subsidy ke liye apply kaise karein

  1. pmsuryaghar.gov.in par apna rajya, bijli company aur consumer number daal kar register karein.
  2. Portal par registered vendor chunein aur chhat par solar lagwayein.
  3. Bijli company net meter lagati hai aur jaanch karti hai.
  4. Commissioning ke baad bank account ki details daalein — subsidy seedhe khaate mein aati hai.

⚠️ Yeh sirf andaaza hai. Subsidy ke niyam aur raashi badal sakti hai — apply karne se pehle pmsuryaghar.gov.in zaroor dekhein. Kuch rajya upar se alag subsidy bhi dete hain. Sirf sarkari portal par apply karein, kisi ko OTP ya paise "subsidy dilane" ke naam par na dein.

Aakhri baar jaancha: 26 Nov 2026 · Source: pmsuryaghar.gov.in (MNRE) · NextGrade sarkari website nahi hai.

🐚 Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Cross ke aakaar ki bisaat (aksar kadhaai wala kapda): chaar baahein, har baah mein 3 kataar × 8 khaane, beech mein char-koni
  • Har khiladi ki 4 goti, alag-alag rang ki (aksar laal, peeli, hari, kaali)
  • 6 kaudiyan (kahin 7) — ya Chaupar ke liye 3 lambe paase
  • 2 ya 4 khiladi; 4 hon to aamne-saamne wale saathi (jodi)

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Har khiladi bisaat ki ek baah ke saamne baithta hai. Baari ulti ghadi ki disha mein chalti hai.
  2. Baari aane par 6 kaudiyan phenko aur gino kitni ka munh upar (khula) hai: 2, 3, 4, 5 ya 6 khuli hon to utne khaane; 1 khuli ho to 10; ek bhi na khule to 25.
  3. 10 aur 25 ko "daan" kehte hain. Daan aane par ek nayi goti bisaat par utar sakti hai, aur khiladi ko ek aur phenk milti hai.
  4. Goti pehle apni baah ki beech wali kataar mein baahar ki or chalti hai, phir ulti ghadi ki disha mein poori bisaat ke baahri khaanon ka chakkar lagaati hai.
  5. Chakkar poora karke goti apni baah ki beech wali kataar se upar chadhti hai aur char-koni mein pahunchti hai. Ghar mein jaane ke liye aam taur par theek utni chaal chahiye.
  6. Agar aapki goti virodhi ki goti wale khaane par theek jaa ke ruke, to virodhi ki goti mar jaati hai aur shuru se chalni padti hai. Kai jagah maarne par ek aur phenk milti hai.
  7. Cross ke nishaan wale khaane "kile" kehlaate hain — wahan khadi goti ko koi nahi maar sakta.
  8. Jodi mein khelte samay saathi ki goti nahi maari jaati, aur jiski goti pehle ghar pahunch jaayein woh saathi ki madad kar sakta hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Jo khiladi sabse pehle apni saari goti char-koni mein pahuncha de, woh jeetta hai.

Jodi wale khel mein dono saathiyon ki saari goti ghar pahunchne par jodi jeetti hai.

Doosre naam

Ise Chaupar, Chausar, Pachisi aur Pachchisi kehte hain; Bengal mein Pasha ya Pochishi. "Pachisi" naam 25 (pachchees) se bana hai, jo kaudiyon ka sabse bada daan hai. Ludo isi purane khel ka aasaan roop hai.

Achhe khel ke liye

  • Khel shuru karne se pehle local rules tay kar lo: daan kaunse hain, ghar ke liye theek chaal chahiye ya nahi, maarne par ek aur phenk milegi ya nahi.
  • Kaudiyan kapde ya dari par phenko taaki tootein nahi.
  • Chhote bachchon ko kaudiyan munh mein na daalne do.
  • Yeh khel sirf mazze ke liye khelo — daav ya paisa nahi.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pachisi mein kaudiyon se chaal kaise ginte hain?

6 kaudiyon mein jitni ka munh upar ho, utni chaal (2 se 6). 1 khuli ho to 10, koi na khule to 25. 10 aur 25 daan hain.

Chaupar aur Pachisi mein kya farq hai?

Bisaat ek jaisi hai. Pachisi kaudiyon se aur Chaupar aksar 3 lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Kai jagah dono naam ek hi khel ke liye bole jaate hain.

Kile wale khaane kya hote hain?

Bisaat par cross ke nishaan wale 12 khaane, jahan khadi goti ko maara nahi jaa sakta.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

⚪

Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔴

Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🌰

Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

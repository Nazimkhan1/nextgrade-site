⚪ Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Zameen par chalk ya koyle se bana board, ya kaagaz par bana board: ek ke andar ek teen chaukor, jinki bhujaaon ke beech wale nishaan seedhi lakeeron se jude hon (kul 24 nishaan)
  • Ek khiladi ke liye 9 ek jaisi gotiyan — jaise kankad
  • Doosre khiladi ke liye 9 alag dikhne wali gotiyan — jaise imli ke beej ya kaudi
  • 2 khiladi

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Toss karke tay karein ki kaun pehle chalega. Board shuru mein khaali rehta hai.
  2. Pehla hissa — rakhna: baari-baari ek-ek goti kisi bhi khaali nishaan par rakhein, jab tak dono ki 9-9 gotiyan na rakh di jaayein.
  3. Jab bhi kisi ki teen gotiyan ek seedhi lakeer par (ek hi bhuja par, ya beech ki jodne wali lakeer par) aa jaayein, use "mill" kehte hain. Mill banane wala saamne wale ki ek goti board se utha leta hai.
  4. Jo goti pehle se kisi mill mein ho, use nahi utha sakte — jab tak saamne wale ki saari gotiyan mill mein na hon.
  5. Doosra hissa — chalna: saari gotiyan rakhne ke baad har baari apni ek goti ko lakeer ke saath bagal wale khaali nishaan par sarkayein. Kisi goti ke upar se kood nahi sakte.
  6. Apni mill khol kar (ek goti bahar nikaal kar) agli baari use wapas laayein, to yeh nayi mill gini jaati hai aur phir se ek goti uthti hai.
  7. Teesra hissa — udna: bahut jagah yeh niyam hai ki jiske paas sirf 3 gotiyan bachein, woh apni goti kisi bhi khaali nishaan par rakh sakta hai. Kai gaon mein yeh niyam nahi chalta — khel shuru hone se pehle tay kar lein.

Jeet, haar aur barabari

Jiske paas sirf 2 gotiyan bachein, woh haar jaata hai — ab woh mill nahi bana sakta.

Jiski baari ho aur uski koi bhi goti chal na sake (sab ghiri hui hon), woh bhi haar jaata hai.

Agar bahut der tak koi goti na kate, ya ek hi sthiti baar-baar aaye, to dono milkar khel barabar maan sakte hain.

Doosre naam aur itihaas

Ise Nau Gutti, Nau Goti, Navkankari aur kahin Char Bhar bhi kehte hain; Bangla mein "Noy Guti" aur angrezi mein "Nine Men's Morris". Aise board kai purane mandiron, kilon aur doosre deshon ki imaraton ke patthar par khude milte hain. Yeh khel kitna purana hai aur sabse pehle kahan shuru hua, is par jaankaaron ki raay alag-alag hai. Kuch jagah konon par tirchhi lakeerein aur 12-12 gotiyon wala roop bhi khela jaata hai.

Achha khelne ke aasaan tips

  • Beech wale chaukor ke chaar jod (jahan chaar raaste milte hain) shuru mein sabse kaam ke nishaan hain.
  • Saamne wale ki do gotiyan ek line mein hon to teesra nishaan pehle rokein.
  • Rakhte samay gotiyon ko aise phailayein ki baad mein chalne ki jagah rahe.
  • Aisi "chakki" banane ki koshish karein jismein ek goti aage-peeche karne se har baari mill bane.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Nau Gutti mein kitni gotiyan hoti hain?

Har khiladi ki 9, yaani kul 18 gotiyan, aur board par 24 nishaan.

Mill banne par kaunsi goti utha sakte hain?

Saamne wale ki koi bhi aisi goti jo kisi mill mein na ho. Agar uski saari gotiyan mill mein hon, to koi bhi.

Kya 3 goti bachne par udna zaroori hai?

Nahi, yeh jagah-jagah ka niyam hai. Khel shuru karne se pehle dono khiladi tay kar lein.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🐚

Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

🔴

Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🌰

Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

🌰 Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Pallanguzhi board — do line mein 7-7 gadde (kul 14)
  • Board na ho to zameen par 14 chhote gadde ya 14 katoriyan
  • 70 beej — imli ke beej, kaudiyan, manjadi beej ya chhote kankad
  • 2 khiladi, aamne-saamne

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Board dono ke beech rakhein. Apni taraf ki 7 gaddon wali line aapki hai. Har gadde mein 5 beej rakhein (kai gharon mein 6 ya zyada rakhte hain).
  2. Tay karein ki kaun pehle chalega — pehli chaal ka faayda hota hai, isliye har baazi mein baari badlein.
  3. Apni baari mein apni taraf ka koi bhara gadda chunein aur uske saare beej utha lein.
  4. Ulti ghadi ki disha mein agle gaddon mein ek-ek beej daalein — saamne wale ke gaddon mein bhi.
  5. Aakhri beej girne ke baad agla gadda dekhein. Usme beej hon to unhe utha kar usi tarah aage bote rahein.
  6. Agar agla gadda khaali ho, to uske baad wale gadde ke saare beej aapke — unhe apne paas rakh lein. Aapki baari khatam.
  7. Agar lagataar do gadde khaali hon, to kuch nahi milta aur baari saamne wale ki.
  8. Pasu (kai gharon ka niyam): bote waqt jis gadde mein theek 4 beej ho jaayein, woh 4 beej us gadde ke maalik ke ho jaate hain.

Round aur jeet

Jiski baari ho aur uski taraf ke saare gadde khaali hon, round khatam. Saamne wala apni taraf ke bache beej le leta hai. Jiske paas zyada beej, woh round jeetta hai.

Poori baazi mein agle round se pehle har khiladi apne beejon se apne gadde phir 5-5 bharta hai. Jo gadda na bhar sake, woh us round band rehta hai. Jo ek bhi gadda na bhar sake, woh haar jaata hai.

Aakhir mein beej bas ghoomte rahein aur koi kuch na jeete, to aapas mein tay karke round khatam kar dein — har koi apni taraf ke beej le le.

Doosre naam

Tamil Nadu mein Pallanguzhi, Karnataka mein Ali Guli Mane, Andhra Pradesh aur Telangana mein Vamana Guntalu. Kerala aur Sri Lanka mein bhi iske milte-julte roop hain. Duniya mein aise beej bone wale khelon ko "mancala" parivaar kaha jaata hai.

Dhyaan rakhein

  • Chhote bachchon ke saath khelein to beej munh mein na daalne dein — bade beej ya kaudiyan lein.
  • Chaal se pehle gin lein ki aakhri beej kahan girega.
  • Khel shuru karne se pehle niyam (beej kitne, pasu hai ya nahi) aapas mein tay kar lein.
  • Khel ke baad beej gin kar dibbe mein rakhein, taaki agli baar poore milein.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pallanguzhi mein ek gadde mein kitne beej rakhte hain?

Aam taur par 5 ya 6, par kai gharon mein 12 tak, aur kahin-kahin beech ke gadde mein alag ginti rakhte hain. Khelne se pehle tay kar lein.

Pallanguzhi mein beej kaise jeette hain?

Aakhri beej ke baad wala gadda khaali ho, to uske baad wale gadde ke saare beej aapke. Pasu wale niyam mein 4 beej wale gadde ke beej bhi uske maalik ko milte hain.

Ali Guli Mane aur Pallanguzhi mein kya farak hai?

Mote taur par dono ek hi khel hain — Ali Guli Mane Kannada naam hai aur Pallanguzhi Tamil naam. Chhote niyam jagah-jagah badalte hain.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

⚪

Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🐚

Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

🔴

Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵 Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Har khiladi ke paas kuch kanche
  • Ek bada kancha (kai jagah ise "lakhota" ya "dumper" kehte hain) — nishana lagaane ke liye
  • Mitti wali samtal zameen
  • Gucchi wale khel ke liye ek chhota gaddha, gole wale khel ke liye zameen par bana gola
  • 2 ya zyada khiladi

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Kancha pakadna seekhein: ek haath ki ungli zameen par tikaayein, usi haath ke angoothe ya doosri ungli se kanche ko peechhe kheench kar chhitkein.
  2. Baari tay karne ke liye sab ek lakeer se apna kancha gucchi (ya ek nishaan) ki taraf phenkte hain. Jiska kancha sabse paas ruke, woh pehle khelta hai.
  3. Gucchi wala khel: pehle apna kancha gucchi mein daalein. Gucchi mein daalne ke baad aap doosron ke kanchon par nishana laga sakte hain.
  4. Agar aapka kancha kisi doosre ke kanche se takraya, to woh kancha aapke naam ka point ho jaata hai (khel shuru hone se pehle tay kar lein ki aakhir mein kanche lautaaye jaayenge).
  5. Nishana chook jaaye, to baari agle khiladi ki.
  6. Gole wala khel: zameen par ek gola bana kar sab barabar kanche uske andar rakhte hain.
  7. Baari-baari se gole ke bahar se apne bade kanche se maarein. Jitne kanche gole se bahar niklein, woh us baari ke points.
  8. Aapka kancha khud gole ke andar ruk jaaye, to kai jagah ise galti maan kar baari khatam kar dete hain.

Kaun jeetta hai

Gucchi wale khel mein jo sabse zyada kanchon ko maare, aur gole wale khel mein jo sabse zyada kanche gole se bahar nikaale, woh jeetta hai.

Har gali ke apne chhote rules hote hain — jaise kitni door se maarna hai ya kancha haath se kitna aage le ja sakte hain. Khel shuru karne se pehle sab mil kar rules tay kar lein, taaki jhagda na ho.

Doosre naam

Kanchon ko alag-alag jagah goli, goti, anti ya banta kehte hain. Angrezi mein inhein marbles kaha jaata hai. Duniya ke bahut se deshon mein bachche sadiyon se yeh khel khelte aaye hain.

Dhyaan rakhne ki baatein

  • Bahut chhote bachchon (3 saal se kam) ko kanche na dein — woh munh mein daal sakte hain.
  • Khel ke baad haath dho lein.
  • Paise ya kisi cheez ki shart laga kar na khelein — maze ke liye khelein.
  • Toote ya chatke kanche se na khelein.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kanche mein bada kancha kis kaam aata hai?

Bada aur bhaari kancha nishana lagaane ke kaam aata hai, kyunki isse takkar zordaar lagti hai aur doosre kanche aasaani se hilte hain.

Pehle kaun khelega, yeh kaise tay karein?

Sab ek lakeer se apna kancha gucchi ya nishaan ki taraf phenkein. Jiska kancha sabse paas ruke, woh pehle khelta hai.

Kya kanche online khel sakte hain?

Haan, NextGrade par kanche ka free online game hai — ungli se kheench kar chhodein aur kanchon ko gole se bahar nikaalein.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

⚪

Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🐚

Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

🔴

Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🌰

Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

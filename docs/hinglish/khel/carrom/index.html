🎯 Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Ek carrom board (chaar konon par pocket)
  • 9 safed aur 9 kaali gotiyan
  • 1 laal goti — Queen
  • 1 striker (baaki gotiyon se bada aur bhaari)
  • Board chikna karne ke liye thoda powder
  • 2 khiladi (aamne-saamne) ya 4 khiladi (do-do ki team)

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Queen ko board ke beech ke gole mein rakhein aur uske chaaron taraf baaki 18 gotiyan safed-kaali baari-baari se sajaayein.
  2. Toss karein. Jeetne wala pehle khelta hai aur aam taur par safed gotiyan leta hai.
  3. Striker ko apni taraf ki do lakeeron (baseline) ke beech rakhein, aise ki woh dono lakeeron ko chhoota ho ya kinaare ke gole ke andar ho.
  4. Pehla shot (break) beech ki gotiyon par maar kar unhein bikhera jaata hai.
  5. Ungli se striker chhitkein — haath ya kalaai baseline se aage nahi jaani chahiye.
  6. Agar aapki koi goti pocket mein gayi, to aapko ek aur baari milti hai. Na jaaye to baari saamne wale ki.
  7. Queen tabhi lein jab aapki kam se kam ek goti pocket ho chuki ho. Queen daalne ke baad agle hi shot mein apni ek goti daalni hoti hai — ise "cover karna" kehte hain.
  8. Agar Queen cover nahi hui, to use wapas beech mein rakh diya jaata hai.
  9. Jo khiladi pehle apni saari 9 gotiyan (aur Queen, agar uske hisse aayi) pocket kar de, woh board jeetta hai.

Foul aur points

Striker khud pocket mein chala jaaye, ya saamne wale ki goti aapki galti se jaaye, to yeh foul hai. Foul par aapki pehle daali gayi ek goti wapas beech mein rakhi jaati hai (ise "due" bhi kehte hain) aur baari khatam.

Board jeetne wale ko saamne wale ki bachi hui har goti ka 1 point milta hai. Agar Queen jeetne wale ne cover ki thi, to 3 point aur (aam niyam mein yeh 3 point tabhi tak milte hain jab tak jeetne wale ka score 22 se kam hai).

Aam taur par jo pehle 25 points tak pahunche, woh poora match jeetta hai. Ghar mein aap kam points bhi rakh sakte hain.

Doosre naam aur itihaas

Carrom ko kai jagah "carrom board" ya bas "carrom" kehte hain. Maana jaata hai ki yeh khel Bharat aur aas-paas ke deshon se shuru ho kar poori duniya mein faila. Aaj iski international pratiyogitayein hoti hain aur Bharat, Sri Lanka va Maldives iske sabse mazboot deshon mein gine jaate hain.

Achha khelne ke aasaan tips

  • Board par halka powder daalein, zyada nahi.
  • Striker ko zor se nahi, sahi disha mein maarna zyada zaroori hai.
  • Kinaare (rebound) se takra kar goti daalna seekhein.
  • Queen jaldi lene ke bajaye pehle cover karne wali goti taiyaar rakhein.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Carrom mein Queen kab le sakte hain?

Jab aapki apni kam se kam ek goti pocket ho chuki ho. Queen daalne ke baad agle shot mein apni ek goti daal kar use cover karna zaroori hai.

Striker pocket mein chala jaaye to kya hota hai?

Yeh foul hai — aapki daali hui ek goti board ke beech wapas rakhi jaati hai aur baari saamne wale ki ho jaati hai.

Carrom mein kitni gotiyan hoti hain?

Kul 19 — 9 safed, 9 kaali aur 1 laal Queen, saath mein 1 striker.

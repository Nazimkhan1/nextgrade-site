🪨 Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • 7 chapte patthar (ya tile ke tukde), jo ek-doosre par tik sakein
  • Ek naram gend (tennis ball ya rubber ki gend)
  • Khula maidaan
  • Zameen par ek lakeer, jahan se gend phenkni hai
  • Do teamein, har team mein 3 ya zyada khiladi

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Beech mein 7 pattharon ko bade se chhote ke kram mein ek ke upar ek jamaayein.
  2. Toss se tay karein ki kaun si team pehle giraayegi (hamla karne wali team).
  3. Hamla karne wali team ka ek khiladi lakeer ke peechhe se gend phenk kar pattharon ka dher giraata hai. Aam taur par har khiladi ko teen mauke milte hain.
  4. Agar phenki gayi gend dher se takraaye bina saamne wali team ne ek tappe mein ya seedhe pakad li, to phenkne wala out.
  5. Dher girte hi hamla karne wali team ke khiladi bikhre pattharon ko phir se jamaane ki koshish karte hain.
  6. Usi samay doosri team gend utha kar un khiladiyon ko gend se chhoone (kamar se neeche halka maarne) ki koshish karti hai. Gend lagi to woh khiladi out.
  7. Gend le kar daudna mana hai — gend ek-doosre ko pass karke aage badhaayi jaati hai.
  8. Agar hamla karne wali team saare patthar phir se jama kar "Pitthu!" bol de, to use point milta hai.

Kaun jeetta hai

Agar dher jamaane wali team ke saare khiladi out ho jaayein, to baari badal jaati hai aur doosri team giraane aati hai.

Tay rounds ke baad jis team ke zyada points hon (jitni baar dher phir se jamaaya), woh jeetti hai.

Doosre naam

Uttar Bharat mein ise Pitthu ya Pitthu Garam, Rajasthan mein Sitoliya, Karnataka mein Lagori, Tamil Nadu mein Ezhu Kallu (yaani saat patthar) aur Maharashtra mein Lingorcha jaise naamon se jaana jaata hai. Angrezi mein ise seven stones kehte hain.

Suraksha ka dhyaan

  • Hamesha naram gend se khelein, cricket ki kathor gend se nahi.
  • Gend kamar se neeche hi maarein — sir ya chehre par kabhi nahi.
  • Patthar bahut bhaari ya nukeele na hon.
  • Daudte samay doosron se takraane se bachein.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pitthu mein kitne patthar hote hain?

Aam taur par 7 chapte patthar, jinhein bade se chhote ke kram mein ek ke upar ek jamaaya jaata hai.

Pitthu mein out kaise hote hain?

Phenki gayi gend ko saamne wali team pakad le, ya dher jamaate samay gend aapko chhoo jaaye — dono mein out.

Lagori aur Pitthu mein kya farq hai?

Koi khaas farq nahi — yeh ek hi khel hai. Karnataka mein ise Lagori, Rajasthan mein Sitoliya aur uttar Bharat mein Pitthu kehte hain.

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

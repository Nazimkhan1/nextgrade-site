🔴 Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Zameen, aangan ya kaagaz par bana board — 5×5 nishaanon ka chaukor jaal, seedhi aur tirchhi lakeeron ke saath, aur upar-neeche ek-ek tikona (kul 37 nishaan)
  • Ek khiladi ke liye 16 ek jaisi gotiyan (jaise kankad)
  • Doosre ke liye 16 alag dikhne wali gotiyan (jaise imli ke beej)
  • 2 khiladi

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Har khiladi apni 16 gotiyan apni taraf ke tikone ke 6 nishaanon aur chaukor ki paas wali do lakeeron ke 10 nishaanon par rakhta hai. Beech wali lakeer ke 5 nishaan khaali rehte hain.
  2. Tay kar lein ki pehle kaun chalega, phir baari-baari se chalein.
  3. Apni baari mein ek goti ko lakeer ke saath paas wale khaali nishaan par le jaayein. Tirchha sirf wahin chal sakte hain jahan tirchhi lakeer bani ho.
  4. Agar aapki goti ke theek bagal mein saamne wale ki goti hai aur uske peeche usi seedhi lakeer par nishaan khaali hai, to uske upar se kood kar khaali nishaan par pahunchein. Jis goti ke upar se koode, woh board se hat jaati hai.
  5. Koodne ke baad agar wahi goti phir kisi goti ko maar sakti hai, to usi baari mein aage kood sakte hain — ise lagaataar maar kehte hain.
  6. Kai jagah "majboori" ka niyam hota hai: maar sakte hain to maarna hi padega. Khel shuru karne se pehle tay kar lein ki yeh niyam laagu hai ya nahi.

Jeet aur barabari

Jo khiladi saamne wale ki saari 16 gotiyan maar de, woh jeetta hai.

Agar kisi khiladi ki baari ho aur uski koi bhi goti chal na sake, to woh haar jaata hai.

Jab dono taraf thodi gotiyan bachein aur bahut der tak koi goti na mare, to aam taur par khel barabar maan liya jaata hai.

Doosre naam aur kahan khela jaata hai

Bengal aur Bangladesh mein ise "Sholo Guti" kehte hain; Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha aur Uttar Pradesh ke gaon mein bhi yeh "Solah Goti" ya milte-julte naamon se khela jaata hai. Angrezi mein ise kabhi-kabhi "Sixteen Soldiers" kaha jaata hai. Iski shuruaat ka pakka itihaas nahi milta, aur jagah-jagah niyam thode badal jaate hain — jaise kahin goti peeche nahi chal sakti.

Achha khelne ke aasaan tips

  • Apni goti ke peeche khaali nishaan mat chhodein — wahin se saamne wala kood kar maarta hai.
  • Gotiyan jodi ya kataar mein rakhein, woh ek-doosre ko bachati hain.
  • Chaal se pehle dekhein ki badle mein saamne wala do gotiyan to nahi maar lega.
  • Beech ke jin nishaanon se tirchhi lakeerein guzarti hain, wahan se 8 taraf chal sakte hain — in par kabza faaydemand hai.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Sholo Guti mein kul kitne nishaan hote hain?

37 — beech ke chaukor mein 25 aur dono tikonon mein 6-6. Shuru mein 32 gotiyan lagti hain aur beech wali lakeer ke 5 nishaan khaali rehte hain.

Kya goti peeche bhi chal sakti hai?

Sabse aam niyam mein haan, goti har disha mein lakeer ke saath chal aur maar sakti hai. Kuchh jagah peeche chalna mana hai, isliye khel se pehle tay kar lein.

Ek baari mein kitni gotiyan maar sakte hain?

Jitni baar wahi goti seedhi lakeer mein kood sake — har kood mein ek goti marti hai, aur disha badal sakti hai.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🏏

Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

⚪

Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🐚

Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

🌰

Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

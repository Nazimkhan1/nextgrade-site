🏏 Gilli-Danda kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Gilli-danda gaon aur gali ka purana khel hai, jise kai log "desi cricket" bhi kehte hain. Ismein ek lambe dande se chhoti gilli ko uchhaal kar door maara jaata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

▶️ Online khelo

Kya-kya chahiye

  • Ek danda — lagbhag dedh-do foot lambi mazboot lakdi
  • Ek gilli — 3 se 6 inch ki lakdi, jiske dono sire nukeele chhile hon
  • Zameen mein ek chhota lamba gaddha (gucchi)
  • Khula maidaan, jahan aas-paas sheeshe ya log na hon
  • 2 ya zyada khiladi (team mein bhi khel sakte hain)

Khelne ka tareeka, step by step

  1. Ek khiladi (ya team) maarne wala banta hai, baaki saamne fielding karte hain.
  2. Pehla shot: gilli ko gucchi ke upar aada rakhein. Dande ko gilli ke neeche laga kar zor se uchhaalein, taaki gilli fielders ki taraf door jaaye.
  3. Agar koi fielder gilli ko hawa mein hi pakad le (catch), to maarne wala out.
  4. Catch na ho, to maarne wala danda gucchi ke upar aada rakh deta hai. Fielder gilli jahan giri wahan se use dande ki taraf phenkta hai. Gilli dande se takra jaaye, ya bahut paas (ek dande ki doori ke andar) gire, to maarne wala out.
  5. Agar maarne wala bach gaya, to gilli jahan giri wahan ja kar uske nukeele sire par dande se halka maarein — gilli hawa mein uchhlegi.
  6. Uchhli hui gilli ko hawa mein hi dande se zor se maar kar door bhejein. Aam taur par aisa teen baar tak karne ka mauka milta hai.
  7. Hawa mein maari gayi gilli agar koi fielder pakad le, to bhi out.
  8. Out hone par agla khiladi maarne aata hai.

Points aur jeet

Aakhri shot ke baad gilli jahan ruki, wahan se gucchi tak ki doori dande se naapi jaati hai. Jitne dande ki doori, utne points.

Jis khiladi ya team ke kul points pehle tay sankhya (jaise 100) tak pahunchein, ya sabki baari ke baad jiske sabse zyada points hon, woh jeetta hai. Har ilaake mein rules thode alag hote hain, khel se pehle tay kar lein.

Doosre naam

Bengal mein ise Danguli, Odisha mein Guli-Badi, Andhra-Telangana aur Karnataka mein Chinni-Dandu, Kerala mein Kuttiyum-Kolum aur Nepal mein Dandi-Biyo kehte hain. Angrezi mein iske milte-julte khel ko tip-cat kaha jaata hai.

Suraksha ka dhyaan

  • Gilli tez udti hai — maarne wale ke saamne aur bahut paas koi khada na ho.
  • Ghar, gaadiyon aur khidkiyon se door khule maidaan mein khelein.
  • Gilli ke sire bahut zyada nukeele na rakhein.
  • Chhote bachche badon ki dekh-rekh mein khelein.

▶️ Online khelo

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Gilli-danda mein out kaise hote hain?

Teen tareekon se: gilli hawa mein catch ho jaaye, fielder ki phenki gilli gucchi par rakhe dande se takra jaaye, ya woh gucchi ke bahut paas (ek dande ki doori ke andar) gire.

Gilli-danda mein points kaise ginte hain?

Gilli jahan ruki wahan se gucchi tak ki doori dande se naapte hain — jitne dande, utne points.

Bengal mein gilli-danda ko kya kehte hain?

Bengal mein ise Danguli (ডাংগুলি) kehte hain.

Aur khelon ke rules

🎯

Carrom kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Carrom Bharat ke ghar-ghar mein khela jaane wala board game hai. Ismein ungli se striker chhitak kar apni gotiyan board ke chaar konon wale chhedon (pocket) mein daalni hoti hain. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🔵

Kanche kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Kanche (kaanch ki rang-birangi goliyan) gali-mohalle ka sabse purana khel hai. Iske kai tareeke hain, yahan do sabse mashhoor — gucchi wala aur gole wala — aasaan bhasha mein.

🪨

Pitthu (Sitoliya / Lagori) kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pitthu saat chapte pattharon aur ek gend se khela jaane wala team ka khel hai. Ek team pattharon ka dher gira kar use phir se jamaane ki koshish karti hai, aur doosri team gend maar kar unhein rokti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein rules hain.

⚪

Nau Gutti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Nau Gutti do logon ka purana khel hai, jo chabutre, aangan ya mandir ke patthar par bane teen chaukor wale board par khela jaata hai. Har khiladi ki 9 gotiyan hoti hain, aur teen gotiyan ek line mein aate hi saamne wale ki ek goti uth jaati hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🐚

Chaupar / Pachisi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Chaupar aur Pachisi Bharat ka purana board khel hai, jo plus ke nishaan jaisi kapde ki bisaat par kaudiyon ya lambe paason se khela jaata hai. Har khiladi apni goti ko poori bisaat ka chakkar lagwa ke beech ke ghar (char-koni) mein pahunchata hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein aam rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farq hota hai.

🔴

Sholo Guti kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Sholo Guti (yaani 16 goti) do logon ka purana desi khel hai, jo zameen par lakeerein kheench kar kankad ya beejon se khela jaata hai. Dono ke paas 16-16 gotiyan hoti hain aur saamne wale ki goti ke upar se kood kar use maarna hota hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein poore rules hain.

🌰

Pallanguzhi kaise khelte hain — poore rules

Pallanguzhi Dakshin Bharat ka purana beej bone wala khel hai, jo lakdi ke lambe board par do log khelte hain. Ismein kismat nahi, sirf ginti aur soch chalti hai. Neeche aasaan bhasha mein ek aam tareeke ke rules hain — har ilaake mein thoda farak hota hai.

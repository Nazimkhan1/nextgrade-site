💞 Shaadi ki Saalgirah · शादी की सालगिरह

Shaadi ki saalgirah saath bitaye saalon ka jashn hai. Yahan jeevansaathi, Mummy-Papa, bhaiya-bhabhi aur doston ke liye anniversary wishes hain.

Shaadi ki Saalgirah ke messages

  • Shaadi ki saalgirah mubarak ho! Aapki jodi hamesha yun hi salaamat rahe.
  • Saath bitaya har saal aapke pyaar ko aur gehra kare. Happy Anniversary!
  • Mummy-Papa, aapka saath hamare liye sabse badi seekh hai. Saalgirah ki dher saari badhaai!
  • Bhagwan aapki jodi ko har khushi aur lambi umar dein. Happy Anniversary!
  • Tumhare saath har din tyohar jaisa hai. Happy Anniversary, mere humsafar!
  • Bhaiya-Bhabhi, aapki jodi pe kisi ki nazar na lage! Happy Anniversary.

Shaadi ki Saalgirah wishes in English

  • Happy Anniversary! May your love grow stronger with every passing year.
  • To a couple who makes love look easy — Happy Anniversary!
  • Wishing you both a lifetime of laughter, trust and togetherness. Happy Anniversary!
  • Happy Anniversary, Mom and Dad. Thank you for showing us what love means.

💌 Wish Card banao

Shaadi ki Saalgirah ke baare mein

Saalgirah pe sabse achha message woh hota hai jisme saath bitaye palon ki yaad ho aur aane wale saalon ke liye dua. Jeevansaathi ke liye dil ki baat likho, aur maa-baap ya rishtedaaron ke liye aadar aur aashirwaad wale shabd chuno.

Maa-baap ki 25vin (Silver Jubilee) ya 50vin (Golden Jubilee) saalgirah pe parivaar aksar chhota sa karyakram rakhta hai — aise mauke pe purani photos ke saath card banana bahut pasand kiya jaata hai.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

25vin aur 50vin saalgirah ko kya kehte hain?

25vin saalgirah ko Silver Jubilee (Rajat Jayanti) aur 50vin ko Golden Jubilee (Swarn Jayanti) kehte hain.

Saalgirah aur varshgaanth mein kya farq hai?

Dono ka matlab ek hi hai — kisi khaas din ka har saal laut kar aana. Aam bolchaal mein shaadi ke liye "saalgirah" zyada kaha jaata hai.

🧩 Paheliyan — uttar ke saath

Paheli padho, socho, phir tap karke uttar dekho. Parivaar aur doston ke saath khelo — ya school ki assembly mein poochho.

🌟 Aaj ki paheli

Tokri mein 6 aam the. 6 bachchon ko ek-ek aam diya, phir bhi tokri mein ek aam bacha raha. Kaise?

Uttar dekhne ke liye tap karo

Uttar: Aakhri bachche ko aam tokri samet de diya.

Bachchon ki aasaan paheliyan

Hari thi, man bhari thi, laakh moti jadi thi; Raja ji ke baagh mein dushaala odhe khadi thi. Batao kya?

Uttar: Bhutta (makka)

Ek ped ki baarah daaliyan, har daali par tees patte; aadhe patte kaale, aadhe safed. Batao kya?

Uttar: Saal — 12 maheene, har maheene lagbhag 30 din, din aur raat

Bina pair ke chalti hai, bina munh ke samay batati hai. Batao kya?

Uttar: Ghadi

Kaala ghoda, safed sawaari; ek utri to doosri ki baari. Batao kya?

Uttar: Tawa aur roti

Paani mein girun to bhi na bheegun, dhoop mein sada saath chalun. Batao main kaun?

Uttar: Parchhaai

Jo karo wahi kare, par baayan haath uthao to daayan uthaaye. Batao kya?

Uttar: Aaina (darpan)

Hara dibba, peela makaan, usmein baithe kaale mehmaan. Batao kya?

Uttar: Papeeta

Ek gufa aur battees chaukidaar. Batao kya?

Uttar: Munh aur daant

Safed murgi, hari poonchh, zameen ke andar ugi — batao kya?

Uttar: Mooli

Paani se nikla ped ek, patte nahi par daalein anek; uski thandi chhaaya mein koi baith na paaya. Batao kya?

Uttar: Fawwaara

Chaar pair hain par chal na paaye, phir bhi sabko baithaaye. Batao kya?

Uttar: Kursi

Jitna zyada sukhaao, utna zyada bheege. Batao kya?

Uttar: Tauliya

Mazedaar paheliyan

Woh kya hai jo aapka hai, par doosre use aapse zyada istemaal karte hain?

Uttar: Aapka naam

Woh kya hai jo jitna badhta hai, utna kam dikhaayi deta hai?

Uttar: Andhera

Mera naam lete hi main toot jaati hoon. Batao main kaun?

Uttar: Chuppi (khaamoshi)

Kis maheene mein 28 din hote hain?

Uttar: Har maheene mein! Har maheene mein kam se kam 28 din hote hain.

Ek aadmi baarish mein bina chhaate ke chala, phir bhi uske sir ka ek baal bhi geela nahi hua. Kaise?

Uttar: Woh ganja tha.

Aisi kaun si cheez hai jo hamesha upar jaati hai, par kabhi neeche nahi aati?

Uttar: Umar

Dimaagi aur ganit ki paheliyan

Tokri mein 6 aam the. 6 bachchon ko ek-ek aam diya, phir bhi tokri mein ek aam bacha raha. Kaise?

Uttar: Aakhri bachche ko aam tokri samet de diya.

Ram ke pita ke paanch bete hain: Nandu, Chandu, Bandu, Sandu… to paanchve ka naam kya hai?

Uttar: Ram

3 billiyan 3 minute mein 3 choohe pakadti hain. To 100 billiyan 100 choohe kitne minute mein pakdengi?

Uttar: 3 minute — har billi 3 minute mein ek chooha pakadti hai.

Ghadi 3 baje 3 baar bajti hai aur ismein 3 second lagte hain. To 6 baje 6 baar bajne mein kitna samay lagega?

Uttar: 7.5 second — 3 baar bajne mein 2 antaraal (1.5 second har ek), 6 baar mein 5 antaraal.

Aadha kilo rui aur aadha kilo loha — kaun zyada bhaari hai?

Uttar: Dono baraabar — dono aadha kilo hain.

Main do ankon ki sankhya hoon; ulta likho to bhi wahi rahun, aur 6 se guna karo to 66 ban jaaun. Main kaun?

Uttar: 11

📖 Aur padho: kahaniyan

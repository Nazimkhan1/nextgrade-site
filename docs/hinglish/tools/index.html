🧰 Free Tools

Form, print aur roz ke kaam ke chhote tools. Sab aapke phone ke browser mein hi chalte hain — photo ya document kisi server par upload nahi hote.

🪪

Passport Size Photo Maker

Safed ya neela background, chehra apne aap beech mein, 3.5×4.5 cm / 2×2 inch, 4×6 print sheet aur KB limit wali digital photo.

 📄

Document Scanner → PDF

Phone se document scan karo: apne aap crop, black & white, kai pages ek PDF mein, aur 100/200 KB se kam.

 🗜️

Image Compressor — Photo 20/50/100 KB

Photo ko 20, 50, 100, 200 KB ya kisi bhi size mein chhota karo. JPG ya WebP, ek saath kai photos, ZIP download.

 📉

PDF Compressor — 100/200/500 KB

PDF ko 100, 200, 500 KB ya 1 MB se chhota karo, form upload ke liye. Scan ke liye black & white bhi.

 🗂️

JPG / Photo se PDF

Kai photos (marksheet, Aadhaar, certificate) ek PDF mein — A4, order badlo, ghumao, aur KB limit.

 📎

PDF Jodo / Alag Karo

Kai PDF ek mein jodo, kuch pages nikalo ya hatao, pages ghumao — text aur quality waisi hi rehti hai.

 📐

Image Resizer — px / cm / inch

Photo ko sahi size mein karo: 3.5×4.5 cm, 200×230, 600×600 ya apna size — crop, background aur KB limit ke saath.

 ✂️

Photo Crop & Rotate

Photo kaato (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, passport), ghumao, seedha karo aur palto — JPG, PNG ya WebP mein.

 ✍️

Signature Maker

Screen par sign karo ya kaagaz wale sign ki photo saaf karo — transparent PNG ya safed JPG, form ke size aur KB mein.

 ⌨️

Hindi & English Typing Test

1 se 15 minute ka typing test: WPM, accuracy aur galat shabd. SSC jaise test ki practice, backspace on/off.

 🌾

Zameen Naap Converter — Bigha, Katha, Acre

Bigha, katha, biswa, kanal, acre, hectare, gaj aur sq ft — state ke hisab se.

 🔳

QR Code Maker — UPI, Link, WhatsApp

UPI payment QR (amount ke saath), link, text, phone, WhatsApp aur Wi-Fi ka QR — PNG ya SVG download.

 😄

Sticker Maker

Photo se sticker: background hatao, safed border, upar-neeche text — 512×512 WebP. Text wale stickers bhi.

 📝

Form Photo & Signature Resizer

SSC, UPSC, IBPS / SBI, NEET, JEE form ke liye photo, signature, angootha aur declaration — sahi px aur KB mein. Kaagaz wala signature saaf bhi.

Aapki files, aapke paas

Yeh tools poori tarah aapke phone/browser mein hi chalte hain (kuch tools apni chhoti library ek baar download karte hain). Aapki photo ya document (Aadhaar, marksheet, certificate) kisi server par nahi jaate, kahin save nahi hote, aur hum unhe dekh bhi nahi sakte. Page band karte hi sab mit jaata hai. Ek baar khulne ke baad zyadatar tools bina internet ke bhi chalte hain.

Kiske kaam ke

Online form bharne wale students (photo ka size aur KB), naukri ya admission ke liye documents ki PDF, dukaan ke product ki saaf photo, ya bas naya look try karne ke liye. Har tool free hai, bina login aur bina watermark.

Dhyan dein: har form/office ke apne niyam hote hain (size, background, KB) — bhejne se pehle unke niyam zaroor padh lein.

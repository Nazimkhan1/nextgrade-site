Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

April mein ghoomne ke liye sabse achhi jagah kaun si hai?

Mausam ke hisaab se April mein Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital jaisi jagahein achhi rehti hain. Apni pasand (pahaad, beach, dharmik) ke hisaab se upar ki list dekhein.

Kya April mein pahaadon par jaana theek hai?

Upar "Pahaad" wali list mein wahi jagahein hain jahan April mein din ka average temperature aaraamdaayak rehta hai aur baarish zyada nahi hoti.

Yeh jaankari kahan se hai?

Temperature aur baarish NASA POWER ke pichhle 10 saal (2015–2024) ke data ka average hai; aaj ka mausam MET Norway se aata hai.

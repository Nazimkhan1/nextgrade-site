February ki yatra ke liye salaah

  • Pahaadon par bahut thand hoti hai — garam kapde, dastaane, topi saath rakhein; barf wale raaste kabhi-kabhi band ho jaate hain.
  • Chhuttiyon aur tyohaaron par bheed rehti hai — train aur hotel pehle se book karein.
  • Jaane se ek din pehle us jagah ka "aaj ka mausam" zaroor dekhein.

Yatra se pehle sadak, permit aur mandir/park khulne ka samay official website par dekh lein — yeh har saal badal sakte hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

February mein ghoomne ke liye sabse achhi jagah kaun si hai?

Mausam ke hisaab se February mein Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling jaisi jagahein achhi rehti hain. Apni pasand (pahaad, beach, dharmik) ke hisaab se upar ki list dekhein.

Kya February mein pahaadon par jaana theek hai?

Upar "Pahaad" wali list mein wahi jagahein hain jahan February mein din ka average temperature aaraamdaayak rehta hai aur baarish zyada nahi hoti.

Yeh jaankari kahan se hai?

Temperature aur baarish NASA POWER ke pichhle 10 saal (2015–2024) ke data ka average hai; aaj ka mausam MET Norway se aata hai.

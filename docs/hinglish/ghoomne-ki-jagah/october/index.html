October ki yatra ke liye salaah

  • Din mein dhoop tez ho sakti hai — topi, paani ki bottle aur sunscreen rakhein.
  • Chhuttiyon aur tyohaaron par bheed rehti hai — train aur hotel pehle se book karein.
  • Jaane se ek din pehle us jagah ka "aaj ka mausam" zaroor dekhein.

Yatra se pehle sadak, permit aur mandir/park khulne ka samay official website par dekh lein — yeh har saal badal sakte hain.

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

October mein ghoomne ke liye sabse achhi jagah kaun si hai?

Mausam ke hisaab se October mein Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital jaisi jagahein achhi rehti hain. Apni pasand (pahaad, beach, dharmik) ke hisaab se upar ki list dekhein.

Kya October mein pahaadon par jaana theek hai?

Upar "Pahaad" wali list mein wahi jagahein hain jahan October mein din ka average temperature aaraamdaayak rehta hai aur baarish zyada nahi hoti.

Yeh jaankari kahan se hai?

Temperature aur baarish NASA POWER ke pichhle 10 saal (2015–2024) ke data ka average hai; aaj ka mausam MET Norway se aata hai.

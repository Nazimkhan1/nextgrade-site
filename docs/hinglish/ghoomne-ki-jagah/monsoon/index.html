🌧️ Monsoon mein ghoomne ki jagah (July–September)

Baarish mein yeh jagahein sabse hari-bhari hoti hain — jharne, baadal aur chai-coffee ke bagaan. Pahaadi raaston par saavdhaani rakhein.

Munnar

Kerala

Door tak faile chai ke bagaan, jharne aur baadal — baarish mein aur bhi hara-bhara.

☀️ Din 20°C🌙 Raat 15°C🌧️ Baarish 285 mm

Shillong

Meghalaya

Meghalaya — "baadalon ka ghar"; paas mein Cherrapunji ke jharne aur living root bridge.

☀️ Din 24°C🌙 Raat 18°C🌧️ Baarish 583 mm

Lonavala

Maharashtra

Mumbai-Pune ke beech — baarish mein jharne, kile aur hari pahaadiyan; chikki mashhoor.

☀️ Din 25°C🌙 Raat 21°C🌧️ Baarish 559 mm

Mahabaleshwar

Maharashtra

Strawberry, ghaatiyon ke view-point aur Venna jheel.

☀️ Din 22°C🌙 Raat 17°C🌧️ Baarish 517 mm

Madikeri

Karnataka

Wayanad

Kerala

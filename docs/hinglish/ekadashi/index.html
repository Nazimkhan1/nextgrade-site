Samay IST mein, khagoliya ganana se (kuch minute ka antar sambhav). Aapke sheher aur parampara (Smarta/Vaishnav) ke hisaab se vrat ki tareekh ek din alag ho sakti hai — apne sthaniya panchang ya pandit ji se zaroor milaayein.

Tareekh kaise tay hoti hai

  • Tithi chandrama aur suraj ki doori se banti hai — har 12° par ek tithi. Isliye tithi din ke beech mein bhi shuru ya khatam ho sakti hai.
  • Vrat ki tareekh aam taur par woh din hai jis din suryoday ke samay woh tithi chal rahi ho (udaya tithi). Yahan New Delhi ke suryoday se nikaali gayi hai.
  • Mahinon ke naam North India ke purnimant panchang ke anusaar hain.

HIV ka full form

HIV ka full form Human Immunodeficiency Virus hai.

HIV ka full form hai Human Immunodeficiency Virus.
The virus that weakens the immune system.

Aasaan bhasha mein

HIV yaani Human Immunodeficiency Virus, ek virus jo shareer ki rog-pratirodhak kshamta par hamla karta hai, khaaskar un white blood cells par jinhe CD4 cells kehte hain. Ilaaj na ho to dheere-dheere shareer doosri bimaariyon se ladne ki taakat kho deta hai. HIV wala vyakti kai saal bina lakshan ke bhi reh sakta hai, isliye sirf test hi pakka bata sakta hai.

HIV phailta hai: asurakshit yaun sambandh se, sankramit khoon chadhaane se, ek hi sui ya syringe share karne se, aur pregnancy, delivery ya stanpaan ke dauraan maa se bachche ko. Yeh haath milaane, gale lagne, saath khaana khaane, ek hi toilet use karne ya machchhar ke kaatne se nahin phailta. Sarkari hospitals ke ICTC centres mein test free aur gopniya hota hai.

Achchhi baat yeh hai ki aaj ART (Antiretroviral Therapy) naam ka ilaaj uplabdh hai, jise niyamit lene par HIV wale log lambi aur saamaanya zindagi jee sakte hain; sarkari ART centres par yeh free milta hai. HIV aur AIDS ek nahin hain; AIDS bina ilaaj wali aakhri avastha hai. Koi bhi shanka ho to doctor se salaah lein.

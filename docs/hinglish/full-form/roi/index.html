ROI ka full form

ROI ka full form Return On Investment hai.

ROI ka full form hai Return On Investment.
The profit earned on an investment.

Aasaan bhasha mein

ROI batata hai ki lagaaye gaye paise pe kitna fayda ya nuksaan hua, percentage mein. Formula: ROI = (kul kamaai − lagaai gayi rakam) ÷ lagaai gayi rakam × 100.

Udaharan: aapne kisi kaam mein ₹10,000 lagaaye aur ₹12,000 wapas mile, to fayda ₹2,000 hua aur ROI = 2,000 ÷ 10,000 × 100 = 20%. Agar sirf ₹9,000 wapas aate, to ROI −10% hota, yaani nuksaan.

ROI se do alag investment ya ads ki tulna aasaan hoti hai, lekin isme samay nahi gina jaata — 20% fayda ek saal mein hua ya paanch saal mein, yeh alag se dekhna chahiye (iske liye CAGR kaam aata hai).

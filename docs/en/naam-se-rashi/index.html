In Indian tradition a child is often named with the syllable of the birth nakshatra. Each of the 27 nakshatras has 4 quarters (padas), and each quarter has a fixed syllable — 108 syllables, 9 for each of the 12 rashis. Reading this chart backwards gives the “name rashi” from the first syllable of a name.

Note: the name rashi and the birth rashi can differ. The real birth rashi is the sign of the Moon at the date, time and place of birth, worked out from a panchang or by an astrologer. This page only shows the traditional name-letter chart — no predictions or horoscopes.

Syllables not directly in the chart (such as Sha, Ba, or short-vowel forms) are matched to the commonly used nearest letter — for example “Sha” to Kumbh and “Ba” to Vrishabh. Some panchangs place a few letters in a different rashi.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find rashi from a name?

Take the first syllable of the name and find it in the chart — “Ra” is Tula, “Mo” is Simha, “A” is Mesh.

Is name rashi the same as birth rashi?

Not always. Birth rashi is the Moon’s sign at birth; name rashi comes only from the first syllable of the name.

What is the rashi of names starting with A?

Names starting with “A” are traditionally taken as Mesh (Aries).

What is the rashi for S or Sh names?

“Sa, Si, Su, Se, So” are in Kumbh; “Sha” is also usually taken as Kumbh.

Do you give horoscopes?

No, this page only gives the name-letter chart.

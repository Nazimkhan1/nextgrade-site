🌳 Essay on the Importance of Trees

Trees are our true friends. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. Trees give us oxygen to breathe.
  2. They take in carbon dioxide and clean the air.
  3. Trees give us fruit, flowers, wood and medicines.
  4. In summer the shade of a tree keeps us cool.
  5. The roots of trees hold the soil and stop erosion.
  6. More trees bring better rainfall.
  7. Trees are the home of many birds and animals.
  8. Cutting trees harms nature and our health.
  9. We should plant new trees every year; this is called tree plantation.
  10. "One tree, a hundred joys" — so saving trees is everyone's duty.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

Trees are our best friends. They give us oxygen to breathe and keep the air clean. From trees we get fruit, flowers, shade, wood and medicines. Sitting in the shade of a tree in summer feels wonderful. Trees are also the home of birds and animals. Where there are more trees, there is good rainfall and the air stays cool. We should not cut trees; instead, we should plant new ones every year. Plant trees and save the earth.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

Nature has given us many things, and among them trees are the greatest gift. Trees stand quietly and keep giving to others all their life, asking for nothing in return. It is rightly said that trees are our truest and most selfless friends.

What trees give us

Trees give us oxygen to breathe and absorb the harmful carbon dioxide from the air. From them we get fruit, vegetables, flowers, wood, rubber, paper and many medicines. In summer their shade gives relief. The roots of trees hold the soil firmly, which prevents floods and land erosion.

Trees and the environment

Trees keep the earth's temperature balanced and help bring good rainfall by attracting clouds. Where there are more trees, the air stays clean and cool. Trees also reduce pollution, dust and noise. That is why forests are called the "lungs of the earth".

Home of living creatures

Trees are the home of countless birds, squirrels, monkeys and insects. Forests shelter thousands of animals and herbs. If trees disappeared, this whole world would become barren and the balance of nature would break.

Cutting of trees — a danger

Today trees are being cut recklessly to build cities, factories and roads. Because of this, rainfall is decreasing, heat is rising, the air is becoming poisonous and many animals are losing their homes. This greed will prove dangerous for all of humankind in the days to come.

Our duty

We should save trees and plant new ones every year — this is called tree plantation. On your birthday or any special day, do plant a tree and take care of it. Also explain to others why trees should not be cut. This is the greatest service we can do for the earth.

Conclusion

Life cannot even be imagined without trees. They give us breath, food, shade and beauty. If we plant and protect trees today, the coming generations will get clean air and a green earth. So let us plant at least one tree.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on the importance of trees?

Introduction, what trees give us, trees and the environment, home of creatures, the danger of cutting, our duty and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

Why are trees called the lungs of the earth?

Because trees absorb carbon dioxide from the air and give us oxygen while keeping the air clean, just as lungs do for the body.

What does tree plantation mean?

Tree plantation means planting new trees and caring for them so that the environment stays green and clean.

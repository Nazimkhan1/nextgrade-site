🏭 Essay on Pollution

Pollution is one of the biggest problems of today. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. The dirtying of our environment is called pollution.
  2. It is mainly of four kinds: air, water, soil and noise.
  3. The smoke of vehicles and factories dirties the air.
  4. Factory waste and rubbish make river water poisonous.
  5. Plastic and chemical fertilizers spoil the soil.
  6. Loud horns and loudspeakers cause noise pollution.
  7. Pollution causes diseases of the lungs, eyes and skin.
  8. Cutting trees and burning stubble increase pollution.
  9. We can stop it by planting more trees and using less plastic.
  10. Protecting the environment is the duty of us all.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

The dirtying of our environment is called pollution. It is of four kinds — air, water, soil and noise pollution. The smoke of vehicles and factories dirties the air, and rubbish makes river water poisonous. Pollution gives us coughs and diseases of the breathing and eyes. We should plant trees, use less plastic and throw rubbish in the right place. Clean air and water are necessary for all of us.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

Nature has given us clean air, pure water and a green earth. But for comfort and greed, humans have made it dirty. This dirtying of the environment is called pollution, and today it has become one of the biggest problems in the whole world.

Types of pollution

Pollution is mainly of four kinds. Air pollution — from the smoke of vehicles and factories. Water pollution — from factory waste and rubbish in rivers. Soil pollution — from plastic and chemical fertilizers. And noise pollution — from loud horns, loudspeakers and the noise of machines.

Causes of pollution

A growing population, factories, millions of vehicles, reckless cutting of trees, the rising use of plastic and the burning of stubble in fields are the main causes of pollution. In our race for comfort, we keep harming nature.

Effects of pollution

Pollution is very harmful to our health. Dirty air causes diseases of the lungs, dirty water causes stomach illness, and noise causes deafness and stress. Animals and birds also die, the weather changes, and the earth's temperature keeps rising, which is called global warming.

Ways to stop pollution

To stop pollution we should plant as many trees as possible and stop trees from being cut. Walk, cycle or use the bus instead of a private vehicle. Use cloth and paper bags instead of plastic, throw rubbish in the right place, and stop factory waste from going into rivers.

Conclusion

Pollution is not the problem of one country or person, but of all humankind. If we do not act today, the coming generations will not get even clean air and water. So we must all together keep the environment clean and green — that is the good of the earth and of ourselves.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on Pollution?

Introduction, types of pollution, causes, effects, ways to stop it and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

How many types of pollution are there?

Mainly four — air, water, soil and noise pollution.

What are easy ways to stop pollution?

Planting more trees, using less plastic, throwing rubbish in the right place, walking/cycling/using the bus instead of a private vehicle, and stopping factory waste water.

❄️ Essay on Winter Season

Winter is one of the most loved seasons in India. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. In India, winter usually lasts from November to February.
  2. December and January are the coldest months.
  3. In winter the days are short and the nights are long.
  4. Mornings are foggy and dew drops shine on the grass.
  5. We wear sweaters, caps, socks and shawls to keep warm.
  6. People love sitting in the sun and around a bonfire.
  7. Carrots, peas, spinach, radish and oranges are found in plenty.
  8. Gajar ka halwa, peanuts, jaggery and til laddoos make winter tasty.
  9. Christmas, New Year, Lohri and Makar Sankranti come in this season.
  10. Winter makes us active and healthy, so I like it very much.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

India has six seasons, and winter is one of them. It lasts from November to February. In winter, cold winds blow, mornings are foggy and the sunshine feels lovely. We wear warm clothes and sleep under a thick quilt at night. Markets are full of fresh green vegetables and fruits like oranges and guavas. My mother makes hot gajar ka halwa. Festivals like Christmas, New Year and Makar Sankranti are celebrated in this season. Food digests well in winter and our body becomes strong. I enjoy playing in the warm afternoon sun. Winter is my favourite season.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

Nature has gifted us different seasons — summer, monsoon, winter and spring. Each has its own beauty, but winter is often called the best season for health and food. When a light chill enters the air after the rains, we know that winter is on its way.

When and why winter comes

In India, winter generally lasts from November to February, with December and January being the coldest. During these months the sun's rays fall slanting on northern India and the days are shorter, so the land gets less heat. North India becomes very cold and the mountains receive snow, while the south and the coastal areas stay only mildly cool.

Winter mornings and nature

A winter morning is special. Thick fog covers everything, and sometimes we cannot see even a few steps ahead. Dew drops sparkle like pearls on grass and leaves. As soon as the sun comes out, everyone sits on the roof or in the courtyard to enjoy its warmth. Yellow mustard flowers and green wheat fields look beautiful. Many birds fly from far-off cold countries to spend the winter in India's lakes.

Food and clothes

Winter is also called the season of good food. Vegetables like carrots, peas, cauliflower, spinach, fenugreek, radish and sweet potato, and fruits like oranges, guavas and amla are found in plenty. Gajar ka halwa, sarson ka saag with makki ki roti, jaggery, peanuts and til laddoos are winter favourites. Hot tea, soup and milk feel comforting. To stay warm we wear woollen sweaters, jackets, caps, gloves and socks.

Festivals of winter

Many festivals fall in this season: Christmas on 25 December, New Year on 1 January, then Lohri, Makar Sankranti, Pongal and Vasant Panchami. Republic Day on 26 January is also celebrated in winter. Schools close for winter holidays, when children travel or spend time with family.

Problems in winter

Winter is pleasant, but it can be hard for some people. Those without warm clothes or a proper house find cold nights very difficult. Dense fog causes road accidents and delays trains and flights. Colds and coughs also spread easily. We should help the needy by giving them old blankets and warm clothes.

Conclusion

Winter brings freshness, good health and delicious food. Exercising and studying feel easier in this season. If we protect ourselves from the cold and help others, winter can be a happy season for everyone. That is why winter is my favourite season.

Frequently asked questions

When is winter in India?

Usually from November to February. December and January are the coldest months.

Why are days short in winter?

In these months the northern half of the Earth is tilted slightly away from the sun, so the sun rises late, sets early and its rays fall at a slant.

What should I write in an essay on winter?

Introduction, when winter comes, winter mornings and nature, food and clothes, festivals, problems and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

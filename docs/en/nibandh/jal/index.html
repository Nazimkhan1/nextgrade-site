💧 Essay on Water — "Water is Life"

Life is not possible without water. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. Water is life — no living being can survive without it.
  2. We use water for drinking, bathing, cooking and cleaning.
  3. Plants and animals also depend on water to live.
  4. Three-fourths of the earth is covered with water, but very little is fit to drink.
  5. We get clean water from rivers, wells, ponds and rain.
  6. Drinking dirty water causes many diseases.
  7. We should not waste water by leaving taps running.
  8. Saving rainwater is called water conservation.
  9. "If there is water, there is a tomorrow" — saving water is everyone's duty.
  10. Every single drop is precious, so use water wisely.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

Water is life. No living being can survive without water. We drink water, bathe, cook food and clean with it. Plants and animals also need water. We get clean water from rivers, wells, ponds and rain. Drinking dirty water makes us ill, so we should always drink clean water. We should not waste water — we must turn off the tap while brushing or bathing. Save water, save life.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

"Water is life" is not just a saying but the truth. After air, water is the most important thing for living. A person can stay hungry for many days, but without water life becomes difficult in just a few days.

The importance of water

About two-thirds of our body is made of water. We use it for drinking, bathing, cooking, washing clothes and cleaning. Farming, factories and even making electricity need a lot of water. Plants, animals, birds and every living creature depend on water to survive.

Where water comes from

About three-fourths of the earth is covered with water, but most of it is the salty water of the sea, which is not fit to drink. Clean drinking water comes from rivers, lakes, wells, ponds, underground sources and rain, and it is very limited. That is why every drop is precious.

The water crisis

Today, because of a growing population, waste and pollution, the water crisis has deepened in many places. In summer, wells and ponds dry up and people have to bring water from far away. Rivers are being polluted by factories and dirt. Drinking dirty water spreads diseases like cholera and jaundice.

How to conserve water

We should make a habit of saving water. Turn off the tap while brushing and bathing, repair dripping taps and bathe using a bucket. Collecting rainwater on roofs and in pits (rainwater harvesting) is a very good step. Do not throw rubbish into rivers and ponds, and plant more trees, because trees increase rainfall and groundwater.

Our duty

Saving water is not the duty of one person but of us all. We should save water ourselves and also make others aware of it. If we are careless today, the coming generations will have to long for even drinking water.

Conclusion

Water is a precious gift of nature that we can neither make nor buy. "If there is water, there is a tomorrow." If we start saving every single drop of water from today, life on earth will always stay green and happy.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on "Water is Life"?

Introduction, the importance of water, where water comes from, the water crisis, ways to conserve water, our duty and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

What are easy ways to save water?

Turning off taps, repairing dripping taps, bathing with a bucket, harvesting rainwater and keeping rivers and ponds clean.

Why is drinking water scarce when the earth has so much water?

Because most of the earth's water is salty sea water, which is not fit to drink. Clean fresh water is very limited, so it must be saved.

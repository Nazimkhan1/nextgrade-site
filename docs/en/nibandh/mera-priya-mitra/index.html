🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Essay on My Best Friend

A true friend is life's greatest treasure. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. The name of my best friend is … .
  2. He studies with me in the same school and class.
  3. We study, play and go to and from home together.
  4. My friend is sharp in studies and very good by nature.
  5. He never tells a lie and helps everyone.
  6. When I am worried, he raises my spirits.
  7. We share our joys and troubles with each other.
  8. We two never have big fights.
  9. My parents also like my friend very much.
  10. I am proud to have such a true friend.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

The name of my best friend is … . He studies with me in the same class. We sit together, play together and go to and from home together. My friend is sharp in studies and very kind at heart. He never tells a lie and helps me in trouble. When I am sad, he makes me laugh. We share our things with each other and never fight. My parents also like him. I am very proud of my best friend.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

In this world, after our parents, the person closest to us is a true friend. It is said that finding a true friend is a matter of luck. I am fortunate to have a dear friend like … .

Who my friend is

The name of my best friend is … . He lives in my neighbourhood and studies with me in the same school and class. Our friendship is several years old. We go to school together, sit together and return home together after school.

His qualities

My friend is very sharp in studies, yet he is not proud of his cleverness. He speaks the truth, respects elders and helps the needy. His nature is cheerful and calm, so everyone likes him. He is also very good at games and sports.

Our friendship

We share our joys and troubles with each other. When I have any difficulty in studies, he explains it with love. When I am sad, he lifts my spirits. Sometimes we have small arguments, but we make up quickly, because our friendship is true.

The value of a true friend

A true friend supports us in both happiness and sorrow. He stops us from going on the wrong path and inspires us to do good work. A good friend teaches us more than books do. That is why it is said that "good company makes a person good."

Our duty

Keeping a friendship is also a responsibility. We should respect our friend's feelings, stand by him in difficulty and never cheat him. There is no selfishness in true friendship, only the wish for each other's good.

Conclusion

I am proud of my dear friend … . Every moment spent with him is my sweetest memory. I pray to God that our friendship stays like this all our life. Truly, a good friend is the most beautiful gift given by God.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on My Best Friend?

Introduction, who my friend is, his qualities, our friendship, the value of a true friend, our duty and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

What qualities should a true friend have?

Honesty, helpfulness, trust, patience and standing by you in both joy and sorrow — these are the qualities of a good friend.

How do I write my friend's name in the essay?

Wherever there is "…", write your real friend's name; keep the rest of the essay the same or change it a little to match your own experience.

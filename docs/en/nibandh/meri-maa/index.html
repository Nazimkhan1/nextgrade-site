👩 Essay on My Mother

"Mother" is the sweetest word in the world. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. Mother is the sweetest and the very first word in the world.
  2. My mother's name is … and she is the heart of our home.
  3. She wakes up first in the morning and sleeps last at night.
  4. She cooks our food, washes our clothes and looks after the house.
  5. When I am ill, she stays awake all night to care for me.
  6. She teaches me, tells me good things and shows me right from wrong.
  7. My mother never gets tired and asks for nothing in return.
  8. Her lap feels like the safest place in the world.
  9. A mother's love is true, selfless and without any condition.
  10. I love my mother very much and always listen to her.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

My mother is the best mother in the world. She takes care of the whole family. She wakes up early and makes breakfast and my tiffin, gets me ready and sends me to school. When I come back home, she welcomes me with a smile. My mother teaches me and gives me good habits — to speak the truth, respect elders and work hard. When I am sick, she sits beside me the whole time. She never thinks about herself and always lives for the family. I love my mother very much and want to make her proud.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

There is no one greater than a mother in this world. It is said that God could not be everywhere, so He made mothers. A mother's love flows like a spring that never stops and asks for nothing. For me, my mother is my first teacher, my best friend and my greatest strength.

A mother's day

My mother wakes up first in the house and sleeps last of all. She cooks the food, keeps the home clean, washes the clothes and looks after everyone's needs. She prepares my warm tiffin and sends me off to school with love. Even after so much work, there is no tiredness on her face, only a smile.

My first teacher

A mother is our very first teacher. The first word, the first step and the first lesson all come from her. She teaches us to speak the truth, to respect elders, to love those younger than us and to work hard. When I make a mistake, she does not scold me but lovingly explains what is right.

A mother's sacrifice

A mother places our happiness before her own. She keeps the best things for her children. When we are ill, she serves us and stays awake all night even while she herself is tired. She thinks less about herself and more about the family. Few others can make such a sacrifice.

The importance of a mother

A mother is the backbone of a family; the home feels incomplete without her. She cares not only for our body but also for our mind — when I am sad, her words lighten my heart. The values and lessons she gives stay with us all our life and make us good human beings.

Our duty

Since our mother does so much for us, it is our duty to respect her, listen to her and never hurt her. When we grow up, we must serve her just as she cared for us in our childhood. Hurting a mother's heart is the greatest wrong.

Conclusion

A mother is the most precious gift given by God. We can never repay the debt of her love. I pray to God that my mother always stays healthy and happy. I am proud of my mother and I love her the most in the whole world.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on My Mother?

Introduction, a mother's day, first teacher, her sacrifice, her importance, our duty and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

Why is a mother called the first teacher?

Because a child learns the very first lessons — the first word, walking, good habits and values — from the mother, even before going to school.

How do I write 10 lines on My Mother for small classes?

Use the 10 easy lines above — your mother's name, what she does, her love and sacrifice, and how much you love her.

🎄 Essay on Christmas

Christmas is a festival of love, kindness and sharing joy. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for older classes.

10 lines

  1. Christmas is celebrated every year on 25 December.
  2. It celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ.
  3. In India, Christmas is also called "Bada Din" (the big day).
  4. People decorate a Christmas tree at home and put a star on top.
  5. The night of 24 December is called Christmas Eve.
  6. Prayers are held in churches and carols are sung.
  7. Children wait for Santa Claus, who is said to bring gifts.
  8. People cut cakes and wish each other "Merry Christmas".
  9. Helping people in need is a good way to celebrate this day.
  10. Christmas teaches us love, kindness and forgiveness.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

Christmas is a lovely festival that comes every year on 25 December. On this day we celebrate the birthday of Jesus Christ. In India it is also called Bada Din. People decorate a Christmas tree with colourful lights, bells and a shining star on top. Prayers are held in churches and sweet carols are sung. Children love Santa Claus, who comes in red clothes with gifts. Everyone eats cake together and says "Merry Christmas" to each other. Christmas teaches us to love everyone and share our happiness. I like Christmas very much.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

India is a land of many religions and festivals, and every festival here is celebrated together. Christmas is the biggest festival of Christianity, but today people of every faith enjoy its cheer. It is celebrated every year on 25 December, and in India it is also called "Bada Din".

Why Christmas is celebrated

According to Christian belief, about two thousand years ago Jesus Christ was born to Mary and Joseph in the town of Bethlehem. It is said that he was born in a simple stable, and a bright star appeared in the sky that guided people to him. Jesus taught people love, kindness, service and forgiveness. Christmas is celebrated in memory of his birth.

Preparations and decorations

Preparations begin many days before Christmas. Homes, churches and markets are decorated with colourful lights, stars and bells. People bring home a Christmas tree and decorate it with toys, ribbons and shiny balls, with a star on top. Many homes also set up a crib — a small scene of the birth of Jesus.

The day of Christmas

On the night of 24 December, Christmas Eve, many churches hold a special midnight prayer. On 25 December people wear new clothes, go to church, sing carols and greet each other. Cakes, cookies and many dishes are made at home. For children, Santa Claus is the biggest attraction — the white-bearded man in red who is believed to give gifts.

Christmas in India

In India, Christmas is celebrated with special joy in Goa, Kerala, north-eastern states such as Nagaland, Mizoram and Meghalaya, and in cities like Kolkata and Mumbai. Schools hold programmes before Christmas in which children dress up as Santa and present plays and carols. 25 December is a holiday across the country.

The message of Christmas

Christmas teaches us that true happiness lies in making others happy. On this day many people visit orphanages, hospitals and poor neighbourhoods to share clothes, blankets and food. The story of Santa Claus also tells us to help others without expecting anything in return.

Conclusion

Christmas is a festival of love, brotherhood and service. It reminds us that all human beings are like one family. Like on Christmas, we should love, forgive and help people in need all through the year.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on Christmas?

Introduction, why Christmas is celebrated, preparations and decorations, the day of Christmas, Christmas in India, the message of Christmas and a conclusion. For small classes, the 10 lines above are enough.

Why is Christmas called Bada Din in India?

In India Christmas is lovingly called "Bada Din" (the big day) because it is the biggest festival of Christianity and is celebrated with joy all over the country.

What is Christmas Eve?

The evening and night of 24 December is called Christmas Eve. Many churches hold a midnight prayer that night to welcome Christmas.

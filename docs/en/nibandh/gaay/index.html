🐄 Essay on Cow

The cow is one of the first essays children learn to write. Below are 10 lines, a short essay and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. The cow is a gentle domestic animal.
  2. A cow has four legs, two horns, two ears, two eyes and a long tail.
  3. Cows can be white, black, brown or spotted.
  4. A cow eats grass, hay, fodder, oilcake and green leaves.
  5. The cow gives us milk, which is very nutritious.
  6. Curd, butter, ghee, paneer and sweets are made from milk.
  7. Cow dung is used as manure and as fuel cakes in villages.
  8. The young one of a cow is called a calf.
  9. In India many people respectfully call the cow "Gau Mata".
  10. We should take good care of cows and never let them eat plastic.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

The cow is a domestic animal. It is very calm and gentle. A cow has four legs, two horns, two ears and a long tail, which it uses to drive away flies. It eats grass, hay and green fodder. The cow gives us sweet and healthy milk. Children grow strong by drinking milk. Curd, butter, ghee and paneer are made from milk. Cow dung is used as manure in the fields. In Indian villages, many families keep a cow at home. We should love cows and look after them well.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

India is a land of farmers, and animals play a big role in their lives. Among them, the cow is considered the most useful and loved. For centuries the cow has been part of Indian families, which is why many people call it "Gau Mata" (mother cow) with respect.

Body of the cow

The cow is a four-legged animal with strong legs ending in hooves. It has two horns, two large ears and two shining eyes. Its long tail ends in a tuft of hair that it uses to drive away flies and mosquitoes. Cows come in many colours — white, black, brown, red or spotted.

Food and nature

The cow is a herbivore. It eats green grass, hay, fodder, oilcake, grains and vegetable peels. The cow is a ruminant: it swallows food quickly and later sits down to bring it back and chew it again, which is called chewing the cud. The cow is very calm by nature. It recognises the person who looks after it and comes when called.

Usefulness of the cow

Cow's milk is very nutritious. It contains calcium and protein, which make our bones and body strong, and doctors advise children to drink it. Curd, buttermilk, butter, ghee, paneer, kheer and many sweets are made from milk. Cow dung is good organic manure for the fields, and in villages it is also used for fuel cakes and biogas. Male calves grow into bullocks that help farmers plough fields and pull carts.

Indian breeds of cow

India has many native breeds of cow, such as Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar, Red Sindhi and Rathi. The Gir comes from Gujarat and the Sahiwal from Punjab. These breeds are known for bearing heat well and giving good milk.

Our duty

Today many cows wander on city roads and eat plastic from garbage, which makes them sick. We should never throw food in plastic bags. Cows need clean water, good fodder, a shady place and timely treatment when ill. Being kind to animals is the duty of all of us.

Conclusion

The cow is truly a friend of human beings. It takes very little from us and gives a lot in return. We must look after cows and all animals; only then will our environment and society stay healthy.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the cow called a ruminant?

Because it swallows its food quickly and later brings it back to its mouth to chew again while resting. This is called chewing the cud.

Which are famous Indian cow breeds?

Sahiwal, Gir, Tharparkar, Red Sindhi and Rathi are well-known native breeds of India.

What should I write in an essay on the cow?

Introduction, body of the cow, food and nature, usefulness, breeds, our duty and conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

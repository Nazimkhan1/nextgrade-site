🧘 Essay on Yoga

Yoga is a treasure of good health. Below are 10 lines and a short essay for small children, and a long essay with headings for higher classes.

10 lines

  1. Yoga keeps both our body and mind healthy.
  2. It is a gift of India, thousands of years old.
  3. Yoga includes asanas, pranayama and meditation.
  4. Doing yoga early in the morning on an empty stomach is best.
  5. Surya Namaskar is one of the best and most complete practices.
  6. Pranayama, the breathing exercises, calm the mind.
  7. Yoga makes the body flexible and keeps diseases away.
  8. International Yoga Day is celebrated every year on 21 June.
  9. Yoga helps us focus in studies and reduces stress.
  10. We should all do a little yoga every day.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

Yoga is very good for our health. It is a very old gift of India. Yoga has different asanas, breathing exercises (pranayama) and meditation. Doing yoga every morning keeps the body strong and the mind calm. Surya Namaskar is a very good practice. Yoga keeps diseases away and helps us focus on studies. International Yoga Day is celebrated every year on 21 June. We should all do a little yoga every day.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

In today's busy life, staying healthy has become a challenge. Yoga is a treasure that keeps us healthy in body and mind without any medicine or cost. Yoga is a gift of India, thousands of years old, and today the whole world is adopting it.

What is yoga

Yoga means union — the joining of body, mind and breath. It mainly has three parts: asanas (body exercises), pranayama (breathing exercises) and meditation (focusing the mind). Together these make a person completely healthy.

Benefits of yoga

Regular yoga makes the body flexible and strong, improves digestion and builds the power to fight disease. Pranayama calms the mind and reduces anger and stress. Meditation improves concentration, which helps students focus on studies and sharpens memory.

When and how to do yoga

The morning is considered the best time for yoga, when the air is clean and the mind is fresh. It should always be done on an empty stomach, in an open and quiet place. Begin with easy asanas and Surya Namaskar, and learn under a knowledgeable person or teacher so that you do not get hurt.

International Yoga Day

Seeing the importance of yoga, the United Nations declared 21 June as "International Yoga Day". It was first celebrated in 2015. On this day millions of people around the world do yoga together, spreading this gift of India across the globe.

Conclusion

Yoga is not just an exercise but a good way of living. It gives us a healthy body, a calm mind and good habits. If we give a little time to yoga every day, we can stay healthy and happy all our life. So let us make yoga a part of our daily routine.

Frequently asked questions

What should I write in an essay on Yoga?

Introduction, what yoga is, benefits of yoga, when and how to do it, International Yoga Day and a conclusion — the headings used in the long essay above.

When is International Yoga Day celebrated?

Every year on 21 June; it was first celebrated in 2015.

What are the main parts of yoga?

Asanas (body exercises), pranayama (breathing exercises) and meditation are the three main parts of yoga.

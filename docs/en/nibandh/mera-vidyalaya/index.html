🏫 Essay on My School

"My School" is asked in almost every class. Below are 10 lines, a short essay and a long essay with headings — add your school's name and real details to make it your own.

10 lines

  1. The name of my school is ______. (write your school's name here)
  2. My school is near my house and it is very beautiful.
  3. It has a big building with many bright, airy classrooms.
  4. There is a library, a science lab and a computer room.
  5. My school has a large playground for games and sports.
  6. Every day begins with the morning assembly.
  7. Our teachers teach us with love and hard work.
  8. Independence Day, Republic Day and the Annual Day are celebrated with joy.
  9. My school teaches us discipline, cleanliness and teamwork.
  10. I am proud of my school and I love it very much.

Short essay (Classes 1 to 4)

The name of my school is ______. It is a short distance from my home. My school is big, clean and beautiful. It has many classrooms, a library and a big playground. Every morning we say our prayer in the assembly and then go to our classes. My teachers are very kind. They teach us lessons and also good habits. During the lunch break I eat with my friends and we play together. Every year our school holds a sports day and an annual function. I love my school very much.

Long essay (Classes 5 to 10)

Introduction

After our home, the place that teaches us the most is our school. A school is called a temple of learning, because here we do not only read books — we also learn to become good human beings. The name of my school is ______, and I am proud to be its student.

The school building

My school stands in an open, peaceful place. The building has two or three floors and is surrounded by green trees and flower beds. The classrooms are large, bright and airy, with boards, fans and comfortable desks. There is clean drinking water and separate toilets for boys and girls.

Library, lab and computer room

The library is my favourite place. It has hundreds of books on stories, science, history and general knowledge, along with newspapers. In the science lab we learn by doing experiments ourselves. In the computer room we learn typing, safe use of the internet and simple programming.

Our teachers

The real strength of a school is its teachers. Our principal is strict about discipline but very caring. Our teachers explain every subject with easy examples and pay extra attention to students who need help. They also teach us to speak the truth, respect elders and work hard.

Sports and other activities

My school gives equal importance to studies and sports. On the big ground we play cricket, football, kho-kho and kabaddi, and there is a sports day every year. There are also competitions in speech, poetry recitation, drawing, essay writing and a science exhibition. Independence Day, Republic Day, Teachers' Day and Children's Day are celebrated with colourful programmes.

Discipline

Discipline is taken seriously in my school. All students come on time in a clean uniform. In the assembly we stand in lines and sing the National Anthem. We keep our classrooms quiet and our school clean. These habits help us throughout life.

Conclusion

My school is like a second home to me. Here I have found good teachers, true friends and a lot of knowledge. I wish to study hard, become a good citizen and make my school proud.

Frequently asked questions

What headings should I use in an essay on My School?

Introduction, the school building, library and lab, teachers, sports and activities, discipline, and conclusion.

Why is a school called a temple of learning?

Because along with lessons, it gives us good values, discipline and the habit of living together — it fills the mind with knowledge the way a temple fills it with peace.

Can I change this essay for my own school?

Yes. Write your school's name in place of ______ and add real details about your building, games and activities — it will make the essay even better.

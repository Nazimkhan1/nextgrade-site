🙏 Hanuman Chalisa — lyrics with meaning

The Hanuman Chalisa by Goswami Tulsidas — two opening dohas, 40 chaupais and a closing doha. Each verse is given in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning.

Doha
श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज, निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु, जो दायकु फल चारि॥

Shri Guru charan saroj raj, nij manu mukuru sudhaari.
Baranau Raghubar bimal jasu, jo daayaku phal chaari.

Cleaning the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru’s lotus feet, I describe the pure glory of Shri Ram, which gives the four fruits of life (dharma, wealth, desire and liberation).

Doha
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।
बल बुद्धि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार॥

Buddhiheen tanu jaanike, sumirau Pavan-kumaar.
Bal buddhi bidya dehu mohi, harahu kalesh bikaar.

Knowing myself to be lacking in wisdom, I remember Hanuman, son of the Wind. Give me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and take away my troubles and faults.

Chaupai 1
जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर। जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥

Jai Hanuman gyaan gun saagar, jai Kapees tihun lok ujaagar.

Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue. Victory to the lord of the monkeys, whose glory lights up the three worlds.

Chaupai 2
राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा। अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥

Ram doot atulit bal dhaama, Anjani-putra Pavansut naama.

You are Ram’s messenger, a home of matchless strength, known as the son of Anjani and son of the Wind.

Chaupai 3
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी। कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥

Mahabir bikram Bajrangi, kumati nivaar sumati ke sangi.

Great hero, mighty Bajrangi with limbs like a thunderbolt — you remove bad thinking and stand by those with good sense.

Chaupai 4
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा। कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥

Kanchan baran biraaj subesa, kaanan kundal kunchit kesa.

Your colour is golden and you are beautifully dressed, with earrings in your ears and curly hair.

Chaupai 5
हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै। काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥

Haath bajra au dhvaja biraajai, kaandhe moonj janeu saajai.

A thunderbolt (mace) and a flag shine in your hands, and a sacred thread of munj grass adorns your shoulder.

Chaupai 6
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन। तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन॥

Sankar suvan Kesari-nandan, tej prataap maha jag bandan.

You are a form of Shiva and the son of Kesari; the whole world honours your radiance and might.

Chaupai 7
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर। राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥

Bidyaavaan guni ati chaatur, Ram kaaj karibe ko aatur.

Learned, virtuous and very wise, you are always eager to do Ram’s work.

Chaupai 8
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया। राम लखन सीता मन बसिया॥

Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya, Ram Lakhan Sita man basiya.

You love to hear the story of the Lord; Ram, Lakshman and Sita live in your heart.

Chaupai 9
सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा। बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥

Sookshma roop dhari Siyahin dikhaava, bikat roop dhari Lank jaraava.

Taking a tiny form you appeared before Sita, and taking a fearsome form you burned Lanka.

Chaupai 10
भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे। रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे॥

Bheem roop dhari asur sanhaare, Ramchandra ke kaaj sanvaare.

In a mighty form you destroyed the demons and completed Ramchandra’s tasks.

Chaupai 11
लाय सजीवन लखन जियाये। श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये॥

Laaye Sajeevan Lakhan jiyaaye, Shri Raghubeer harashi ur laaye.

You brought the Sanjeevani herb and revived Lakshman; overjoyed, Shri Ram held you to his heart.

Chaupai 12
रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई। तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई॥

Raghupati keenhi bahut badaai, tum mam priya Bharatahi sam bhaai.

Ram praised you greatly and said, “You are as dear to me as my brother Bharat.”

Chaupai 13
सहस बदन तुम्हरो जस गावैं। अस कहि श्रीपति कंठ लगावैं॥

Sahas badan tumharo jas gaavain, as kahi Shripati kanth lagaavain.

“May the thousand-mouthed serpent sing your glory,” said Ram, and embraced you.

Chaupai 14
सनकादिक ब्रह्मादि मुनीसा। नारद सारद सहित अहीसा॥

Sanakaadik Brahmaadi muneesa, Naarad Saarad sahit Aheesa.

Sanaka and the sages, Brahma and the gods, the great seers, Narad, Saraswati and the serpent king —

Chaupai 15
जम कुबेर दिगपाल जहाँ ते। कबि कोबिद कहि सके कहाँ ते॥

Jam Kuber Digpaal jahaan te, kabi kobid kahi sake kahaan te.

Yama, Kubera and the guardians of the directions — no poet or scholar can fully describe your glory.

Chaupai 16
तुम उपकार सुग्रीवहिं कीन्हा। राम मिलाय राज पद दीन्हा॥

Tum upkaar Sugreevahin keenha, Ram milaaye raaj pad deenha.

You did Sugriva a great favour: you brought him to Ram and he regained his kingdom.

Chaupai 17
तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना। लंकेस्वर भए सब जग जाना॥

Tumharo mantra Bibheeshan maana, Lankeshwar bhaye sab jag jaana.

Vibhishan followed your advice and became king of Lanka — the whole world knows it.

Chaupai 18
जुग सहस्र जोजन पर भानू। लील्यो ताहि मधुर फल जानू॥

Jug sahasra jojan par bhaanu, leelyo taahi madhur phal jaanu.

The sun, thousands of yojanas away, you (as a child) swallowed, thinking it a sweet fruit.

Chaupai 19
प्रभु मुद्रिका मेलि मुख माहीं। जलधि लाँघि गये अचरज नाहीं॥

Prabhu mudrika meli mukh maahin, jaladhi laanghi gaye achraj naahin.

Holding the Lord’s ring in your mouth you leapt across the ocean — no wonder at all for you.

Chaupai 20
दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते। सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते॥

Durgam kaaj jagat ke jete, sugam anugrah tumhare tete.

Every difficult task in the world becomes easy by your grace.

Chaupai 21
राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे। होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे॥

Ram duaare tum rakhvaare, hot na aagya binu paisaare.

You guard Ram’s door; no one enters without your permission.

Chaupai 22
सब सुख लहै तुम्हारी सरना। तुम रच्छक काहू को डर ना॥

Sab sukh lahai tumhaari sarna, tum rakshak kaahu ko dar na.

Whoever takes refuge in you finds every comfort; with you as protector, there is nothing to fear.

Chaupai 23
आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै। तीनों लोक हाँक तें काँपै॥

Aapan tej samhaaro aapai, teenon lok haank te kaanpai.

Only you can hold your own power; the three worlds tremble at your roar.

Chaupai 24
भूत पिसाच निकट नहिं आवै। महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै॥

Bhoot pisaach nikat nahin aavai, Mahabir jab naam sunaavai.

Where the name of Mahavir is spoken, ghosts and evil spirits do not come near.

Chaupai 25
नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा। जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा॥

Naasai rog harai sab peera, japat nirantar Hanumat beera.

Chanting the name of brave Hanuman again and again removes illness and every pain — so devotees believe.

Chaupai 26
संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै। मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै॥

Sankat te Hanuman chhudaavai, man kram bachan dhyaan jo laavai.

Hanuman frees from trouble whoever remembers him in thought, deed and word.

Chaupai 27
सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा। तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा॥

Sab par Ram tapasvi raaja, tin ke kaaj sakal tum saaja.

Ram, the ascetic king, is above all, and you completed all his works.

Chaupai 28
और मनोरथ जो कोई लावै। सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै॥

Aur manorath jo koi laavai, soi amit jeevan phal paavai.

Whoever comes to you with any wish receives the boundless fruit of life.

Chaupai 29
चारों जुग परताप तुम्हारा। है परसिद्ध जगत उजियारा॥

Chaaron jug partaap tumhaara, hai parsiddh jagat ujiyaara.

Your glory spans all four ages; your fame is known and lights up the whole world.

Chaupai 30
साधु संत के तुम रखवारे। असुर निकंदन राम दुलारे॥

Saadhu sant ke tum rakhvaare, asur nikandan Ram dulaare.

You protect saints and good people, destroy demons and are dear to Ram.

Chaupai 31
अष्ट सिद्धि नौ निधि के दाता। अस बर दीन जानकी माता॥

Ashta siddhi nau nidhi ke daata, as bar deen Jaanki maata.

Mother Sita blessed you to be the giver of the eight powers and nine treasures.

Chaupai 32
राम रसायन तुम्हरे पासा। सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा॥

Ram rasaayan tumhare paasa, sada raho Raghupati ke daasa.

You hold the nectar of devotion to Ram; may you always remain his servant.

Chaupai 33
तुम्हरे भजन राम को पावै। जनम जनम के दुख बिसरावै॥

Tumhare bhajan Ram ko paavai, janam janam ke dukh bisraavai.

Through devotion to you one reaches Ram and forgets the sorrows of many lives.

Chaupai 34
अंत काल रघुबर पुर जाई। जहाँ जन्म हरि-भक्त कहाई॥

Ant kaal Raghubar pur jaai, jahaan janma Hari-bhakt kahaai.

At the end the devotee goes to Ram’s abode, and wherever born again is known as a devotee of Hari.

Chaupai 35
और देवता चित्त न धरई। हनुमत सेइ सर्ब सुख करई॥

Aur devta chitt na dharai, Hanumat sei sarb sukh karai.

Even without keeping other gods in mind, serving Hanuman brings every happiness.

Chaupai 36
संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा। जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥

Sankat katai mitai sab peera, jo sumirai Hanumat balbeera.

Whoever remembers the mighty hero Hanuman sees troubles cut away and all pain wiped out.

Chaupai 37
जय जय जय हनुमान गोसाईं। कृपा करहु गुरुदेव की नाईं॥

Jai jai jai Hanuman Gosaain, kripa karahu Gurudev ki naain.

Victory, victory, victory to Lord Hanuman! Be gracious to me as a Guru is.

Chaupai 38
जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई। छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥

Jo sat baar paath kar koi, chhootahi bandi maha sukh hoi.

Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bonds and finds great joy.

Chaupai 39
जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा। होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा॥

Jo yah padhai Hanuman Chalisa, hoy siddhi saakhi Gaurisa.

Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains success — Lord Shiva himself is witness.

Chaupai 40
तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा। कीजै नाथ हृदय महँ डेरा॥

Tulsidas sada Hari chera, keejai naath hriday mahan dera.

Tulsidas is forever the Lord’s servant; O Lord, make my heart your home.

Doha
पवन तनय संकट हरन, मंगल मूरति रूप।
राम लखन सीता सहित, हृदय बसहु सुर भूप॥

Pavan tanay sankat haran, mangal moorati roop.
Ram Lakhan Sita sahit, hriday basahu sur bhoop.

O son of the Wind, remover of troubles, embodiment of all that is auspicious, king among the gods — dwell in my heart with Ram, Lakshman and Sita.

About the Hanuman Chalisa

The Hanuman Chalisa was written in Awadhi by Goswami Tulsidas in the 16th century. “Chalisa” means forty — it has forty chaupais (verses) praising Hanuman’s strength, wisdom, devotion and his service to Shri Ram.

Spellings of a few words differ slightly between editions — this is the common Gita Press reading. The meanings are our own plain-language summaries.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote the Hanuman Chalisa?

Goswami Tulsidas, who also wrote the Ramcharitmanas. It is in the Awadhi language.

How many verses are in the Hanuman Chalisa?

40 chaupais — hence the name “Chalisa” — with two dohas at the start and one at the end.

When should I read the Hanuman Chalisa?

Devotees read it every morning or evening, especially on Tuesdays and Saturdays, in a calm, clean place.

How long does it take to read?

About 7–10 minutes at a relaxed pace.

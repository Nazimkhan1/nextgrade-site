🎄 Christmas speech for students

Easy speeches for your school Christmas programme — short and medium — with 10 lines and speaking tips. Tap "Listen" to practise, or print it.

All about the day

How to begin your speech

Respected Principal, honourable teachers and my dear friends, a very Merry Christmas to all of you!

I thank you all for giving me the chance to say a few words on this joyful occasion of Christmas.

Short speech (1 minute) — Classes 1 to 4

Respected Principal, teachers and my dear friends, Merry Christmas to all of you!

Christmas comes every year on 25 December. On this day we celebrate the birthday of Jesus Christ. We decorate a Christmas tree with lights and a shining star.

Santa Claus comes in red clothes with gifts for children. We eat cake and sing carols.

Christmas teaches us to love everyone and share what we have with others. Thank you! Merry Christmas!

Medium speech (2–3 minutes) — Classes 5 to 12

Respected Principal, honourable teachers and my dear friends, warm Christmas greetings to all of you.

Every year on 25 December, Christmas is celebrated all over the world. In India it is lovingly called "Bada Din". According to Christian belief, Jesus Christ was born on this day to Mother Mary in Bethlehem. He was born in a simple stable, but his message reached the whole world — a message of love, kindness, service and forgiveness.

Before Christmas, homes and churches are decorated with lights, stars and bells. On the night of 24 December, Christmas Eve, prayers are held in churches, and on 25 December people greet each other, share cakes and sing carols. Santa Claus, whom children love so much, reminds us that giving happiness without asking for anything is the greatest gift.

Friends, our India is a beautiful garden of many faiths. Just as we celebrate Diwali, Eid and Gurpurab together, Christmas is a festival for all of us. This Christmas, let us help someone in need — give someone a warm blanket, bring a smile to someone's face. That is the true joy of Christmas.

With these words I end my speech. Thank you, and Merry Christmas!

Essay: Christmas (about 150 words)

Christmas is the biggest festival of Christianity, celebrated every year on 25 December. On this day we celebrate the birth of Jesus Christ, who gave the world a message of love and service.

Before the festival, people decorate their homes and put up a Christmas tree with a star, bells and lights. On Christmas Eve, prayers are held in churches. On 25 December people cut cakes, sing carols and give gifts. Santa Claus is the favourite guest of children.

In India, Christmas is celebrated with special joy in Goa, Kerala, the north-east and big cities. The festival teaches us that happiness grows when it is shared, and that we should always help others.

10 lines on Christmas

  1. The festival of Christmas comes every year on 25 December.
  2. It is celebrated to mark the birth of Jesus Christ.
  3. Jesus Christ was born in Bethlehem.
  4. His mother's name was Mary.
  5. The Christmas tree is decorated with colourful lights and a star.
  6. Christmas songs are called carols.
  7. Santa Claus is also called Father Christmas.
  8. On this day people go to church to pray.
  9. Cakes and cookies are special Christmas treats.
  10. Christmas gives a message of love, peace and brotherhood.

Closing lines

  • Share joy, spread love — that is Christmas!
  • Where there is kindness in the heart, every day is Christmas.
  • Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year!

Easy tips for giving the speech

  • Smile and say "Merry Christmas!" at the start and the end.
  • You can wear a Santa cap or hold a small star while speaking — children love it.
  • Remember the dates: Christmas Eve is 24 December, Christmas is 25 December.
  • Speak slowly and clearly; small children can say just the short speech or the 10 lines.

Frequently asked questions

When is Christmas celebrated?

Every year on 25 December. The evening of 24 December is Christmas Eve.

How long should a Christmas speech be?

A 1-minute short speech for Classes 1–4 and a 2–3 minute medium speech for Classes 5–12 work well.

Why is Christmas called Bada Din in India?

In India Christmas is lovingly called "Bada Din" (the big day) because it is the biggest festival of Christianity.

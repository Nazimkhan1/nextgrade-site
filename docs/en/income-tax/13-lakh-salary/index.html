Income tax on a ₹13 lakh salary (FY 2026-27)

On a yearly salary of ₹13 lakh, total tax under the new regime is about ₹26,000 — roughly ₹2,167 a month.

Which regime should you choose

If your deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, home-loan interest and so on) add up to more than about ₹6,88,000, the old regime is cheaper; below that, choose the new regime.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax on a ₹13 lakh salary in the new regime?

About ₹26,000 for FY 2026-27 including cess, or ₹2,167 a month. After the ₹75,000 standard deduction, taxable income is ₹12,25,000.

How much tax on a ₹13 lakh salary in the old regime?

₹1,95,000 with only the ₹50,000 standard deduction; ₹1,40,400 if you also claim ₹1.5 lakh under 80C and ₹25,000 under 80D.

Are these figures exact?

They are estimates under FY 2026-27 rules (checked 27 Nov 2026), not tax advice. Other income such as interest or capital gains changes the tax.

📝 UP Board Exams 2027 — time table and countdown

UP Board High School and Intermediate exams run from Friday 5 February 2027 to Wednesday 24 February 2027, in two shifts a day — morning 8:30–11:45 and afternoon 2:00–5:15. The first day has Class 10 Hindi (morning) and Class 12 Hindi (afternoon).

Key facts

  • The Board announced the full programme in a notice dated 12 October 2026 — High School and Intermediate exams run together.
  • Morning shift 8:30–11:45, afternoon 2:00–5:15: a 3 hour 15 minute slot per paper. Reach the centre at least half an hour early.
  • Almost all main Class 10 papers are in the morning; most main Class 12 papers (English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, History, Geography…) are in the afternoon.
  • Class 12 Biology and Mathematics share the same slot (12 February, afternoon) — students take one of them.
  • Get practical exam dates and admit cards from your school and upmsp.edu.in; if anything changes, the Board’s notice is final.

The last few weeks

  • Class 10: the big days are 13 Feb (Maths) and 17 Feb (Science), three days apart — plan revision around that gap.
  • Class 12 science: only one day separates Chemistry (15 Feb) and Physics (17 Feb), so lock in the formulas of both early.
  • NCERT books (used by UP Board) and the last five years’ board papers help the most.
  • For the 8:30 am shift, start waking early from January — winter mornings in February are cold.
  • Pack your admit card, pens, pencils, geometry box and water bottle the night before.

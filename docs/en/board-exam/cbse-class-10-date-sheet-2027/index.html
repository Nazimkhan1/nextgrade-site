📅 CBSE Class 10 Date Sheet 2027

CBSE Class 10 board exams run from Wednesday, 17 February to Tuesday, 9 March — 12 papers in the main subjects. Every paper starts at 10:30 am.

Key facts

  • Theory papers start at 10:30 am; most are 3 hours (10:30–1:30), some skill subjects 2 hours.
  • Class 10: 16 February to 9 March 2027; Class 12: 16 February to 9 April 2027.
  • Practicals: 1 January to 13 February 2027 in most schools; 9 November to 19 December 2026 in winter-bound schools.
  • Holi falls on 22 March 2027, when some smaller Class 12 papers are scheduled — watch for any CBSE notice.

Board exam preparation tips

  • Solve the last 5 years’ papers and CBSE sample papers — you learn the pattern of questions.
  • Make one-page quick notes for each subject — read only these on the last day.
  • Sleep 7–8 hours; don’t stay up late the night before a paper.
  • Use the first 15 minutes to read the paper; answer the questions you know best first.
  • Pack your admit card, pens, pencils, geometry box and a transparent water bottle the night before.

Easy riddles for kids

What has hands but cannot clap?

Answer: A clock

What gets wetter the more it dries?

Answer: A towel

What has many teeth but cannot bite?

Answer: A comb

What has a neck but no head?

Answer: A bottle

What has one eye but cannot see?

Answer: A needle

I am tall when I am young and short when I am old. What am I?

Answer: A candle

Green outside, yellow-orange inside, full of little black seeds. What is it?

Answer: A papaya

A cave with thirty-two guards. What is it?

Answer: Your mouth and teeth

What can you catch but never throw?

Answer: A cold

What has keys but cannot open a single lock?

Answer: A piano (or a keyboard)

Funny riddles

What belongs to you, but other people use it more than you do?

Answer: Your name

What breaks as soon as you say its name?

Answer: Silence

What runs but never walks, and has a mouth but never talks?

Answer: A river

The more you take, the more you leave behind. What are they?

Answer: Footsteps

What goes up but never comes down?

Answer: Your age

Brain and maths riddles

Which month has 28 days?

Answer: All of them — every month has at least 28 days.

Which is heavier: a kilo of cotton or a kilo of iron?

Answer: Neither — both weigh one kilo.

A basket has 6 mangoes. Six children each get one mango, yet one mango is still in the basket. How?

Answer: The last child was given the mango with the basket.

If 3 cats catch 3 mice in 3 minutes, how long do 100 cats take to catch 100 mice?

Answer: 3 minutes — each cat catches one mouse in 3 minutes.

Which number becomes nothing when you cut it in half?

Answer: 8 — cut it across the middle and you get two zeros.

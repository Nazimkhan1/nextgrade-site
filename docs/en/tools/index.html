🧰 Free Tools

Small tools for forms, printing and everyday jobs. They all run inside your phone's browser — photos and documents are never uploaded to any server.

🪪

Passport Size Photo Maker

White or blue background, face centred automatically, 3.5×4.5 cm / 2×2 inch, a 4×6 print sheet and a digital photo under any KB limit.

 📄

Document Scanner → PDF

Scan documents with your phone: auto crop, black & white, many pages in one PDF, and under 100/200 KB.

 🗜️

Image Compressor — 20/50/100 KB

Make a photo 20, 50, 100, 200 KB or any size. JPG or WebP, many photos at once, ZIP download.

 📉

PDF Compressor — 100/200/500 KB

Make a PDF smaller than 100, 200, 500 KB or 1 MB for form uploads. Black & white option for scans.

 🗂️

JPG / Photo to PDF

Many photos (marksheet, ID, certificates) into one PDF — A4, reorder, rotate, and a KB limit.

 📎

PDF Merge & Split

Join PDFs into one, take out or delete pages, rotate pages — text and quality stay the same.

 📐

Image Resizer — px / cm / inch

Resize a photo to an exact size: 3.5×4.5 cm, 200×230, 600×600 or your own — with crop, background and a KB limit.

 ✂️

Image Crop & Rotate

Crop a photo (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, passport), rotate, straighten and flip — save as JPG, PNG or WebP.

 ✍️

Signature Maker

Sign on the screen or clean up a photo of a signature on paper — transparent PNG or white JPG, at form size and KB.

 ⌨️

Hindi & English Typing Test

1 to 15 minute typing test: WPM, accuracy and wrong words. Practise for SSC-style tests, backspace on/off.

 🌾

Land Unit Converter — Bigha, Katha, Acre

Bigha, katha, biswa, kanal, acre, hectare, gaj and sq ft — by state.

 🔳

QR Code Maker — UPI, Link, WhatsApp

UPI payment QR (with amount), link, text, phone, WhatsApp and Wi-Fi QR codes — download PNG or SVG.

 😄

Sticker Maker

Turn a photo into a sticker: background removed, white outline, top/bottom text — 512×512 WebP. Text stickers too.

 📝

Exam Form Photo & Signature Resizer

Photo, signature, thumb and declaration at the exact px and KB that SSC, UPSC, IBPS / SBI, NEET and JEE forms ask for. Cleans up a signature on paper.

 🔤

Krutidev to Unicode Converter (and back)

Paste old Kruti Dev 010 text and get proper Unicode Hindi for Word, email, WhatsApp and websites — or convert Unicode to Krutidev. Nothing is uploaded.

Your files stay with you

These tools run entirely inside your phone/browser (some download their small library once). Your photos and documents (Aadhaar, marksheets, certificates) are not sent to any server, not saved anywhere, and we cannot see them. Closing the page clears everything. After the first visit most tools work offline too.

Who they are for

Students filling online forms (photo size and KB), PDFs of documents for jobs or admissions, clean product photos for a shop, or just trying a new look. Every tool is free, with no login and no watermark.

Note: every form or office has its own rules (size, background, KB) — always read them before you send.

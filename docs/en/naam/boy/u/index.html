About boy names starting with U

In Hindi, the English letter U can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: उ (उचित, उदय, उद्भव); ऊ (ऊर्जित). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Uddhav (Friend of Lord Krishna), Umakant (Husband of Uma; Lord Shiva), Umashankar (Uma and Shankar; Lord Shiva), Umesh (Lord of Uma; Lord Shiva), Upendra (Lord Vishnu)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Uday (Rising, dawn), Urvish (Lord of the earth), Utpal (Blue lotus)
  • Virtues and feelings: Uchit (Right, proper), Udbhav (Origin, creation), Udit (Risen, awakened), Ugrasen (King of Mathura), Ujjwal (Bright), Ulhas (Joy, delight), Umaid (Hope), Umang (Enthusiasm), Unmesh (Revelation, blossoming), Upamanyu (Devoted disciple; name of a sage), Urjit (Energetic, powerful), Utkarsh (Progress, prosperity), Utsav (Festival, celebration), Uttam (The best)

Short names that are easy to call out: Uday, Udit. Longer, traditional names: Umashankar, Upamanyu — they also give an easy pet name.

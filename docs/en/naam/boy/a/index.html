About boy names starting with A

In Hindi, the English letter A can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: अ (अभिनव, अभिषेक, अद्विक); आ (आरव, आदित्य, आकाश); अं (अंकित, अंश). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Anirudh (Unstoppable; grandson of Lord Krishna), Ayaan (God's gift)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Aditya (The sun), Agastya (A revered sage; mountain), Akash (Sky), Alok (Light, brightness), Arnav (Ocean, sea)
  • Virtues and feelings: Aarav (Peaceful, wise), Abhinav (New, novel), Abhishek (Sacred bath, consecration), Advik (Unique, matchless), Akshay (Imperishable), Aman (Peace), Amar (Immortal), Amit (Infinite, boundless), Anand (Joy, bliss), Ankit (Marked, inscribed), Ansh (A part, portion), Arjun (Bright, shining), Aryan (Noble, of high honour), Atharv (Knowledge (the Atharva Veda)), Ayush (Long life)

Short names that are easy to call out: Alok, Aman, Amar, Amit, Ansh. Longer, traditional names: Abhishek — they also give an easy pet name.

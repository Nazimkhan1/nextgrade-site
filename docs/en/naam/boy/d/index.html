About boy names starting with D

In Hindi, the English letter D can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: दे (देव, देवांश, देवदत्त); ध (धनंजय, धनुष, धर्मेंद्र); दि (दिग्विजय, दिलीप, दिनेश); द (दक्ष, दर्श, दयानंद); दा (दामोदर); दी (दीपक); धै (धैर्य); धी (धीरज); ध् (ध्रुव); दु (दुष्यंत). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Damodar (Lord Krishna), Dev (God, divine), Devansh (Part of God), Devdutt (Gift of God), Devendra (King of gods, Lord Indra), Devraj (King of gods, Lord Indra), Dilip (King of the Raghu clan, ancestor of Lord Ram)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Dhruv (Constant, the pole star), Dinesh (Lord of the day, the sun)
  • Virtues and feelings: Daksh (Skilful, capable), Darsh (Sight, vision), Dayanand (One who delights in compassion), Deepak (Lamp), Dhairya (Patience), Dhananjay (Winner of wealth; Arjun), Dhanush (Bow), Dharmendra (Lord of righteousness), Dhaval (White, pure), Dhiraj (Patience), Digvijay (Conqueror of all directions), Divij (Born of the heavens), Dushyant (Legendary king, father of Bharat)

Short names that are easy to call out: Dev. Longer, traditional names: Dayanand, Devendra, Dhananjay, Dharmendra, Digvijay, Dushyant — they also give an easy pet name.

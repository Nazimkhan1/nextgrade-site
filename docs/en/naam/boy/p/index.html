About boy names starting with P

In Hindi, the English letter P can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: प् (प्रभात, प्रदीप, प्रकाश); पु (पुलकित, पुनीत, पुरुषोत्तम); प (पराग, पवन); पा (पारस, पार्थ); पं (पंकज); पी (पीयूष); पृ (पृथ्वीराज). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Purushottam (Supreme being; Lord Vishnu)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Pankaj (Lotus), Pawan (Wind, breeze), Piyush (Nectar), Prabhat (Morning, dawn), Pradeep (Lamp, light), Prakash (Light), Prithviraj (King of the earth), Pushkar (Lotus; a holy lake)
  • Virtues and feelings: Parag (Pollen), Paras (Touchstone that turns iron to gold), Parth (Arjun (the warrior)), Pranav (The sacred syllable Om), Pranay (Love), Prashant (Calm, tranquil), Pratap (Glory, valour), Pratham (First, foremost), Pratik (Symbol), Prem (Love), Pritam (Beloved), Pulkit (Thrilled, happy), Puneet (Pure, holy)

Short names that are easy to call out: Prem. Longer, traditional names: Prashant, Prithviraj, Purushottam — they also give an easy pet name.

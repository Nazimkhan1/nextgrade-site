About boy names starting with K

In Hindi, the English letter K can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: क (कबीर, कमल, कमलेश); कु (कुबेर, कुलदीप, कुणाल); कौ (कौशल, कौशिक, कौस्तुभ); के (केदार, केशव, केतन); कि (कियान, किशोर); कै (कैलाश); का (कार्तिक); कृ (कृष्ण); क् (क्षितिज). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Kailash (Abode of Lord Shiva), Kamlesh (Lord of the lotus; Lord Vishnu), Kartik (Son of Shiva; a month), Kaustubh (Gem of Lord Vishnu), Kedar (Lord Shiva), Keshav (Lord Krishna), Kishore (Youthful; Lord Krishna), Krishna (Lord Krishna), Kuber (God of wealth), Kush (Son of Lord Rama)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Kamal (Lotus), Kunal (Lotus; son of Emperor Ashoka)
  • Virtues and feelings: Kabir (Great; the saint Kabir), Kanishk (Famous Kushan emperor), Kapil (Name of a great sage), Karan (Generous warrior Karna; skilful), Kaushal (Skill), Kaushik (Sage Vishwamitra), Ketan (Home, flag), Kiaan (King, grace), Kshitij (Horizon), Kuldeep (Lamp of the family)

Short names that are easy to call out: Kush. Longer, traditional names: Kaustubh — they also give an easy pet name.

Short, easy names: Kush.

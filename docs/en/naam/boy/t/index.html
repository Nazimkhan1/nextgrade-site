About boy names starting with T

In Hindi, the English letter T can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: त (तनिष्क, तन्मय, तनुज); त् (त्रिभुवन, त्रिलोचन, त्रिलोक); ता (ताहिर, तारक, तारिक़); तु (तुहिन, तुलसीदास, तुषार); ते (तेजस, तेजपाल); ती (तीर्थ); ति (तिलक). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Trilochan (Three-eyed; Lord Shiva), Trivikram (Lord Vishnu as Vamana)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Tanishq (Jewel), Tapan (The sun, warmth), Tariq (Morning star)
  • Virtues and feelings: Tahir (Pure, chaste), Tanmay (Absorbed, engrossed), Tanuj (Son), Tanvir (Enlightened, radiant), Tapas (Penance, meditation), Tarak (Protector, saviour), Tarun (Young), Tathagat (Lord Buddha), Teerth (Holy place of pilgrimage), Tejas (Brilliance, radiance), Tejpal (Protector of brilliance), Tilak (Mark on the forehead; the best), Tribhuvan (The three worlds), Trilok (The three worlds), Tuhin (Snow, dew), Tulsidas (Devotee of Tulsi; poet of Ramcharitmanas), Tushar (Frost, dew)

Longer, traditional names: Tathagat, Tribhuvan, Trilochan, Trivikram, Tulsidas — they also give an easy pet name.

Frequently asked questions

How many boy names starting with T are listed here?

This page lists 22 boy names starting with T, each with its meaning. A few: Tahir, Tanishq, Tanmay, Tanuj, Tanvir.

Which boy names starting with T are linked to God?

In this list these names are linked to God or deities: Trilochan, Trivikram.

What is a good short boy name starting with T?

Names for this letter are mostly long; the first part of the name often works as a short pet name.

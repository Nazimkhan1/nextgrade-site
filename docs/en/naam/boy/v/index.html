About boy names starting with V

In Hindi, the English letter V can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: वि (विहान, विजय, विकास); वे (वेद, वेदांत); वै (वैभव); व (वरुण); वा (वासुदेव); वी (वीर). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Varun (God of water), Vasudev (Lord Krishna), Vishnu (Lord Vishnu, the preserver)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Vihaan (Dawn, a new beginning), Vivaan (Full of life; rays of the sun)
  • Virtues and feelings: Vaibhav (Prosperity, grandeur), Ved (Sacred knowledge, the Vedas), Vedant (Essence of the Vedas), Veer (Brave, warrior), Vijay (Victory), Vikas (Progress, development), Vikram (Valour), Vikrant (Brave, powerful), Vinay (Humility, modesty), Vineet (Humble, polite), Vinod (Joy, humour), Vipul (Abundant), Viraj (Shining, sovereign), Virat (Giant, immense), Vishal (Huge, vast), Vishwas (Faith, trust), Vivek (Wisdom, conscience)

Short names that are easy to call out: Ved, Veer.

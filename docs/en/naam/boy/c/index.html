About boy names starting with C

In Hindi, the English letter C can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: चं (चंदन, चंद्र, चंद्रगुप्त); च (चक्रधर, चक्रपाणि, चक्षु); चि (चिदानंद, चिन्मय, चिराग); चै (चैतन्य); चा (चाणक्य); चे (चेतन); चिं (चिंतन). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Chakradhar (Holder of the discus; Lord Vishnu), Chakrapani (Discus in hand; Lord Vishnu), Chandrashekhar (Moon-crested; Lord Shiva), Charan (Feet (of the divine)), Chaturbhuj (Four-armed; Lord Vishnu), Chidanand (Consciousness and bliss; Lord Shiva)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Chaman (Garden), Chandra (The moon), Chandragupt (Protected by the moon; Maurya emperor), Chandrakant (Beloved of the moon, moonstone), Chandramohan (Charming like the moon), Chandresh (Lord of the moon), Chirag (Lamp, light)
  • Virtues and feelings: Chaitanya (Consciousness), Chakshu (Eye), Chanakya (Great scholar, Kautilya), Chandan (Sandalwood), Chetan (Aware, full of life), Chinmay (Full of knowledge), Chintan (Thought, reflection), Chiranjeev (Long-lived), Chirantan (Eternal)

Longer, traditional names: Chaitanya, Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chanakya, Chandragupt, Chandrakant — they also give an easy pet name.

Frequently asked questions

How many boy names starting with C are listed here?

This page lists 22 boy names starting with C, each with its meaning. A few: Chaitanya, Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chakshu, Chaman.

Which boy names starting with C are linked to God?

In this list these names are linked to God or deities: Chakradhar, Chakrapani, Chandrashekhar, Charan, Chaturbhuj, Chidanand.

What is a good short boy name starting with C?

Names for this letter are mostly long; the first part of the name often works as a short pet name.

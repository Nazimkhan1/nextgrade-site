About boy names starting with N

In Hindi, the English letter N can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: न (नचिकेत, नकुल, नमन); नि (निहाल, निखिल, निमिष); नी (नील, नीरज, नीलेश); नं (नंदन, नंदकिशोर); ना (नारायण). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Nandkishore (Son of Nand; Lord Krishna), Narayan (Lord Vishnu), Nilesh (Blue lord; Lord Krishna), Niranjan (Spotless; Lord Shiva)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Neeraj (Lotus), Nishant (Dawn, end of night)
  • Virtues and feelings: Nachiket (Boy who learned wisdom from Yama), Nakul (One of the Pandavas), Naman (Salutation), Nandan (Son; one who gives joy), Narendra (King of men), Naresh (King), Navdeep (New lamp), Naveen (New, fresh), Navneet (Fresh butter), Neel (The colour blue), Nihal (Happy, content), Nikhil (Whole, complete), Nimish (Blink of an eye, moment), Nirmal (Pure, clean), Nirvaan (Liberation, bliss), Nitin (Master of the right path)

Short names that are easy to call out: Neel. Longer, traditional names: Nachiket, Nandkishore, Narendra, Niranjan — they also give an easy pet name.

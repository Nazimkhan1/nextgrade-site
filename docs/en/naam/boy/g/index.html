About boy names starting with G

In Hindi, the English letter G can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: ग (गगन, गजानन, गजेंद्र); गौ (गौरांग, गौरव, गौरीश); गु (गुलशन, गुणवंत, गुरदीप); गि (गिरधर, गिरिराज, गिरीश); गो (गोपाल, गोविंद); ज् (ज्ञान, ज्ञानेंद्र); गं (गंधर्व); घ (घनश्याम). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Gaurish (Lord of Gauri; Lord Shiva), Ghanshyam (Dark as a cloud; Lord Krishna), Girdhar (Holder of the mountain; Lord Krishna), Girish (Lord of the mountains (Shiva)), Gopal (Krishna, the cowherd), Govind (Krishna, protector of cows)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Gagan (The sky), Gautam (The Buddha; bringer of light), Gulshan (Flower garden)
  • Virtues and feelings: Gajanan (Elephant-faced; Lord Ganesha), Gajendra (King of elephants), Gandharv (Celestial musician), Ganesh (Lord Ganesha, lord of the ganas), Garv (Pride), Gaurang (Fair-bodied; Chaitanya Mahaprabhu), Gaurav (Pride, honour), Giriraj (King of mountains; Govardhan), Gunwant (Virtuous), Gurdeep (Lamp of the Guru), Gurpreet (Love of the Guru), Gyan (Knowledge), Gyanendra (Lord of knowledge)

Short names that are easy to call out: Garv, Gyan. Longer, traditional names: Gajendra, Gandharv, Ghanshyam, Gurpreet, Gyanendra — they also give an easy pet name.

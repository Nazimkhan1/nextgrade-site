About boy names starting with H

In Hindi, the English letter H can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: ह (हनुमंत, हरि, हरिदास); हि (हिमांशु, हिम्मत, हितेश); हा (हामिद, हार्दिक); हे (हेमंत, हेमेंद्र); हं (हंसराज); हृ (हृदय); ऋ (ऋतिक). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Hanumant (Lord Hanuman), Hari (Lord Vishnu), Haridas (Devotee of Lord Vishnu), Harish (Lord Vishnu), Harivansh (Family of Lord Hari (Vishnu)), Harjeet (Victory of God), Harpreet (Love of God)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Himanshu (The moon)
  • Virtues and feelings: Hamid (One who praises), Hansraj (King of swans), Hardik (Heartfelt, sincere), Harsh (Joy, happiness), Harshal (Cheerful, happy), Harshit (Joyful), Harshvardhan (One who increases joy; famous emperor), Hasan (Handsome, good), Hemant (The early-winter season), Hemendra (Lord of gold), Himmat (Courage), Hitesh (Lord of goodness), Hriday (Heart), Hrithik (From the heart)

Short names that are easy to call out: Hari. Longer, traditional names: Hanumant, Harivansh, Harpreet, Harshvardhan, Hemendra, Himanshu — they also give an easy pet name.

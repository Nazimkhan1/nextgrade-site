About boy names starting with R

In Hindi, the English letter R can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: रा (राघव, राहुल, राजीव); र (रजत, रमन, रमेश); ऋ (ऋषभ, ऋषि, ऋत्विक); रे (रेहान, रेयांश); रो (रोहन, रोहित); रौ (रौनक); रु (रुद्र); रू (रूपेश). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Raghav (Lord Rama), Ram (Lord Ram), Ramesh (Husband of Lakshmi; Lord Vishnu), Reyansh (Ray of light; Lord Vishnu), Rudra (Lord Shiva)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Rajeev (Lotus), Rakesh (Full moon, lord of the night), Ravi (The sun), Rohit (Red; the rising sun)
  • Virtues and feelings: Rahul (Efficient; son of Gautam Buddha), Rajat (Silver), Rajesh (King of kings), Raman (Pleasing, beloved), Ranjit (Victorious in battle), Ranveer (Hero of the battle), Rehan (Fragrant herb, sweet basil), Rishabh (Superior, excellent), Rishi (Sage), Ritvik (A priest who performs rituals), Rohan (Ascending, growing), Ronak (Brightness, glow), Rupesh (Lord of beauty)

Short names that are easy to call out: Ram, Ravi.

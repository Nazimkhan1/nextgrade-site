About boy names starting with E

In Hindi, the English letter E can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: ए (एहसान, एहतेशाम, एजाज़); ई (ईश्वर). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Ekamjeet (Victory of the one God), Ekdev (The one supreme God), Ekjot (One divine light), Ekling (Lord Shiva), Ekonkar (God is one), Eshan (Lord Shiva), Eshwar (God)
  • Virtues and feelings: Ehsan (Kindness, favour), Ehtesham (Dignity, grandeur), Ejaz (Miracle), Ekagra (Focused), Ekam (One, unique), Ekansh (Whole, complete), Ekdant (Single-tusked; Lord Ganesha), Eklavya (The devoted student of the Mahabharata), Eknath (The one lord; saint Eknath), Ekveer (Bravest of all), Elango (Young prince), Elavarasan (Crown prince), Etash (Shining, luminous)

Short names that are easy to call out: Ejaz, Ekam. Longer, traditional names: Ehtesham, Ekamjeet, Elavarasan — they also give an easy pet name.

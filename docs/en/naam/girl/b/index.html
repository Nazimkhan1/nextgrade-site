About girl names starting with B

In Hindi, the English letter B can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: भा (भागीरथी, भाग्यश्री, भानुमती); ब (बरखा, बसंती); भू (भूमि, भूमिका); बा (बानी); बे (बेला); भै (भैरवी); भ (भक्ति); भु (भुवि); बिं (बिंदिया); बि (बिपाशा); ब् (ब्राह्मी); बु (बुलबुल). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Bani (Speech; Goddess Saraswati), Bhagyashree (Fortunate; Goddess Lakshmi), Bhairavi (Goddess Durga; a raga), Bharati (Goddess Saraswati; India), Bhargavi (Goddess Lakshmi), Bhavini (Emotional; Goddess Parvati), Brahmi (Goddess Saraswati; a script)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Barkha (Rain), Basanti (Of spring), Bela (A jasmine flower), Bhagirathi (River Ganga), Bhanumati (Bright like the sun), Bhumi (The earth), Bhumika (Earth; role), Bhuvi (Earth; heaven), Bipasha (Name of a river)
  • Virtues and feelings: Bhakti (Devotion), Bhavika (Full of feeling, kind), Bhavna (Feeling, emotion), Bhavya (Grand, splendid), Bindiya (A bindi), Bulbul (Songbird)

Short names that are easy to call out: Bani, Bela. Longer, traditional names: Bhagirathi, Bhagyashree, Bhairavi, Bhanumati, Bhargavi — they also give an easy pet name.

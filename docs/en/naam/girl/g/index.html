About girl names starting with G

In Hindi, the English letter G can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: ग (गरिमा, गर्विता, ग़ज़ाला); गु (गुलनाज़, गुनगुन, गुणिता); गी (गीता, गीतिका, गीतांजलि); गो (गोदावरी, गोमती, गोपिका); गा (गार्गी, गायत्री); गौ (गौरी, गौतमी); गं (गंगा); गि (गिरिजा); ग् (ग्रीष्मा); गुं (गुंजन). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Gauri (Goddess Parvati; fair), Gayatri (A Vedic mantra; a goddess), Girija (Daughter of the mountain; Goddess Parvati), Gopika (Cowherd girl; devotee of Krishna)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Ganga (Holy river Ganga), Gautami (River Godavari), Godavari (Holy river Godavari), Gomti (A holy river), Gulnaz (Delicate as a flower)
  • Virtues and feelings: Gargi (A learned woman of the Upanishads), Garima (Dignity), Garvita (Proud), Gazala (Gazelle), Geeta (The holy Bhagavad Gita), Geetika (A little song), Gehna (Ornament, jewellery), Gitanjali (Offering of songs), Grishma (Summer season), Gungun (Humming), Gunita (Virtuous, skilled), Gunjan (The humming of bees), Gurleen (Absorbed in the Guru)

Longer, traditional names: Gitanjali, Godavari — they also give an easy pet name.

Frequently asked questions

How many girl names starting with G are listed here?

This page lists 22 girl names starting with G, each with its meaning. A few: Ganga, Gargi, Garima, Garvita, Gauri.

Which girl names starting with G are linked to God?

In this list these names are linked to God or deities: Gauri, Gayatri, Girija, Gopika.

What is a good short girl name starting with G?

Names for this letter are mostly long; the first part of the name often works as a short pet name.

About girl names starting with K

In Hindi, the English letter K can stand for more than one sound. The names on this page begin in Hindi with: क (कल्पना, कल्याणी, कमला); का (काजल, कामाक्षी, काश्वी); को (कोकिला, कोमल); कृ (कृपा, कृति); कौ (कौशिकी); के (केतकी); खु (खुशी); कि (कियारा); की (कीर्ति); कु (कुसुम). When you pick a name, also settle one Hindi spelling so the birth certificate, Aadhaar and school records match.

Names by meaning

  • Linked to God and deities: Kalyani (Auspicious; Goddess Parvati), Kamakshi (A form of Goddess Parvati), Kamala (Lotus; Goddess Lakshmi), Kaushiki (Goddess Durga)
  • From nature (sun, moon, flowers, rivers…): Kaveri (Holy river Kaveri), Ketaki (A fragrant flower), Kiara (Bright, light), Kusum (Flower)
  • Virtues and feelings: Kajal (Kohl), Kalpana (Imagination), Kanika (Small particle), Karishma (Miracle), Kashvi (Shining, radiant), Kasturi (Musk, fragrance), Kavita (Poem), Kavya (Poetry), Khushi (Happiness, joy), Kirti (Fame, glory), Kokila (Cuckoo), Komal (Soft, gentle), Kripa (Grace, kindness), Kriti (Creation)

Longer, traditional names: Kamakshi, Karishma, Kaushiki — they also give an easy pet name.

Frequently asked questions

How many girl names starting with K are listed here?

This page lists 22 girl names starting with K, each with its meaning. A few: Kajal, Kalpana, Kalyani, Kamakshi, Kamala.

Which girl names starting with K are linked to God?

In this list these names are linked to God or deities: Kalyani, Kamakshi, Kamala, Kaushiki.

What is a good short girl name starting with K?

Names for this letter are mostly long; the first part of the name often works as a short pet name.

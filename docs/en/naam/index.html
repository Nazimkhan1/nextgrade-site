Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a baby name?

A good name is short, easy to say and write, and has a positive meaning. Every name here lists its meaning — pick one by its first letter.

Are these names by rashi or nakshatra?

Names here are organised by first letter (A–Z). Many families pick a name beginning with the letter of the birth nakshatra/rashi — just open that letter to see names starting with it.

Are these modern names?

Yes — the list mixes popular modern names and classic traditional ones, all with meanings.

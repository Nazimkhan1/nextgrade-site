🎮 Festival games

A small, fun game for every festival — play right in your phone's browser, set a score and challenge friends on WhatsApp. The games run on your phone; we don't ask for an account or any personal details.

🛶 Puzzle game

Nadi Paar — River Crossing Puzzle

The classic brain puzzle as a game: take the farmer, tiger, goat and grass across without anything getting eaten — then Dad and two kids, and 3 guards with 3 thieves. Hints, stars for fewest moves, WhatsApp challenge.

 🗼 Puzzle game

Brahma ki Meenar — Tower of Hanoi

Move the tower of golden discs to another peg — one at a time, never a big one on a small one. 3 to 7 discs, hints, stars for fewest moves.

 🏺 Puzzle game

Do Matke — Water Jug Puzzle

Measure exactly 4 litres with a 3- and a 5-litre pot — fill, empty, pour. Then 6 litres with 4 and 9, and split 8 into 4 + 4.

 🔦 Puzzle game

Raat ka Pul — Bridge & Torch

A dark night, a weak bridge and one torch — get 4 people across in 17 minutes. Two at a time, at the slower pace. Three levels.

 🪔 Puzzle game

Diye Jalao — Light the Lamps

Tap a diya — it and its neighbours flip. Light every lamp on 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards. Hints and stars.

 ⚪ Puzzle game

Gotiyan — Peg Solitaire

The old Brainvita puzzle: jump marbles over each other and leave just one — up to the full 32-marble board. Undo and hints.

 ✨ Puzzle game

Jaadui Varg — Magic Square

Fill the square so every row, column and diagonal adds up the same — from 3×3 to the 1,000-year-old Khajuraho 4×4 square.

 🧩 Puzzle game

Khiskao Tasveer — Sliding Puzzle

Slide the pieces into the empty space and rebuild a sunset palace picture — 3×3 and 4×4. Hints and stars.

 🇮🇳 Daily GK game

Bharat Quiz — Daily India GK

10 questions a day, 10 seconds each: states and capitals, national symbols, history, geography, science, sports and culture. A fact after every answer, topic practice, and a WhatsApp score challenge.

 🔤 Daily game

Aaj ka Shabd — Word of the Day

A new 5-letter word every day: guess it in 6 tries. Hindi words in Roman letters (with meaning) or English words. Colours show which letters are right; share your result on WhatsApp and keep your streak.

 🪁 Makar Sankranti game

Kite Fight

Steer your kite, cut other kites’ strings — “Wo kaata!” — protect your own, catch til-gud laddoos and keep away from birds. 60 seconds, combos and a WhatsApp challenge.

 💃 Fitness game

Home Zumba

A home dance workout for women — 8 fun moves to music, a cartoon coach who gets fitter with every move, a voice coach, a daily 🔥 streak and challenges for friends.

 🌅 Chhath Puja game

Chhath Ghat

Take the holy dip at dawn, offer arghya to the rising sun at the right moment, then catch thekua, bananas and sugarcane in your soop to break the fast. Three stages, temple bells and splashes, WhatsApp challenge.

 💪 Daily game

Morning 2-Minute Challenge

8 easy moves with a cartoon coach — a timer, a daily 🔥 streak, badges and WhatsApp challenges for friends. Just 2 minutes (15 seconds a move), no camera, no login.

 🎆 New Year game

Fireworks Pop

Tap the rockets rising into the sky and burst them — at the very top for a Perfect, quickly for combos. With a live countdown to the new year.

 🎅 Christmas game

Catch Santa's Gifts

Steer Santa's sleigh, catch the falling gifts, dodge the coal and challenge friends on WhatsApp. In Hindi, Hinglish, English and Bengali.

 🎇 New Year game

Journey of the Year 2026 → 2027

Run through 2026 touching the year's memories, count down 10…1 and enter 2027 with fireworks, dhol and dancing.

 🐦 Bachpan game

Chidiya Udd (Birdie, Fly!)

The childhood game: tap only if it really flies! Parrot, fly… buffalo, fly? 48 things, 3 lives, faster every round, with a voice caller.

 🐍 Board game game

Saanp-Seedhi (Snakes & Ladders)

The childhood board game: play the computer or 2–4 friends on one phone. Every ladder a good habit, every snake a bad one.

 🇮🇳 Republic Day game

Parade ki Taal (Parade Beat)

Tap on the drum beat so the Republic Day parade marches in step: Army, BSF camels, NCC band, then the tricolour flypast.

 🌙 Ramzan game

Chaand Dhundo (Find the Crescent)

Use the telescope to find the thin new crescent in the evening sky before it sets — Venus, planes and kites will try to fool you! 10 evenings.

 🙏 Gurpurab game

Langar Seva

Do seva in the langar this Gurpurab: serve prashada, dal, kheer and water to the sangat in pangat. 60 seconds, no music, with Guru Nanak Dev Ji's teachings.

 🌼 Vasant Panchami game

Basant ki Sargam

The petals of a spring flower play Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni like Maa Saraswati's veena — repeat the tune, one note longer every round. Vasant Panchami game with a practice mode.

 🎨 Holi game

Rang Barsao (Holi)

Throw dry gulal at friends peeping out of a Braj lane — favourite colour scores double. No colour on anyone who says no, and never on animals! 60 seconds.

 🔵 Bachpan game

Kanche (Marbles)

The childhood marbles game: pull back and flick to knock marbles out of the circle or roll into the gucchi. 6 levels with stars — no bets, just aim.

 🏏 Bachpan game

Gilli-Danda

The childhood street game: tap to flip the gilli up, tap again to strike it far. High shot or flat — the village kids are waiting to catch it. Score in danda lengths.

 🪨 Bachpan game

Pitthu (Lagori / Seven Stones)

The childhood Pitthu game: knock down the pile of stones with the ball, then rebuild it biggest to smallest while ducking the throws. Rebuild it and shout "Pitthu!"

 🥭 Bachpan game

Aam Todo (Slingshot Mangoes)

The childhood slingshot game: pull back and knock ripe yellow mangoes off the tree — leave the green ones, mind the wind and never hit the beehive. 6 levels with stars.

 🐯 Bachpan game

Bagh-Bakri (Tigers and Goats)

The old tigers-and-goats board game: trap the 4 tigers with your 20 goats, or play the tigers and eat 5 goats. Against the computer (easy, medium, hard) or a friend on one phone.

 ❌ Bachpan game

Kattam-Katti (Tic Tac Toe)

Noughts and crosses on a school slate: play the computer (easy, medium, or a hard level that never loses) or a friend on one phone. Three in a row wins; best of 5.

 🔴 Bachpan game

Sholo Guti (16 Pieces)

The village board game drawn on the ground: 16 pieces each, step along the lines and jump over enemy pieces to capture them — chain jumps too. Against the computer (easy, medium, hard) or a friend on one phone.

 🐚 Bachpan game

Chaupar / Pachisi (Cowrie Board Game)

The old Indian cross-shaped cloth board with six cowrie shells: graces on 10 and 25, castle squares, captures, and a race to bring all four pieces home. Play the computer or 2–4 people on one phone, even 2 vs 2 partners. Only for fun.

 👑 Bachpan game

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

The old chit game on one phone: 4 friends pass it round to see their chits, or play the computer. Mantri ji, who is the Chor? 5 or 10 rounds.

How to play

Tap any game card — it opens straight in your browser, with nothing to download. Each game lasts between 45 seconds and 5 minutes, so it fits into a short break. When a game ends, add your name and tap "Send challenge"; your friend gets a link that shows your score and can try to beat it.

For kids and families

Our games have no violence, gambling or in-app purchases. Sound only turns on when you choose. Best scores are saved only on your phone — we never ask for an account, email or phone number.

Offline and install

Once opened, most games work without the internet. You can also install them like an app by choosing "Add to Home screen" from your browser menu.

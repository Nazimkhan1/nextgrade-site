🍚 Pongal · पोंगल

📅 When is Pongal 2027?

15 January 2027, Friday · 51 days to go

Pongal is on Friday, 15 January 2027. Bhogi is on 14 January; Thai Pongal on 15 January. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Pongal is the harvest festival of Tamil Nadu, a thank-you to the Sun, the earth, the rain and the animals that help in the fields. In North India the same season brings Makar Sankranti and Lohri.

WhenUsually four days from 14 or 15 January — the start of the Tamil month of Thai
WhereTamil Nadu, Puducherry and Tamil families around the world

What "Pongal" means

In Tamil, "pongal" means to boil over. New rice, milk and jaggery are cooked in a new clay pot, and when the milk bubbles over everyone shouts "Pongalo Pongal!" — may prosperity overflow in the home.

Besides sweet sakkarai pongal, savoury ven pongal is also made. Beautiful kolam designs are drawn with rice flour at the doorstep, and sugarcane is a must.

The four days of Pongal

Bhogi: clearing out old, unused things and starting fresh.

Thai Pongal: the main day, when pongal is offered to the Sun.

Mattu Pongal: the cows and bullocks that help on the farm are bathed, decorated and honoured.

Kaanum Pongal: visiting relatives, going out together and taking the blessings of elders.

Pongal wishes

  • Pongalo Pongal! May happiness overflow in your home just like the Pongal pot. Happy Pongal!
  • New harvest, new sweetness, new beginnings — wishing you and your family a very happy Pongal.
  • May the Sun bless your year with good health and plenty. Iniya Pongal Nalvazhthukkal!

Frequently asked questions

When is Pongal 2027?

Pongal is on Friday, 15 January 2027. Bhogi is on 14 January; Thai Pongal on 15 January.

Why is Pongal celebrated?

Pongal is the harvest festival of Tamil Nadu, a time to thank the Sun God, the earth and the cattle that help with farming. A dish called pongal is cooked from new rice, milk and jaggery; letting it boil over the pot is a sign of plenty in the home. It lasts four days.

What does Pongal mean?

In Tamil, "pongal" means boiling over. Rice, milk and jaggery boiling over in a new pot is a sign of plenty coming into the home.

What is Mattu Pongal?

The third day of Pongal, when cows and bullocks that help with farming are bathed, their horns painted and garlanded, and they are honoured.

🌾 Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) · बैसाखी

📅 When is Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 2027?

14 April 2027, Wednesday · 140 days to go

Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) is on Wednesday, 14 April 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Baisakhi celebrates the ripening of the rabi harvest, especially wheat. Punjab dances bhangra and giddha to the dhol, fairs are held, and for Sikhs the day is very special as the founding day of the Khalsa.

WhenUsually 13 April (14 April in some years)
WherePunjab, Haryana and across North India; the same day is New Year in several parts of the country

The joy of the harvest

By Baisakhi the golden wheat is ready to be cut. Farmers thank God for the fruit of their hard work, wear new clothes, and fairs are held in village after village.

Founding of the Khalsa (1699)

On Baisakhi 1699, Guru Gobind Singh Ji founded the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib and gave amrit to the Panj Pyare. So gurdwaras hold kirtan, ardas, Nagar Kirtan and langar on this day.

One day, many names

Around the same time Bengal celebrates Poila Boishakh (Bengali New Year), Assam Bohag (Rongali) Bihu, Tamil Nadu Puthandu (Tamil New Year) and Kerala Vishu.

On Baisakhi day, 13 April 1919, British troops fired on unarmed people at Jallianwala Bagh in Amritsar. Those martyrs are remembered on this day too.

Frequently asked questions

When is Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) 2027?

Baisakhi (Vaisakhi) is on Wednesday, 14 April 2027.

Why is Baisakhi celebrated?

Baisakhi celebrates the harvest of the rabi crop (wheat) in Punjab and north India, and the start of the solar new year. For Sikhs it is special because on this day in 1699 Guru Gobind Singh Ji founded the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib.

When is Baisakhi celebrated?

Usually on 13 April, and on 14 April in some years. It follows the solar calendar, so the date stays almost the same.

How is Baisakhi connected to the Khalsa?

On Baisakhi 1699, Guru Gobind Singh Ji founded the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib, so for Sikhs it is also Khalsa Sajna Diwas.

🎄 Christmas · क्रिसमस

📅 When is Christmas 2026?

25 December 2026, Friday · 29 days to go

Christmas is on Friday, 25 December 2026. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ. Today it is not only a festival of the Christian community but of everyone who believes in love, sharing and the hope of a new year.

Why Christmas is celebrated

According to Christian belief, Jesus was born in a simple stable in Bethlehem. His message was to love one another, help those in need and learn to forgive. Christmas is the day to remember that message.

The date of 25 December began to be observed in Rome in the fourth century and was gradually adopted around the world.

How it is celebrated

The night of 24 December is Christmas Eve. Churches hold midnight mass and people sing carols.

Homes put up a Christmas tree decorated with stars, bells, fairy lights and small gifts. Children love the story of Santa Claus bringing presents.

In India people bake plum cake and cookies, Goa and Kerala make special dishes, and neighbours send each other sweets and cake.

Do something kind this Christmas

Spend some time at an orphanage or old-age home, give warm clothes to people who need them, or take a piece of cake to a neighbour who lives alone. That is what Christmas is really about.

Frequently asked questions

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas is on Friday, 25 December 2026.

What date is Christmas?

Christmas is celebrated on 25 December every year. The evening of 24 December is called Christmas Eve.

Why do people decorate a Christmas tree?

An evergreen tree stays green even in winter, so it is seen as a sign of life and hope. A decorated tree brings joy and colour to the home.

Where in India is Christmas celebrated the most?

In Goa, Kerala, Mizoram, Nagaland, Meghalaya and in cities such as Mumbai, Kolkata and Bengaluru.

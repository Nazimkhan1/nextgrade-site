🔱 Maha Shivratri · महाशिवरात्रि

📅 When is Maha Shivratri 2027?

6 March 2027, Saturday · 100 days to go

Maha Shivratri is on Saturday, 6 March 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Maha Shivratri is the greatest night of worship of Lord Shiva. It is believed that Shiva and Parvati were married on this night. Devotees fast and stay awake through the night in prayer.

WhenChaturdashi of the dark half of Phalgun (usually February or March)
WhereAll over India and Nepal

How the puja is done

The Shiva linga is bathed with water, milk, curd, honey and Ganga water. Bel leaves, dhatura, aak flowers and bhang are offered, and devotees chant "Om Namah Shivaya".

Puja is done in four parts (prahar) through the night, and many temples hold bhajans and kirtan all night.

Fasting

Some people keep a fast without water, while others eat fruit, milk and fasting foods such as sabudana and kuttu. Elderly people, the sick and pregnant women should fast only as their health allows.

Famous places

Kashi Vishwanath (Varanasi), Mahakaleshwar in Ujjain, Somnath, Pashupatinath in Nepal and the twelve jyotirlingas draw huge crowds on this day.

Frequently asked questions

When is Maha Shivratri 2027?

Maha Shivratri is on Saturday, 6 March 2027.

Why is Maha Shivratri celebrated?

Maha Shivratri is a night of devotion to Lord Shiva. It is believed to be the night Shiva married Parvati, and also the night he appeared as a pillar of light (jyotirlinga). Devotees fast, offer water and bel leaves to the Shivling and stay awake through the night.

What is the difference between Maha Shivratri and the monthly Shivratri?

A monthly Shivratri falls on Chaturdashi of the dark half of every month. The one in Phalgun is the greatest, so it is called Maha Shivratri.

What is offered to the Shiva linga?

Water, milk, bel leaves, dhatura, aak flowers, sandalwood and bhasma. Tulsi is usually not offered to the Shiva linga.

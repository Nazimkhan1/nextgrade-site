🦚 Janmashtami · जन्माष्टमी

📅 When is Janmashtami 2027?

24 August 2027, Tuesday · 269 days to go

Janmashtami is on Tuesday, 24 August 2027. Smarta tradition: 24 August; ISKCON/Vaishnava tradition: 25 August. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Janmashtami celebrates the birth of Lord Krishna. He is said to have been born at midnight in Kansa's prison in Mathura, so on this day people fast and celebrate Kanha's birth at 12 midnight.

WhenBhadrapada Krishna Ashtami — usually August or September (the Smarta and Vaishnava dates sometimes differ by a day; check your panchang)
WhereAll over India; especially Mathura, Vrindavan, Dwarka and ISKCON temples

The story of Krishna's birth

Kansa, king of Mathura, feared the eighth son of his sister Devaki, so he locked Devaki and Vasudeva in prison. On the night Krishna, the eighth son, was born, the guards fell asleep, the locks opened, and Vasudeva carried the baby across the flooded Yamuna to Nanda and Yashoda's home in Gokul.

Krishna's childhood in Gokul and Vrindavan — stealing butter, dancing the raas with the gopis, lifting Govardhan hill — lives on in songs and tableaux.

How it is celebrated

People fast all day, decorate jhankis in temples and homes, dress Laddu Gopal in new clothes and rock him in a swing. At midnight the birth aarti is sung with conches and bells, and panjiri, makhan-mishri and panchamrit are shared as prasad.

In Maharashtra the next day is "Dahi Handi" — a pot hung high up is broken by forming a human pyramid, in memory of Krishna stealing butter.

Frequently asked questions

When is Janmashtami 2027?

Janmashtami is on Tuesday, 24 August 2027. Smarta tradition: 24 August; ISKCON/Vaishnava tradition: 25 August.

Why is Janmashtami celebrated?

Janmashtami celebrates the birth of Lord Krishna. He is believed to have been born at midnight on the eighth day of the dark half of Bhadrapada, in a prison in Mathura where King Kansa had locked up his parents Devaki and Vasudeva. Devotees fast and celebrate his birth at midnight.

When is Janmashtami celebrated?

On Krishna Ashtami of Bhadrapada, usually in August or September. Sometimes it is celebrated over two days, so check your panchang.

What is Dahi Handi?

On the day after Janmashtami, a pot of curd and butter hung high up is broken by forming a human pyramid, recalling Krishna stealing butter.

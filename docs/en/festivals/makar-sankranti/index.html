🪁 Makar Sankranti · मकर संक्रांति

📅 When is Makar Sankranti 2027?

15 January 2027, Friday · 55 days to go

Makar Sankranti is on Friday, 15 January 2027. Most panchangs give 15 January; some panchangs and several state holiday lists give 14 January. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Makar Sankranti is the day the sun moves from Sagittarius (Dhanu) into Capricorn (Makar). It is one of the few Hindu festivals set by the sun, which is why its date stays almost the same every year.

Uttarayan and its meaning

From this day the sun's northward journey (Uttarayan) is said to begin and the days slowly grow longer. It is considered an auspicious time — people bathe in the Ganga or other holy rivers and donate sesame, jaggery, khichdi and blankets.

One festival, many names

In Gujarat it is called Uttarayan and the whole sky fills with kites. In Tamil Nadu the same season is Pongal, in Assam Magh Bihu (Bhogali Bihu), in Uttar Pradesh and Bihar Khichdi, and in Maharashtra people share tilgul saying "Tilgul ghya, god god bola" — take sesame and jaggery, and speak sweetly.

Why sesame and jaggery

In winter, sesame and jaggery give the body warmth and strength. Sharing til-gud is also a sign of keeping relationships sweet.

When flying kites, avoid Chinese manja — it is deadly for birds and passers-by, and is banned in many states.

Frequently asked questions

When is Makar Sankranti 2027?

Makar Sankranti is on Friday, 15 January 2027. Most panchangs give 15 January; some panchangs and several state holiday lists give 14 January.

Why does the date of Makar Sankranti stay almost the same?

Because it is set by the sun entering Capricorn, not by the moon. So it usually falls on 14 January and sometimes on 15 January.

What do people donate on Sankranti?

Sesame, jaggery, khichdi (rice and dal), blankets and warm clothes.

🏹 Ram Navami · राम नवमी

📅 When is Ram Navami 2027?

15 April 2027, Thursday · 141 days to go

Ram Navami is on Thursday, 15 April 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Ram Navami celebrates the birth of Lord Rama. It is believed that Shri Ram was born in Ayodhya to King Dasharath and Queen Kaushalya at midday on Chaitra Shukla Navami. Chaitra Navratri also ends on this day.

WhenNavami of the bright half of Chaitra (usually March or April)
WhereAll over India; the biggest celebration is in Ayodhya

Maryada Purushottam

Shri Ram is called "Maryada Purushottam" — the ideal man who honoured every relationship and duty with complete integrity. Accepting fourteen years of exile to keep his father's word is the greatest example of his character.

How it is celebrated

Temples hold the aarti of Ram's birth at midday, along with recitation of the Ramcharitmanas and bhajans. Processions are taken out in many places. Devotees fast and share panjiri, panchamrit and fruit as prasad.

Those who keep Chaitra Navratri end their fast on this day with kanya pujan.

Frequently asked questions

When is Ram Navami 2027?

Ram Navami is on Thursday, 15 April 2027.

Why is Ram Navami celebrated?

Ram Navami celebrates the birth of Lord Rama. He is believed to have been born on the ninth day of the bright half of the month of Chaitra, in Ayodhya, to King Dasharatha and Queen Kaushalya. Chaitra Navratri also ends on this day.

On which tithi is Ram Navami?

On Navami of the bright half of Chaitra, which usually falls in March or April.

At what time is the Ram Navami puja done?

Shri Ram is believed to have been born at midday, so the main puja and aarti are held at midday.

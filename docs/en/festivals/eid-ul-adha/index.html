🕌 Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) · ईद-उल-अज़हा (बक़रीद)

📅 When is Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 2027?

17 May 2027, Monday (expected) · 171 days to go

Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) is expected on Monday, 17 May 2027. Eid depends on the sighting of the moon, so the date can move by a day. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Eid ul-Adha, also called Bakrid, is the Eid of sacrifice and trust in Allah. It remembers the sacrifice of Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him), who was ready to give up what he loved most at Allah's command. It falls during the days of Hajj.

When10 Dhul Hijjah, the 12th month of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar — about 10–11 days earlier every year, fixed by the moon sighting
WhereAll over India and the world

The story of the sacrifice

According to Islamic tradition, Prophet Ibrahim (AS) saw in a dream that he was sacrificing his son, Prophet Ismail (AS), for Allah. Father and son were both ready to obey. Then Allah sent a ram in Ismail's place.

The story teaches complete trust in Allah and the readiness to give up even what we love in His way.

How Eid is celebrated

In the morning people bathe, wear new or clean clothes and offer the Eid ul-Adha prayer at the Eidgah or mosque, then embrace each other saying "Eid Mubarak".

Those for whom it is required perform qurbani (animal sacrifice). The meat is shared in three parts — one for the family, one for relatives and friends, and one for people in need.

Keeping things clean, following local laws and respecting the feelings of neighbours while doing qurbani is also part of the spirit of Eid.

Hajj and Dhul Hijjah

Dhul Hijjah is the last month of the Islamic year. Hajj takes place in Makkah between the 8th and 12th of the month, and the 9th is the Day of Arafah. Eid ul-Adha comes in these days of Hajj.

Frequently asked questions

When is Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) 2027?

Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) is expected on Monday, 17 May 2027. Eid depends on the sighting of the moon, so the date can move by a day.

Why is Eid ul-Adha (Bakrid) celebrated?

It remembers the readiness of Prophet Ibrahim to sacrifice what he loved most — his son — on Allah's command, after which Allah sent a ram in the son's place. It falls on the 10th of Dhul Hijjah, during the days of Hajj. The meat of the sacrifice is shared in three parts: for the family, relatives and the poor.

When is Bakrid celebrated?

On 10 Dhul Hijjah in the Islamic calendar, about two months and ten days after Eid ul-Fitr. The exact date is fixed by the moon-sighting announcement.

What is the difference between Eid ul-Fitr and Eid ul-Adha?

Eid ul-Fitr comes after the fasts of Ramzan (the "sweet Eid"). Eid ul-Adha is the Eid of sacrifice during the days of Hajj.

🙏 Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti · गुरु गोबिंद सिंह जयंती

📅 When is Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti 2027?

15 January 2027, Friday · 48 days to go

Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti is on Friday, 15 January 2027. This is the panchang date (Paush Shukla Saptami); by the Nanakshahi calendar it is 5 January. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Guru Gobind Singh Ji was the tenth Sikh Guru. His birthday is celebrated as Prakash Purab or Gurpurab with kirtan, Akhand Path, Nagar Kirtan and langar, and the sangat remembers his teachings of courage, equality and seva.

WhenPaush Shukla Saptami — usually late December or January (check the exact date with your gurdwara)
WhereGurdwaras across India and the world; especially Patna Sahib, Anandpur Sahib and Hazur Sahib (Nanded)

The life of Guru Gobind Singh Ji

He was born in 1666 at Patna Sahib, Bihar. His father, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji, was the ninth Guru. After his father's martyrdom in 1675 he became Guru at just nine years of age.

He was both a warrior and a poet, and knew Punjabi, Braj, Persian and Sanskrit. All four of his sons, the Sahibzade, were martyred; the younger Sahibzade are remembered on 26 December as "Veer Baal Diwas".

Founding the Khalsa

On Baisakhi 1699 at Anandpur Sahib, the Guru founded the Khalsa. He gave amrit to the first five Sikhs, the Panj Pyare, and then took amrit from them himself — showing that the Guru and the Sikh are equal.

The five Ks of the Khalsa are kesh (uncut hair), kanga (comb), kara (steel bangle), kachera (undergarment) and kirpan. Every Sikh man took the name "Singh" and every woman "Kaur".

Guru Granth Sahib — the eternal Guru

In 1708 at Hazur Sahib (Nanded, Maharashtra), the Guru declared that there would be no human Guru after him; the Guru Granth Sahib is the Guru forever. That is why it is honoured as the Guru in every gurdwara.

Frequently asked questions

When is Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti 2027?

Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti is on Friday, 15 January 2027. This is the panchang date (Paush Shukla Saptami); by the Nanakshahi calendar it is 5 January.

Why is Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti celebrated?

It is the Prakash Purab (birth anniversary) of Guru Gobind Singh Ji, the tenth Sikh Guru. He founded the Khalsa on Baisakhi in 1699 and declared the Guru Granth Sahib the eternal Guru of the Sikhs. He is remembered for courage, service and standing up against injustice.

When is Guru Gobind Singh Jayanti celebrated?

On Paush Shukla Saptami, which usually falls in late December or January. The date changes every year, so check with your gurdwara or calendar.

Who were the Panj Pyare?

The first five Sikhs to take amrit in 1699: Bhai Daya Singh, Bhai Dharam Singh, Bhai Himmat Singh, Bhai Mohkam Singh and Bhai Sahib Singh.

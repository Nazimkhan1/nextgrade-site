🙏 Guru Nanak Jayanti · गुरु नानक जयंती

📅 When is Guru Nanak Jayanti 2027?

14 November 2027, Sunday · 351 days to go

Guru Nanak Jayanti is on Sunday, 14 November 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Guru Nanak Jayanti celebrates the birth (prakash) of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, the founder of Sikhism and the first Sikh Guru. It is also called Gurpurab or Prakash Parv and is the biggest festival of the Sikh community.

Guru Nanak Dev Ji

Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born in 1469 at Rai Bhoi di Talwandi, known today as Nankana Sahib in Pakistan. In Sikh tradition his Prakash Parv is celebrated on Kartik Purnima.

He gave the message of "Ik Onkar" — God is one. His teachings rest on three principles: Naam Japna (remembering God), Kirat Karni (earning an honest living) and Vand Chhakna (sharing your earnings with those in need). He opposed caste and inequality and treated everyone as equal.

How Gurpurab is celebrated

Two days before the festival an Akhand Path begins in gurdwaras — an unbroken reading of the Guru Granth Sahib for about 48 hours.

A day before, a Nagar Kirtan procession is led by the Panj Pyare. The Guru Granth Sahib is carried in a palki, the sangat sings shabad kirtan and gatka (Sikh martial art) is performed.

In the days before the festival, Prabhat Pheris go out early in the morning. On the day, gurdwaras hold kirtan, katha and ardas, and in the langar people of every faith and caste sit together to eat.

Langar and seva

The tradition of langar (community kitchen) was started by Guru Nanak Dev Ji. Everyone sits in rows on the floor and eats the same food — rich or poor, of any faith. On Gurpurab many people do seva in the gurdwara — helping in the kitchen, washing dishes or looking after shoes.

Frequently asked questions

When is Guru Nanak Jayanti 2027?

Guru Nanak Jayanti is on Sunday, 14 November 2027.

Why is Guru Nanak Jayanti celebrated?

Guru Nanak Jayanti (Prakash Purab) marks the birth of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, the first Sikh Guru. He taught belief in one God, equality of all people, honest work and sharing food with everyone (langar). It is celebrated on Kartik Purnima.

When is Guru Nanak Jayanti celebrated?

On the full moon of Kartik, which usually falls in November. The calendar date changes every year.

What is an Akhand Path?

An unbroken reading of the Guru Granth Sahib from start to finish in about 48 hours, which begins two days before Gurpurab.

How do people greet each other on Gurpurab?

"Gurpurab diyan lakh lakh vadhaiyan" or "Happy Gurpurab" — both are common.

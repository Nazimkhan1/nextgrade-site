🎆 New Year · नया साल

📅 When is New Year 2027?

1 January 2027, Friday · 36 days to go

New Year is on Friday, 1 January 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

New Year is celebrated on 1 January, the first day of the Gregorian calendar. It is a day to thank the year gone by and make fresh plans for the year ahead.

Celebrating the New Year

On the night of 31 December people gather with family and friends and welcome the new year with a countdown at midnight. Fireworks, music and "Happy New Year" messages mark the night.

Many people in India start the year by visiting a temple, gurdwara, mosque or church on the first day.

New Year resolutions

The new year is a good time to set yourself small goals — a daily walk, saving a little every month, learning a new language or skill. Keep resolutions small and clear, and they are easier to keep.

Keeping track of your spending is a good resolution too — you can use our free app Hisab Kitab for it.

India's other new years

India also celebrates many regional new years — Chaitra Shukla Pratipada (the Hindu Nav Samvatsar), Gudi Padwa, Ugadi, Baisakhi, Pohela Boishakh, Puthandu and Vishu. Most of them fall in March or April.

Frequently asked questions

When is New Year 2027?

New Year is on Friday, 1 January 2027.

Why is New Year celebrated on 1 January?

Most of the world uses the Gregorian calendar, whose first day is 1 January, so the international new year is celebrated on this day.

When is the Hindu new year?

The Hindu Nav Samvatsar begins on Pratipada of the bright half of Chaitra, which usually falls in March or April.

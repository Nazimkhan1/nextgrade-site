🎨 Holi · होली

📅 When is Holi 2027?

22 March 2027, Monday · 114 days to go

Holi is on Monday, 22 March 2027. Holika Dahan is the evening before, 21 March. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Holi is the festival of colours, fun and togetherness. It celebrates the victory of good over evil and the arrival of spring. People forget old quarrels, put colour on each other and hug.

WhenHolika Dahan on Phalgun Purnima, the festival of colours the next day (usually March)
WhereAll over India and Nepal; Holi in Braj (Mathura, Vrindavan, Barsana) is the most famous

Holika Dahan and the story of Prahlad

King Hiranyakashipu wanted everyone to worship him, but his son Prahlad was a devotee of Lord Vishnu. The king's sister Holika had a boon that fire could not burn her. She sat in a fire with Prahlad in her lap, but by God's grace Prahlad was saved and Holika burned.

In memory of this, Holika Dahan is held the night before Holi — a pile of wood and cow-dung cakes is lit and people walk around it.

The festival of colours

The next day (Dhulandi) people play with colours, gulal and water from the morning. In Braj, Holi goes on for many days — Lathmar Holi in Barsana, the flower Holi of Vrindavan and the Holi of Mathura are famous worldwide.

Food

Gujiya, dahi bhalle, malpua, papdi, namakpare and thandai are the taste of Holi.

A safe Holi

Use natural or herbal colours, keep colour away from eyes and mouth, never put colour on animals and waste less water. Respect anyone who does not want to be coloured.

Frequently asked questions

When is Holi 2027?

Holi is on Monday, 22 March 2027. Holika Dahan is the evening before, 21 March.

Why is Holi celebrated?

Holi celebrates the victory of good over evil and the arrival of spring. In the story behind Holika Dahan, the devotee Prahlad survived the fire while Holika was burnt. Playing with colours is linked to the playful Holi of Radha and Krishna in Braj.

When is Holika Dahan?

On the night of Phalgun Purnima, a day before the festival of colours. The exact muhurat is seen in the panchang.

Where is Lathmar Holi played?

In Barsana and Nandgaon in Uttar Pradesh, where women playfully hit men with sticks and the men protect themselves with shields.

How do I get Holi colour off my skin?

Before playing, put coconut or mustard oil on your face and hair. Afterwards wash with mild soap or besan and curd — don't scrub hard.

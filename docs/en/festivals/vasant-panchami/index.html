🌼 Vasant Panchami · वसंत पंचमी

Vasant Panchami welcomes spring and is the day of worship of Goddess Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge. People wear yellow and cook yellow dishes on this day.

WhenPanchami of the bright half of Magh (usually late January or February)
WhereEspecially in North and East India; in Bengal as Saraswati Puja

Worship of Goddess Saraswati

Goddess Saraswati is the goddess of learning, music and the arts. On this day students place their books, pens and musical instruments before her and pray. In Bengal, Bihar and Odisha, schools and colleges celebrate Saraswati Puja with great enthusiasm.

Many families hold a child's "vidyarambh" — writing the first letters — on this day.

Why yellow

In spring the mustard fields turn yellow with flowers. Yellow is seen as the colour of energy, light and knowledge, so people wear yellow and make saffron rice, boondi or yellow rice.

Kites and spring festivities

In Punjab and nearby regions kites are flown on Vasant Panchami too. Many places hold music and poetry events.

  • Bright as yellow mustard fields — wishing you a joyful spring. Happy Basant Panchami!
  • Happy Saraswati Puja! May learning always light your way.

Frequently asked questions

On which tithi is Vasant Panchami?

On Panchami of the bright half of Magh. The calendar date changes every year, usually late January or February.

Why do people wear yellow on this day?

Yellow stands for spring, mustard flowers and knowledge, and is considered dear to Goddess Saraswati.

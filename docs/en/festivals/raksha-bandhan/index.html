🪢 Raksha Bandhan · रक्षा बंधन

📅 When is Raksha Bandhan 2027?

17 August 2027, Tuesday · 272 days to go

Raksha Bandhan is on Tuesday, 17 August 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

Raksha Bandhan is the festival of love and trust between brothers and sisters. The sister ties a rakhi on her brother's wrist and prays for his long life; the brother promises to always stand by her.

WhenShravan Purnima (usually August)
WhereAll over India and Nepal

The rakhi tradition

In the morning the sister decorates a puja thali with roli, akshat (rice), a diya, sweets and the rakhi. She puts a tilak on her brother's forehead, does aarti, ties the rakhi and feeds him a sweet. The brother gives her a gift.

The panchang is checked for "Bhadra kaal" before tying the rakhi, because tying it during that time is not considered auspicious.

Stories

In the Mahabharata, Krishna cut his finger at the time of Shishupal's death, and Draupadi tied a strip of her sari on it. Krishna kept that bond by protecting her honour later.

Today Raksha Bandhan is not limited to siblings — people tie rakhis to cousins, friends, soldiers and even trees.

Frequently asked questions

When is Raksha Bandhan 2027?

Raksha Bandhan is on Tuesday, 17 August 2027.

When is Raksha Bandhan?

On the full moon of Shravan, which usually falls in August.

Why is rakhi not tied during Bhadra kaal?

Bhadra kaal is considered inauspicious for good work in the panchang, so the rakhi is tied after it ends.

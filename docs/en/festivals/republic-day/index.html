🇮🇳 Republic Day · गणतंत्र दिवस

📅 When is Republic Day 2027?

26 January 2027, Tuesday · 63 days to go

Republic Day is on Tuesday, 26 January 2027. 📅 All 2027 festivals and dates

On 26 January 1950 the Constitution of India came into force and India became a sovereign, democratic republic. Republic Day remembers that historic day.

The story of the Constitution

After almost three years of work, the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949 (now observed as Constitution Day). Dr B. R. Ambedkar chaired its drafting committee.

The date 26 January was chosen because on 26 January 1930 the Congress had resolved to seek "Purna Swaraj" (complete independence).

How it is celebrated

A grand parade takes place on Kartavya Path in New Delhi, with contingents of the armed forces, tableaux from the states and cultural performances. The President hoists the national flag on this day.

Schools and offices hoist the flag, sing the national anthem and hold patriotic programmes.

Respecting the tricolour

When hoisting the flag, keep saffron at the top, never let the flag touch the ground and never use a torn flag. After the programme, don't throw paper flags on the road — keep them respectfully.

Frequently asked questions

When is Republic Day 2027?

Republic Day is on Tuesday, 26 January 2027.

Why is Republic Day celebrated?

Because the Constitution of India came into force on 26 January 1950 and India became a sovereign, democratic republic. The date was chosen because the pledge of Purna Swaraj (complete independence) was taken on 26 January 1930.

What is the difference between Republic Day and Independence Day?

Independence Day (15 August) marks freedom from British rule in 1947. Republic Day (26 January) marks the Constitution coming into force in 1950.

Who hoists the flag on 26 January?

The President hoists the flag on Republic Day; on Independence Day the Prime Minister hoists it at the Red Fort.

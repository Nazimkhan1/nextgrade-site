🍽️ Dua Before and After Eating

Before eating

Reference: Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim — “Say Bismillah, eat with your right hand and eat from what is in front of you”

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillah

In the name of Allah.

If you forgot Bismillah at the start

Reference: Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ

Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu

In the name of Allah, at its beginning and at its end.

After eating

Reference: Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مُسْلِمِينَ

Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at‘amana wa saqana wa ja‘alana muslimin

All praise is for Allah, who fed us, gave us drink and made us Muslims.

Another dua after eating

Reference: Jami at-Tirmidhi, Sunan Abu Dawud — reported with the virtue of forgiveness of past sins

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَٰذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ

Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at‘amani hadha wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah

All praise is for Allah, who fed me this and provided it for me without any power or strength of my own.

About these duas

Food is a blessing from Allah, and the Islamic manners of eating are simple: say “Bismillah” at the start, eat with the right hand, eat from what is in front of you, and thank Allah when you finish. This page gives the duas for those moments in Arabic, in English letters and with the meaning.

The shortest and most important dua is “Bismillah” — it is the first one children learn. Many families also say “Bismillahi wa ‘ala barakatillah” (in the name of Allah and with the blessing of Allah). If you forget at the start, say “Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu” when you remember — in the name of Allah at its beginning and its end.

After the meal, “Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at‘amana…” is the one most often recited. The second dua, ending “…min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah”, carries a lovely idea: this provision did not come from my own power but from Allah’s giving. It keeps pride down and gratitude up.

Small habits are part of the Sunnah too: sit while eating, do not waste food, finish what is on your plate, and never criticise food — if you do not like it, simply leave it. Teach children these habits along with the duas.

How to make dua — the simple way

  • Begin by praising Allah (e.g. “Alhamdulillah”) and sending durood on the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Raise your hands and ask with full conviction and attention — a dua from the heart is the most precious.
  • If you do not know Arabic, you may ask in your own language — Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, English; Allah understands every language.
  • Do not be hasty or say “my dua was not answered”; be patient and keep asking.
  • Pray for your parents, family and the whole ummah along with yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the dua before eating?

“Bismillah” — in the name of Allah. Many also say “Bismillahi wa ‘ala barakatillah”.

What if I forget Bismillah?

Say “Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu” as soon as you remember.

Which dua is read after eating?

“Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at‘amana wa saqana wa ja‘alana muslimin”.

Is there a dua for drinking water?

Say “Bismillah” before and “Alhamdulillah” after; drinking while seated and in three breaths is Sunnah.

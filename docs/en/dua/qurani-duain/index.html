📗 Rabbana Duas from the Quran

Good in this world and the next (Rabbana atina)

Reference: Quran 2:201 (Surah Al-Baqarah)

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina ‘adhaban-nar

Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and save us from the punishment of the Fire.

Forgiveness for forgetting and mistakes

Reference: Quran 2:286 (part of the last verse of Surah Al-Baqarah)

رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِنْ نَسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا

Rabbana la tu’akhidhna in nasina aw akhta’na

Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake.

For spouse, family and children

Reference: Quran 25:74 (Surah Al-Furqan)

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a‘yunin waj‘alna lil-muttaqina imama

Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and children the coolness of our eyes, and make us leaders of the God-conscious.

About these duas

Many duas in the Quran begin with “Rabbana” — Our Lord. They are known as the Rabbana duas, and what makes them special is that they are taught in Allah’s own words: even the words for asking come from Him. This page gives the three most recited Rabbana duas with their meaning.

“Rabbana atina…” (2:201) is perhaps the most recited dua among Muslims. A report in Sahih Bukhari says the Prophet ﷺ made this dua most often. It covers everything — good in this world (health, provision, a good family), good in the Hereafter, and protection from the Fire. It is also recited in tawaf between the Yemeni corner and the Black Stone.

“Rabbana la tu’akhidhna…” is part of the last verse of Surah Al-Baqarah. Sahih Bukhari reports the virtue of reciting the last two verses of Al-Baqarah at night. This dua admits human weakness — we forget and we err — and asks Allah for gentleness.

“Rabbana hab lana…” (25:74) is recited for marriage, newlyweds and the good upbringing of children. “Coolness of the eyes” means a family whose sight brings joy to the heart.

How to make dua — the simple way

  • Begin by praising Allah (e.g. “Alhamdulillah”) and sending durood on the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Raise your hands and ask with full conviction and attention — a dua from the heart is the most precious.
  • If you do not know Arabic, you may ask in your own language — Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, English; Allah understands every language.
  • Do not be hasty or say “my dua was not answered”; be patient and keep asking.
  • Pray for your parents, family and the whole ummah along with yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Which surah has “Rabbana atina”?

Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 201.

How many Rabbana duas are in the Quran?

Commonly 40 are counted; this page has the three most recited.

Which dua is for marriage and family?

“Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina…” (25:74).

Can these be recited in salah?

Yes — Quranic duas are recited after the durood in the final sitting; “Rabbana atina” is very common.

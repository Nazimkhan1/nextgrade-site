🚗 Travel Dua and Dua for Leaving Home

When leaving home

Reference: Sunan Abu Dawud, Jami at-Tirmidhi

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

Bismillahi tawakkaltu ‘alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah

In the name of Allah; I put my trust in Allah; there is no power and no strength except with Allah.

On mounting a vehicle / starting a journey

Reference: Quran 43:13–14 (Surah Az-Zukhruf); Sahih Muslim — the Prophet ﷺ said “Allahu Akbar” three times and then this when setting out

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar

Allah is the Greatest (three times).

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ

Subhanal-ladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin

Glory to Him who has made this subject to us, for we could never have done it ourselves,

وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ

Wa inna ila rabbina lamunqalibun

and to our Lord we shall surely return.

About these duas

Whether it is the daily trip to work, a visit to the village or a long train journey, remembering Allah as you step out brings peace of mind. This page gives the dua for leaving home and the travel dua said on a vehicle, in Arabic, in English letters and with the meaning.

Of the dua for leaving home, “Bismillahi tawakkaltu ‘alallah…”, it is reported that the one who says it is told: you are guided, you are provided for and you are protected. It holds three things — Allah’s name, trust in Allah, and the belief that all power is His.

The heart of the travel dua is from the Quran, Surah Az-Zukhruf (43:13–14). The verse speaks of riding animals and ships; today the same dua is said in cars, on bikes, buses, trains and planes. In the report in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ first said “Allahu Akbar” three times, then this verse, and then a longer prayer for goodness, ease on the journey and protection of the family left behind.

Good habits for a long journey: tell your family before you leave, learn the rules of shortening prayers (qasr — two rak‘ahs instead of four) from a scholar, and help others on the way. You can check prayer times for your city on our namaz time page.

How to make dua — the simple way

  • Begin by praising Allah (e.g. “Alhamdulillah”) and sending durood on the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Raise your hands and ask with full conviction and attention — a dua from the heart is the most precious.
  • If you do not know Arabic, you may ask in your own language — Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, English; Allah understands every language.
  • Do not be hasty or say “my dua was not answered”; be patient and keep asking.
  • Pray for your parents, family and the whole ummah along with yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the dua for leaving home?

“Bismillahi tawakkaltu ‘alallah, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah”.

Where is the travel dua in the Quran?

Surah Az-Zukhruf, verses 13 and 14.

Can it be said on a bike or a train?

Yes — on any vehicle: car, bike, bus, train or plane.

What is said on returning from a journey?

The Prophet ﷺ said the same dua and added “Ayibuna, ta’ibuna, ‘abiduna, li-rabbina hamidun” (returning, repenting, worshipping and praising our Lord).

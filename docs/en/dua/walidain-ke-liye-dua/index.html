🤲 Dua for Parents

Dua for mercy on parents

Reference: Quran 17:24 (Surah Al-Isra)

رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira

My Lord, have mercy on them both, as they raised me when I was small.

Dua for forgiveness of parents and all believers

Reference: Quran 14:41 (Surah Ibrahim) — the dua of Prophet Ibrahim (AS)

رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ

Rabbanagh-fir li wa li-walidayya wa lil-mu’minina yawma yaqumul-hisab

Our Lord, forgive me, my parents and the believers on the Day the account is taken.

About these duas

In the Quran, the command to treat parents well comes right after the command to worship Allah. Surah Al-Isra (17:23–24) says: do not say even “uff” to them in old age, speak to them gently, lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy — and pray: “Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira”.

The beauty of this dua is its comparison: just as they raised me when I was small and helpless, O Allah, have mercy on them. A child who says it daily is reminded every day of all their parents have done.

The second is the dua of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) (14:41), which many people also recite in salah after the tashahhud and durood. It asks forgiveness for oneself, one’s parents and all believers.

The same duas are said for parents who have passed away. A hadith tells us that the prayer of a righteous child continues to benefit a person after death. For living parents, the greatest dua is service — spending time with them and caring for their medicines and needs.

How to make dua — the simple way

  • Begin by praising Allah (e.g. “Alhamdulillah”) and sending durood on the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Raise your hands and ask with full conviction and attention — a dua from the heart is the most precious.
  • If you do not know Arabic, you may ask in your own language — Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, English; Allah understands every language.
  • Do not be hasty or say “my dua was not answered”; be patient and keep asking.
  • Pray for your parents, family and the whole ummah along with yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Quranic dua for parents?

“Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira” (17:24) and “Rabbanagh-fir li wa li-walidayya…” (14:41).

Can it be recited for parents who have passed away?

Yes — the prayer of a righteous child benefits parents even after death.

Which one is said in salah?

Many recite “Rabbij‘alni muqimas-salati…” with “Rabbanagh-fir li wa li-walidayya…” after the durood in the final sitting.

When should it be recited daily?

After each prayer or before sleep — any time is fine.

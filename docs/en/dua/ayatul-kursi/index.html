✨ Ayatul Kursi — Transliteration and Meaning

Ayatul Kursi (Surah Al-Baqarah, verse 255)

Reference: Quran 2:255

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ

Allahu la ilaha illa huwal-hayyul-qayyum

Allah — there is no god but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all.

لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ

La ta’khudhuhu sinatun wa la nawm

Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him.

لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ

Lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ard

To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth.

مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِنْدَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ

Man dhal-ladhi yashfa‘u ‘indahu illa bi-idhnih

Who could intercede with Him except by His permission?

يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ

Ya‘lamu ma bayna aydihim wa ma khalfahum

He knows what is before them and what is behind them,

وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ

Wa la yuhituna bi-shay’im-min ‘ilmihi illa bima sha’

and they grasp nothing of His knowledge except what He wills.

وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ

Wasi‘a kursiyyuhus-samawati wal-ard

His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth,

وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ

Wa la ya’uduhu hifzuhuma

and guarding them both does not tire Him.

وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

Wa huwal-‘aliyyul-‘azim

He is the Most High, the Most Great.

About these duas

Ayatul Kursi is verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah, the second surah of the Quran. It is named after the word “Kursi” that appears in it. In a report in Sahih Muslim, the Prophet ﷺ called it the greatest verse of the Quran.

In this single verse Allah is fully described: He is One, Ever-Living, untouched by sleep; the heavens and earth are His; none can intercede without His permission; He knows everything; and holding up the whole universe does not tire Him. That is why it is called the essence of tawhid — belief in One God.

When to recite it: after every obligatory prayer (reported in An-Nasa’i), before sleep (a report in Sahih Bukhari mentions protection through the night), and many also recite it when leaving home or when afraid.

The Arabic above is the authenticated Quran text from Tanzil (tanzil.net), unchanged. The English letters are only an aid — some Arabic sounds (like ع, ح, ق, ض) cannot be written exactly in English, so learning by listening to a qari is best.

How to make dua — the simple way

  • Begin by praising Allah (e.g. “Alhamdulillah”) and sending durood on the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Raise your hands and ask with full conviction and attention — a dua from the heart is the most precious.
  • If you do not know Arabic, you may ask in your own language — Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, English; Allah understands every language.
  • Do not be hasty or say “my dua was not answered”; be patient and keep asking.
  • Pray for your parents, family and the whole ummah along with yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Ayatul Kursi in the Quran?

Surah Al-Baqarah (the 2nd surah), verse 255 — near the start of the 3rd juz.

When should Ayatul Kursi be recited?

After every obligatory prayer and at night before sleeping.

How many parts does it have?

It is usually read in 9–10 phrases; above it is shown in 9 parts following the pause marks (waqf).

Can it be recited without wudu?

Reciting from memory, without touching the mushaf, is generally not held to require wudu; ask a scholar about touching the printed Quran.

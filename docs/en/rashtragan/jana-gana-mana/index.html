🇮🇳 Jana Gana Mana — National Anthem with meaning

India’s national anthem Jana Gana Mana — full text in Roman, Hindi and Bengali script, line-by-line meaning, who wrote it, when it was adopted, playing time (52 and 20 seconds) and the rules of respect.

Full text

Jana-gana-mana adhinayaka jaya he, Bharata-bhagya-vidhata! Punjaba-Sindhu-Gujarata-Maratha, Dravida-Utkala-Banga, Vindhya-Himachala-Yamuna-Ganga, uchchhala-jaladhi-taranga, Tava shubha name jage, tava shubha ashisha mage, gahe tava jaya-gatha. Jana-gana-mangala-dayaka jaya he, Bharata-bhagya-vidhata! Jaya he, jaya he, jaya he, jaya jaya jaya, jaya he.

जन-गण-मन अधिनायक जय हे, भारत-भाग्य-विधाता! पंजाब-सिंधु-गुजरात-मराठा, द्राविड़-उत्कल-बंग, विंध्य-हिमाचल-यमुना-गंगा, उच्छल-जलधि-तरंग, तव शुभ नामे जागे, तव शुभ आशिष मागे, गाहे तव जय-गाथा। जन-गण-मंगलदायक जय हे, भारत-भाग्य-विधाता! जय हे, जय हे, जय हे, जय जय जय जय हे।

Line-by-line meaning

  • Jana-gana-mana adhinayaka jaya he, Bharata-bhagya-vidhata!Victory to you, ruler of the minds of all people, maker of India’s destiny!
  • Punjaba-Sindhu-Gujarata-Maratha, Dravida-Utkala-Banga,Punjab, Sindh, Gujarat, the Maratha country, the Dravida south, Utkal (Odisha) and Bengal —
  • Vindhya-Himachala-Yamuna-Ganga, uchchhala-jaladhi-taranga,the Vindhya and Himalaya mountains, the Yamuna and the Ganga, and the leaping waves of the sea —
  • Tava shubha name jage, tava shubha ashisha mage, gahe tava jaya-gatha.all wake up to your auspicious name, ask for your blessing and sing the song of your victory.
  • Jana-gana-mangala-dayaka jaya he, Bharata-bhagya-vidhata!Victory to you, giver of good to all the people, maker of India’s destiny!
  • Jaya he, jaya he, jaya he, jaya jaya jaya, jaya he.Victory, victory, victory to you — victory for ever!

Key facts

  • Written by: Rabindranath Tagore. The original song is in Bengali (Sanskritised literary Bengali) and has five stanzas; the anthem is only the first stanza.
  • First sung: 27 December 1911, at the Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress.
  • Adopted as the national anthem: 24 January 1950, by the Constituent Assembly.
  • Playing time: the full anthem takes about 52 seconds. On some occasions a short version — the first and last lines — is played in about 20 seconds.

Rules of respect

  • When the anthem is played or sung, stand to attention; do not talk, joke or use your phone during it.
  • Keep the fixed words and tune; changing it or singing only part of it on your own is not right.
  • Intentionally preventing the singing of the anthem or disturbing an assembly singing it is an offence under the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971.
  • Schools usually begin the day with the anthem sung together; on 15 August and 26 January the full anthem follows the flag hoisting.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote Jana Gana Mana?

Rabindranath Tagore, in Bengali. He also composed the tune.

How long is the national anthem?

The full anthem is about 52 seconds; the short version (first and last lines) about 20 seconds.

When was Jana Gana Mana adopted as the anthem?

On 24 January 1950 by the Constituent Assembly.

What is the difference between the national anthem and the national song?

The anthem is “Jana Gana Mana” (Tagore); the national song is “Vande Mataram” (Bankim Chandra). Both get equal respect.

Why does it say “Sindhu” when Sindh is in Pakistan?

The song dates from 1911, when Sindh was part of undivided India. Today “Sindhu” is also read as the Indus river and the culture around it.

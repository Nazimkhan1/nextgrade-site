🚩 Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti (Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Lord Hanuman — 12 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की।
दुष्ट दलन रघुनाथ कला की॥

Aarti keejai Hanuman Lala ki.
Dusht dalan Raghunaath kala ki.

Let us do the aarti of dear Hanuman, who crushes the wicked and is a form of Shri Ram’s power.

Verse 1
जाके बल से गिरिवर काँपे।
रोग दोष जाके निकट न झाँके॥

Jaake bal se girivar kaanpe.
Rog dosh jaake nikat na jhaanke.

Mountains tremble at his strength, and illness and faults dare not even peep near him.

Verse 2
अंजनि पुत्र महा बलदाई।
सन्तन के प्रभु सदा सहाई॥

Anjani putra maha baldaai.
Santan ke prabhu sada sahaai.

The son of Anjani gives great strength and is always the helper of good people.

Verse 3
दे बीरा रघुनाथ पठाए।
लंका जारि सिया सुधि लाए॥

De beera Raghunaath pathaaye.
Lanka jaari Siya sudhi laaye.

Shri Ram gave him the task and sent him; he burned Lanka and brought back news of Sita.

Verse 4
लंका सो कोट समुद्र सी खाई।
जात पवनसुत बार न लाई॥

Lanka so kot samudra si khaai.
Jaat Pavansut baar na laai.

A fortress like Lanka with a moat as wide as the sea — yet the son of the Wind took no time at all to get there.

Verse 5
लंका जारि असुर संहारे।
सियारामजी के काज सँवारे॥

Lanka jaari asur sanhaare.
Siyaraamji ke kaaj sanvaare.

He burned Lanka, destroyed the demons and accomplished the work of Sita and Ram.

Verse 6
लक्ष्मण मूर्छित पड़े सकारे।
आनि सजीवन प्राण उबारे॥

Lakshman moorchhit pade sakaare.
Aani sajeevan praan ubaare.

When Lakshman lay unconscious on the battlefield, Hanuman brought the Sanjeevani herb and saved his life.

Verse 7
पैठि पाताल तोरि जम-कारे।
अहिरावण की भुजा उखारे॥

Paithi paataal tori jam-kaare.
Ahiraavan ki bhuja ukhaare.

He entered the underworld, broke its deadly bonds and tore off Ahiravan’s arms, freeing Ram and Lakshman.

Verse 8
बाएँ भुजा असुरदल मारे।
दाहिने भुजा संतजन तारे॥

Baayein bhuja asurdal maare.
Daahine bhuja santjan taare.

With his left arm he strikes down the demon armies; with his right he carries good people across.

Verse 9
सुर नर मुनि आरती उतारें।
जय जय जय हनुमान उचारें॥

Sur nar muni aarti utaarein.
Jai jai jai Hanuman uchaarein.

Gods, people and sages perform his aarti and call out, “Victory, victory, victory to Hanuman!”

Verse 10
कंचन थार कपूर लौ छाई।
आरती करत अंजना माई॥

Kanchan thaar kapoor lau chhaai.
Aarti karat Anjana maai.

In a golden plate the camphor flame glows, and mother Anjana herself performs the aarti.

Verse 11
जो हनुमानजी की आरती गावे।
बसि बैकुण्ठ परम पद पावे॥

Jo Hanumanji ki aarti gaave.
Basi Baikunth param pad paave.

Whoever sings this aarti of Hanuman dwells in Vaikunth and attains the highest state — so devotees believe.

About Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti

“Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki” is the best-known aarti of Lord Hanuman. It is sung on Tuesdays and Saturdays, on Hanuman Jayanti, and after reading the Hanuman Chalisa or the Sundarkand. “Lala” means a dear child — devotees lovingly call him “Hanuman Lala”.

In a few short verses the aarti retells the main events of the Ramayana: the search for Sita, the leap across the sea, the burning of Lanka, bringing the Sanjeevani herb for Lakshman and defeating Ahiravan in the underworld. That also makes it an easy way to tell children Hanuman’s story.

The author is not known for sure; it is often linked to the Tulsidas tradition, and some books add a last line naming Tulsidas. This page gives the common printed reading; the meanings are our own.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

On which day is Hanuman’s aarti done?

Especially on Tuesdays and Saturdays, and every evening if you like. It is always sung on Hanuman Jayanti.

Aarti first or Hanuman Chalisa first?

Usually the Chalisa is read first and the aarti is sung at the end — aarti closes the puja.

What does “Hanuman Lala” mean?

“Lala” means a beloved child; devotees call Hanuman this out of affection.

🪕 Saraswati Ji Ki Aarti (Jai Saraswati Mata) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Goddess Saraswati — 7 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
जय सरस्वती माता, मैया जय सरस्वती माता।
सदगुण वैभव शालिनी, त्रिभुवन विख्याता॥

Jai Saraswati maata, maiya jai Saraswati maata.
Sadgun vaibhav shaalini, tribhuvan vikhyaata.

Victory to Mother Saraswati, rich in every good quality and splendour, famed in all three worlds.

Verse 1
चंद्रवदनि पद्मासिनि, द्युति मंगलकारी।
सोहे शुभ हंस सवारी, अतुल तेजधारी॥

Chandravadani padmaasini, dyuti mangalkaari.
Sohe shubh hans savaari, atul tejdhaari.

Moon-faced, seated on a lotus, your radiance brings blessings. You ride a lovely swan, shining with matchless light.

Verse 2
बाएँ कर में वीणा, दाएँ कर माला।
शीश मुकुट मणि सोहे, गल मोतियन माला॥

Baayein kar mein veena, daayein kar maala.
Sheesh mukut mani sohe, gal motiyan maala.

A veena in your left hand, a rosary in your right; a jewelled crown on your head and a pearl necklace at your throat.

Verse 3
देवी शरण जो आए, उनका उद्धार किया।
पैठी मंथरा दासी, रावण संहार किया॥

Devi sharan jo aaye, unka uddhaar kiya.
Paithi Manthara daasi, Raavan sanhaar kiya.

O Goddess, you uplift all who come to you. The story goes that you entered the mind of the maid Manthara, which sent Ram to the forest and led in the end to Ravan’s fall.

Verse 4
विद्या ज्ञान प्रदायिनि, ज्ञान प्रकाश भरो।
मोह अज्ञान और तिमिर का, जग से नाश करो॥

Vidya gyaan pradaayini, gyaan prakaash bharo.
Moh agyaan aur timir ka, jag se naash karo.

Giver of learning and wisdom, fill us with the light of knowledge, and wipe attachment, ignorance and darkness from the world.

Verse 5
धूप दीप फल मेवा, माँ स्वीकार करो।
ज्ञानचक्षु दे माता, जग निस्तार करो॥

Dhoop deep phal meva, maa sweekaar karo.
Gyaanchakshu de maata, jag nistaar karo.

Mother, accept the incense, lamp, fruit and dry fruit. Give us the eyes of wisdom and set the world free.

Verse 6
माँ सरस्वती की आरती, जो कोई जन गावे।
हितकारी सुखकारी, ज्ञान भक्ति पावे॥

Maa Saraswati ki aarti, jo koi jan gaave.
Hitkaari sukhkaari, gyaan bhakti paave.

Whoever sings this aarti of Mother Saraswati is blessed and made happy, and gains knowledge and devotion.

About Saraswati Ji Ki Aarti

“Jai Saraswati Mata” is the most popular aarti of Goddess Saraswati, goddess of learning, music and the arts. It is always sung on Vasant Panchami (Saraswati Puja) in schools, colleges and homes, and many schools sing it at morning assembly and before exams.

The aarti draws her form: a moon-like face, a lotus seat, a swan to ride, a veena in one hand and a rosary in the other. The swan is said to separate milk from water — the wisdom to tell right from wrong; the veena stands for music and art, the rosary for focus.

The fourth verse recalls a folk telling of the Ramayana: at the gods’ request Saraswati entered Manthara’s mind, which sent Ram into exile and led to Ravan’s end. No author is known; it comes from tradition. The meanings are our own plain summaries.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Saraswati aarti sung?

On Vasant Panchami (Saraswati Puja), at school assemblies, before starting studies and around exams.

What is Saraswati’s mount?

The swan; some pictures also show a peacock.

When is Vasant Panchami 2027?

On Magh Shukla Panchami, in February 2027 — see our festival pages for the exact date and day.

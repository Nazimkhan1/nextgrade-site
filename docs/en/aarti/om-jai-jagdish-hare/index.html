🪔 Om Jai Jagdish Hare — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Lord Vishnu — 8 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
ॐ जय जगदीश हरे, स्वामी जय जगदीश हरे।
भक्त जनों के संकट, दास जनों के संकट, क्षण में दूर करे॥

Om jai Jagdish hare, swami jai Jagdish hare.
Bhakt janon ke sankat, daas janon ke sankat, kshan mein door kare.

Victory to you, Hari, Lord of the universe. You remove the troubles of your devotees and servants in a moment.

Verse 1
जो ध्यावे फल पावे, दुख बिनसे मन का, स्वामी दुख बिनसे मन का।
सुख सम्पति घर आवे, कष्ट मिटे तन का॥

Jo dhyaave phal paave, dukh binse man ka, swami dukh binse man ka.
Sukh sampati ghar aave, kasht mite tan ka.

Whoever meditates on you is rewarded: sorrow leaves the mind, happiness and plenty come home, and the body’s pain goes away.

Verse 2
मात पिता तुम मेरे, शरण गहूँ मैं किसकी, स्वामी शरण गहूँ मैं किसकी।
तुम बिन और न दूजा, आस करूँ मैं जिसकी॥

Maat pita tum mere, sharan gahoon main kiski, swami sharan gahoon main kiski.
Tum bin aur na dooja, aas karoon main jiski.

You are my mother and father — whose shelter else should I seek? There is no one but you to place my hope in.

Verse 3
तुम पूरण परमात्मा, तुम अन्तर्यामी, स्वामी तुम अन्तर्यामी।
पारब्रह्म परमेश्वर, तुम सब के स्वामी॥

Tum pooran Parmaatma, tum antaryaami, swami tum antaryaami.
Parabrahm Parmeshwar, tum sab ke swami.

You are the complete Supreme Soul who knows every heart from within — the Supreme Brahman, the highest Lord, master of all.

Verse 4
तुम करुणा के सागर, तुम पालनकर्ता, स्वामी तुम पालनकर्ता।
मैं मूरख खल कामी, कृपा करो भर्ता॥

Tum karuna ke saagar, tum paalankarta, swami tum paalankarta.
Main moorakh khal kaami, kripa karo bharta.

You are an ocean of compassion and the one who sustains all. I am foolish and full of desires — O Sustainer, be kind to me.

Verse 5
तुम हो एक अगोचर, सबके प्राणपति, स्वामी सबके प्राणपति।
किस विधि मिलूँ दयामय, तुमको मैं कुमति॥

Tum ho ek agochar, sabke praanpati, swami sabke praanpati.
Kis vidhi miloon dayaamay, tumko main kumati.

You are the One, beyond the senses, lord of every life. O merciful one, how can someone of little understanding like me reach you?

Verse 6
दीन बन्धु दुख हर्ता, ठाकुर तुम मेरे, स्वामी रक्षक तुम मेरे।
अपने हाथ उठाओ, अपने शरण लगाओ, द्वार पड़ा तेरे॥

Deen bandhu dukh harta, Thaakur tum mere, swami rakshak tum mere.
Apne haath uthaao, apne sharan lagaao, dwaar pada tere.

Friend of the helpless and remover of sorrow, you are my Lord and protector. I lie at your door — reach out your hand and take me into your shelter.

Verse 7
विषय विकार मिटाओ, पाप हरो देवा, स्वामी पाप हरो देवा।
श्रद्धा भक्ति बढ़ाओ, सन्तन की सेवा॥

Vishay vikaar mitaao, paap haro deva, swami paap haro deva.
Shraddha bhakti badhaao, santan ki seva.

O Lord, wipe away my cravings and faults, take away my sins, and grow in me faith, devotion and the wish to serve good people.

About Om Jai Jagdish Hare

“Om Jai Jagdish Hare” is probably the most sung aarti in India. It was written around 1870 by Pandit Shardha Ram Phillauri of Phillaur, Punjab. It praises Lord Vishnu (Jagdish, “Lord of the world”), yet it closes almost every puja, katha and festival — Satyanarayan katha, Diwali, a housewarming or the daily evening prayer.

Its strength is its plain language. There is no hard Sanskrit, just simple Hindi — a line like “Maat pita tum mere” (you are my mother and father) is understood even by a child. The repeated “swami …” half-line in every verse lets everyone join in, which is why families and temples sing it together.

A word or two differs between books (for example “main sevak tum swami” instead of “main moorakh khal kaami”), and many homes add a closing verse “tan man dhan sab hai tera”. This page gives the common printed reading; the meanings are our own plain summaries.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote Om Jai Jagdish Hare?

Pandit Shardha Ram Phillauri (1837–1881), around 1870. He was from Phillaur in Punjab.

Which god is this aarti for?

Lord Vishnu — “Jagdish” means Lord of the whole world. It is still sung at the end of almost any puja.

How many verses does it have?

The common reading has 8 verses including the refrain; some families add one or two more.

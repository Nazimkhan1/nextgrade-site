🔱 Shiv Ji Ki Aarti (Om Jai Shiv Omkara) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Lord Shiva — 8 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
ॐ जय शिव ओंकारा, स्वामी जय शिव ओंकारा।
ब्रह्मा विष्णु सदाशिव, अर्द्धांगी धारा॥

Om jai Shiv Omkaara, swami jai Shiv Omkaara.
Brahma Vishnu Sadaashiv, arddhaangi dhaara.

Victory to Shiva, the form of Om. You are Brahma, Vishnu and Sadashiv, and you hold Parvati as half of your own body.

Verse 1
एकानन चतुरानन, पंचानन राजे।
हंसासन गरुड़ासन, वृषवाहन साजे॥

Ekaanan chaturaanan, panchaanan raaje.
Hansaasan garudaasan, vrishvaahan saaje.

One-faced (Vishnu), four-faced (Brahma) and five-faced (Shiva) shine, seated on the swan, on Garuda and on the bull Nandi.

Verse 2
दो भुज चार चतुर्भुज, दसभुज अति सोहे।
त्रिगुण रूप निरखते, त्रिभुवन जन मोहे॥

Do bhuj chaar chaturbhuj, dasbhuj ati sohe.
Trigun roop nirakhte, tribhuvan jan mohe.

Forms with two, four and ten arms look splendid. Seeing your form of the three qualities, people of all three worlds are enchanted.

Verse 3
अक्षमाला वनमाला, मुण्डमाला धारी।
त्रिपुरारी कंसारी, कर माला धारी॥

Akshmaala vanmaala, mundmaala dhaari.
Tripuraari Kansaari, kar maala dhaari.

Wearing a rudraksha rosary, a garland of forest flowers and a garland of skulls — Shiva, destroyer of Tripura, and Vishnu, slayer of Kansa, hold rosaries in hand.

Verse 4
श्वेताम्बर पीताम्बर, बाघम्बर अंगे।
सनकादिक गरुड़ादिक, भूतादिक संगे॥

Shvetaambar peetaambar, baaghambar ange.
Sanakaadik Garudaadik, bhootaadik sange.

Dressed in white, in yellow and in tiger skin; accompanied by sages like Sanaka, by Garuda and by Shiva’s band of spirits.

Verse 5
कर के मध्य कमण्डलु, चक्र त्रिशूलधारी।
सुखकारी दुखहारी, जगपालन कारी॥

Kar ke madhya kamandalu, chakra trishooldhaari.
Sukhkaari dukhhaari, jagpaalan kaari.

In your hands are the water pot, the discus and the trident. You give happiness, remove sorrow and look after the world.

Verse 6
ब्रह्मा विष्णु सदाशिव, जानत अविवेका।
प्रणवाक्षर में शोभित, ये तीनों एका॥

Brahma Vishnu Sadaashiv, jaanat aviveka.
Pranavaakshar mein shobhit, ye teenon eka.

Only the unwise think Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva are separate; in the sacred syllable Om all three shine as one.

Verse 7
त्रिगुणस्वामी जी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावे।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख संपत्ति पावे॥

Trigunswaami ji ki aarti, jo koi nar gaave.
Kahat Shivaanand swami, sukh sampatti paave.

Swami Shivanand says: whoever sings this aarti of the Lord of the three qualities gains happiness and prosperity.

About Shiv Ji Ki Aarti

“Om Jai Shiv Omkara” is the most sung aarti of Lord Shiva. It is sung on Mondays, through the month of Sawan, on the night of Maha Shivratri and on Pradosh vrat, in Shiva temples and at home. The last verse says “kahat Shivanand swami”, so it is credited to Swami Shivanand.

Its deepest idea sits in the seventh verse: “Brahma Vishnu Sadashiv, jaanat aviveka” — only the unwise see them as separate; in Om the three are one. That is why the aarti mixes the signs of all three: Vishnu (Garuda, discus, yellow cloth, slayer of Kansa), Brahma (swan, four faces) and Shiva (Nandi, trident, tiger skin).

The order of verses and a few words differ between books, and many people sing “Jai Shiv Omkara, Har Shiv Omkara”. This page gives the common printed reading; the meanings are our own plain summaries.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote Om Jai Shiv Omkara?

Its last verse names Swami Shivanand, so it is credited to him.

When is Shiva’s aarti sung?

On Mondays, in the month of Sawan, on Maha Shivratri and Pradosh vrat — and in any evening puja.

Why does a Shiva aarti mention Vishnu and Brahma?

Its message is that Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva are one in Om, so it remembers all three together.

🐘 Ganesh Ji Ki Aarti (Jai Ganesh Deva) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Lord Ganesh — 6 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
जय गणेश जय गणेश, जय गणेश देवा।
माता जाकी पार्वती, पिता महादेवा॥

Jai Ganesh jai Ganesh, jai Ganesh deva.
Maata jaaki Paarvati, pita Mahaadeva.

Victory to Lord Ganesh, whose mother is Parvati and whose father is Mahadev (Shiva).

Verse 1
एक दन्त दयावन्त, चार भुजा धारी।
माथे सिन्दूर सोहे, मूसे की सवारी॥

Ek dant dayaavant, chaar bhuja dhaari.
Maathe sindoor sohe, moose ki savaari.

You have one tusk, you are kind, and you have four arms. Vermilion shines on your forehead and a mouse is your mount.

Verse 2
पान चढ़े फूल चढ़े, और चढ़े मेवा।
लड्डुअन का भोग लगे, सन्त करें सेवा॥

Paan chadhe phool chadhe, aur chadhe meva.
Ladduan ka bhog lage, sant karein seva.

Betel leaves, flowers and dry fruits are offered to you; laddoos are your favourite offering, and saints serve you.

Verse 3
अंधन को आँख देत, कोढ़िन को काया।
बाँझन को पुत्र देत, निर्धन को माया॥

Andhan ko aankh det, kodhin ko kaaya.
Baanjhan ko putra det, nirdhan ko maaya.

Devotees believe you give sight to the blind, a healthy body to the sick, a child to those longing for one, and wealth to the poor.

Verse 4
दीनन की लाज रखो, शम्भु सुतकारी।
कामना को पूर्ण करो, जाऊँ बलिहारी॥

Deenan ki laaj rakho, Shambhu sutkaari.
Kaamna ko poorn karo, jaaun balihaari.

O son of Shiva, protect the honour of the humble and fulfil our wishes — we offer ourselves to you.

Verse 5
सूर श्याम शरण आए, सफल कीजे सेवा।
माता जाकी पार्वती, पिता महादेवा॥

Soor Shyaam sharan aaye, saphal keeje seva.
Maata jaaki Paarvati, pita Mahaadeva.

Soor, devotee of Shyam, has come to your shelter — make our worship fruitful. Your mother is Parvati, your father Mahadev.

About Ganesh Ji Ki Aarti

“Jai Ganesh Jai Ganesh Deva” is the best-known aarti of Lord Ganesh. In Hindu tradition every good beginning starts with Ganesh, so this aarti is sung first at Ganesh Chaturthi, at the Lakshmi–Ganesh puja on Diwali, at weddings, at the opening of a shop or a new home. It is also sung on Wednesdays and on Sankashti Chaturthi each month.

The last verse mentions “Soor Shyam”, so many link it to the tradition of the poet-saint Surdas, but no author is known for certain — it comes down from folk and temple tradition. The language is simple Hindi with Braj and Awadhi words.

The aarti paints Ganesh’s form: one tusk (Ekdant), four arms, vermilion on the forehead, a mouse to ride and laddoos as his offering. A line or two changes from place to place (for example “maathe par tilak sohe”). The meanings here are our own plain summaries.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Ganesh aarti sung?

At the start of any puja, through the ten days of Ganesh Chaturthi, on Wednesdays and on Sankashti Chaturthi.

What food is offered to Ganesh?

Modak and laddoos — the aarti itself says “ladduan ka bhog lage”. Durva grass is also offered.

Why is Ganesh called Ekdant?

He has one broken tusk. Stories give different reasons, such as breaking it to use as a pen while writing the Mahabharata.

🪷 Lakshmi Ji Ki Aarti (Om Jai Lakshmi Mata) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Goddess Lakshmi — 8 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता, मैया जय लक्ष्मी माता।
तुमको निसदिन सेवत, हर विष्णु विधाता॥

Om jai Lakshmi maata, maiya jai Lakshmi maata.
Tumko nisdin sevat, Har Vishnu Vidhaata.

Victory to you, Mother Lakshmi. Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma honour you day and night.

Verse 1
उमा रमा ब्रह्माणी, तुम ही जग-माता।
सूर्य-चन्द्रमा ध्यावत, नारद ऋषि गाता॥

Uma Rama Brahmaani, tum hi jag-maata.
Soorya chandrama dhyaavat, Naarad rishi gaata.

Uma, Rama and Brahmani — you alone are the Mother of the world. Sun and moon meditate on you and the sage Narad sings your praise.

Verse 2
दुर्गा रूप निरंजनी, सुख सम्पत्ति दाता।
जो कोई तुमको ध्यावत, ऋद्धि-सिद्धि धन पाता॥

Durga roop niranjani, sukh sampatti daata.
Jo koi tumko dhyaavat, riddhi-siddhi dhan paata.

In your pure Durga form you give happiness and prosperity. Whoever meditates on you gains success, abundance and wealth.

Verse 3
तुम पाताल-निवासिनि, तुम ही शुभदाता।
कर्म-प्रभाव-प्रकाशिनी, भवनिधि की त्राता॥

Tum paataal-nivaasini, tum hi shubhdaata.
Karm-prabhaav-prakaashini, bhavnidhi ki traata.

You dwell even in the underworld and bring all that is good. You reveal the fruit of our actions and carry us across the ocean of worldly life.

Verse 4
जिस घर में तुम रहतीं, सब सद्गुण आता।
सब सम्भव हो जाता, मन नहीं घबराता॥

Jis ghar mein tum rahteen, sab sadgun aata.
Sab sambhav ho jaata, man nahin ghabraata.

In the home where you live, every good quality arrives, everything becomes possible and the mind does not panic.

Verse 5
तुम बिन यज्ञ न होते, वस्त्र न कोई पाता।
खान-पान का वैभव, सब तुमसे आता॥

Tum bin yagya na hote, vastra na koi paata.
Khaan-paan ka vaibhav, sab tumse aata.

Without you no sacrifice can be held and no one gets clothes; all the plenty of food and drink comes from you.

Verse 6
शुभ-गुण मन्दिर सुन्दर, क्षीरोदधि-जाता।
रत्न चतुर्दश तुम बिन, कोई नहीं पाता॥

Shubh-gun mandir sundar, ksheerodadhi-jaata.
Ratna chaturdash tum bin, koi nahin paata.

You are a beautiful temple of good qualities, born from the ocean of milk. Without you no one could gain the fourteen jewels of the churning of the ocean.

Verse 7
महालक्ष्मी जी की आरती, जो कोई नर गाता।
उर आनन्द समाता, पाप उतर जाता॥

Mahaalakshmi ji ki aarti, jo koi nar gaata.
Ur aanand samaata, paap utar jaata.

Whoever sings this aarti of Mahalakshmi has a heart filled with joy, and their sins fall away.

About Lakshmi Ji Ki Aarti

“Om Jai Lakshmi Mata” is the aarti of Goddess Lakshmi, giver of wealth, prosperity and good fortune. The Lakshmi–Ganesh puja on Diwali night ends with this aarti. It is also sung on Fridays, on Dhanteras, on Sharad Purnima (Kojagari) and during the Vaibhav Lakshmi vrat, and at the puja of new account books in shops and offices.

The aarti makes clear that Lakshmi is not just money — “jis ghar mein tum rahteen, sab sadgun aata”: where she lives, good qualities, courage and peace come too. It also recalls the churning of the ocean, from which Lakshmi rose out of the sea of milk, counted among the fourteen jewels.

No author is known; it comes from tradition. In “Har Vishnu Vidhaata”, Har is Shiva and Vidhaata is Brahma. The order of verses differs in some books. The meanings are our own plain summaries.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

When is Lakshmi’s aarti sung?

Especially on Diwali night, Dhanteras, Fridays and Sharad Purnima — and in any evening puja.

On Diwali, Ganesh aarti first or Lakshmi aarti?

Usually Ganesh is worshipped first, then Lakshmi, and the puja closes with “Om Jai Jagdish Hare”.

What does “Har Vishnu Vidhaata” mean?

Har is Shiva, then Vishnu, and Vidhaata is Brahma — even the three great gods serve Lakshmi.

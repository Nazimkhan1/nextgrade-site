🦁 Durga Ji Ki Aarti (Jai Ambe Gauri) — lyrics with meaning

The aarti of Goddess Durga — 13 verses, each in Devanagari, in English letters and with a simple English meaning. The first verse is the refrain, repeated after each verse.

Refrain
जय अम्बे गौरी, मैया जय श्यामा गौरी।
तुमको निसदिन ध्यावत, हरि ब्रह्मा शिवरी॥

Jai Ambe Gauri, maiya jai Shyaama Gauri.
Tumko nisdin dhyaavat, Hari Brahma Shivri.

Victory to Mother Ambe Gauri, to Shyama Gauri. Vishnu, Brahma and Shiva meditate on you day and night.

Verse 1
माँग सिन्दूर विराजत, टीको मृगमद को।
उज्ज्वल से दोउ नैना, चन्द्रवदन नीको॥

Maang sindoor viraajat, teeko mrigmad ko.
Ujjval se dou naina, chandravadan neeko.

Vermilion in your hair parting, a musk mark on your forehead, two bright eyes and a face lovely as the moon.

Verse 2
कनक समान कलेवर, रक्ताम्बर राजै।
रक्तपुष्प गल माला, कण्ठन पर साजै॥

Kanak samaan kalevar, raktaambar raajai.
Raktpushp gal maala, kanthan par saajai.

Your body glows like gold, red garments adorn you and a garland of red flowers graces your neck.

Verse 3
केहरि वाहन राजत, खड्ग खप्पर धारी।
सुर-नर-मुनि-जन सेवत, तिनके दुखहारी॥

Kehari vaahan raajat, khadg khappar dhaari.
Sur-nar-muni-jan sevat, tinke dukhhaari.

You ride a lion and hold a sword and a skull-bowl. Gods, people and sages serve you, and you take away their sorrows.

Verse 4
कानन कुण्डल शोभित, नासाग्रे मोती।
कोटिक चन्द्र दिवाकर, सम राजत ज्योती॥

Kaanan kundal shobhit, naasaagre moti.
Kotik chandra divaakar, sam raajat jyoti.

Earrings shine in your ears and a pearl at your nose; your radiance glows like millions of moons and suns.

Verse 5
शुम्भ-निशुम्भ बिदारे, महिषासुर घाती।
धूम्र विलोचन नैना, निसदिन मदमाती॥

Shumbh-Nishumbh bidaare, Mahishaasur ghaati.
Dhoomra vilochan naina, nisdin madmaati.

You tore apart Shumbh and Nishumbh and slew Mahishasur; you also destroyed the demon Dhumralochan, and are ever filled with bliss.

Verse 6
चण्ड-मुण्ड संहारे, शोणित बीज हरे।
मधु-कैटभ दोउ मारे, सुर भयहीन करे॥

Chand-Mund sanhaare, Shonit Beej hare.
Madhu-Kaitabh dou maare, sur bhayheen kare.

You destroyed Chand and Mund and Raktabeej, killed both Madhu and Kaitabh, and freed the gods from fear.

Verse 7
ब्रह्माणी रुद्राणी, तुम कमला रानी।
आगम निगम बखानी, तुम शिव पटरानी॥

Brahmaani Rudraani, tum Kamla raani.
Aagam nigam bakhaani, tum Shiv patraani.

You are Brahmani, Rudrani and Kamala (Lakshmi). The scriptures sing your glory; you are Shiva’s chief queen.

Verse 8
चौंसठ योगिनी गावत, नृत्य करत भैरों।
बाजत ताल मृदंगा, अरु बाजत डमरू॥

Chaunsath yogini gaavat, nritya karat Bhairon.
Baajat taal mridanga, aru baajat damroo.

The sixty-four yoginis sing, Bhairav dances, and cymbals, drums and the damru play.

Verse 9
तुम ही जग की माता, तुम ही हो भरता।
भक्तन की दुख हरता, सुख सम्पति करता॥

Tum hi jag ki maata, tum hi ho bharta.
Bhaktan ki dukh harta, sukh sampati karta.

You are the Mother of the world and its sustainer. You remove your devotees’ sorrow and give happiness and plenty.

Verse 10
भुजा चार अति शोभित, वर मुद्रा धारी।
मनवांछित फल पावत, सेवत नर नारी॥

Bhuja chaar ati shobhit, var mudra dhaari.
Manvaanchhit phal paavat, sevat nar naari.

Your four arms are beautiful, one raised in blessing. Men and women who serve you receive what their hearts wish for.

Verse 11
कंचन थाल विराजत, अगर कपूर बाती।
श्रीमालकेतु में राजत, कोटि रतन ज्योती॥

Kanchan thaal viraajat, agar kapoor baati.
Shri Maalketu mein raajat, koti ratan jyoti.

In a golden plate burn incense, camphor and wicks. In the shrine of Shri Malketu shines a light like millions of jewels.

Verse 12
श्री अम्बेजी की आरती, जो कोई नर गावै।
कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख-सम्पति पावै॥

Shri Ambeji ki aarti, jo koi nar gaavai.
Kahat Shivaanand swami, sukh-sampati paavai.

Swami Shivanand says: whoever sings this aarti of Mother Ambe gains happiness and prosperity.

About Durga Ji Ki Aarti

“Jai Ambe Gauri” is the best-known aarti of Goddess Durga. Through the nine days of Navratri it is sung every morning and evening, and always at Durga Puja, jagratas and Mata ki chowki. Its last verse says “kahat Shivanand swami”, so it is credited to Swami Shivanand — the same name found in “Om Jai Shiv Omkara”.

The first verses describe the Mother — vermilion in her parting, red clothes, riding a lion, sword in hand. The middle verses retell the Durga Saptashati (Devi Mahatmya): the slaying of Mahishasur, Shumbh–Nishumbh, Chand–Mund, Raktabeej and Madhu–Kaitabh. In short, the aarti is the whole Devi story in miniature.

A few words change from place to place (for example “madmaati/madhumaati”); this is the common printed reading. The meanings are our own plain summaries. Bengal’s Durga Puja has its own Sanskrit arati with the dhak, but this Hindi aarti is widely sung at home there too.

How to do aarti — the simple way

The lamp plate is moved clockwise in front of the idol or picture. One common way: four times before the feet, twice before the navel, once before the face, then seven times around the whole form. Every family has its own custom — what your home does is right.

Frequently asked questions

Who wrote Jai Ambe Gauri?

Its last verse names Swami Shivanand, so it is credited to him.

When is Durga’s aarti sung?

Every morning and evening of Navratri, at Durga Puja, on Ashtami–Navami, at jagratas, and at Friday or Tuesday pujas.

Which demons does the aarti mention?

Mahishasur, Shumbh–Nishumbh, Dhumralochan, Chand–Mund, Raktabeej and Madhu–Kaitabh — all stories from the Durga Saptashati.

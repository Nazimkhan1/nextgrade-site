☀️ 3 kW solar system: cost, subsidy and savings

3 kW solar: subsidy ₹78,000, about ₹87,000 – ₹1,32,000 after subsidy

ItemValue
Power (per month)~360 units (315–450)
Roof area~300 sq ft
Total cost₹1,65,000 – ₹2,10,000
Subsidy (general states)₹78,000
Subsidy (special category)₹85,800

Savings (₹7/unit, per month)~₹2,520
Good for~250–360 units a month (cooler or one AC)

How it is calculated

  • Subsidy (PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, residential rooftop): ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW — so 1 kW = ₹30,000, 2 kW = ₹60,000, 3 kW or more = ₹78,000 (capped).
  • Special category states/UTs (North-East, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep): ₹33,000 per kW up to 2 kW + ₹19,800 for the 3rd kW = up to ₹85,800.
  • 1 kW of solar in India makes about 4 units a day on average, ~120 units a month (3.5–5 a day depending on sunshine and season).
  • Each kW needs roughly 100 sq ft of shadow-free roof.
  • Installed cost (on-grid, with net meter) is typically ₹55,000–70,000 per kW (November 2026 estimate) — varies by vendor and panel.
  • Portal guidance: 0–150 units a month → 1–2 kW, 150–300 → 2–3 kW, above 300 → more than 3 kW.

How to apply for the subsidy

  1. Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in with your state, electricity company and consumer number.
  2. Choose a registered vendor on the portal and get the rooftop system installed.
  3. Your discom installs the net meter and inspects the system.
  4. After commissioning, submit your bank details — the subsidy is paid directly to your account.

⚠️ This is only an estimate. Subsidy rules and amounts can change — always check pmsuryaghar.gov.in before applying. Some states add their own subsidy on top. Apply only on the government portal and never share an OTP or pay anyone who promises to "get you the subsidy".

Last checked: 26 Nov 2026 · Source: pmsuryaghar.gov.in (MNRE) · NextGrade is not a government website.

How much subsidy does PM Surya Ghar give?

₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW or larger (up to ₹85,800 in special category states).

How many kW of solar for 300 units a month?

About 3 kW — it makes roughly 360 units a month.

How much power does 1 kW of solar produce?

About 4 units a day on average, roughly 120 units a month.

📱 Application to change mobile number in bank

Fill in your name, bank, branch, account number and old and new mobile numbers — a letter to the branch manager is ready.

Fill in your details

🔒 What you type stays in this page — nothing is sent or saved.

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Branch Manager, {bank}, {branch} Branch. Date: {today} Subject: Request to change the registered mobile number Dear Sir/Madam, I hold a savings account in your branch, account number {acno}. My old mobile number {oldmob} registered with this account is no longer in use. Kindly update my registered mobile number to {newmob} so that I continue to receive transaction alerts and OTPs. A copy of my ID proof is attached. Thank you. Yours faithfully, {name} A/c No. {acno} Address: {address}

About this letter

Every OTP, transaction SMS and UPI payment runs on the mobile number linked to your account. If that number is closed or changed, update it quickly or net banking and UPI may stop working.

Many banks let you do this at an ATM, in net banking or in the app, but a written application at the branch is the surest way. Carry ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN) and your passbook; the bank may also ask you to fill its own form.

If no number is linked to the account yet, change the subject to “Request to register my mobile number” and drop the line about the old number. For a joint account, get every holder to sign. You can add one more line to update your email address in the same letter. After the change, check that an SMS arrives for your next transaction; if not, visit the branch again with the acknowledgement. Never share the OTP the bank sends to confirm the new number with anyone who calls you.

Keep in mind

  • Sign exactly as in the bank’s records.
  • Attach a self-attested copy of your ID.
  • Preferably use a number registered in your own name.
  • You may need to register UPI again after the change.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a mobile number change take?

Usually 1–3 working days; many banks do it the same day.

Can I change it if the old number is closed?

Yes, with an application and ID proof at the branch.

Which documents are needed?

Usually the passbook and ID such as Aadhaar or PAN; the bank may ask for its own form.

What if no number was linked at all?

Write “Request to register my mobile number” in the subject and remove the old-number line; the rest stays the same.

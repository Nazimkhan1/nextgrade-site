🏢 Leave application for office

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Manager, {company}. Date: {today} Subject: Application for {days} days’ leave Dear Sir/Madam, I, {name}, {designation} (Employee ID: {empid}), am unable to attend office from {from} to {to} ({days} days) due to {reason}. I request you to kindly grant me leave for these days. I will be reachable on phone for anything urgent. Thank you. Yours sincerely, {name} {designation}

About this letter

Office leave is mostly asked for by email or an HR portal, but many government and small offices still take a written letter. Either way keep it short and polite: how many days, from when to when, and who covers your work.

For sick leave you usually inform by phone or message first and send the application afterwards. Many companies ask for a medical certificate for more than two or three days — check your leave policy.

The same letter works for urgent personal work or a family function — just change the reason. To ask for work from home instead, replace “unable to attend office” with “request permission to work from home on these days”. For half a day, use one date and mention the time you need to leave. If you are writing an email, put the dates in the subject line, keep the body to three or four lines, and copy your team lead or HR if your company expects it. Reply to any question from your manager quickly so the leave can be approved in time.

Keep in mind

  • Use a clear email subject: “Sick leave on 12–13 March”.
  • Say in one line who is covering your urgent work.
  • Mention how you can be reached.
  • Attach a doctor’s certificate for longer leave.

Which reason to write, and how

  • For illness, inform by phone or message first and send the application after.
  • Give the reason in one line; no need for private details.
  • Many companies ask for a medical certificate for sick leave longer than 2–3 days.
  • Ask for work from home only when your work can really be done from home.

Frequently asked questions

When should I send a sick leave application?

As early as possible — for sudden illness, call or message the same day and send the application when you can.

Can I apply by email?

Yes, most offices accept email; paste this letter into the email.

Is a medical certificate needed?

It depends on company policy; usually for sick leave longer than two or three days.

How do I ask for half-day leave?

Use one date, write “half-day leave” in the subject and mention from what time.

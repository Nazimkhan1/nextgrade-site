🙏 Application for fee concession

Fill in your name, father’s name, class and approximate family income — a fee concession letter to the principal is ready.

Fill in your details

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Principal, {school}, {city}. Date: {today} Subject: Application for fee concession Respected Sir/Madam, I, {name}, son/daughter of Mr. {father}, am a student of Class {cls} (Roll No. {roll}) in your school. My family’s financial condition is weak; our yearly income is about {income}, which makes it hard to meet both household expenses and my school fees. I am sincere in my studies and will keep working hard for good marks. I humbly request you to grant me a fee concession so that my studies can continue without a break. Thanking you. Yours obediently, {name} Class {cls}, Roll No. {roll}

About this letter

When family income is low or a sudden hardship hits (illness, job loss), a student can request the principal for a full or partial fee concession. Many schools have a trust, scholarship or “freeship” for this.

Honesty and politeness matter most: state the family situation clearly, mention your effort or good marks, and attach documents like an income certificate. This letter is also a common exam question.

If you want a partial concession rather than a full waiver, write “partial fee concession” or “waiver of three months’ fees” in the last paragraph. To pay in instalments instead, ask for “permission to deposit the fees in instalments” — many schools agree to that easily. Submit the letter early in the session, before fee deadlines and late fines start, and keep a copy with the school’s receiving stamp. If the school has a scholarship committee, ask when it meets so you can follow up at the right time.

Keep in mind

  • Mention good marks or attendance if you have them.
  • Attach an income certificate or a BPL/ration card copy.
  • Ask the school about government scholarships too.
  • Get a parent’s signature on the letter.

Frequently asked questions

To whom do I write for fee concession?

To the school principal; in college, to the principal of the college.

Can the whole fee be waived?

It depends on school policy — full, half or a few months’ concession.

Which documents should I attach?

Income certificate, BPL/ration card, or proof of the hardship (e.g. a doctor’s note).

Can I ask to pay fees in instalments?

Yes — ask for “permission to deposit the fees in instalments” in the last paragraph; many schools allow it.

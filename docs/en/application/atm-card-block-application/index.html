💳 Application to block an ATM card

If your card is lost, block it immediately through your bank’s app or helpline first. Then give this application at the branch for the record and for a new card.

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Branch Manager, {bank}, {branch} Branch. Date: {today} Subject: Request to block my ATM/debit card and issue a new card Dear Sir/Madam, I hold a savings account in your branch, account number {acno}. My ATM/debit card for this account (last four digits {card4}) was lost on {lostdate}. Please block this card permanently at once and issue me a new card. I will not be responsible for any transaction made with the card after it was lost. Thank you. Yours faithfully, {name} A/c No. {acno} Mobile: {mobile}

About this letter

When an ATM or debit card is lost, every minute counts. The fastest way is your bank’s mobile app, net banking or the 24×7 helpline printed on the card — the card is blocked instantly. Many banks also allow blocking by SMS.

After blocking, a written application at the branch is useful: it creates a record, and you can ask for a new card in the same letter. If any transaction you did not make has happened, report it to the bank at once and on cybercrime.gov.in or the 1930 helpline.

If the card was stolen rather than lost, write “stolen” instead of “lost” and attach a copy of the police report. If the card is damaged (cracked, chip not working), change the subject to “Request for a replacement ATM card” and say you are returning the old card instead of asking to block it. Keep the acknowledgement the branch gives you, and check your account statement for the next few days. If the new card arrives by post, activate it only through the bank’s own ATM or app and set a fresh PIN.

Keep in mind

  • Block the card first, write the application after — don’t wait.
  • Write only the last 4 digits of the card number.
  • If money was taken without your consent, call 1930 immediately.
  • No bank employee ever asks for your PIN or OTP on the phone.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first if my ATM card is lost?

Block it immediately through the bank app, net banking or the 24×7 helpline.

How do I get a new card?

Through a branch application or “Reissue card” in the app; many banks charge a small fee.

What if a wrong transaction happened?

Complain to the bank in writing at once and report on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.

What if the card is only damaged?

Ask for a replacement card in the subject and return the old card; no blocking is needed.

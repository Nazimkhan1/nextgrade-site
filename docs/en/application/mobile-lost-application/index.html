🚓 Mobile lost application (to the police)

Fill in the phone model, IMEI number, date and place it was lost — a letter to the station house officer is ready. The steps to block the phone are below.

Fill in your details

🔒 What you type stays in this page — nothing is sent or saved.

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Station House Officer, {thana} Police Station. Date: {today} Subject: Report of a lost mobile phone Sir/Madam, I, {name}, son/daughter of Mr. {father}, resident of {address}, lost my mobile phone at {lostplace} on {lostdate}. Its details are: Model: {model} IMEI number: {imei} I could not find it despite searching. Kindly register my complaint and give me a copy so that I can block the SIM and the phone. I will not be responsible for any misuse of the phone. Thank you. Yours faithfully, {name} Mobile (contact): {mobile}

About this letter

When a phone is lost, first get the SIM blocked (through your operator’s customer care) so the number can’t be misused for OTPs or UPI. Then report to the police — many states let you file a “lost report” online, elsewhere you give a written application at the police station.

With a copy of the police report, block the phone’s IMEI on the Government of India’s CEIR portal (ceir.gov.in). The phone then won’t work on any network, and you can unblock it there if it is found. The IMEI is printed on the phone’s box and bill.

If the phone was stolen (snatched or pick-pocketed) rather than lost, write “stolen” instead of “lost”; theft is registered as an FIR. If your wallet, Aadhaar or ATM card went with the phone, list them in the same letter — the report copy then helps you get duplicates.

Keep in mind

  • Block the SIM first and get a new SIM with the same number.
  • Change UPI/bank app passwords; tell your bank if needed.
  • Block the IMEI on ceir.gov.in with the police report copy.
  • On Android, try “Find My Device” to locate or erase the phone.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I report a lost mobile?

To the SHO of the police station where it was lost; many states also take online lost reports.

Where do I find the IMEI number?

On the phone’s box, the purchase bill, or in your Google account’s “Find My Device”.

How do I block the phone?

Use “Block Stolen/Lost Mobile” on ceir.gov.in with the police report copy, ID and IMEI.

What if my Aadhaar or ATM card was lost with the phone?

List them in the same letter; the report copy helps you get duplicate cards.

📄 Application for a Transfer Certificate (TC)

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Principal, {school}, {city}. Date: {today} Subject: Application for Transfer Certificate (TC) Respected Sir/Madam, I, {name}, son of Mr. {father}, am a student of Class {cls} (Roll No. {roll}) in your school. Due to {reason}, I will not be able to continue my studies here. Kindly issue me my Transfer Certificate. No fees or library books are due from me. Thanking you. Yours obediently, {name} Class {cls}, Roll No. {roll}

About this letter

When a student changes schools, the new school asks for the old school’s Transfer Certificate (TC). It states the class studied up to, that no fees are due and the student’s conduct. You get it by giving the principal a written application.

Common reasons are a parent’s transfer, the family moving to another city, or further studies at another school. The application can be written by a parent or guardian; for young children that is the usual way.

If a parent is writing, begin with “I am the father/mother of …, a student of Class … in your school” and end with “Yours faithfully” and the parent’s name. In college, the same letter works for a Migration or Transfer Certificate. Ask the school office whether a character certificate is issued along with the TC, since many new schools ask for both at admission time. Keep two photocopies of the TC; the original usually stays with the new school.

Keep in mind

  • The school clears pending fees and library books before issuing a TC.
  • Check the date of birth and name spelling on the TC carefully — it follows you everywhere.
  • Ask for a copy of the marksheet/report card along with the TC.
  • For admission in another state, the TC may need the District Education Officer’s counter-signature.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of TC?

Transfer Certificate.

How long does it take to get a TC?

Usually 2–7 days; pending fees must be paid first.

Can parents write the application?

Yes, an application from a parent or guardian is accepted; that is usual for young children.

How does a parent write the TC application?

Start with “I am the father/mother of …”, give the child’s class and roll number, and sign as the parent with “Yours faithfully”.

🏦 Application to close a bank account

Fill in your name, bank, branch, account number and reason — a closure letter to the branch manager is ready.

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Branch Manager, {bank}, {branch} Branch. Date: {today} Subject: Request to close my savings account Dear Sir/Madam, I hold a savings account in your branch, account number {acno}. Due to {reason}, I wish to close this account. Kindly close the account and pay me the balance amount. I am returning the passbook, unused cheque leaves and ATM card with this letter. Thank you. Yours faithfully, {name} A/c No. {acno} Address: {address}

About this letter

An unused account keeps losing money to minimum-balance penalties and yearly card fees, so closing it makes sense. You give a written application (or the bank’s account closure form) at the branch.

Before closing, check that no EMI, SIP, insurance, gas subsidy or government scheme money is linked to the account — move them to another account first. The balance is paid in cash, by transfer to another account or by demand draft.

If you want the balance sent to another account, add that account number, bank name and IFSC to the letter. Closing the account of a holder who has died is a different process — it needs the nominee’s or legal heir’s claim, a death certificate and the bank’s claim form. Before you hand over the letter, download or print the last statement of the account; you may need it later for tax records or as proof of old transactions, and it is harder to get once the account is closed.

Keep in mind

  • Take the passbook, unused cheque leaves and ATM card — the bank takes them back.
  • A joint account needs every holder’s signature.
  • Some banks charge a fee if you close within a year of opening.
  • Get an acknowledgement of the closure.

Frequently asked questions

How long does closing an account take?

Usually the same day or a few working days.

Can I close it online?

A few banks allow it in the app, but most need a branch visit.

How will I get the remaining money?

In cash, by transfer to your other account or by demand draft — say which in the letter.

How do I get the balance moved to another account?

Write: “Please transfer the balance to my account no. …, bank …, IFSC ….”

🏫 Leave application for school

Letter language:

Your letter

To The Principal, {school}, {city}. Date: {today} Subject: Application for {days} days’ leave Respected Sir/Madam, I am a student of Class {cls} in your school. Due to {reason}, I will not be able to attend school from {from} to {to} ({days} days). Kindly grant me leave for these days. I will complete the missed lessons with the help of my classmates. Thanking you. Yours obediently, {name} Class {cls}, Roll No. {roll}

About this letter

To take leave from school you give a written application to the principal or class teacher. “Write an application for leave” is also the most common letter question in exams, so the correct format matters.

It is a formal letter with a fixed order: “To”, the principal’s title and school address, the date, the subject, the salutation (“Sir/Madam”), the body, thanks, and finally “Yours obediently” with your name, class and roll number.

You can adapt the same letter: for a sibling’s wedding write “my elder sister’s wedding” as the reason, for illness “high fever”, and for a trip home “urgent work at my native place”. If the leave has already been taken, change “will not be able to attend” to “could not attend” — that turns it into an application for leave already taken. For half a day, keep one date and say “half-day leave” in the subject. Keep the tone respectful and the letter short; principals read many of these every day, so the subject line and dates should be clear at a glance.

Keep in mind

  • Write a one-line subject — “Application for two days’ leave”.
  • State the number of days and the exact dates.
  • For a long sick leave, attach the doctor’s note.
  • For young children, get a parent’s signature on the application.

Which reason to write, and how

  • Write the real reason — trust breaks if the teacher learns otherwise.
  • Keep the reason short: a phrase is enough, like “high fever” or “my elder sister’s wedding”.
  • For 3 or more days of illness, attach the doctor’s note.
  • For leave you know about in advance (a wedding, a trip home) give the letter a few days before; after an illness, give the “leave already taken” letter when you return.
  • For a competition, attach the organiser’s letter or notice.

Frequently asked questions

To whom do I write a leave application?

To the principal; in many schools the class teacher also accepts it.

How do I end the application?

With “Yours obediently”, then your name, class, roll number and date.

Can I use this format in exams?

Yes, it is the standard formal letter format. In exams you usually write “ABC” instead of your real name.

How do I write for leave already taken?

Use the past tense — “I could not attend school from … to …” — and end with “kindly grant me leave for these days”.

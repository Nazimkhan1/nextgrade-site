✍️ Application formats — fill in, ready to send

The applications people need most for school, office, bank and police. Fill in your details — the letter writes itself in English or Hindi.

  • 🏫 School leave application
  • 🏢 Sick leave application (office)
  • 📱 Bank mobile number change application
  • 💳 ATM card block application
  • 🏦 Bank account closing application
  • 📄 TC (transfer certificate) application
  • 🙏 Fee concession application
  • 🚓 Mobile lost application (police)

An application is a formal letter requesting an official to do something — grant leave, issue a certificate, reduce fees, update bank details or record a report. It follows a fixed order: To (the recipient’s title and address), date, subject, salutation, body, thanks, and the sender’s name and details.

Every letter here is written by us in simple, current language. The names, dates and numbers you enter stay in this page — nothing is sent or saved. Copy the letter to WhatsApp or email, or print it and sign.

The 7 parts of a formal letter: (1) To — the title of the person you are writing to; (2) the address — school, branch or police station; (3) the date; (4) the subject — the whole request in one line; (5) the salutation — Sir/Madam; (6) the body — who you are, what happened, what you need; (7) the closing — “Yours obediently” for school, “Yours faithfully/sincerely” for office and bank — then your name, any reference numbers and contact. Keep it short, stick to one request, and mention any documents you attach. Print on plain white paper, sign in blue or black ink, and keep a photocopy or a photo of the signed letter for your own record.

Frequently asked questions

What is the correct application format?

To → title and address → date → subject → Sir/Madam → body → thanking you → name and details.

Can I use these in exams?

Yes, the school letters (leave, TC, fee concession) follow the exam format.

Is my information saved?

No. Everything stays in your browser.

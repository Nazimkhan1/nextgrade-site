In Bhayandar, on the first roza of Ramzan 2027 (about 9 February), Sehri ends at 5:56 AM and Iftar is at 6:35 PM. By the last roza, Sehri ends at 5:39 AM and Iftar is at 6:47 PM.

Sehri = Subah Sadiq (Fajr, sun 18° below the horizon); Iftar = sunset. As a precaution, finish Sehri 3–5 minutes earlier. Times are calculated (±1–2 min) — always follow your local masjid or committee. The start of Ramzan can shift by a day with the moon sighting.

How long is the fast in Bhayandar

About 12 h 39 min on the first roza and 13 h 8 min on the last — days get longer from February to March, so Sehri gets earlier and Iftar later.

Difference from nearby cities

  • Iftar in Borivali is at about the same time as in Bhayandar.
  • Iftar in Vasai-Virar is at about the same time as in Bhayandar.
  • Iftar in Bhiwandi is about 1 minutes earlier than in Bhayandar.

Sehri and Iftar dua

Intention for the fast (Sehri): Wa bi-sawmi ghadin nawaitu min shahri Ramadan — “I intend to keep tomorrow’s fast of Ramadan.”

Iftar dua: Allahumma inni laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa alaika tawakkaltu wa ala rizqika aftartu — “O Allah, I fasted for You, believed in You, put my trust in You and break my fast with Your provision.”

Staying healthy while fasting

  • Don’t skip Sehri — eat slow foods like oats, eggs, curd and bananas.
  • Open the fast with dates and water, then eat slowly; keep fried food low.
  • Drink 8–10 glasses of water between Iftar and Sehri. With diabetes, BP or pregnancy, ask your doctor before fasting.

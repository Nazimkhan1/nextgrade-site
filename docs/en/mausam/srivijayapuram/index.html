What is the weather like in Srivijayapuram?

Srivijayapuram is hottest in April, when daytime highs average about 30°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 27°C. Most rain falls in July, August and September; the yearly total is about 2210 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Srivijayapuram: January, February, March, April and December.

What is the weather like in Dharmanagar?

Dharmanagar is hottest in April, when daytime highs average about 33°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 11°C. Most rain falls in May, June and July; the yearly total is about 4030 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Dharmanagar: January, February, March, November and December.

Current weather in Shillong (Meghalaya), today and tomorrow, and the next 10 days — updated every hour.

🧳 For travellers

Meghalaya, "abode of clouds"; Cherrapunji waterfalls and living root bridges nearby. Where to go in which month →

What is the weather like in Shillong?

Shillong is hottest in August, when daytime highs average about 24°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 5°C. Most rain falls in June, July and August; the yearly total is about 4120 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Shillong: February, March, November and December.

📍 Where is Shillong

Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya. The nearest big city is Guwahati, about 70 km to the north. Nearby towns: Silchar (120 km), Dharmanagar (140 km), Tezpur (150 km), Kaziranga (170 km).

🌡️ Shillong vs Guwahati: how the weather differs

  • In August, days in Shillong are about 11°C cooler than in Guwahati (24°C vs 35°C).
  • January nights in Shillong are 8°C colder (5°C vs 13°C).
  • Shillong gets more rain in a year — about 4,120 mm, against 2,180 mm in Guwahati.

👕 What to wear in Shillong, month by month

  • April, May, June, July, August, September, October — Rainy: umbrella or raincoat, quick-dry clothes, shoes with grip.
  • March, November — Cool: a sweater or light jacket for mornings and evenings.
  • January, February, December — Cold: a warm jacket, cap and socks; take extra care of elders and children.

🧳 Places to visit near Shillong

  • Kaziranga (170 km) — Home of the one-horned rhino (UNESCO) — elephant and jeep safaris.
  • Tawang (220 km) — Arunachal's great Buddhist monastery, high passes and lakes. An Inner Line Permit is needed.

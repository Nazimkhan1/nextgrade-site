What is the weather like in Chalisgaon?

Chalisgaon is hottest in May, when daytime highs average about 42°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 14°C. Most rain falls in July, August and September; the yearly total is about 740 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Chalisgaon: January, October, November and December.

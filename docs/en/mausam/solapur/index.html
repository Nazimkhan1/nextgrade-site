🌦️ Solapur Weather Today

Current weather in Solapur (Maharashtra), today and tomorrow, and the next 10 days — updated every hour.

What is the weather like in Solapur?

Solapur is hottest in April, when daytime highs average about 41°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 15°C. Most rain falls in June, July and September; the yearly total is about 690 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Solapur: January, August, October, November and December.

📍 Where is Solapur

Solapur is about 360 km south-east of Mumbai, the state capital. The nearest big city is Pune, about 240 km to the north-west. Nearby towns: Dharashiv (60 km), Pandharpur (60 km), Barshi (65 km), Vijayapura (95 km).

🌡️ Solapur vs Pune: how the weather differs

  • In April, days in Solapur are about 2°C hotter than in Pune (41°C vs 39°C).
  • January nights in Solapur are 2°C milder (15°C vs 13°C).
  • Solapur gets less rain in a year — about 690 mm, against 2,170 mm in Pune.

👕 What to wear in Solapur, month by month

  • March, April, May — Very hot: loose cotton, a cap or scarf, carry water; avoid the sun from 12 to 4 pm.
  • February, June — Hot: light cotton clothes, sunglasses, a water bottle.
  • September — Rainy: umbrella or raincoat, quick-dry clothes, shoes with grip.
  • January, July, August, October, November, December — Pleasant: light clothes are enough; a thin layer for the evening.

🧳 Places to visit near Solapur

  • Mahabaleshwar (240 km) — Strawberries, valley viewpoints and Venna lake.
  • Aurangabad (250 km) — The Ajanta and Ellora caves (UNESCO), Bibi Ka Maqbara and Daulatabad Fort.
  • Hampi (270 km) — Ruins of the Vijayanagara empire, boulder hills and the Virupaksha temple.

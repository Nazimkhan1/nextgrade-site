What is the weather like in Kolkata?

Kolkata is hottest in April, when daytime highs average about 39°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 11°C. Most rain falls in July, August and September; the yearly total is about 1700 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Kolkata: January, February, November and December.

📍 Where is Kolkata

Kolkata is the capital of West Bengal. Nearby towns: Budge Budge (20 km), Serampore (20 km), Barasat (20 km), Uluberia (30 km).

🌡️ Kolkata vs Delhi: how the weather differs

  • In April daytime temperatures in both cities are about the same (39°C).
  • January nights in Kolkata are 4°C milder (11°C vs 7°C).
  • Kolkata gets more rain in a year — about 1,700 mm, against 780 mm in Delhi.

👕 What to wear in Kolkata, month by month

  • April — Very hot: loose cotton, a cap or scarf, carry water; avoid the sun from 12 to 4 pm.
  • March — Hot: light cotton clothes, sunglasses, a water bottle.
  • May, June, July, August, September — Rainy: umbrella or raincoat, quick-dry clothes, shoes with grip.
  • February, October, November — Pleasant: light clothes are enough; a thin layer for the evening.
  • January, December — Cool: a sweater or light jacket for mornings and evenings.

🧳 Places to visit near Kolkata

  • Digha (140 km) — A popular beach close to Kolkata — good for a short family break.
  • Shantiniketan (140 km) — Rabindranath Tagore's Visva-Bharati, Sonajhuri haat and the Poush Mela.

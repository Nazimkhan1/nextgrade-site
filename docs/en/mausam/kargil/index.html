What is the weather like in Kargil?

Kargil is hottest in August, when daytime highs average about 27°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging -11°C. Most rain falls in January, March and April; the yearly total is about 470 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Kargil: May, June, July, August and September.

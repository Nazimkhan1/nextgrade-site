What is the weather like in Gwalior?

Gwalior is hottest in May, when daytime highs average about 43°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 9°C. Most rain falls in July, August and September; the yearly total is about 750 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Gwalior: January, February, October, November and December.

📍 Where is Gwalior

Gwalior is about 340 km north of Bhopal, the state capital. The nearest big city is Kanpur, about 220 km to the east. Nearby towns: Morena (35 km), Dholpur (60 km), Datia (70 km), Bhind (70 km).

🌡️ Gwalior vs Kanpur: how the weather differs

  • In May, days in Gwalior are about 1°C hotter than in Kanpur (43°C vs 42°C).
  • January nights in Gwalior are 1°C milder (9°C vs 8°C).
  • Gwalior gets less rain in a year — about 750 mm, against 900 mm in Kanpur.

👕 What to wear in Gwalior, month by month

  • April, May, June — Very hot: loose cotton, a cap or scarf, carry water; avoid the sun from 12 to 4 pm.
  • March — Hot: light cotton clothes, sunglasses, a water bottle.
  • July, August — Rainy: umbrella or raincoat, quick-dry clothes, shoes with grip.
  • September, October, November — Pleasant: light clothes are enough; a thin layer for the evening.
  • January, February, December — Cool: a sweater or light jacket for mornings and evenings.

🧳 Places to visit near Gwalior

  • Agra (110 km) — The Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri nearby.
  • Mathura (150 km) — Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, the Yamuna ghats and Mathura pedas.
  • Vrindavan (160 km) — Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir and ISKCON — the town of Krishna devotion.

🌦️ Purulia Weather Today

Current weather in Purulia (West Bengal), today and tomorrow, and the next 10 days — updated every hour.

What is the weather like in Purulia?

Purulia is hottest in May, when daytime highs average about 39°C. The coldest month is January, with nights averaging 10°C. Most rain falls in July, August and September; the yearly total is about 1520 mm.

Best time to visit

The most comfortable months to visit Purulia: January, February, October, November and December.

📍 Where is Purulia

Purulia is about 220 km north-west of Kolkata, the state capital. The nearest big city is Ranchi, about 110 km to the west. Nearby towns: Bokaro (45 km), Jharia (45 km), Dhanbad (50 km), Jamshedpur (60 km).

🌡️ Purulia vs Ranchi: how the weather differs

  • In May daytime temperatures in both cities are about the same (39°C).
  • January nights in Purulia are 1°C milder (10°C vs 9°C).
  • Both get about the same rain in a year (around 1,520 mm).

👕 What to wear in Purulia, month by month

  • April, May — Very hot: loose cotton, a cap or scarf, carry water; avoid the sun from 12 to 4 pm.
  • March — Hot: light cotton clothes, sunglasses, a water bottle.
  • June, July, August, September — Rainy: umbrella or raincoat, quick-dry clothes, shoes with grip.
  • October, November — Pleasant: light clothes are enough; a thin layer for the evening.
  • January, February, December — Cool: a sweater or light jacket for mornings and evenings.

🧳 Places to visit near Purulia

  • Shantiniketan (140 km) — Rabindranath Tagore's Visva-Bharati, Sonajhuri haat and the Poush Mela.
  • Bodh Gaya (210 km) — The Mahabodhi temple and the Bodhi tree — where the Buddha attained enlightenment.
  • Kolkata (220 km) — Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, College Street and sweets.

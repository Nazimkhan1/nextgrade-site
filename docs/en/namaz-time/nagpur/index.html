Qibla direction from Nagpur

From Nagpur the Qibla (the Kaaba, Makkah) is at about 278° — that is 278° clockwise from north, roughly towards the west. Makkah is about 4,060 km away. Keep your phone compass away from iron or magnets when you check.

Jumma prayer

Jumma is prayed at Zuhr time — today Zuhr begins at 12:04 PM. Khutba and jamaat times differ from masjid to masjid.

Why Hanafi and Shafi Asr differ

In the Shafi (and Maliki, Hanbali) view, Asr begins when an object’s shadow equals its length (plus its noon shadow); in the Hanafi view, when it is twice the length. So Hanafi Asr is about 45–60 minutes later.

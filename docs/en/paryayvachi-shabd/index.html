🔁 Paryayvachi Shabd — Hindi synonyms

Paryayvachi shabd are words with the same meaning — for the sun: ravi, dinkar, bhaanu. Here are the synonyms of 63 words that school exams ask most, with English meanings.

Paryayvachi (or samaanaarthi) shabd are words with nearly the same meaning. “Paryay” means a substitute — one word that can take the place of another. Poets and good writers use them so the same word does not repeat again and again.

Synonyms are similar, not identical. “Ghar”, “bhavan” and “aalay” all mean a dwelling, but “bhavan” suits a large building and “aalay” usually joins another word (vidyaalay = school, devaalay = temple). When you swap in a synonym, check that it sounds right in the sentence.

Some synonyms belong to more than one word: “kar” is both hand and ray of light; “Neelkanth” is both Shiva and the peacock; “sona” is gold and also “to sleep”. In an exam, decide the meaning from the sentence. Our list leans on Sanskrit-origin (tatsam) words because school books and exams ask for those.

Easy ways to learn them

  • Fix the first three synonyms of each word — exams usually ask for two or three.
  • Notice how the words are built: jalaj, neeraj, pankaj — “ja” means “born of”; born of water or mud = lotus.
  • Play the quiz below once a day.

Frequently asked questions

What is paryayvachi shabd?

Words with the same meaning, e.g. jal (water) — neer, vaari, salil.

What are the synonyms of soorya (sun)?

Ravi, dinkar, bhaanu, divaakar, bhaaskar, sooraj.

What are the synonyms of kamal (lotus)?

Pankaj, jalaj, saroj, neeraj, ambuj.

Difference between paryayvachi and vilom?

Paryayvachi have the same meaning (din–divas), vilom have the opposite meaning (din–raat).

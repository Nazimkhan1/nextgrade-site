❄️ Winter home remedies — from Nani and Dadi

Winter brings back ginger with honey, turmeric milk and salt-water gargles in every Indian home. Here are those simple, time-tested remedies — for comfort, not as a cure. Each one says who should be careful.

⚠️ This information comes from household tradition and general advice; it is not medical treatment. For any illness, medicine or allergy, ask your doctor.

1. 🍯 Ginger and honey for cough

You need: Half a teaspoon of ginger juice, one teaspoon of honey

How: Mix and lick slowly 2–3 times a day. It soothes the throat.

⚠️ Caution: Never give honey to a baby under 1 year. Keep honey low if you have diabetes.

2. 🧂 Salt-water gargle for a sore throat

You need: A glass of warm water, half a teaspoon of salt

How: Gargle 3–4 times a day; do not swallow.

⚠️ Caution: Not for small children who cannot gargle.

3. ♨️ Steam for a blocked nose

You need: A bowl of hot water, a towel

How: Cover your head with the towel and breathe the steam for 5–7 minutes, eyes closed.

⚠️ Caution: Never leave children alone with hot water — risk of burns. Do not use boiling water.

4. 🥛 Turmeric milk (haldi doodh)

You need: A cup of milk, a quarter teaspoon of turmeric

How: Boil the milk with turmeric and drink it warm before bed.

⚠️ Caution: Skip it if you are allergic to milk. If you take blood thinners, ask your doctor.

5. 🍵 Tulsi-ginger tea

You need: 5–6 tulsi leaves, a little ginger, a cup of water

How: Boil tulsi and ginger in the water, strain and sip warm. Add a little jaggery if you like.

⚠️ Caution: In pregnancy do not take too much tulsi; 1–2 cups a day is enough.

6. 🫙 Ajwain (carom) warm compress

You need: 2 teaspoons of ajwain, a cotton cloth, a pan

How: Warm the ajwain lightly, tie it in the cloth and press gently on the chest or back.

⚠️ Caution: Test the heat on your wrist first; children's and elders' skin burns easily.

7. 👄 Chapped lips

You need: Desi ghee, coconut oil or milk cream (malai)

How: Apply a thin layer at bedtime. Do not lick your lips.

⚠️ Caution: See a doctor if the lips bleed or a sore does not heal.

8. 🧴 Dry skin

You need: Mustard, coconut or sesame oil

How: Apply a little oil on damp skin right after a bath. Avoid very hot baths — they dry the skin more.

⚠️ Caution: Stop any oil that causes itching or a rash.

9. 🦶 Cracked heels

You need: Warm water, soap, ghee or petroleum jelly, socks

How: Soak feet in warm water for 10 minutes at night, dry, apply ghee or jelly and sleep in cotton socks.

⚠️ Caution: With diabetes, do not treat foot wounds at home — see a doctor.

10. ☀️ Stiff joints

You need: Sunlight, a hot water bottle

How: Sit in the morning sun for 15–20 minutes, do light exercise and use a warm compress on stiff joints.

⚠️ Caution: If a joint is swollen, red or very painful, see a doctor instead.

11. 🥜 Jaggery, sesame and peanuts

You need: A little gud, til and peanuts

How: Eat small amounts in winter for warmth and energy.

⚠️ Caution: Jaggery is sugar too — be careful with diabetes; avoid peanuts if allergic.

12. 💧 Drink water in winter too

You need: Warm water

How: You feel less thirsty, but keep sipping warm water through the day — it helps the throat and skin.

⚠️ Caution: With kidney or heart disease, ask your doctor how much water to drink.

🏠 Winter safety at home (very important)

  • Never sleep in a closed room with an angithi, coal or wood fire — it makes carbon monoxide, a gas with no smell that can kill. Keep a window slightly open.
  • Do not run a room heater all night; keep it away from clothes and curtains.
  • A bathroom with a gas geyser must have ventilation.
  • Put quilts and blankets in the sun — it removes damp and smell.
  • Keep the head, ears and feet of elders and small children covered.

🩺 When to see a doctor

  • Fever for more than 3 days, or very high fever
  • Breathlessness, chest pain or bluish lips
  • Cough for more than 2–3 weeks, or blood in phlegm
  • Any fever in a baby under 3 months
  • An elder, a pregnant woman or someone already ill gets worse

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest home remedy for a cold and cough?

Warm salt-water gargles, ginger with honey and steam are the most common. They give comfort, but see a doctor for fever or breathing trouble.

Can children have honey?

Never give honey to a baby under 1 year. Older children can have a little.

Why is sleeping with an angithi dangerous?

Burning coal or wood in a closed room makes carbon monoxide, which has no smell or colour and can kill during sleep.

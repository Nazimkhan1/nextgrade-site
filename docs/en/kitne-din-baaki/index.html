Every countdown here runs on your phone or computer clock, in Indian Standard Time (IST). For a festival it counts to midnight at the start of that day; for an exam, to the start time of the first paper.

Festival dates are the same as on our festival pages, checked against several panchangs. Tithi-based festivals (Holi, Shivratri, Janmashtami) sometimes differ by a day between panchangs, so check your city’s panchang too. Eid and Ramzan depend on the moon sighting, so they are marked “expected”.

Tap any card for the full festival page with its story and wishes, or for the exam date sheet.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are left for Christmas?

Christmas is on Friday, 25 December 2026 — 28 days from today.

Up to what time does the countdown count?

To the start of the festival day (midnight IST); for exams, to the first paper’s start time.

Why is the Eid date “expected”?

Eid depends on the moon sighting, so the date can move by a day.

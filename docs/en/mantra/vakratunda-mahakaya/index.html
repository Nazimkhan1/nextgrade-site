🐘 Vakratunda Mahakaya (Ganesh mantra)

Source: Traditional Ganesh shloka

वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय

Vakratunda mahakaya

O you of the curved trunk and mighty body (Ganesh),

सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ।

suryakoti samaprabha.

whose radiance equals a crore suns,

निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव

Nirvighnam kuru me deva

O Lord, make all my work free of obstacles —

सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा॥

sarvakaryeshu sarvada.

in every task, always.

About this mantra

“Vakratunda Mahakaya” is the most popular shloka of Lord Ganesh. In Hindu tradition every new start — studies, an exam, a shop, a journey, a puja — begins by invoking Ganesh, and this short verse is said for exactly that.

Recalling his form — curved trunk, mighty body, radiance of a crore suns — it asks one thing: may none of my work ever meet obstacles. Ganesh is called Vighnaharta, the remover of obstacles.

It is often the first verse children learn, and is said on Ganesh Chaturthi, on Wednesdays and at every auspicious beginning; many repeat it 11 or 21 times.

Pronunciation: say the “kra” and “nda” of “vakratunda” clearly; break “suryakoti” as surya-koti and “samaprabha” as sama-prabha; in “nirvighnam” both the “r” and “gh” are half sounds — nir-vigh-nam. When teaching children, have them repeat one line at a time, then the whole verse. It is often followed by the verse “Gajananam bhutaganadi sevitam”. Because it is short and rhythmic, it is also a good first step for children before they learn longer prayers like the Gayatri Mantra or the Hanuman Chalisa.

How to chant — the simple way

  • Sit upright in a quiet place, eyes closed or half-open, and take a few deep breaths.
  • Say the mantra slowly and clearly — aloud, in a whisper or silently; all three are accepted.
  • On a mala, one chant per bead; after 108 beads don’t cross the sumeru (big bead) — turn the mala and continue. No mala? Use the counter above.
  • The meaning matters more than the number — that is why every line has its meaning below it.

Frequently asked questions

What does Vakratunda mean?

The one with the curved trunk — Ganesh.

When is this shloka said?

Before starting any new work, studies, an exam or a puja.

What does it ask for?

That every task always be free of obstacles.

How do I help a child learn it?

Add one line each morning before studies; in four days the whole verse is learned.

How the date is decided

  • A tithi is set by the angle between the Moon and the Sun — one tithi every 12°. So a tithi can begin or end in the middle of a day.
  • The vrat date is usually the day on which the tithi is running at sunrise (udaya tithi). Here it is worked out for sunrise in New Delhi.
  • Month names follow the North Indian purnimanta calendar.

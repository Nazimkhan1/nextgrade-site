💌 Wish Card Maker

Pick an occasion, add names and make your greeting card in seconds. The card is made on your own phone or computer — your names and message are never saved or sent anywhere.

🖼️ Make an image with a name

How to use it

  1. Pick an occasion or festival — the card colours and messages change to match.
  2. Add the receiver's name and yours (you can leave them empty too).
  3. Choose a ready message or write your own.
  4. Tap Share to send it straight to WhatsApp/Instagram, or Download to save it to your gallery.

How to write a good wish

The best wish carries the person’s name and one small personal line. A ready message is a good start, but add a line of your own — "thank you for your help last year" or "let’s celebrate Diwali together this time" — and the card feels heartfelt.

Quick tips

  • Check the spelling of the name — a wrong name is the first thing people notice.
  • Use respectful words for elders and light, friendly words for friends.
  • Send it on the morning of the festival; keep it short — two or three lines are enough.
  • Before sending to a group, make sure the message suits everyone in it.

Does it cost anything to make a card?
No. Making, downloading and sharing cards is free.

Is my name or message saved anywhere?
No. The card is made on your own phone or computer; nothing reaches us.

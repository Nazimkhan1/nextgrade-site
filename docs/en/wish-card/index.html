💌 Wish Card Maker

Pick an occasion, add names and make your greeting card in seconds. The card is made on your own phone or computer — your names and message are never saved or sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Pick an occasion or festival — the card colours and messages change to match.
  2. Add the receiver's name and yours (you can leave them empty too).
  3. Choose a ready message or write your own.
  4. Tap Share to send it straight to WhatsApp/Instagram, or Download to save it to your gallery.

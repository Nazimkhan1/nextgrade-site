🏛️ Government guides — from home, step by step

How to do everyday government work like Aadhaar, PAN and Ayushman — every step with the right (.gov.in) website link and which field to fill with what.

📱

How to change the mobile number in Aadhaar

Where: UIDAI's new Aadhaar App (Android/iPhone) — or an Aadhaar centre · You need: Old number working (OTP) + face authentication

🏠

How to change your address in Aadhaar (online)

Where: myAadhaar portal (from uidai.gov.in) · You need: Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)

🔗

How to link PAN with Aadhaar

Where: Income Tax e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in) · Fee: ₹1,000 (late linking fee) — paid first

🪪

How to get a new PAN card

Easiest: Instant e-PAN with Aadhaar — on incometax.gov.in, free · You need: Aadhaar + the mobile linked to it

🏥

How to get an Ayushman card (PM-JAY)

Where: beneficiary.nha.gov.in or the Ayushman App · Fee: Making the card is free

📥

How to download e-Aadhaar (Aadhaar PDF)

Where: myAadhaar portal (from uidai.gov.in) · Fee: Free

🗳️

How to get a new voter ID (Form 6)

Where: voters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline app · Fee: Free

🛂

How to apply for a passport (online)

Where: Passport Seva portal (passportindia.gov.in) / mPassport Seva app · Fee (36 pages): Normal ₹2,500 · Tatkaal ₹5,000 (from 1 July 2026)

🗂️

How to use DigiLocker

Where: digilocker.gov.in or the DigiLocker app · Fee: Free

🌾

How to check PM Kisan status and do eKYC

Where: pmkisan.gov.in → Farmers Corner, or the PM-KISAN mobile app · Money: ₹6,000 a year — three instalments of ₹2,000 into an Aadhaar-seeded bank account

🏦

How to withdraw PF online

Where: EPFO member portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) — with UAN and password · You need: Active UAN + Aadhaar, PAN and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account (KYC)

🚗

How to apply for a learner licence (LL) online

Where: sarathi.parivahan.gov.in (via parivahan.gov.in) — choose your state · Age: Gearless two-wheeler up to 50cc: 16 (with parent consent); other vehicles: 18 — as shown on the portal

🗳️ How to get a new voter ID (Form 6)

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

Wherevoters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline app
FeeFree
WhoIndian citizens aged 18 or above
FormNew name: Form 6 (living abroad: 6A)

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Proof of identity — e.g. Aadhaar, passport, driving licence
  • Proof of address
  • Proof of age/date of birth
  • Recent passport-size photo

1Create an account

voters.eci.gov.in → Sign up with mobile and OTP.

What to fill in:

  • Mobile number
  • Name, password
  • OTP

Open the official website ↗

2Choose Form 6

"New registration for general electors (Form 6)".

What to fill in:

  • State, district, assembly constituency
  • Name, date of birth, gender
  • Relative’s name (father/mother/spouse)

Open the official website ↗

3Address and documents

Enter your full address and upload the documents.

What to fill in:

  • House no., street, village/town, PIN
  • Photo (size shown on the portal)
  • Identity/address/age proof

Open the official website ↗

4Submit and keep the reference number

Read the declaration and submit; keep the reference number. A BLO may visit to verify.

What to fill in:

  • Reference number

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

Enter the reference number under "Track Application Status" on voters.eci.gov.in. After approval, download the digital voter card free via "e-EPIC Download"; the printed card comes by post.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Blurred or wrong-size photo
  • Address proof for a different address
  • Name already enrolled elsewhere — being enrolled in two places is not allowed
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee for a voter ID?

No, a new voter ID is free.

Can I apply at 17?

You can apply in advance once you turn 17; your name is added after you turn 18 (on set qualifying dates) — check the portal.

What is e-EPIC?

The digital (PDF) version of the voter card, downloadable free.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

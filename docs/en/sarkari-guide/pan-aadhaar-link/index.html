🔗 How to link PAN with Aadhaar

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

WhereIncome Tax e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in)
Fee₹1,000 (late linking fee) — paid first
You needPAN, Aadhaar, mobile linked to Aadhaar
WhyAn unlinked PAN can become inoperative

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar number
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)
  • Name, date of birth and gender must match in PAN and Aadhaar

1First check the link status

incometax.gov.in → "Link Aadhaar Status" — it may already be linked.

What to fill in:

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar number

Open the official website ↗

2Pay the ₹1,000 fee (e-Pay Tax)

"e-Pay Tax" on the portal → enter PAN and mobile → OTP → "Income Tax" → Assessment Year → Type of Payment "Other Receipts (500)" → pay.

What to fill in:

  • PAN (twice)
  • Mobile number and OTP
  • Assessment Year (the portal shows it)
  • Type: Other Receipts (500)
  • Payment — net banking/UPI/card (bank list on the portal)

Open the official website ↗

3Submit the link request

A while after paying (it can take up to 4–5 days) → home page → "Link Aadhaar".

What to fill in:

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar number
  • Name as in Aadhaar
  • OTP on your mobile

Open the official website ↗

4Confirmation and status

You get a message after submitting. A few days later "Link Aadhaar Status" should show linked.

What to fill in:

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar number

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

incometax.gov.in → "Link Aadhaar Status" (no login needed). After linking, an inoperative PAN may take a few days to become operative again.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Name/date of birth/gender differ between PAN and Aadhaar — correct the wrong one first
  • Fee paid under the wrong "Type of Payment"
  • Trying to link immediately after paying — wait a little
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PAN-Aadhaar link fee?

₹1,000 — the late linking fee, paid before the link request.

Does everyone have to link?

Most PAN holders, yes; some (e.g. people over 80, some NRIs) are exempt — see the rules on the portal.

The name does not match — what now?

Correct Aadhaar on myAadhaar or apply for a PAN correction, then link.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

🪪 How to get a new PAN card

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

EasiestInstant e-PAN with Aadhaar — on incometax.gov.in, free
You needAadhaar + the mobile linked to it
Physical cardFrom Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL — fee shown on the portal
Timee-PAN in minutes to hours; card by post in a few weeks

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Aadhaar (identity, address and date of birth come from it)
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
  • For a physical card: photo and signature — sizes shown on the portal (our form tool can fix them)

1e-PAN: open "Instant e-PAN"

incometax.gov.in home page → "Instant e-PAN" → "Get New e-PAN".

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number
  • Consent checkboxes

Open the official website ↗

2OTP and confirm details

An OTP comes to your Aadhaar-linked mobile; your Aadhaar details are shown — check and submit.

What to fill in:

  • OTP
  • Email (optional) — the e-PAN is also emailed

Open the official website ↗

3Physical PAN: via Protean (NSDL)

On Protean’s site choose "New PAN — Indian Citizen (Form 49A)"; fill it with Aadhaar e-KYC and pay.

What to fill in:

  • Application type: New PAN – Indian Citizen (Form 49A)
  • Name, date of birth, email, mobile
  • KYC mode — Aadhaar e-KYC
  • Fee (shown on the portal)

Open the official website ↗

4Or via UTIITSL

You can also apply with "Apply PAN Card" on UTIITSL’s site; the process is similar.

What to fill in:

  • Personal details
  • Documents/e-KYC
  • Fee

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

e-PAN: incometax.gov.in → Instant e-PAN → "Check Status / Download PAN". Protean/UTIITSL: "Track PAN status" on their site with the acknowledgement number.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Fix Aadhaar first if the name/date of birth there is wrong
  • Do not apply if you already have a PAN — holding two PANs is not allowed
  • Photo/signature in the wrong size
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Is a PAN card free?

Instant e-PAN with Aadhaar on incometax.gov.in is free; a printed card from Protean/UTIITSL has a fee.

Is e-PAN valid?

Yes, an e-PAN is a valid PAN.

Can I have more than one PAN?

No — one person, one PAN; a second PAN is against the rules.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

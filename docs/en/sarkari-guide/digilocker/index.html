🗂️ How to use DigiLocker

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

Wheredigilocker.gov.in or the DigiLocker app
FeeFree
You needMobile number + Aadhaar
WhyOfficial documents on your phone, valid like originals

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Mobile number
  • Aadhaar number (to link the account)

1Create an account

DigiLocker app or digilocker.gov.in → Sign up.

What to fill in:

  • Name (as in Aadhaar), date of birth, gender
  • Mobile number + OTP
  • 6-digit security PIN

Open the official website ↗

2Link Aadhaar

Enter Aadhaar and confirm with OTP — this unlocks the other documents.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number
  • OTP

Open the official website ↗

3Get documents ("Issued Documents")

In "Search Documents" pick the issuer — e.g. Income Tax (PAN), transport (DL/RC), board (marksheets) — and "Get Document".

What to fill in:

  • Issuer/board
  • Number — e.g. DL number, roll number, year of passing

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

Documents under "Issued Documents" come straight from the issuing department and are valid like originals under the rules — e.g. showing your DL/RC to traffic police.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Name in DigiLocker differs from Aadhaar — use the Aadhaar spelling
  • Wrong roll number/year
  • A photo you upload under "Uploaded" is not as valid as an "Issued" document
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Is DigiLocker free?

Yes, completely free.

Is a driving licence in DigiLocker valid?

Yes, the DL/RC under "Issued Documents" is valid.

How do I get my marksheet?

Search Documents → your board/university → roll number and year (if your board is on DigiLocker).

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

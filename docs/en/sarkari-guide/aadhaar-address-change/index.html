🏠 How to change your address in Aadhaar (online)

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

WheremyAadhaar portal (from uidai.gov.in)
You needMobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)
FeeShown on the portal — ₹75 at present as per UIDAI
TimeUsually a few days, up to 30 days

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Proof of address — e.g. electricity/water/gas bill (not older than 3 months), bank passbook/statement, passport, voter ID, registered rent agreement
  • Your name on the document must match Aadhaar
  • Clear scan/photo — PDF, JPG or PNG (the portal shows the size limit)

1Log in to myAadhaar

Open myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in → Login.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number (12 digits)
  • Captcha
  • OTP sent to your mobile

Open the official website ↗

2Choose "Address Update"

On the dashboard: "Document Update / Address Update" (the label may differ slightly) → "Update Aadhaar Online".

What to fill in:

  • Select Address
  • "Proceed to Update Aadhaar"

Open the official website ↗

3Enter the new address

Your current address is shown; type the new one below.

What to fill in:

  • C/O (care of)
  • House number, street, locality
  • Village/town, post office, district, state, PIN code
  • Check the local-language address too

Open the official website ↗

4Upload the document

Pick your document from the list and upload the file.

What to fill in:

  • Document type — e.g. Electricity Bill
  • File — clear, full page, name and address visible

Open the official website ↗

5Pay and keep the URN

Pay online (UPI/card/net banking). You get a URN (Update Request Number) at the end — take a screenshot.

What to fill in:

  • Payment
  • Note down the URN

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

Check the status under "Check Enrolment & Update Status" on myAadhaar with your URN. Once updated, download the new e-Aadhaar with "Download Aadhaar".

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Name on the document differs from Aadhaar
  • Blurred or cropped image
  • Old bill (more than 3 months)
  • Wrong PIN code or district
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an Aadhaar address change take?

Usually a few days; up to 30 days. Track it with the URN.

Can I change the address without my own document?

There is also an option using the Head of Family’s document and consent; see the options on the portal.

My mobile is not linked to Aadhaar — what now?

Link it first at an Aadhaar centre (book at appointments.uidai.gov.in). The online update needs an OTP.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

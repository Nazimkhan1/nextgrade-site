🌾 How to check PM Kisan status and do eKYC

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

Wherepmkisan.gov.in → Farmers Corner, or the PM-KISAN mobile app
Money₹6,000 a year — three instalments of ₹2,000 into an Aadhaar-seeded bank account
You needeKYC done + bank account seeded with Aadhaar + land records on the portal
Next instalmentDates are announced on pmkisan.gov.in — don't trust guesswork in the news

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Aadhaar number
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile — for the OTP
  • Registration number (if you don't know it, the portal finds it from your mobile/Aadhaar)
  • A bank account seeded with Aadhaar (for DBT)
  • New registration: land papers (khasra/khatauni etc.) and, in many states, a Farmer ID

1Open "Know Your Status"

On pmkisan.gov.in, go to Farmers Corner → "Know Your Status". Enter your registration number and the captcha, then the OTP sent to your mobile. Forgot the registration number? Use "Know your registration no." on the same page with your mobile or Aadhaar. (Button labels may differ slightly.)

What to fill in:

  • Registration number
  • Captcha
  • OTP

Open the official website ↗

2Read the status lines

The page shows each instalment plus a few key checks — such as eKYC, Aadhaar-bank seeding and land records (land seeding). Whichever line says "No" or "Pending" is what you need to fix; do the steps below for that one.

What to fill in:

  • eKYC status
  • Aadhaar-bank seeding
  • Land seeding

Open the official website ↗

3eKYC — by OTP on the portal

In Farmers Corner, choose "e-KYC", enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. This is the easiest route, but your mobile number must be linked to Aadhaar.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number
  • OTP on the Aadhaar mobile

Open the official website ↗

4eKYC — face in the app, or fingerprint at a CSC

If your mobile isn't linked to Aadhaar, install the official PM-KISAN app from Play Store and do eKYC by face authentication (it may ask you to install a UIDAI face app too). Or get it done by biometrics at a nearby Common Service Centre (CSC) — ask the centre about its charge.

What to fill in:

  • Official PM-KISAN app
  • Face scan
  • Or fingerprint at a CSC

Open the official website ↗

5Get your bank account seeded with Aadhaar

DBT money goes only to the account that is seeded with Aadhaar (NPCI mapping). Ask your bank branch to seed Aadhaar for DBT, or open an India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) account at the post office. Your name should match in Aadhaar and the bank.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar seeding form at the bank
  • Or an IPPB account
  • Match the name spelling

Open the official website ↗

6New registration and Farmer ID

Not enrolled yet? Use "New Farmer Registration" in Farmers Corner or visit a CSC. The government told the Lok Sabha (August 2025) that a Farmer ID is required for new registrations in 14 states — check the rule for your state on the portal or with the agriculture department. The state verifies land records before approval.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar and mobile
  • State/district/village
  • Land details
  • Farmer ID (where your state requires it)

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

Payments and every check line show in "Know Your Status", and in the PM-KISAN app. For questions, use the portal's Kisan-eMitra chatbot (11 languages including Hindi and Bengali). Complaints go to the portal's Help Desk or pgportal.gov.in (CPGRAMS). See helpline numbers on pmkisan.gov.in itself.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • eKYC pending — finish it by OTP, face in the app, or at a CSC
  • Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar — get it done at the bank or IPPB
  • Name differs between Aadhaar, bank and records — make them match
  • Land records not yet verified by the state — ask the tehsil/agriculture office
  • Excluded groups such as income-tax payers and government employees — they don't get the benefit
  • OTP shared with an "agent" — never share an OTP; use only .gov.in sites
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

When will the next PM Kisan instalment come?

The government announces each instalment date on pmkisan.gov.in and PIB. "Dates" on social media are often guesses. Meanwhile, keep eKYC and bank seeding complete so your instalment doesn't get stuck.

When was the 22nd instalment paid?

According to PIB, the 22nd instalment was released on 13 March 2026 from Guwahati, to about 9.32 crore farmers. Check the portal for later instalments.

Will I get the money without eKYC?

No — the portal says eKYC is mandatory for registered farmers. Do it by OTP, face in the PM-KISAN app, or biometrics at a CSC.

Status shows "Yes" everywhere but no money?

Ask your bank which account your Aadhaar is mapped to for DBT — the money goes there. If it still doesn't arrive, file a complaint on the portal's Help Desk.

Is this a government website?

No, NextGrade is not a government site; we only explain the process. Do the actual work on pmkisan.gov.in or the PM-KISAN app.

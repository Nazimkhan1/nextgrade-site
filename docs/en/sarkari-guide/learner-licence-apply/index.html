🚗 How to apply for a learner licence (LL) online

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

Wheresarathi.parivahan.gov.in (via parivahan.gov.in) — choose your state
AgeGearless two-wheeler up to 50cc: 16 (with parent consent); other vehicles: 18 — as shown on the portal
FeeShown on the portal for your state
ValidityLL valid 6 months; permanent DL test after 30 days and before the LL expires

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Aadhaar number and the Aadhaar-linked mobile (for the OTP)
  • Address as in Aadhaar — your RTO is picked from it automatically
  • Photo and signature (the photo usually comes from Aadhaar e-KYC)
  • Above 40: a doctor's medical certificate (Form 1A)
  • Under 18: consent of a parent/guardian
  • For the online test: a phone/laptop with a camera and good light

1Open the Sarathi portal and choose your state

Go to parivahan.gov.in → Online Services → Driving Licence Related Services, or open sarathi.parivahan.gov.in directly. Pick your state from the list — each state's page can look a little different.

What to fill in:

  • State
  • Language (Hindi/English)

Open the official website ↗

2Choose "Apply for Learner Licence" and do Aadhaar e-KYC

In the Learner Licence menu, pick the option for a new LL. Enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Choose the option saying you hold no driving/learner licence. After e-KYC your name, address and photo are filled in automatically.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number
  • Aadhaar OTP
  • Applicant category

Open the official website ↗

3Fill the form and upload documents

Check the father's/guardian's name, qualification, blood group and mobile. Choose the Class of Vehicle — e.g. gearless two-wheeler (MCWOG), geared two-wheeler (MCWG), car (LMV). Fill the self-declaration (Form 1). If you are over 40, upload Form 1A. Your application number arrives by SMS — note it down.

What to fill in:

  • Class of Vehicle
  • Self-declaration
  • Upload required documents
  • Save the application number

Open the official website ↗

4Pay the fee and watch the road-safety video

The portal shows the fee for your state and the classes you chose — pay by UPI, card or net banking and keep the receipt. In many states you must watch a road-safety tutorial video in full before the test (it can't be skipped).

What to fill in:

  • Fee payment
  • Download receipt
  • Tutorial video (with OTP)

Open the official website ↗

5Take the online LL test and download the licence

In states that offer the test from home, you take it on a phone/laptop after Aadhaar and face authentication; in other states you book a slot and take it at the RTO. Questions cover traffic signs, road rules and safe driving. Once you pass, download the LL right there.

What to fill in:

  • Test password (SMS)
  • Face authentication
  • Download the LL

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

On sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, choose your state and open "Application Status" or "Complete your pending application" with your application number and date of birth. You can finish an incomplete application from there. Between 30 days and 6 months after getting the LL, book your permanent driving licence (DL) test slot on the same portal.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Did not get the pass marks — retake the test on the portal (the retest fee is shown there)
  • Face authentication failed — sit alone, in good light, facing the camera; another person's voice or help can cancel the test
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile not with you — no OTP; fix the number in Aadhaar first
  • Wrong Class of Vehicle chosen — pick the class of the vehicle you will actually drive
  • Paid a fake website or agent — use only parivahan.gov.in and sarathi.parivahan.gov.in
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a learner licence from home?

Yes, in many states — do Aadhaar e-KYC on Sarathi and take the face-authenticated online test from home, with no RTO visit. Some states still hold the test at the RTO; check the options for your state on the portal.

What is the learner licence fee?

It depends on your state and vehicle class, and is shown on the portal at payment. Avoid agents who ask for extra money — you do not need an agent.

How long is an LL valid, and when can I get the permanent DL?

A learner licence is valid for 6 months. You can take the permanent DL driving test at least 30 days after getting the LL and before it expires.

What is asked in the learning licence test online?

Multiple-choice questions on traffic signs, road rules, overtaking, parking and safety. The pass mark is set by state rules and shown before the test.

Can I drive alone on an LL?

No. While driving on an LL, a person with a permanent licence must be with you (check your state's rule for two-wheelers), and the vehicle must show an "L" sign.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

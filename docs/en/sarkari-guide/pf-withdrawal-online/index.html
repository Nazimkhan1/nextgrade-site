🏦 How to withdraw PF online

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

WhereEPFO member portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) — with UAN and password
You needActive UAN + Aadhaar, PAN and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account (KYC)
FeeNone — EPFO's online service is free
Helpline14470 / 1800-118-005 (EPFO)

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • UAN (Universal Account Number) and password
  • Aadhaar-linked mobile — for the OTP
  • PAN — linked to your UAN
  • Bank account and IFSC — in your name, seeded with Aadhaar
  • For full withdrawal: your last employer must have entered your Date of Exit

1Log in to the member portal with your UAN

Open unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in and enter your UAN, password and captcha. If your UAN is not activated, the portal says this is now done in the UMANG app with Aadhaar face authentication. Forgot the password? Use "Forgot Password" and the OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile.

What to fill in:

  • UAN
  • Password
  • Captcha

Open the official website ↗

2Check that your KYC is complete

Under Manage → KYC, Aadhaar, PAN and the bank account should show as "Approved/Verified". Your name, date of birth and gender must match between EPFO and Aadhaar — fix any mismatch first, or the claim may get stuck.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar — Verified
  • PAN — Verified
  • Bank account + IFSC — Verified

Open the official website ↗

3Open Online Services → Claim

From the top menu choose Online Services → Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D) (the label may differ slightly). Your bank account is shown — enter its last digits, click Verify, accept the terms and continue.

What to fill in:

  • Last digits of the bank account
  • Verify
  • Accept the terms

Open the official website ↗

4Pick the claim type, reason and amount

Still working and need part of the money? Choose PF Advance (Form 31), pick the reason group and enter the amount — the portal itself shows how much you can get. Left the job and want everything? Form 19 (PF) plus Form 10C for the pension part — when you become eligible follows the conditions shown on the portal. Fill in your address; in some cases you may be asked to upload a document.

What to fill in:

  • Form 31 / 19 / 10C
  • Reason for withdrawal
  • Amount
  • Full address

Open the official website ↗

5Submit with Aadhaar OTP and track it

Click "Get Aadhaar OTP", enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile and submit. Save the claim/reference number. Then follow it under Online Services → Track Claim Status.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar OTP
  • Save the claim number
  • Track Claim Status

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

The member portal shows progress under Online Services → Track Claim Status; you can also check it on passbook.epfindia.gov.in and in the UMANG app, and SMS updates come to your registered mobile. EPFO says claims that clear all checks can be settled automatically (auto mode) in under three days; others take longer. If it is stuck, file a grievance at epfigms.gov.in or call 14470.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Bank account not seeded with Aadhaar, or the account name differs from EPFO records — fix your KYC first
  • Name/date of birth differ between Aadhaar and EPFO — correct the profile, then claim again
  • Full withdrawal requested but the last employer has not entered your Date of Exit — ask them to update it
  • Less than 12 months of service for a partial withdrawal, or an amount above the limit shown on the portal
  • UAN password or OTP shared with an agent/cyber café — risk of misuse; change the password at once
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

How many days does an online PF withdrawal take?

EPFO says claims that pass all checks can be settled in auto mode in under three days; other claims can take longer. Keep an eye on Track Claim Status.

How much PF can I withdraw while still working?

EPFO has grouped partial-withdrawal reasons into three types — essential needs (illness, education, marriage), housing, and special circumstances. You need at least 12 months of service, and part of the contributions must stay in the account as a minimum balance (the claim form shows how much — the rule changed recently). The claim form on the portal shows exactly how much you can take.

When can I withdraw my full PF after leaving a job?

EPFO changed the waiting period for full withdrawal after unemployment in 2025-26 and reports differ, so we are not printing a fixed number — the condition shown in your claim form is what applies. Full withdrawal is allowed on retirement at 58.

Should I pay an agent to withdraw my PF?

No. EPFO's online services are free, and EPFO itself has warned members against unauthorised agents and cyber cafés. Use only epfindia.gov.in / .gov.in websites and never share your UAN password or OTP — EPFO never asks for money or OTPs over the phone.

My claim was rejected — what now?

Track Claim Status shows the reason. Fix that gap (KYC, bank details, a name mismatch, etc.) and file the claim again. If unclear, file a grievance at epfigms.gov.in or call 14470.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

📱 How to change the mobile number in Aadhaar

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

WhereUIDAI's new Aadhaar App (Android/iPhone) — or an Aadhaar centre
You needOld number working (OTP) + face authentication
FeeShown in the app — reported as ₹75
Helpline1947 (UIDAI)

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Aadhaar number
  • Old (Aadhaar-linked) mobile — for the OTP
  • New mobile number — it also gets an OTP
  • Your face in good light (face authentication)
  • If the old number is gone: visit an Aadhaar centre; no documents — just Aadhaar and biometrics

1Install the official Aadhaar App

Search "Aadhaar" on Play Store/App Store — take only the app published by UIDAI (avoid look-alikes). The app was launched on 28 January 2026.

What to fill in:

  • Publisher: Unique Identification Authority of India

Open the official website ↗

2Add your Aadhaar profile

Enter your Aadhaar number, the OTP sent to your old mobile, and scan your face. One phone can hold up to 5 family profiles.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar number
  • OTP (on the old number)
  • Face scan

Open the official website ↗

3Choose "Mobile Number Update"

Services → My Aadhaar Update → Mobile Number Update (labels may differ slightly) → enter the new number → enter the OTP sent to the new number.

What to fill in:

  • New mobile number
  • OTP on the new number

Open the official website ↗

4Confirm with your face and pay

Do the face authentication and pay the fee shown in the app (UPI/card). Keep a screenshot of the request number (URN/SRN) at the end.

What to fill in:

  • Face authentication
  • Fee payment
  • Save the request number

Open the official website ↗

5Old number not with you? Visit a centre

If the old number is closed or lost, the app can't do it. Book an Aadhaar Seva Kendra slot at appointments.uidai.gov.in (or walk into a nearby Aadhaar centre/post office); the new number is linked after biometric verification.

What to fill in:

  • City and centre
  • Service: mobile update
  • Carry your Aadhaar

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

The app shows the update status; you can also check it on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in under "Check Enrolment & Update Status" with the request number. Once updated, OTPs start coming to the new number. Help: 1947.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Old number not available — the app can't do it; go to a centre
  • Face authentication fails in poor light or with glare on glasses — retry in good light
  • OTP shared with a fake app or agent — use only UIDAI's app
  • New number inactive — use a working SIM
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my Aadhaar mobile number from home?

Yes — in UIDAI's new Aadhaar App (from 28 January 2026), if you still have the old number. UIDAI said 1 million people had done it through the app by February 2026.

What if my old number is closed?

Then visit an Aadhaar centre; you can book a slot at appointments.uidai.gov.in. No documents are needed; you are verified by biometrics.

What is the fee?

Pay the fee shown in the app or at the centre — reported as ₹75. If someone asks for more, complain on 1947.

Can I change my email too?

Yes, email can also be updated in Aadhaar; look for the "Email" option in the app/portal.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

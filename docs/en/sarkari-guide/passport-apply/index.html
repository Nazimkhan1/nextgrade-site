🛂 How to apply for a passport (online)

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

WherePassport Seva portal (passportindia.gov.in) / mPassport Seva app
Fee (36 pages)Normal ₹2,500 · Tatkaal ₹5,000 (from 1 July 2026)
60 pagesNormal ₹3,500 · Tatkaal ₹6,000
Under 1836 pages normal ₹1,750

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Proof of address — e.g. Aadhaar, electricity/water bill, bank passbook with photo
  • Proof of date of birth — e.g. birth certificate, Class 10 marksheet, PAN, Aadhaar
  • Carry originals to the PSK

1Register on the portal

passportindia.gov.in → "Register Now".

What to fill in:

  • Passport office (by your address)
  • Name, date of birth, email, login ID

Open the official website ↗

2Fill the application

Log in → "Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue".

What to fill in:

  • Fresh, Normal/Tatkaal, 36/60 pages
  • Personal, family, address, emergency contact
  • Previous passport (if any)

Open the official website ↗

3Pay and book an appointment

"Pay and Schedule Appointment" — pay online and pick a date at your nearest PSK/POPSK.

What to fill in:

  • PSK/POPSK
  • Date and time
  • Payment

Open the official website ↗

4Visit the PSK

Go on time with the appointment receipt and originals — photo and fingerprints are taken there.

What to fill in:

  • Appointment receipt
  • Originals + photocopies

Open the official website ↗

5Police verification

Police verify at your address; the passport then comes by post.

What to fill in:

  • Be at home
  • Keep ID documents ready

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

passportindia.gov.in → "Track Application Status" with the file number (on the receipt) and date of birth. You also get SMS updates.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Name/date of birth differ between form and documents
  • Incomplete address on the address proof
  • Arriving late or forgetting originals
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

What is the passport fee?

From 1 July 2026: 36-page normal ₹2,500, Tatkaal ₹5,000; 60 pages ₹3,500/₹6,000. Check the portal before paying.

How long does it take?

Normal usually a few weeks (depends on police verification); Tatkaal is faster.

Do I need an agent?

No, you can do the whole application online yourself.

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. Government websites change their pages, buttons and fees from time to time — some names may look different when you do this; then go by the official site. We will keep updating this guide.

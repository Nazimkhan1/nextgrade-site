🏥 How to get an Ayushman card (PM-JAY)

Last checked: 26 November 2026

At a glance

Wherebeneficiary.nha.gov.in or the Ayushman App
FeeMaking the card is free
You needAadhaar + mobile (OTP)
Helpline14555

Open the official website ↗

📄 What you need

  • Aadhaar card
  • Mobile number
  • Ration card/family details (help find eligibility)

1Log in on the portal

Open beneficiary.nha.gov.in → log in as "Beneficiary".

What to fill in:

  • Mobile number
  • Captcha
  • OTP

Open the official website ↗

2Choose scheme and state, search eligibility

Scheme: PMJAY (if aged 70+, choose the "PMJAY Vay Vandana" option) → state, district → search by Aadhaar or ration card.

What to fill in:

  • Scheme
  • State, District
  • Search by: Aadhaar / Family ID

Open the official website ↗

3Do e-KYC

When your name appears, press "eKYC" — Aadhaar OTP or face authentication.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar OTP
  • Photo (camera)
  • Mobile number

Open the official website ↗

4Download the card

After approval, "Download Card" — confirm with Aadhaar OTP and download the PDF.

What to fill in:

  • Aadhaar OTP

Open the official website ↗

🔍 How to check the status

Log in and see the status next to your name (Approved/Pending). For help call 14555 or visit an empanelled hospital/CSC centre.

❌ Why applications get stuck or rejected

  • Name not in the list — check eligibility first
  • Name/date of birth differ from Aadhaar
  • Wrong scheme picked for 70+
⚠️ Stay safe from fraud
  • Use only the official .gov.in (or linked) websites.
  • Never share an OTP, full Aadhaar details or passwords — not even on the phone.
  • Avoid agents who ask for extra money for a "guaranteed" job.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee for the Ayushman card?

No — making the card is free. Be careful if anyone asks for money.

What about people aged 70 and above?

All senior citizens aged 70+, regardless of income, can get the Ayushman Vay Vandana card; age is checked by Aadhaar e-KYC.

How much treatment is covered?

Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year at empanelled hospitals; see pmjay.gov.in for the current rules.

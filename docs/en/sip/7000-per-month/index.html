A SIP of ₹7,000 a month for 20 years, at an average 12% a year, can grow to about ₹69,94,035 — while you invest only ₹16,80,000 in total.

When will it reach ₹1 crore?

At 12% a year, this SIP can reach ₹1 crore in about 23 years.

Ways to lower your EMI

  • Mutual fund returns are not guaranteed — 12% is only an example; equity funds have often given that over long periods, but they can also lose money.
  • Raise your SIP by 10% every year (step-up) — it makes a big difference to the final amount.
  • Don’t stop your SIP when markets fall — you buy more units cheaply.

🪔 Navratri 2027 — which goddess on which day

There are two main Navratris in 2027: Chaitra Navratri from 7 to 15 April (Ram Navami), and Sharad Navratri from 30 September to 8 October, followed by Dussehra on 9 October. Below is a day-by-day list for both — if today is a Navratri day, its row is highlighted automatically.

Dates follow the Drik Panchang (New Delhi) 2027 calendar. Because tithis can shorten or lengthen, Ashtami/Navami may shift by a day in some places — confirm fasting and puja timings with your local panchang or priest.

The nine forms of Durga — what each one means

1. Maa Shailputri

Daughter of the mountain king Himavan — the first form of Parvati. She rides the bull Nandi, with a trident in her right hand and a lotus in her left. She is worshipped on day one along with the kalash; she stands for steadiness and new beginnings.

2. Maa Brahmacharini

The form of penance — Parvati performed years of hard tapas to win Shiva. A japa mala in one hand, a water pot (kamandal) in the other, barefoot. She teaches patience, self-control and dedication — a special inspiration for students.

3. Maa Chandraghanta

A bell-shaped half-moon on her forehead gives her the name. Ten arms, riding a lion, ready for battle yet calm in face. The sound of her bell is believed to drive away fear and negativity.

4. Maa Kushmanda

The story says that when nothing existed, the Goddess created the universe with a gentle smile — “ku” (a little), “ushma” (warmth), “anda” (the cosmic egg). With eight arms she is also called Ashtabhuja; she rides a lion.

5. Maa Skandamata

Mother of Lord Skanda (Kartikeya). She holds the child Skanda in her lap and sits on a lotus, so she is also called Padmasana. This is the day to honour the loving, motherly form of the Goddess.

6. Maa Katyayani

Named after the sage Katyayana, in whose hermitage she appeared. In this form the Goddess slew Mahishasura. In the Bhagavata story the gopis of Braj kept the Katyayani vrat for a good husband, so unmarried girls also worship her.

7. Maa Kalaratri

The fiercest form — dark in colour, hair loose, riding a donkey. She destroys darkness, fear and evil. Her look is terrifying but she always gives good results, so she is also called Shubhankari.

8. Maa Mahagauri

A radiant, very fair form, dressed in white and riding a white bull. The story says that after her long penance Shiva washed her with Ganga water and her brightness returned. She is worshipped on Ashtami (Durga Ashtami), when many homes hold kanya pujan.

9. Maa Siddhidatri

The giver of all siddhis — success and wisdom. Seated on a lotus, with four arms. Shiva’s Ardhanarishvara form is said to come from her grace. Navratri is completed on Navami with her worship, a havan and kanya pujan.

How to do ghatasthapana — the simple way

  • On day one, bathe, clean the puja corner and spread a red cloth.
  • Fill a clay pot with clean soil and sow barley — over nine days it sprouts into “jaware”.
  • Fill a copper or clay kalash with water, a betel nut, a coin and rice; place mango leaves on its mouth and a coconut wrapped in a red chunri on top.
  • Set the kalash on or beside the barley pot, place a picture of the Goddess and light a lamp. Families who keep an akhand jyot keep it burning for all nine days.
  • Each day, meditate on that day’s form, sing the Durga aarti and, if you can, read the Durga Saptashati or Durga Chalisa.

What to eat and avoid while fasting

  • Allowed: fruit, milk and curd, paneer, sabudana, kuttu (buckwheat) and singhara (water chestnut) flour, sama (barnyard millet) rice, potato, sweet potato, makhana, peanuts and sendha (rock) salt.
  • Avoided: wheat, rice and lentils, ordinary salt, onion and garlic, meat, eggs and alcohol. Many families also skip turmeric and some spices — follow your home tradition.
  • Drink plenty of water and avoid too much fried food at once. People with diabetes, pregnant women and anyone on medication should fast only on a doctor’s advice — eating only fruit, or one meal a day, also counts as a full fast.

Kanya pujan

  • On Ashtami or Navami (as per family custom), nine girls aged 2 to 10 and one small boy (as Langur/Bhairav) are invited home.
  • Their feet are washed, a tilak is applied, and they are served halwa, puri and chana, then given dakshina or a small gift as they leave.
  • The message is simple: every girl is a form of the Goddess — respecting her, educating her and keeping her safe is the real worship.

Chaitra vs Sharad Navratri — what is the difference?

There are four Navratris in a year, but two are the main ones. Chaitra Navratri comes in spring and begins with the Hindu new year — the same day is Gudi Padwa in Maharashtra and Ugadi in the south — and its ninth day is Ram Navami, the birthday of Lord Rama. Sharad Navratri comes in autumn; the tenth day after it is Vijayadashami (Dussehra), and Diwali follows about twenty days later.

The nine forms worshipped are the same in both, but the mood differs. Sharad Navratri brings Gujarat’s garba and dandiya, Bengal’s Durga Puja and the Ramlila of north India. Chaitra Navratri is quieter — the kalash, fasting and Ramayana readings at home.

The spirit of Navratri is victory over the evil within: nine days of restraint, simple food, prayer and helping others. Even if you do not fast, helping someone in need, honouring daughters and singing the aarti together at home are just as beautiful ways to keep it.

Frequently asked questions

When does Chaitra Navratri 2027 start?

Wednesday 7 April 2027, ending Thursday 15 April 2027, which is Ram Navami.

When is Sharad Navratri 2027?

Ghatasthapana on Thursday 30 September 2027; Maha Navami on 8 October and Dussehra on Saturday 9 October 2027.

Which goddess is worshipped on the first day?

Maa Shailputri, along with ghatasthapana.

When is Durga Ashtami 2027?

14 April in Chaitra Navratri and 7 October in Sharad Navratri 2027 (confirm with your local panchang).

Can I drink tea during the fast?

Most families allow tea with milk; it depends on your home tradition.

Should kanya pujan be on Ashtami or Navami?

Both are correct — follow your family’s custom.

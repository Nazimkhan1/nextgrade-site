Why do the two Bengals have different dates?

  • In West Bengal the panjika starts a new month on the day after the Sun moves into the next sign (sankranti). So months are 29 to 32 days long and Pohela Boishakh falls on 14 or 15 April.
  • Bangladesh has used a revised fixed calendar since 2019 (1426): Pohela Boishakh is always 14 April; Boishakh to Ashwin have 31 days, Kartik to Magh and Chaitra 30, Falgun 29 (30 in a leap year).
  • So the same day can have a Bangla date 1–2 days apart in the two countries. Bangabda = English year − 593 (after Pohela Boishakh), − 594 before it.
  • We calculate with astronomical formulas (Lahiri ayanamsa); some printed panjikas (for example Gupta Press) can differ by one day now and then.

Frequently asked questions

Which Bengali year is it now?

Bangabda 1433 is running; 1434 starts on Pohela Boishakh — 15 April 2027 in West Bengal, 14 April 2027 in Bangladesh.

When is Pohela Boishakh 2027?

Thursday 15 April 2027 in West Bengal (Mesha sankranti on 14 April); Wednesday 14 April 2027 in Bangladesh.

What are the 12 Bengali months?

Boishakh, Joishtho, Asharh, Shrabon, Bhadro, Ashwin, Kartik, Agrahayan, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra — two months make each of the six seasons.

When does the Bangla date change?

Traditionally at sunrise; this page changes it at midnight IST for convenience.

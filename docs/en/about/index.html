About NextGrade

NextGrade (nextgrade.in) is a small independent website made in India.

What we do

We make three kinds of things: simple, reliable guides to Indian festivals — their story, meaning, traditions, food and wishes; small festival games; and free web apps such as Hisab Kitab (an expense and lend/borrow tracker) and the Wish Card Maker. The site is available in Hindi, English, Hinglish and Bengali.

Where our information comes from

Festival pages are based on widely followed traditions, well-known stories from religious texts and public sources. For festivals that follow the lunar calendar we give the month and tithi, because the calendar date changes every year. If you spot a mistake, please tell us and we will fix it.

Our apps and your data

We don't keep your information on any server or database. Our apps keep your data on your own device, and the Wish Card Maker never sends your names or message anywhere — the card is made in your browser.

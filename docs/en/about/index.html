About NextGrade

NextGrade (nextgrade.in) is a small independent website made in India.

What we do

We make three kinds of things: simple, reliable guides to Indian festivals — their story, meaning, traditions, food and wishes; small festival games; and free web apps such as Hisab Kitab (an expense and lend/borrow tracker) and the Wish Card Maker. The site is available in Hindi, English, Hinglish and Bengali.

Where our information comes from

Festival pages are based on widely followed traditions, well-known stories from religious texts and public sources. For festivals that follow the lunar calendar we give the month and tithi, because the calendar date changes every year. If you spot a mistake, please tell us and we will fix it.

Our apps and your data

We don't keep your information on any server or database. Our apps keep your data on your own device, and the Wish Card Maker never sends your names or message anywhere — the card is made in your browser.

What you'll find here

  • Festival guides — the story, meaning, customs, food and common questions for every major festival, from Christmas to Diwali.
  • Wishes — messages we write ourselves for everyday moments like Good Morning, birthdays, anniversaries and Jumma Mubarak, with copy and WhatsApp buttons.
  • Stories — classic tales from the Panchatantra, Akbar-Birbal, Tenali Raman and more, retold in our own words, with read-aloud and night mode.
  • An Islamic calendar, festival games, animated wishes with a name and the Wish Card Maker.

How we write

We write every page ourselves — nothing is copied from other websites or books. Before writing, we cross-check several public sources, almanacs (panchang) and well-known religious traditions. Where customs differ between regions, we say so. We write about faith with respect and without taking sides, and for dates we always suggest confirming with your local panchang, gurdwara, mosque or church.

Ads and costs

NextGrade is completely free. The cost of running it is covered by ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are never placed over a game or a card, and we never ask anyone to click on ads. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Corrections and suggestions

We re-check festival dates and facts every year. If you find a mistake or something out of date, or want to suggest a story or festival, write to us through the contact page — every email is read.

⚖️ BMI Calculator

Enter your weight and height to find your Body Mass Index (BMI) and whether it is in the healthy range.

What is BMI

BMI stands for "Body Mass Index", a simple way to check whether your weight is right for your height. It uses weight and height to give a number that shows whether the weight is low, normal or high.

Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ (height × height) [height in metres]. If the height is in centimetres, first divide it by 100 to get metres.

Example

Suppose the weight is 60 kg and the height is 165 cm (1.65 m). Then BMI = 60 ÷ (1.65 × 1.65) = 60 ÷ 2.7225 ≈ 22.0, which falls in the "Normal" category.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is usually considered healthy. Below that is underweight, and above it is overweight.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ (height in metres × height in metres). This calculator does the maths for you.

Does BMI show complete health?

No. BMI is only a general guide. It can differ with muscle, age and body type, so see a doctor for a full check-up.

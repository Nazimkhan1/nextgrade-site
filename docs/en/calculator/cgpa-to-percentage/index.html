🎓 CGPA to Percentage Calculator

Enter your CGPA — see the approximate percentage by the CBSE formula (CGPA × 9.5) instantly.

CGPA and percentage

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average grade point out of 10. CBSE’s marksheets said that an indicative subject-wise percentage is the grade point multiplied by 9.5, and the same method is used for the overall CGPA.

CBSE arrived at 9.5 from the average marks of its top students. Remember, it is an approximate percentage. Many universities (engineering colleges, for example) use their own formula, printed on the back of the marksheet or in their rules — use that one in forms.

Formula

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

Reverse: CGPA = percentage ÷ 9.5

Examples

9.2 CGPA × 9.5 = 87.4%

8 CGPA × 9.5 = 76%

10 CGPA × 9.5 = 95%

75% ÷ 9.5 = about 7.9 CGPA

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

By the CBSE formula: percentage = CGPA × 9.5. For example 9 CGPA = 85.5%.

What is 9.2 CGPA in percentage?

9.2 × 9.5 = 87.4%.

Why is 10 CGPA not 100%?

The formula multiplies by 9.5, so 10 CGPA = 95%. It is an approximate percentage.

Does every university use this formula?

No. Many universities have their own formula — check the marksheet or the university’s rules.

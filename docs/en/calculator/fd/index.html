🏧 FD (Fixed Deposit) Calculator

Enter the deposit, interest rate and time to find how much interest your FD earns and the total at maturity.

What is an FD

FD stands for Fixed Deposit — putting a lump sum in a bank or post office for a fixed period at a fixed interest rate. Most banks compound FD interest quarterly (added every 3 months), and this calculator works it out the same way.

Formula

Maturity A = P × (1 + r ÷ 400)^(4 × t), where P = deposit, r = yearly rate, t = years. The 400 and 4 are because interest is added quarterly (4 times a year). Interest = A − P.

Example

An FD of ₹1,00,000 at 7% per year for 5 years: maturity ≈ ₹1,41,478, which is about ₹41,478 of interest.

Frequently asked questions

How is FD maturity calculated?

A = P × (1 + r/400)^(4t), with interest compounded quarterly. This calculator does the maths for you.

How often is FD interest added?

In most banks quarterly, i.e. every 3 months (4 times a year). That is why the real FD return is a little more than the stated rate.

Is FD interest tax-free?

No, FD interest is taxable, and beyond a limit the bank also deducts TDS. This calculator shows the amount before tax.

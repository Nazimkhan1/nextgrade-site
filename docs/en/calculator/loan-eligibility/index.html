✅ Loan Eligibility Calculator

Enter the EMI you can comfortably pay each month, the interest rate and tenure, to find how much loan that EMI can get you.

How it works

The first question when taking a loan is — how much loan can I get? It depends on how much EMI you can pay each month. This calculator works backwards from the EMI to show how much loan that instalment can support.

Formula

Loan P = EMI × [(1+i)ⁿ − 1] ÷ [i × (1+i)ⁿ], where i = monthly interest rate (yearly ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = number of months. It is the reverse of the EMI formula.

Example

If you can pay ₹15,000 EMI a month at 9% per year for 20 years, you can get a loan of about ₹16.67 lakh.

Frequently asked questions

How much loan can I get?

It depends on how much EMI you can pay each month. Banks usually allow EMIs up to 40–50% of your income. Enter your EMI here to estimate.

Why might this differ from the real approval?

The real approval also depends on income, credit score, existing loans and bank policy. This is only an EMI-based estimate.

What part of income should the EMI be?

It is advised to keep all EMIs together under 40–50% of your monthly income, so the rest of your expenses stay comfortable.

📈 SIP Calculator

Enter how much you invest each month, for how many years and the expected return, and see how much your money can grow.

What is SIP

SIP stands for "Systematic Investment Plan" — investing a fixed amount every month in a mutual fund. Over a long time, compounding turns small amounts into a large one. This calculator shows an estimate; actual returns depend on the market.

Formula

Total = M × [((1+i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i] × (1+i), where M = monthly amount, i = monthly return rate (yearly ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = number of months.

Example

₹5,000 a month at 12% expected yearly return for 10 years. You invest ₹6,00,000, and the total value is about ₹11.6 lakh — roughly ₹5.6 lakh of estimated gain.

Frequently asked questions

How does the SIP calculator work?

It applies compounding to your monthly investment to show how much money can build up over the chosen years. The return rate is your estimate.

Are these returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual funds are market-linked, so actual returns can be lower or higher. This is only an estimate.

How much will a ₹5000 SIP grow in 10 years at 12%?

About ₹11.6 lakh, of which ₹6 lakh is your investment and the rest is the estimated gain.

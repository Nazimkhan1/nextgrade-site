🏷️ Discount Calculator

Enter the original price and the discount percentage to instantly see how much you save and the new price.

How to find a discount

A sale says "40% off", but what will you actually pay? This calculator finds the saving and the final price from the price and the discount percentage in one tap.

Formula

Saving = price × discount% ÷ 100. Final price = price − saving.

Example

A ₹2,000 item at 25% off: saving = 2000 × 25 ÷ 100 = ₹500, final price = 2000 − 500 = ₹1,500.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price after 25% off on ₹2000?

₹1,500. You save ₹500.

How do I calculate a discount?

Saving = price × discount% ÷ 100, and final price = price − saving.

Does this add GST?

No, it only applies the discount. For GST, use our GST calculator.

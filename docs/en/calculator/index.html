🧮 Calculators

Handy free calculators for everyday use — all run on your own phone, with nothing sent anywhere. Each one comes with its formula, an example and FAQ.

🎂

Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to find how many years, months and days old you are — and how many days are left until your next birthday.

%

Percentage Calculator

Quickly find a percentage of any number — for example, "10% of 500 = 50".

🏦

EMI Calculator

Enter the loan amount, interest rate and tenure to find your monthly instalment (EMI), total interest and total payment.

⚖️

BMI Calculator

Enter your weight and height to find your Body Mass Index (BMI) and whether it is in the healthy range.

📈

SIP Calculator

Enter how much you invest each month, for how many years and the expected return, and see how much your money can grow.

🧾

GST Calculator

Enter an amount and the GST rate — whether you want to add GST or find the base price from a GST-inclusive amount.

🏷️

Discount Calculator

Enter the original price and the discount percentage to instantly see how much you save and the new price.

📅

Date Difference Calculator

Choose two dates and find the difference between them in days, weeks, months and years.

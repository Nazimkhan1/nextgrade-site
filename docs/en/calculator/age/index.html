🎂 Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to find how many years, months and days old you are — and how many days are left until your next birthday.

How to find your age

An age calculator finds the difference between your date of birth and today's (or any chosen) date and shows it in years, months and days. All the maths happens on your own phone — nothing is sent anywhere.

How it is calculated

Age = today's date − date of birth. First the full years are counted, then the remaining months, then the remaining days. Leap years (29 February) are taken into account, so the result is exact.

Example

Suppose someone was born on 15 August 2010 and today is 20 November 2026. Their age would be 16 years, 3 months and 5 days — about 5,942 days in all.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the age calculator?

Choose your date of birth above. The "Age on this date" field is already filled with today's date; you can change it if you like. Then press "Calculate age".

Is the age fully accurate?

Yes. It counts years, months and days separately and accounts for leap years, so the result is exact.

Is my date of birth saved anywhere?

No. All the calculation happens in your browser; no data is sent or stored.

📅 Date Difference Calculator

Choose two dates and find the difference between them in days, weeks, months and years.

What it is for

Knowing how many days lie between two dates is often useful — days left until an event, how many days of leave you took, or how long since something happened. This calculator shows the difference in days, weeks, months and years.

How it is calculated

Difference = second date − first date. It is shown as total days, and then split into years, months and days. Leap years are taken into account.

Example

Between 1 January 2026 and 20 November 2026 the difference is 323 days — about 10 months and 19 days, or 46 weeks and 1 day.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count days between two dates?

Choose both dates; the calculator shows the difference in days, weeks, months and years.

Are leap years counted?

Yes, the 29 February leap day is included, so the result is accurate.

Does the order of the dates matter?

No. Enter the dates in any order; the calculator always shows the correct (positive) difference.

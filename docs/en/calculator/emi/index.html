🏦 EMI Calculator

Enter the loan amount, interest rate and tenure to find your monthly instalment (EMI), total interest and total payment.

What is an EMI

EMI stands for "Equated Monthly Instalment" — the equal amount you pay every month. When we take a loan, we repay it with interest in small equal amounts over several months; that monthly amount is the EMI.

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1], where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n = number of months.

Example

Suppose a loan of ₹5,00,000 at 9% per year for 5 years (60 months). The monthly EMI would be about ₹10,379, the total interest about ₹1,22,734, and the total payment about ₹6,22,734.

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ [(1+r)ⁿ − 1], where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate and n the number of months. This calculator does the maths for you.

Should I enter the yearly or monthly rate?

Enter the yearly (per year) rate, such as 9%. The calculator converts it to a monthly rate automatically.

Does the EMI include interest?

Yes. Each EMI includes part of the principal and part of the interest; early on more goes to interest, later more goes to the principal.

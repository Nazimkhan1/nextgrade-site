📝 Marks Percentage Calculator

Enter your marks obtained and the total marks to instantly find your percentage and grade.

How to find percentage

An exam percentage tells you what percent of the total marks you scored. It is often needed for report cards, admissions and filling forms.

Formula

Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total marks) × 100. Add the marks of all subjects and divide by the total marks (for example 5 subjects × 100 = 500).

Example

Suppose you scored 425 out of 500 in 5 subjects. Then percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate marks percentage?

Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total marks) × 100. This calculator does the maths for you.

What percentage is 425 out of 500?

85%. (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Many boards use CGPA × 9.5 for an approximate percentage, but this calculator gives the exact percentage directly from marks.

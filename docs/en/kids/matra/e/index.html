✍️ E matra (े) words

Hindi E matra (े) words: केला, सेब, शेर, पेड़, रेल, पेन, खेत, बेटी. Tap a word to hear it.

Tap a card 🔊 to hear the name. Press “Slideshow” to see and hear every name one by one.

If you hear no sound, turn on your phone's sound/volume and tap a card once (some phones only allow sound after the first tap).

How to use

  1. Tap any card — you will hear its name spoken aloud.
  2. Press “Slideshow” — each card grows and speaks its name automatically.
  3. The sound is made on your own phone — it works even on a slow connection.

The writing rule (े)

The e matra (े) is one slanting stroke on top of the letter: क + े = के.

More words

मेज़ (mez) खेल (khel) बेटा (beta) देश (desh) रेत (ret) पेन (pen) सेना (sena) टेलीफ़ोन (telifon) तेल (tel) भेड़ (bhed)

A common mistake

Mixing e (े) and ai (ै) — e has one stroke on top, ai has two.

Read these

  • बेटा खेल रहा है।
  • मेज़ पर पेन है।
  • रेल तेज़ चली।

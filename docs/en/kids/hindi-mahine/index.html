🪔 Hindi Months (Chaitra to Phalgun)

The 12 months of the Hindu calendar — Chaitra, Vaishakh, Jyeshtha to Phalgun — with the English months they fall in, spoken aloud.

Tap a card 🔊 to hear the name. Press “Slideshow” to see and hear every name one by one.

If you hear no sound, turn on your phone's sound/volume and tap a card once (some phones only allow sound after the first tap).

Full list: Hindi Months (Chaitra to Phalgun)

☀️ज्येष्ठमई–जून
🌦️आषाढ़जून–जुलाई
🌧️श्रावणजुलाई–अगस्त

🌤️आश्विनसितंबर–अक्टूबर
🪔कार्तिकअक्टूबर–नवंबर
🍂मार्गशीर्षनवंबर–दिसंबर
❄️पौषदिसंबर–जनवरी
🌫️माघजनवरी–फ़रवरी
🎨फाल्गुनफ़रवरी–मार्च

How to use

  1. Tap any card — you will hear its name spoken aloud.
  2. Press “Slideshow” — each card grows and speaks its name automatically.
  3. The sound is made on your own phone — it works even on a slow connection.

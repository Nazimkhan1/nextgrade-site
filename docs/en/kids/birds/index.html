🦜 Birds Names

Learn bird names with pictures and audio — Parrot (तोता), Peacock (मोर), Pigeon (कबूतर), Crow (कौआ) and more.

Tap a card 🔊 to hear the name. Press “Slideshow” to see and hear every name one by one.

If you hear no sound, turn on your phone's sound/volume and tap a card once (some phones only allow sound after the first tap).

How to use

  1. Tap any card — you will hear its name spoken aloud.
  2. Press “Slideshow” — each card grows and speaks its name automatically.
  3. The sound is made on your own phone — it works even on a slow connection.

For parents and teachers

Bird names are one of the most fun places to start, because children can spot most of these birds around the house — crow, sparrow, pigeon, parrot, peacock. Tap each card to hear the name, then ask your child which of these birds they saw today.

Activities to try at home

  • Count birds from the roof or balcony for five minutes in the morning and tick off the ones you see.
  • Guess the sound: imitate a koel, a crow’s caw or a rooster’s call and let your child name the bird.
  • In summer, leave water in a clay bowl and watch which birds come to drink.

Questions and answers

What is the national bird of India?
The peacock is India’s national bird.

From what age can children learn bird names?
From about two years children start recognising birds in pictures; by three or four they say the names themselves.

🔢 Numbers 1 to 100

Learn numbers 1 to 100 in words with sound: One to One Hundred, with the Hindi name (एक, दो, तीन…) under each number. Free for kids, tap to hear.

Tap a card 🔊 to hear the name. Press “Slideshow” to see and hear every name one by one.

If you hear no sound, turn on your phone's sound/volume and tap a card once (some phones only allow sound after the first tap).

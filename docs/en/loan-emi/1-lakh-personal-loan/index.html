EMI on a ₹1 lakh personal loan

On a ₹1 lakh loan at 13% a year for 3 years, the EMI is about ₹3,369 a month. Total interest over the loan comes to ₹21,298.

Salary needed for this loan

Banks usually want all your EMIs together to stay under about 50% of your monthly income. So an EMI of ₹3,369 needs a net monthly income of at least about ₹6,739 (with no other EMIs).

Ways to lower your EMI

  • Even 0.5% lower interest changes the total by lakhs on a long loan — compare 2–3 banks and keep a good CIBIL score (750+).
  • Prepay something once a year — most banks charge no prepayment fee on floating-rate home loans.
  • A longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises the total interest a lot — choose the shortest tenure whose EMI you can comfortably pay.

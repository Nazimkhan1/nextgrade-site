📜 Rules of desi games

How to play India's traditional games — what you need, step-by-step rules, outs and winning — in simple words, with a free online version of each game.

🎯

How to play Carrom — full rules

Carrom is the board game found in almost every Indian home. You flick a striker with your finger to send your coins into the four corner pockets. Here are the full rules in simple words.

🔵

How to play Kanche (Marbles) — full rules

Kanche — colourful glass marbles — is one of the oldest street games in India. There are many ways to play; here are the two most popular, the pit game and the circle game, in simple words.

🏏

How to play Gilli-Danda — full rules

Gilli-danda is an old village and street game that many call "desi cricket". A long stick (danda) is used to flick a small wooden piece (gilli) into the air and hit it far. Here are the rules in simple words.

🪨

How to play Pitthu (Lagori / Seven Stones) — full rules

Pitthu is a team game played with seven flat stones and a ball. One team knocks down the pile and tries to rebuild it, while the other team tries to stop them by hitting them with the ball. Here are the rules in simple words.

🏏 How to play Gilli-Danda — full rules

Gilli-danda is an old village and street game that many call "desi cricket". A long stick (danda) is used to flick a small wooden piece (gilli) into the air and hit it far. Here are the rules in simple words.

▶️ Play online

What you need

  • A danda — a strong stick about one and a half to two feet long
  • A gilli — a 3 to 6 inch piece of wood with both ends shaved to a point
  • A small, narrow pit in the ground
  • An open ground with no glass or people nearby
  • 2 or more players (can also be played in teams)

How to play, step by step

  1. One player (or team) bats; the others field in front.
  2. First shot: lay the gilli across the pit. Put the danda under it and lift it hard so the gilli flies far towards the fielders.
  3. If a fielder catches the gilli in the air, the batter is out.
  4. If it is not caught, the batter lays the danda across the pit. A fielder throws the gilli from where it landed towards the danda. If it hits the danda, or lands very close (within one danda length), the batter is out.
  5. If the batter survives, they go to where the gilli lies and tap one pointed end with the danda — the gilli jumps up.
  6. While it is in the air, hit it hard with the danda to send it far. Usually you get up to three such hits.
  7. If a fielder catches a gilli hit from the air, the batter is also out.
  8. When a batter is out, the next player bats.

Points and winning

After the last hit, the distance from where the gilli stopped to the pit is measured in danda lengths. Each danda length is one point.

The player or team that first reaches an agreed total (such as 100), or has the most points after everyone has batted, wins. Rules vary from place to place, so agree on them before playing.

Other names

It is called danguli in Bengal, guli-badi in Odisha, chinni-dandu in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka, kuttiyum-kolum in Kerala and dandi-biyo in Nepal. A similar game in English is called tip-cat.

Stay safe

  • The gilli flies fast — nobody should stand right in front of or very close to the batter.
  • Play in an open ground away from houses, cars and windows.
  • Do not make the gilli's ends too sharp.
  • Small children should play with an adult nearby.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get out in gilli-danda?

In three ways: the gilli is caught in the air, the fielder's throw hits the danda laid across the pit, or the throw lands very close to the pit (within one danda length).

How are points counted in gilli-danda?

The distance from where the gilli stopped to the pit is measured with the danda — each danda length is one point.

What is gilli-danda called in Bengal?

In Bengal it is called danguli (ডাংগুলি).

🎯 How to play Carrom — full rules

Carrom is the board game found in almost every Indian home. You flick a striker with your finger to send your coins into the four corner pockets. Here are the full rules in simple words.

What you need

  • A carrom board with four corner pockets
  • 9 white and 9 black coins
  • 1 red coin — the Queen
  • 1 striker (bigger and heavier than the coins)
  • A little powder to make the board smooth
  • 2 players (facing each other) or 4 players (two teams of two)

How to play, step by step

  1. Place the Queen in the centre circle and arrange the other 18 coins around it, white and black in turn.
  2. Toss a coin. The winner plays first and usually takes the white coins.
  3. Place the striker between the two lines (baseline) on your side, touching both lines or inside the end circle.
  4. The first shot (the break) hits the coins in the centre to spread them out.
  5. Flick the striker with a finger — your hand or wrist must not cross the baseline.
  6. If you pocket one of your coins, you get another turn. If not, the turn passes to the other player.
  7. You may pocket the Queen only after pocketing at least one of your own coins. Right after the Queen, you must pocket one of your coins in the next shot — this is called "covering" the Queen.
  8. If the Queen is not covered, it goes back to the centre.
  9. The first player to pocket all 9 coins (and the Queen, if it is theirs) wins the board.

Fouls and points

Pocketing the striker, or sending in your opponent's coin by mistake, is a foul. After a foul, one of the coins you already pocketed goes back to the centre (also called a "due"), and your turn ends.

The winner of a board gets 1 point for each coin the opponent still has on the board, plus 3 points if the winner covered the Queen (under common rules the Queen's 3 points count only while the winner's score is below 22).

Usually the first to reach 25 points wins the match. At home you can play to a smaller score.

Other names and history

It is called carrom, carrom board or simply "carom" in many places. The game is believed to have started in India and nearby countries and spread across the world. Today there are international championships, and India, Sri Lanka and the Maldives are among the strongest carrom nations.

Easy tips to play better

  • Use only a little powder on the board.
  • Aim matters more than force.
  • Learn to pocket coins off the side cushion (rebound).
  • Before taking the Queen, keep a coin ready to cover it.

Frequently asked questions

When can I take the Queen in carrom?

Only after you have pocketed at least one of your own coins. After the Queen, you must cover it by pocketing one of your coins in the next shot.

What happens if the striker goes into a pocket?

It is a foul — one of your pocketed coins goes back to the centre and the turn passes to the other player.

How many coins are there in carrom?

19 in total — 9 white, 9 black and 1 red Queen, plus 1 striker.

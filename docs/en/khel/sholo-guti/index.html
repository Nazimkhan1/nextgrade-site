🔴 How to play Sholo Guti — full rules

Sholo Guti ("sixteen pieces" in Bengali) is an old two-player village game played on lines drawn on the ground, with pebbles or seeds as pieces. Each side has 16 pieces and captures by jumping over the other side's pieces. Here are the full rules in simple words.

▶️ Play online

What you need

  • A board drawn on the ground, a floor or paper — a 5×5 grid of points with straight and diagonal lines, plus a triangle at the top and bottom (37 points in all)
  • 16 matching pieces for one player (for example pebbles)
  • 16 different-looking pieces for the other (for example tamarind seeds)
  • 2 players

How to play, step by step

  1. Each player puts 16 pieces on the 6 points of their own triangle and the 10 points of the two nearest rows of the square. The 5 points of the middle row stay empty.
  2. Decide who goes first, then take turns.
  3. On your turn, move one piece along a line to the next empty point. Diagonal moves are allowed only where a diagonal line is drawn.
  4. If an enemy piece is right next to yours and the point behind it on the same straight line is empty, jump over it to that point. The piece you jumped over is removed.
  5. After a jump, if the same piece can capture again, it may keep jumping in the same turn — a chain capture.
  6. Many places play "majboori": if you can capture, you must. Agree before the game whether this rule is on.

Winning and draws

The player who captures all 16 of the other side's pieces wins.

If it is your turn and none of your pieces can move, you lose.

When both sides have only a few pieces left and nobody has captured for a long time, the game is usually agreed as a draw.

Other names and where it is played

It is called "Sholo Guti" in Bengal and Bangladesh, and is also played in villages of Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha and Uttar Pradesh as "Solah Goti" or similar names. In English it is sometimes called "Sixteen Soldiers". There is no firm record of where it began, and the rules change a little from place to place — for example, some villages do not allow backward moves.

Easy tips to play better

  • Never leave an empty point behind your piece — that is where the enemy lands after jumping.
  • Keep pieces in pairs or rows; they protect each other.
  • Before moving, check whether the other side can capture two pieces in return.
  • Central points crossed by diagonals allow 8 directions — they are worth holding.

Frequently asked questions

How many points does a Sholo Guti board have?

37 — 25 in the square and 6 in each triangle. At the start 32 pieces are placed and the 5 points of the middle row are empty.

Can pieces move backwards?

In the most common rules, yes: pieces move and capture in every direction along the lines. Some places forbid backward moves, so agree before you play.

How many pieces can be captured in one turn?

As many as the same piece can jump in straight lines — one piece per jump, and the direction may change between jumps.

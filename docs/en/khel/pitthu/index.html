🪨 How to play Pitthu (Lagori / Seven Stones) — full rules

Pitthu is a team game played with seven flat stones and a ball. One team knocks down the pile and tries to rebuild it, while the other team tries to stop them by hitting them with the ball. Here are the rules in simple words.

▶️ Play online

What you need

  • 7 flat stones (or tile pieces) that can sit on top of one another
  • A soft ball (tennis ball or rubber ball)
  • An open ground
  • A line on the ground to throw from
  • Two teams of 3 or more players each

How to play, step by step

  1. In the middle, stack the 7 stones one on top of the other, biggest at the bottom.
  2. Toss to decide which team knocks first (the attacking team).
  3. A player from the attacking team throws the ball from behind the line to knock down the pile. Usually each player gets three tries.
  4. If the ball misses the pile and the other team catches it directly or after one bounce, the thrower is out.
  5. As soon as the pile falls, the attacking team tries to stack the scattered stones again.
  6. At the same time the other team picks up the ball and tries to touch those players with it (a gentle hit below the waist). A player hit by the ball is out.
  7. Running with the ball is not allowed — it moves by passing between team-mates.
  8. If the attacking team rebuilds the whole pile and shouts "Pitthu!", they score a point.

Who wins

If all players of the rebuilding team are out, the turn changes and the other team becomes the attackers.

After the agreed number of rounds, the team with more points (more piles rebuilt) wins.

Other names

It is called pitthu or pitthu garam in north India, sitoliya in Rajasthan, lagori in Karnataka, ezhu kallu ("seven stones") in Tamil Nadu and lingorcha in Maharashtra. In English it is often called seven stones.

Stay safe

  • Always use a soft ball, never a hard cricket ball.
  • Hit only below the waist — never at the head or face.
  • Stones should not be too heavy or sharp.
  • Watch out for others while running.

Frequently asked questions

How many stones are used in pitthu?

Usually 7 flat stones, stacked one on top of the other with the biggest at the bottom.

How do you get out in pitthu?

If the other team catches your thrown ball, or if the ball touches you while you are rebuilding the pile.

Is lagori the same as pitthu?

Yes, it is the same game. It is called lagori in Karnataka, sitoliya in Rajasthan and pitthu in north India.

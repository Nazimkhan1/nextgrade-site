🔵 How to play Kanche (Marbles) — full rules

Kanche — colourful glass marbles — is one of the oldest street games in India. There are many ways to play; here are the two most popular, the pit game and the circle game, in simple words.

▶️ Play online

What you need

  • A few marbles for each player
  • One bigger marble (often called a "lakhota" or "dumper") for shooting
  • Flat, soft ground
  • A small pit for the pit game, or a circle drawn on the ground for the circle game
  • 2 or more players

How to play, step by step

  1. Learn to shoot: rest one finger on the ground and flick the marble by pulling it back with the thumb or another finger of the same hand.
  2. To decide the order, everyone tosses a marble from a line towards the pit (or a mark). The closest marble plays first.
  3. Pit game: first get your marble into the pit. After that you may aim at other players' marbles.
  4. If your marble hits another marble, it counts as a point for you (decide before playing that all marbles are returned at the end).
  5. If you miss, the turn passes to the next player.
  6. Circle game: draw a circle on the ground and everyone puts the same number of marbles inside it.
  7. Taking turns, shoot your big marble from outside the circle. Every marble knocked out of the circle is a point for that turn.
  8. If your own shooter stops inside the circle, many groups count it as a mistake and your turn ends.

Who wins

In the pit game, the player who hits the most marbles wins; in the circle game, the one who knocks the most marbles out of the circle wins.

Every street has its own small rules — how far you shoot from, or how far you may move your hand. Agree on the rules together before you start, so there are no fights.

Other names

Kanche are also called goli, goti, anti or banta in different parts of India. In English they are marbles. Children in many countries have played marbles for centuries.

Things to keep in mind

  • Do not give marbles to very small children (under 3) — they may put them in their mouth.
  • Wash your hands after playing.
  • Do not play for money or anything else — play for fun.
  • Do not play with cracked or broken marbles.

Frequently asked questions

What is the big marble used for?

The bigger, heavier marble is used for shooting, because it hits harder and moves the other marbles easily.

How do we decide who plays first?

Everyone tosses a marble from a line towards the pit or a mark. The one whose marble stops closest plays first.

Can I play kanche online?

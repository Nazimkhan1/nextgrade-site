🔀 Anekarthi Shabd — one word, many meanings

Anekarthi shabd are Hindi words with more than one meaning — “kal” is both yesterday and tomorrow. Here are 37 important ones with English meanings.

“Anek” means many and “arth” means meaning: a word with several meanings is an anekarthi shabd. Many Sanskrit-origin Hindi words are like this, so only the sentence tells you which meaning is meant.

For example, in “kisaan ne hal chalaaya” hal is a plough, but in “is sawaal ka hal nikaalo” it means a solution. “Maali ne haar banaaya” — a garland; “team ki haar hui” — a defeat. Poets use such words on purpose so that one word carries two meanings; this figure of speech is called shlesh alankaar.

Anekarthi words are the reverse of synonyms. Paryayvachi means many words, one meaning (soorya — ravi, bhaanu); anekarthi means one word, many meanings (kar — hand, tax, ray). Exams usually ask for two meanings of each word, sometimes with a sentence for each.

Easy ways to learn them

  • Make a short sentence for two meanings of each word.
  • Keep a separate list of words with many meanings, like saarang and hari.
  • Play the quiz below — it asks a different meaning each time.

Frequently asked questions

What is anekarthi shabd?

A word with more than one meaning, e.g. “kar” — hand, tax, ray of light.

How many meanings does “kal” have?

Yesterday, tomorrow, machine and peace.

Meanings of “saarang”?

Peacock, deer, cloud, snake, elephant.

Anekarthi vs paryayvachi?

Anekarthi: one word, many meanings. Paryayvachi: one meaning, many words.

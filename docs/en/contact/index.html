Contact us

Suggestions, a mistake on a page or a question about one of our apps — email us.

Email: contact@nextgrade.in

We usually reply within 2-3 working days. Please never send passwords, OTPs or bank details by email.

What to write to us about

  • Corrections — if a festival date, story or fact is wrong, send the page link and the correct information.
  • Suggestions — a festival, story, wish or game you would like to see here.
  • Problems with an app or game — tell us your phone model, browser (Chrome/Safari) and what happened.
  • Copyright or content concerns — send the page link and a clear description, and we will act quickly.
  • Partnerships and business — for schools, organisations or brands that want to work with us.

About us

NextGrade is an independent website run from India. We don't publish a phone number or office address; email is the most reliable way to reach us. To learn more, see our About page.

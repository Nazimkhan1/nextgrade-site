How it is calculated

  • Inverter VA = total watts ÷ 0.8 (power factor), then the next available size.
  • Battery (Ah) = watts × hours ÷ (voltage × 0.8). A 12V 150Ah battery stores about 1,800 Wh.
  • Up to 1100 VA usually runs on 12V (1 battery), up to 2500 VA on 24V (2 batteries), larger on 48V.
  • Fridges and pumps draw 2–3× current when starting — pick a slightly bigger inverter. Do not run ACs or geysers on a normal home inverter.

⚠️ This is only an estimate — real backup depends on battery health, brand and temperature. Ask an electrician before buying.

Frequently asked questions

What can an 800 VA inverter run?

About 640 watts: 4 fans, 6 bulbs, a TV and the router comfortably.

How long does a 150Ah battery last?

About 4–5 hours at 300 watts; about 3 hours at 500 watts.

Can I connect solar panels to my inverter?

Yes, with a solar (hybrid/PCU) inverter. The subsidised on-grid system has no battery.

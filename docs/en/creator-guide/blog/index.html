✍️ How to start a blog and begin earning

Blog about what you know — recipes, studies, farming, tech. Each step has the official link.

1Create a free blog on Blogger

Sign in to blogger.com with Google → "Create your blog".

What to fill in:

  • Title — the blog’s name
  • Address — yourname.blogspot.com (you can add your own domain later)
  • Display name

Open the official page ↗

2Must-have pages and first posts

Under Pages create About, Contact and Privacy Policy; then at least 15–20 good posts you wrote yourself.

What to fill in:

  • Post title — what people search for
  • Labels — topics
  • Search description — 1–2 lines
  • Photos — your own or free-licence

Open the official page ↗

3Add the blog to Google Search Console

search.google.com/search-console → "Add property" → your blog address.

What to fill in:

  • URL prefix — the full address
  • Sitemap — submit sitemap.xml

Open the official page ↗

4Apply for AdSense

adsense.google.com → Sign up → add your site and place the code. Review takes from a few days to a few weeks.

What to fill in:

  • Site URL
  • Country and payment address
  • AdSense code — Blogger links it under Earnings

Open the official page ↗

5Read the rules

AdSense policies — which content is not allowed.

What to fill in:

  • No copied content
  • Never click your own ads

Open the official page ↗

What grows the income

  • New posts on fixed days
  • Direct, correct answers to people’s questions
  • A fast blog that looks clean on phones

⚠️ Avoid these mistakes

  • Copy-pasting from other sites
  • Photos without permission
  • Clicking your own ads or asking for clicks

Blog earning estimate: pick "Blog / website" in the earning calculator →

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. YouTube, Instagram and Google change their pages and rules from time to time — when you do this, buttons or names may look a little different. If so, check the official link above for the latest. We will keep updating this guide too.

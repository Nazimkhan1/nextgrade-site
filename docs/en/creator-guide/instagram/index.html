📸 How to earn on Instagram as a creator

Most Instagram income comes from brand deals and Instagram’s own features (where available). Each step has the official link.

1Create an Instagram account

Sign up in the app or on the website.

What to fill in:

  • Mobile number or email
  • Full name
  • Username — e.g. apna.rasoi (short, easy)
  • Password
  • Date of birth

Open the official page ↗

2Switch to a professional (Creator) account

Profile → ☰ Settings → "Account type and tools" → "Switch to professional account" → Creator.

What to fill in:

  • Category — e.g. "Digital creator", "Education"
  • Contact options — show email or not

Open the official page ↗

3Complete your profile

Profile → "Edit profile".

What to fill in:

  • Profile photo
  • Bio — what you post + when (150 characters)
  • Links — YouTube/website

Open the official page ↗

4Post Reels

+ button → Reel → pick or record a video.

What to fill in:

  • Caption — the point in line one, then 3–5 relevant hashtags
  • Cover — a clear photo/text
  • Audio — from Instagram’s library (to avoid copyright issues)
  • Location / topics

Open the official page ↗

5Earning: check the Professional dashboard

Profile → "Professional dashboard" → monetisation status/earning tools — it shows which features your country and account can use.

What to fill in:

  • Paid partnerships with brands — use the "Paid partnership" label
  • Payment details — bank/PAN where asked

Open the official page ↗

Common ways to earn

  • Brand deals (most common) — make a simple rate card
  • Selling your own products/services
  • Sending followers to YouTube/a blog that earns from ads

⚠️ Avoid these mistakes

  • Do not buy followers/likes — reach drops and brands notice
  • Do not repost other people’s Reels/songs
  • Never hide paid promotions — the label is required

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. YouTube, Instagram and Google change their pages and rules from time to time — when you do this, buttons or names may look a little different. If so, check the official link above for the latest. We will keep updating this guide too.

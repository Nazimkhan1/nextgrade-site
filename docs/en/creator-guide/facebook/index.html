📘 How to start earning from videos on Facebook

Facebook’s earning tools (ads, Stars, subscriptions) are offered to Pages or profiles in professional mode once they meet the conditions. Each step has the official link.

1Create a Facebook Page (or turn on professional mode on your profile)

Open facebook.com/pages/create.

What to fill in:

  • Page name — like a channel name
  • Category — e.g. "Video creator", "Education"
  • Bio — one line on what you make

Open the official page ↗

2Complete the Page

Add profile and cover photos, contact details and a username.

What to fill in:

  • Profile picture and cover photo
  • Username — facebook.com/yourname
  • Contact — email/website

Open the official page ↗

3Post videos and Reels

From the Page choose "Reel" or "Video".

What to fill in:

  • Title/caption — the point in line one
  • Thumbnail
  • Your own video — no re-uploads of others’ content

Open the official page ↗

4Earning: Professional dashboard

Professional dashboard → "Monetisation" shows which tools you can get and their conditions.

What to fill in:

  • Conditions (followers, minutes viewed) — check the current ones there
  • Payout account — bank details, tax information

Open the official page ↗

5Read the rules

Meta’s creator and monetisation policies — which content cannot earn.

What to fill in:

  • Original content
  • No misleading titles or “like/share” baiting

Open the official page ↗

Ways to earn

  • Ads on videos (where available)
  • Stars — viewers support you directly
  • Brand deals — with the "Paid partnership" label

⚠️ Avoid these mistakes

  • Do not re-upload other people’s videos
  • Do not buy likes/followers
  • Avoid clickbait and misleading thumbnails

Estimate earnings: pick "Facebook video" in the earning calculator →

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. YouTube, Instagram and Google change their pages and rules from time to time — when you do this, buttons or names may look a little different. If so, check the official link above for the latest. We will keep updating this guide too.

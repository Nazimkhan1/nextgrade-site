▶️ How to start a YouTube channel and begin earning

Each step below has the official link and tells you which field to fill with what. It all works from a phone too.

1Create a Google account (if you have none)

You need a Google (Gmail) account for YouTube. A separate Gmail for the channel is a good idea.

What to fill in:

  • Name
  • Date of birth and gender
  • New Gmail address
  • Strong password
  • Phone number (to keep the account safe)

Open the official page ↗

2Create the YouTube channel

Sign in on youtube.com → your photo at the top right → "Create a channel".

What to fill in:

  • Channel name — short and easy to remember
  • @handle — e.g. @apnarasoi (can be changed later)
  • Profile picture — your face or a logo

Open the official page ↗

3Set up the channel (YouTube Studio → Customisation)

Open studio.youtube.com → "Customisation" on the left.

What to fill in:

  • Banner image (2560×1440 px)
  • Description — what the channel is about and when new videos come
  • Links — Instagram/website
  • Contact info — business email

Open the official page ↗

4Upload your first video

In Studio: "Create → Upload videos" at the top → pick the video.

What to fill in:

  • Title — the words people will search for (up to 100 characters)
  • Description — 2–3 lines summing up the video
  • Thumbnail — a clear face/big letters (after phone verification)
  • Audience — "Is this video made for kids?" must be answered correctly
  • Visibility — Public / Unlisted / Schedule

Open the official page ↗

5Apply to the YouTube Partner Program (earning)

Studio → "Earn". When you meet the conditions an Apply button appears; you link an AdSense account and the channel is reviewed.

What to fill in:

  • Conditions (subscribers, watch time or Shorts views) — check the official page for the current ones
  • AdSense account — name and address exactly as on your bank/ID
  • Tax information (Studio → AdSense)

Open the official page ↗

6Getting paid

Earnings collect in AdSense and are paid to your bank once you reach the minimum; your address is first verified with a PIN sent by post.

What to fill in:

  • Bank account name, number, IFSC
  • Enter the PIN (arrives by post) to verify your address

Open the official page ↗

Other ways to earn

  • Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Thanks (after YPP)
  • Sponsored videos with brands — tick "Paid promotion" on the video
  • Your own website/blog — AdSense there too

⚠️ Never make these mistakes

  • Do not use other people’s videos, songs or film clips without permission — copyright strikes can remove the channel
  • Do not buy views/subscribers or do "sub4sub"
  • Never click your own ads or ask others to
  • No misleading thumbnails or dangerous stunts

📌 Note: these steps are written as things are today. YouTube, Instagram and Google change their pages and rules from time to time — when you do this, buttons or names may look a little different. If so, check the official link above for the latest. We will keep updating this guide too.

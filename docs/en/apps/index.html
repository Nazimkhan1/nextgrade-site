Free apps and tools

Small, light, free web apps that install on your phone like an app. Most of them keep your data on your own phone.

📒 Free app

Hisab Kitab

Note daily expenses, income and lend/borrow. Your data stays on your phone or in your own Google Drive. Installs and works offline.

 💌 Free tool

Wish Card Maker

Pick a festival, add names, make a beautiful greeting card and send it on WhatsApp or download it.

How to install

On Android, open the app in Chrome and choose Install app / Add to Home screen from the menu (⋮). On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.

Free apps and tools

Small, light, free web apps that install on your phone like an app. Most of them keep your data on your own phone.

📒 Free app

Hisab Kitab

Note daily expenses, income and lend/borrow. Your data stays on your phone or in your own Google Drive. Installs and works offline.

 💌 Free tool

Wish Card Maker

Pick a festival, add names, make a beautiful greeting card and send it on WhatsApp or download it.

▶️ Hisab Kitab: watch the video, try the app

How to install

On Android, open the app in Chrome and choose Install app / Add to Home screen from the menu (⋮). On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen.

Why we built these apps

Small everyday jobs — who you lent money to, where your money went this month, or sending someone a pretty greeting card — shouldn't need a heavy download or a new account. Our web apps open in seconds, take very little space on your phone and run smoothly even on budget phones.

Your data stays with you

Hisab Kitab keeps all your records in your own phone's browser, and you can export a backup file whenever you like. The Wish Card Maker never sends your names or message to a server. Note: clearing your browser data can also clear the app's data, so keep a backup of important records.

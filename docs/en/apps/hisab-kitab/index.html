Hisab Kitab: watch the video, try the app

Daily expenses, income and money you lend or borrow, all in one place. This 80-second video shows how the app works — then send it to your family and friends.

📒 Open the app

Share the video and the app

The first button sends the video file itself to WhatsApp, Instagram or any app, together with the app link. On a computer or an older phone it shares the link instead.

How to use the app

  1. Open Hisab Kitab at hisab.nextgrade.in. Tap "Install" if you want to keep it on your phone like an app.
  2. To add an expense, pick "Expense", type the amount, choose a category (food, bills, travel…) and tap "Add entry".
  3. Money came in? Pick "Income". Lent or borrowed money? Pick "Lent" or "Borrowed" and write the person's name.
  4. Don't want to type? Tap "Speak" and say, for example, "vegetables 50 rupees, milk 60 rupees". The app makes the whole list; check it and tap "Add entries".
  5. See the month's spending and income under "Daily", who owes what under "Lend/borrow", and the full month under "Statement" — save it as Excel (CSV) or PDF.

Why Hisab Kitab

Whether it is a small shop, the household budget or money lent to friends, many people still keep accounts in a notebook — and if the notebook is lost, everything is gone. Hisab Kitab is that notebook on your phone, only easier.

Your data stays with you: without a login, entries are saved on this phone; if you sign in with Google, they go to your own Google Drive. Your records never come to our servers.

The app works in Hindi, English, Hinglish, Bengali and Urdu, opens even without internet, and is completely free.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hisab Kitab free?

Yes, completely free. No subscription and no hidden charges.

Do I need to log in?

No. Everything works without a login. If you sign in with Google, your records are saved in your Google Drive and come back when you change phones.

How does speaking an entry work?

Your phone's browser turns what you say into text, and the app picks out the items and amounts. You can fix a name or amount before saving.

Where are my records from the old address?

Hisab Kitab's new address is hisab.nextgrade.in. The records this phone had at the old address move to the new one by themselves. Just install the app again.

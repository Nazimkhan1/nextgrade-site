💍 Shaadi Card Maker

Add the groom's and bride's names, the date and the venue, pick a design and colours — and make a beautiful wedding (Shubh Vivah) invitation card in seconds. Free, made right on your phone, download it or share on WhatsApp.

Design (template) 👇

How to make a wedding card

  1. Fill in the groom's and bride's names, the wedding date, day, time and venue.
  2. Pick one of the designs (templates) below and set your favourite colour / combination.
  3. Tap “Download” to save the card, then send it on WhatsApp or print it.
  4. Everything is drawn on your own device — no name or detail is ever uploaded.

Wedding invitation card — free, from home

Wedding invitation cards usually cost ₹100–₹900 at shops or on other sites. Here you can do the same thing completely free — fill in the names, date and venue, choose a design and colours, and make a beautiful Shubh Vivah invitation card to send straight on WhatsApp.

There are many ready designs — Royal Red, Maroon Gold, Rose Gold, Royal Blue, Green Gold and more. In every design you can change the colours, or pick your own two-colour combination. The card is made in HD so it is crisp enough to print too.

The tool runs fully on your phone or computer — no name, photo or detail is uploaded or saved anywhere. It works even on a slow connection.

FAQ

Is the wedding card really free?

Yes, completely free. No hidden charge and no watermark. Make as many cards as you like.

How do I download or share it on WhatsApp?

Tap “Download” and the card is saved to your phone as a PNG image, which you can share on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere. The “Share on WhatsApp” button also shares it directly where the phone supports it.

Can I change the colours and design?

Yes. There are many templates and in each one you can change the colour / combination — red-gold, maroon, royal blue, green, purple, or your own colour.

Can I print it?

Yes, the card is made in HD (1080×1500), good for printing. The “Print” button prints it directly.

Is my information safe?

Yes. The whole card is drawn in your browser — no name or detail is sent or saved anywhere.

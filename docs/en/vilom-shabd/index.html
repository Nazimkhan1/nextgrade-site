↔️ Vilom Shabd — Hindi antonyms

Vilom shabd are words with opposite meanings, like din–raat (day–night) and sukh–dukh (happiness–sorrow). Here are 140 important pairs with English meanings, the ones school exams ask most.

“Vilom” means reverse. A word whose meaning is the exact opposite of another is its vilom shabd; they are also called viprītārthak shabd. Learning them widens a child’s vocabulary and helps express ideas clearly — and they appear in almost every Hindi grammar paper.

Many Hindi antonyms are made by adding a small prefix: “a” (safal–asafal, poorn–apoorn), “an” (aadar–anaadar, uchit–anuchit), “nir/ni” (rogi–nirogi), “dur/du” (sajjan–durjan, saubhaagya–durbhaagya) and “ku” (sumati–kumati). Many others are completely different words — din–raat, aay–vyay, mitra–shatru — which simply need to be learned.

An antonym is always the same kind of word: a noun pairs with a noun (jay–paraajay), an adjective with an adjective (kathin–saral) and a verb with a verb (hansna–rona). Writing “haarna” as the opposite of “jay” loses marks; the answer is “paraajay”. Some words have more than one opposite — “uttar” is “dakshin” for direction (north–south) but “prashn” for question–answer.

Easy ways to learn them

  • Read 10 pairs a day and play the quiz below — the whole list sticks in about two weeks.
  • Use each pair in a sentence: “Subah school jaata hoon, shaam ko khelta hoon.”
  • Learn the prefix pairs (a, an, nir, dur) together — the pattern becomes obvious.

Frequently asked questions

What is vilom shabd?

A word with the exact opposite meaning of another word, e.g. satya–asatya (truth–untruth), aadi–ant (beginning–end).

What is the vilom of “aasha” (hope)?

“Niraasha” (despair).

What is the vilom of “uday” (rise)?

“Ast” (setting) — as in sooryoday and sooryaast.

What is the vilom of “kritagya” (grateful)?

“Kritaghn” — someone who does not acknowledge help.

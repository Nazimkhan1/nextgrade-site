🪁 Happy Makar Sankranti Image with Name

Write a name on a Makar Sankranti image — kites, sun and til-gud designs, free.

How to make it

  1. Type the name above — it appears on the image at once.
  2. Pick a ready message or write your own, and choose a design.
  3. Choose square for WhatsApp or tall for a status.
  4. Tap Send on WhatsApp, or download it and send it anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a Makar Sankranti image with a name?

Type the name, pick a message and a design, then tap "Send on WhatsApp" or "Download". No app or sign-up needed.

Is it free? Is there a watermark?

Yes, completely free. Only a small nextgrade.in appears in the corner.

Is my name saved anywhere?

No. The image is made inside your phone's browser; the name and message are never sent or stored.

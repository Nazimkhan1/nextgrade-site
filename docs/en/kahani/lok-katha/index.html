🏡 Folk tales and grandma's tales

Folk tales and grandma's stories passed down for generations, for bedtime.

Folk tales are stories that were not written in a book but passed on by word of mouth, from one generation to the next, in villages and homes. That is why the same story has different versions in different regions. We have retold it in our own words for children.

This is a grandma's tale — the kind of story elders have told children at bedtime for generations. It has no single author; every family adds its own touch. We have retold it in our own words.

🐦 Folk tale · 2 min

The Thirsty Crow

It was a hot summer afternoon.

 🐕 Folk tale · 2 min

The Greedy Dog

A dog lived in a village.

 🧢 Folk tale · 3 min

The Cap Seller and the Monkeys

A cap seller went from village to village selling colourful caps.

 🐤 Grandma’s tale · 3 min

The Sparrow’s Grain

A tiny sparrow found a single gram seed.

 🫏 Folk tale · 3 min

The Father, the Son and the Donkey

A farmer and his son were taking their donkey to the market to sell.

 🐈 Folk tale · 2 min

Two Cats and the Monkey

Two cats lived in a village, and they were best friends.

 🏠 Grandma’s tale · 3 min

The Sparrow's House and the Crow's House

A sparrow and a crow were neighbours on a tree.

🌟 Moral

  • The Thirsty Crow — Instead of panicking in trouble, think and find a way. Small, steady efforts can do big things.
  • The Greedy Dog — Greed is a bad habit. Learn to be happy with what you have.
  • The Cap Seller and the Monkeys — In trouble, instead of getting angry, watch carefully and think. The answer is often right in front of you.
  • The Sparrow’s Grain — Being small doesn’t make you weak. Don’t give up — even a tiny bit of help can do a big job.
  • The Father, the Son and the Donkey — You can’t please everyone. Listen to people, but make your own decisions with good sense.
  • Two Cats and the Monkey — When we fight among ourselves, someone else takes the gain. Settle things together, even if it means giving a little.
  • The Sparrow's House and the Crow's House — What is built with effort and patience lasts; what is rushed does not. And help even those who laughed at you when they are in trouble.

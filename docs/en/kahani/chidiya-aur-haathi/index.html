🐘 The Little Sparrow and the Proud Elephant

A sparrow had a nest in a tree, with her eggs in it. One day a proud elephant, bothered by the heat, came and broke a branch of that very tree. The nest fell to the ground and all the eggs broke.

The sparrow began to cry. Her friend the woodpecker came and said, “Don’t cry. We will teach that proud elephant a lesson.” He called his friend the fly and a frog too, and the four of them made a plan.

At noon the fly went to the elephant’s ear and hummed a sweet tune. The elephant enjoyed it so much that he closed his eyes in delight. Just then the woodpecker flew down and pecked at his eyes, and the elephant could not see.

Now the elephant, desperately thirsty, began to look for water. Then the frog sat at the edge of a deep pit and croaked loudly — “Croak, croak!” The elephant thought, “Where there are frogs, there must be a pond.”

The elephant walked towards the sound and fell — thud! — into the pit. He was stuck there for a long time, and all his pride was gone. The sparrow thanked her friends, and together they built her a new nest.

🌟 Moral

There is great strength in unity. When small friends join hands, they can defeat even the biggest bully.

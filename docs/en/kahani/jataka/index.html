🪷 Jataka tales

Jataka tales that teach kindness, honesty and wisdom, retold simply for children.

Jataka tales come from the Buddhist tradition and tell of the earlier lives of the Buddha. In them the Bodhisattva appears as an animal, a king or an ordinary person, and shows the way of kindness, honesty and wisdom. These stories have been told for thousands of years.

🐒 Jataka tale · 3 min

The Monkey King’s Bridge

Long, long ago a huge mango tree grew on the bank of the Ganga.

 🦌 Jataka tale · 3 min

The Golden Deer

In a thick forest lived a golden deer named Ruru.

🌟 Moral

  • The Monkey King’s Bridge — A true leader is one who suffers hardship to protect others.
  • The Golden Deer — Never forget a kindness done to you, and don’t break a promise out of greed. Kindness is rewarded in the end.

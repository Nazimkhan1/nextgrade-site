Grandma’s tale · 3 min

🐤 The Sparrow’s Grain

A tiny sparrow found a single gram seed. She put it on a wooden peg in the carpenter’s yard to eat it, but the seed slipped and got stuck in a crack in the peg. She pecked and pecked, but it would not come out.

She went to the carpenter. “Carpenter brother, split the peg and get my seed out.” “Split a peg for one seed? Go away!” said the carpenter. The sparrow went to the king. “King, scold the carpenter — he won’t split the peg.” The king just laughed it off.

She went to the queen — the queen would not listen. She went to the snake. “Snake brother, frighten the queen.” The snake turned away. She went to the stick. “Stick sister, beat the snake.” The stick said nothing. She went to the fire. “Fire, burn the stick.” The fire refused too.

At last the sparrow went to the sea. “Sea brother, put out the fire.” The sea refused. Then she went to an elephant. “Grandpa elephant, drink up the sea.” The elephant laughed. But a tiny ant had heard everything. “I’ll crawl into the elephant’s trunk!” said the ant.

And that did it! The frightened elephant said, “I’ll drink the sea!” The sea said, “I’ll put out the fire!” The fire said, “I’ll burn the stick!” The stick, the snake, the queen, the king — one after another, they all agreed. The king called the carpenter, the carpenter split the peg, and the sparrow took out her seed, ate it happily and flew away chirping.

🌟 Moral

Being small doesn’t make you weak. Don’t give up — even a tiny bit of help can do a big job.

Questions to ask your child

  1. Where did the sparrow’s seed get stuck?
  2. Whom did the sparrow go to first?
  3. Whose words finally made everyone agree?
See the answers
  1. In a crack in a wooden peg in the carpenter’s yard.
  2. The carpenter.
  3. A tiny ant’s — she offered to crawl into the elephant’s trunk.

Words from the story

  • peg — a piece of wood fixed in the ground
  • carpenter — someone who works with wood
  • crack — a thin split

In everyday life

Even the smallest creature can make a big difference, and whoever doesn’t give up gets their work done. It is also a fun chain story to repeat with children.

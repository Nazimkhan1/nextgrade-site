🥚 The Hen That Laid Golden Eggs

A farmer and his wife lived in a village, and they had a hen. One morning the farmer found that the hen had laid a shining golden egg! The two of them were overjoyed.

From that day the hen laid one golden egg every day. The farmer sold the eggs and slowly became rich. They built a proper house and the family lived in comfort.

But after a while, greed crept into the farmer's heart. He said to his wife, "Why wait for one egg a day? The hen's stomach must be full of golden eggs. Why not take them all out at once!"

His wife tried to stop him, but the farmer would not listen. He killed the hen and cut it open — but there was no gold inside. It was just like any ordinary hen.

The farmer sat down holding his head. In his greed to get everything at once, he had lost the very hen that gave him gold every day.

🌟 Moral

Patience brings sweet rewards. Greed for everything at once takes away even what you have.

Questions to ask your child

  1. What did the hen lay every day?
  2. Why did the farmer kill the hen?
  3. What was inside the hen?
See the answers
  1. One golden egg.
  2. Out of greed — he thought her stomach was full of golden eggs.
  3. Nothing; she was just like any ordinary hen.

Words from the story

  • greed — wanting more and more
  • patience — being able to wait calmly
  • ordinary — nothing special

In everyday life

Value the good things that come little by little. Rushing to get everything at once often loses what you already have.

About Aesop's fables

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world. 🦊 Aesop's fables →

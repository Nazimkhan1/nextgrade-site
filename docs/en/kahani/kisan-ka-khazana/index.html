🌾 The Farmer’s Treasure

In a village lived a hard-working farmer. He had three sons who were very lazy. They slept or gossiped all day and never even looked towards the fields.

The farmer grew old and ill. He called his three sons to him and said, “My sons, there is treasure buried in our field. After I am gone, dig it up.” A few days later the farmer passed away.

Eager for the treasure, the three brothers picked up their spades and dug up the whole field — every corner, deep down. But they found no treasure, and they were very disappointed.

Then the rainy season came. Their mother said, “Why leave such a well-dug field empty? Sow seeds in it.” So the sons sowed seeds. In the soft, freshly dug soil the crop grew so well that no field in the whole village had a harvest like it.

The sons sold the crop and earned plenty of money. Then the eldest son understood. “The treasure Father spoke of was our own hard work!” From that day on, the three brothers farmed with all their heart.

🌟 Moral

Hard work is the real treasure. The earth never sends back empty-handed those who work hard.

About Aesop's fables

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world. 🦊 Aesop's fables →

It comes from Aesop's fables, retold in simple words for children.

🦊 Aesop's fables

Aesop's short animal fables, each ending with a clear lesson, retold for children.

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world.

The Tortoise and the Hare

A hare lived in a forest.

 🦁 Aesop's fable · 2 min

 🥚 Aesop's fable · 2 min

 🍇 Aesop’s fable · 2 min

 🦊 Aesop’s fable · 2 min

 🐺 Aesop’s fable · 3 min

The Boy Who Cried Wolf

 🐜 Aesop’s fable · 2 min

The Ant and the Dove

On a hot day a thirsty ant went to the riverbank for a drink.

 🌾 Aesop’s fable · 3 min

The Farmer’s Treasure

In a village lived a hard-working farmer.

🌟 Moral

  • The Tortoise and the Hare — Pride makes us lose. The one who keeps going slowly but steadily wins.
  • The Lion and the Mouse — Never think of anyone as small. Kindness always comes back to you one day.
  • The Hen That Laid Golden Eggs — Patience brings sweet rewards. Greed for everything at once takes away even what you have.
  • The Fox and the Sour Grapes — It is easy to say bad things about what we cannot get. It is better to admit the truth and try again.
  • The Clever Fox and the Crow — Be careful of people who give false praise. When someone talks very sweetly, think about what they want.
  • The Boy Who Cried Wolf — Lies break trust, and broken trust is not easily mended.
  • The Ant and the Dove — Kindness is never wasted. However small someone is, they can be a big help when the time comes.
  • The Farmer’s Treasure — Hard work is the real treasure. The earth never sends back empty-handed those who work hard.

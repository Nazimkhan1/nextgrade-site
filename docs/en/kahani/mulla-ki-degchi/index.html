🍲 Mulla Nasruddin's Cooking Pot

One day Mulla Nasruddin borrowed a big cooking pot from his neighbour. The next day he went to return it — and inside it sat a small bowl.

The surprised neighbour asked, "Mulla, whose bowl is this?" The Mulla said, "Good news, brother! Last night your pot had a baby. Now mother and baby are both yours."

The neighbour laughed to himself. "The Mulla is a fool — but what do I lose? I got a free bowl!" He happily kept both.

A few days later the Mulla came to borrow the same big pot again. The neighbour gave it at once. But this time many days passed, and the pot did not come back. At last the neighbour went to ask.

The Mulla made a sad face. "Brother, I have bad news — your pot has died." "A pot can't die!" shouted the neighbour. The Mulla replied, "If a pot can have a baby, it can die too!"

🌟 Moral

If greed makes you accept something silly, it will cost you later. Even when there is a profit in it, stay with truth and good sense.

About Mulla Nasruddin

Mulla Nasruddin is a famous character from the folk tales of Turkey, Iran and Central Asia. Sometimes he seems simple, sometimes very clever — and behind his funny antics there is often a deep lesson.

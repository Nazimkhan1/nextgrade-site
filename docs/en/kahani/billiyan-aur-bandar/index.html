🐈 Two Cats and the Monkey

Two cats lived in a village, and they were best friends. One day, while wandering around, they found a big roti lying on the path. Both pounced on it at the same moment.

The first said, "I saw it first, so the bigger share is mine!" The second said, "No, we'll share it equally!" The quarrel grew louder. Just then a clever monkey came down from a tree and said, "Give it to me — I'll divide it fairly."

The monkey brought a pair of scales. He broke the roti into two pieces and put one in each pan. One side was heavier, so he bit a little off that piece and ate it himself.

Now the other side was heavier, so he ate a little from that piece too. On it went — a bite from here, a bite from there — and the roti kept shrinking. The cats cried, "Stop! We'll share it ourselves!"

"What's left is my fee for all this hard work!" said the monkey. He popped the last two pieces into his mouth and climbed up the tree. The two cats were left empty-handed, staring at each other.

🌟 Moral

When we fight among ourselves, someone else takes the gain. Settle things together, even if it means giving a little.

About folk tales

Folk tales are stories that were not written in a book but passed on by word of mouth, from one generation to the next, in villages and homes. That is why the same story has different versions in different regions. We have retold it in our own words for children. 🏡 Folk tales and grandma's tales →

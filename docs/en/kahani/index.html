🌙 Bedtime stories

Short 2-3 minute stories to tell children at night, each ending with a small lesson. Too tired to read? Tap "Read aloud" and your phone will tell the story.

A little help for parents
🔊 Read aloud — your phone reads the story. 🌙 Night mode — a dark screen with big letters so the light doesn't bother your child. ⏲️ Sleep timer — after 5/10/15 minutes the voice stops and the screen goes dark.

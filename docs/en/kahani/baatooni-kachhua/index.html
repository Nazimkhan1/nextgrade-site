🐢 The Talkative Tortoise

A tortoise lived in a pond. His two best friends were a pair of swans. The tortoise was very nice, with just one fault: he talked about everything and simply could not keep quiet.

One year there was no rain, and the pond began to dry up. The swans said, “We are going to a big lake far away. We’ll take you too. We’ll each hold one end of a stick in our beaks, and you hold the middle with your teeth.”

“But remember,” said the swans, “don’t open your mouth on the way, whatever happens.” The tortoise agreed. The swans flew off with the stick, and the tortoise hung on in the middle.

As they passed over a village, children below shouted, “Look, look! The swans are carrying a tortoise!” The tortoise got annoyed. He wanted to say, “What’s it to you?”

As soon as he opened his mouth to speak, he lost his grip on the stick and fell. Luckily he landed on a haystack and was safe, but he had to make the long, hard journey on foot. From then on he learned when to speak and when to keep quiet.

🌟 Moral

You don’t have to speak about everything. Keeping quiet at the right time is also great wisdom.

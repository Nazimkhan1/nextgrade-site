🐭 The Town Mouse and the Village Mouse

A mouse lived at the edge of a field in a village. One day his cousin from the town came to visit. The village mouse lovingly put out whatever he had — grains of wheat, a few peanuts and fresh water.

The town mouse wrinkled his nose. "Cousin, do you call this food? Come with me to the town — I'll give you a real feast." Curious, the village mouse went along.

In the kitchen of a big house in the town, the table was full of everything — sweets, cheese, cake and fruit! The village mouse's eyes shone. They had only just taken their first bite when the door opened and a fat cat walked in.

The two ran for their lives into a hole. A little later they came out again — and this time a dog came barking. It went on like this all night: take one bite, then run.

In the morning the village mouse said, "Cousin, enjoy your feasts. I prefer the plain food of my field, which I can eat in peace." And he went happily back to his village.

🌟 Moral

Simple food eaten in peace is better than a feast eaten in fear. Learn to value what you have.

About Aesop's fables

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world. 🦊 Aesop's fables →

🐦‍⬛ How Many Crows in the City?

One morning Emperor Akbar was walking in the garden with Birbal. Lots of crows were cawing in the trees, and Akbar thought of a funny question.

“Birbal,” he asked, “tell me, how many crows are there in our city?” The courtiers were puzzled — how could anyone count crows?

Birbal thought for a moment, smiled and said, “Your Majesty, there are exactly ninety thousand nine hundred and ninety crows in our city.”

Akbar laughed. “And if we count and find fewer?” Birbal said, “Then some crows have gone to visit their relatives in another city.” “And if there are more?” “Then crows from other cities have come here as guests, Your Majesty!”

Akbar burst out laughing, and so did the whole court. The emperor rewarded Birbal for his quick wit.

🌟 Moral

Don’t panic at a tricky question. A calm mind and a little humour can find an answer to any puzzle.

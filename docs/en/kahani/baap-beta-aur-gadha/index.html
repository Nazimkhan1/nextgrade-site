🫏 The Father, the Son and the Donkey

A farmer and his son were taking their donkey to the market to sell. Both were walking beside the donkey. On the way some people laughed. “What fools! They have a donkey, and still they walk.”

Hearing this, the farmer put his son on the donkey. A little further on, some old men said, “Look at children these days! The young boy sits comfortably while his old father walks.” Embarrassed, the son got down and the father sat on the donkey.

Further on, some women said, “What a hard-hearted father! He sits in comfort while his little son trudges in the sun.” So the farmer pulled his son up onto the donkey with him.

Now some other people said, “Poor donkey! Two people loaded on such a small animal.” Upset, the farmer and his son tied the donkey’s legs to a pole and carried it on their shoulders. People who saw this burst out laughing.

On a bridge, the noise frightened the donkey. It kicked and struggled and fell into the river. The farmer held his head. “Trying to please everyone, I lost my donkey too.”

🌟 Moral

You can’t please everyone. Listen to people, but make your own decisions with good sense.

About folk tales

Folk tales are stories that were not written in a book but passed on by word of mouth, from one generation to the next, in villages and homes. That is why the same story has different versions in different regions. We have retold it in our own words for children.

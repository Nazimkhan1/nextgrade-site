🏠 The Sparrow's House and the Crow's House

A sparrow and a crow were neighbours on a tree. The rains were coming, so both decided to build a house. Day and night the sparrow worked hard, joining twigs, threads and wet clay into a strong nest.

All this seemed far too slow to the crow. He picked up lumps of salt lying nearby, quickly stuck them together into a house and laughed at the sparrow. "Look, my house was built in a day! You are still carrying twigs."

The sparrow said nothing and kept working. Then one night a heavy rain came. The sparrow's nest held firm — but the crow's salt house melted in the water and washed away.

Wet and shivering, the crow came to the sparrow's door. "Sister Sparrow, please give me a little room, or I'll die of cold." The sparrow remembered how he had laughed, but her heart was soft. She opened the door and even gave him warm porridge.

Tears came to the crow's eyes. "Forgive me for laughing at your hard work." After the rain they built a strong house for the crow together, and they stayed good friends ever after.

🌟 Moral

What is built with effort and patience lasts; what is rushed does not. And help even those who laughed at you when they are in trouble.

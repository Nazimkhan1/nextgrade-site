🦁 The Clever Hare and the Lion

In a forest lived a proud lion named Bhasuraka. Every day he hunted many animals for no reason. The frightened animals got together and said to him, “Your Majesty, rest in your cave. Each day we will take turns to send one animal to you.” The lion agreed.

One day it was the turn of a small hare. He was scared, but he was also clever. He took his time on the way on purpose, thinking about how to escape the lion.

When he arrived in the evening, the lion roared angrily, “Why are you so late?” Trembling, the hare said, “Your Majesty, I met another lion on the way. He says that he is the real king of this forest, not you!”

The lion was furious. “Where is he? Show me at once!” The hare led him to a deep well and said, “He is hiding inside this fort.” The lion looked into the well and saw his own reflection in the water.

The lion roared — and the echo of his roar came back from the well. He thought the other lion was challenging him. In a rage he jumped into the well and was never able to climb out. All the animals praised the hare’s cleverness and danced with joy.

🌟 Moral

Wisdom is greater than strength. Pride and anger take away our power to think.

About Panchatantra

The Panchatantra is a very old collection of Indian fables, believed to be around two thousand years old and credited to Pandit Vishnu Sharma. He is said to have told these animal stories to teach young princes good sense and wise conduct. It has five parts (tantras), which gives it its name, and its stories have travelled into many languages around the world. 🦁 Panchatantra stories →

🐺 The Blue Jackal

A jackal named Chandarava lived in a forest. One night, very hungry, he crept into a nearby village to look for food. The village dogs saw him and chased after him, barking loudly.

Running for his life, the jackal dashed into a dyer's house. In the dark he fell straight into a big tub of indigo dye. When he climbed out, he was deep blue from his nose to the tip of his tail.

Back in the forest the next morning, the animals were scared of him — nobody had ever seen such a strange blue creature. The clever jackal saw his chance and said, "Don't be afraid! God has sent me to be your king."

The lion, the tiger, the elephant — they all began to serve him. He always got the best food. But he had his fellow jackals driven out of the forest, so that no one would recognise him.

One moonlit night a pack of jackals far away began to howl, "Hoo-aan, hoo-aan!" Out of habit, the blue jackal lifted his head and howled too. Now everyone knew the truth, and together the animals chased the cheat out of the forest.

🌟 Moral

Pretending to be what we are not never lasts long. Never be ashamed of who you really are or of your own people.

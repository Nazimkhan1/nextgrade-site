👑 Akbar and Birbal stories

Funny tales of Birbal's quick wit and wisdom, retold simply for children.

Birbal was a courtier of the Mughal emperor Akbar and was famous for his quick wit and good sense. The Akbar-Birbal stories have been passed down like folk tales for generations — many are not real events but fun stories that show off Birbal's cleverness.

 🍲 Akbar and Birbal · 3 min

Birbal’s Khichdi

It was the middle of a bitter winter.

 👀 Akbar and Birbal · 3 min

Birbal Counts the Blind

One day in court, Emperor Akbar asked, "Birbal, are there more blind people in our empire, or more people who can see?" Birbal smiled.

🌟 Moral

  • Akbar and Birbal: The Shorter Line — Instead of pulling others down, make yourself better.
  • How Many Crows in the City? — Don’t panic at a tricky question. A calm mind and a little humour can find an answer to any puzzle.
  • Birbal’s Khichdi — Never take away what someone has earned by hard work. Admitting a mistake and putting it right is true greatness.
  • Birbal Counts the Blind — Having eyes is not enough; you must look carefully too. Before asking, look and think for yourself once.

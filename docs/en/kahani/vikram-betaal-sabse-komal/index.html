🌙 Vikram and Betaal: The Most Delicate Queen

Once again King Vikramaditya took Betaal down from the tree, put him on his shoulder and set off. Betaal laughed. “King, the road is long. Listen to a story. But remember — if you know the answer and stay silent, your head will burst into pieces; and if you speak, I will fly back to my tree.”

Betaal began: a king had three queens, and all three were very delicate. One day in the garden, a single lotus petal fell on the first queen’s hand — it left a mark, and she fainted with pain.

That same night the second queen sat on the roof. When the cool moonlight fell on her, blisters rose on her skin.

The third queen was inside the palace. Far away, in some house, someone was pounding rice with a heavy pestle. As soon as she heard the “thud, thud”, her hands began to hurt and blisters came up on her palms.

“Tell me, Vikram,” asked Betaal, “which of the three was the most delicate?” Vikram said, “The third queen. A petal touched the first, moonlight touched the second — but nothing touched the third at all. Just a sound gave her blisters.” “Right answer!” giggled Betaal. “But you spoke!” And off he flew, back to hang from his tree.

🌟 Moral

Listen carefully and think — the right answer often hides in a small detail. And like Vikram, never be afraid to tell the truth.

About Vikram and Betaal

The Vikram and Betaal stories come from the "Betaal Pachisi", an old collection of twenty-five tales. King Vikram carries the spirit Betaal on his shoulder, and on the way Betaal tells a story that ends with a riddle. When the king answers correctly, Betaal flies back to his tree.

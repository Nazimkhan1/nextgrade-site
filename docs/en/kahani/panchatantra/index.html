🦁 Panchatantra stories

Short Panchatantra stories for children, each with its moral — and a read-aloud button.

The Panchatantra is a very old collection of Indian fables, believed to be around two thousand years old and credited to Pandit Vishnu Sharma. He is said to have told these animal stories to teach young princes good sense and wise conduct. It has five parts (tantras), which gives it its name, and its stories have travelled into many languages around the world.

🐒 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Monkey and the Crocodile

A jamun tree stood by a river, and a monkey lived in it.

 🕊️ Panchatantra · 3 min

The Pigeons and the Hunter's Net

A big flock of pigeons lived in a forest.

 🦁 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Clever Hare and the Lion

In a forest lived a proud lion named Bhasuraka.

 🦴 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Scholars Who Brought a Lion to Life

Four friends lived in a village.

 🐘 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Little Sparrow and the Proud Elephant

A sparrow had a nest in a tree, with her eggs in it.

 🐢 Panchatantra · 2 min

The Talkative Tortoise

A tortoise lived in a pond.

 🐺 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Blue Jackal

A jackal named Chandarava lived in a forest.

 🦀 Panchatantra · 3 min

The Crane and the Crab

An old crane lived beside a pond.

🌟 Moral

  • The Monkey and the Crocodile — In trouble, use your wits instead of panicking. And never cheat a friend.
  • The Pigeons and the Hunter's Net — There is great strength in unity. And a good leader thinks of his companions first.
  • The Clever Hare and the Lion — Wisdom is greater than strength. Pride and anger take away our power to think.
  • The Scholars Who Brought a Lion to Life — Knowledge needs good sense too. Before you do something, think about what will happen next.
  • The Little Sparrow and the Proud Elephant — There is great strength in unity. When small friends join hands, they can defeat even the biggest bully.
  • The Talkative Tortoise — You don’t have to speak about everything. Keeping quiet at the right time is also great wisdom.
  • The Blue Jackal — Pretending to be what we are not never lasts long. Never be ashamed of who you really are or of your own people.
  • The Crane and the Crab — Don't trust someone blindly just because their words are sweet. Even when you are scared, think before you decide — and face the truth with courage.

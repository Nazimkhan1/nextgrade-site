Akbar and Birbal · 3 min

👀 Birbal Counts the Blind

One day in court, Emperor Akbar asked, "Birbal, are there more blind people in our empire, or more people who can see?" Birbal smiled. "Your Majesty, there are more blind people." Akbar laughed. "Prove it!"

The next day Birbal sat in the middle of the market and began weaving a charpai, a string cot. Two clerks sat beside him with paper and pen. Birbal whispered something to them and got to work.

Everyone who passed by stopped and asked, "Birbal ji, what are you doing?" Birbal kept weaving quietly, and the clerk wrote that person's name on the list of "the blind". By evening the list was very long.

The news reached the Emperor. Akbar came himself and asked, "Birbal, what are you doing?" Birbal pointed to the clerk — and the clerk added the Emperor's name to the same list.

Akbar was startled when he saw the list. Birbal said, "Your Majesty, whoever asks about what is plainly in front of their eyes is looking without seeing." Akbar roared with laughter and rewarded Birbal.

🌟 Moral

Having eyes is not enough; you must look carefully too. Before asking, look and think for yourself once.

About Akbar and Birbal stories

Birbal was a courtier of the Mughal emperor Akbar and was famous for his quick wit and good sense. The Akbar-Birbal stories have been passed down like folk tales for generations — many are not real events but fun stories that show off Birbal's cleverness. 👑 Akbar and Birbal stories →

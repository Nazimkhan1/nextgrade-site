🧢 The Cap Seller and the Monkeys

A cap seller went from village to village selling colourful caps. One hot afternoon he stopped, tired, under a big tree. He put his basket of caps beside him, kept his own cap on his head, and fell asleep.

Lots of monkeys lived in that tree. They saw the basket, climbed down, picked up one cap each, put it on, and climbed back up the tree.

When the cap seller woke up, his basket was empty! He looked up — every monkey was wearing one of his caps. He waved his hands. “Give me back my caps!” The monkeys waved their hands too. He made a face, and the monkeys made faces too.

Then the cap seller understood — the monkeys copy whatever he does! He thought of a trick. He took the cap off his own head and threw it on the ground.

And that did it! All the monkeys took off their caps and threw them down too. The cap seller quickly filled his basket, smiled and said “thank you” to the monkeys, and went on his way.

🌟 Moral

In trouble, instead of getting angry, watch carefully and think. The answer is often right in front of you.

About folk tales

Folk tales are stories that were not written in a book but passed on by word of mouth, from one generation to the next, in villages and homes. That is why the same story has different versions in different regions. We have retold it in our own words for children. 🏡 Folk tales and grandma's tales →

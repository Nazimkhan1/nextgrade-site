🪣 Tenali Raman and the Treasure in the Well

One summer the plants in Tenali Raman's garden began to wilt. The well was deep and there was no one to draw water. In those same days there had been many thefts in the city.

One night Tenali noticed some thieves hiding in the bushes near his house. He smiled and said loudly to his wife, "Listen, I'm worried about thieves. Let's put all our jewellery in a box and drop it into the well — nobody will look there!"

Then he filled a box with stones and dropped it — splash! — into the well. The thieves heard everything. At midnight they came and began drawing water out, bucket after bucket, to reach the jewels.

Tenali had already dug a channel from the well to his garden. The thieves drew water all night, and all of it flowed straight to the plants. By dawn they finally reached the box — and it was full of stones!

Just then Tenali called from the window, "Thank you, brothers, for watering my garden so hard! Now run, the guards will be here soon!" The embarrassed thieves ran off, and Tenali's garden turned green again.

🌟 Moral

Instead of panicking, use your head — with clever thinking, even trouble can be turned into a gain.

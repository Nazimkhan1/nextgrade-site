🧠 Tenali Raman stories

Tales of the wit and humour of Tenali Raman, the poet of Vijayanagara, for children.

Tenali Raman (Tenali Ramakrishna) was a poet in the court of King Krishnadevaraya of Vijayanagara. He is remembered for his cleverness and sense of humour. His stories began in South India and are now told to children all over the country.

🪣 Tenali Raman · 3 min

Tenali Raman and the Thieves

That year there was little rain in Vijayanagara, and the water in the well had gone very low.

 🐈 Tenali Raman · 3 min

Tenali Raman’s Cat

Vijayanagara was overrun by rats.

 🐴 Tenali Raman · 3 min

Tenali Raman’s Horse

To build up his cavalry, King Krishnadevaraya gave each courtier a horse, along with money every month for its feed.

🌟 Moral

  • Tenali Raman and the Thieves — Cleverness makes big problems easy. And hard work on the wrong path never pays.
  • Tenali Raman’s Cat — Judge a thing by how well it does its real job, not just by how it looks.
  • Tenali Raman’s Horse — What is given for someone else should go to them. Cleverness may save you for a while, but honesty is the right path.

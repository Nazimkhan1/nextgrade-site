🍇 The Fox and the Sour Grapes

One afternoon a hungry fox was looking for food in the forest. As she walked, she came to an orchard where bunches of ripe, juicy grapes hung from a tall vine.

The sight of the grapes made her mouth water. “What a feast I’ll have today!” she thought. She stepped back, ran and leapt as high as she could — but the grapes were too high.

She tried again, and once more. Each time her mouth stopped just short of the grapes. In the end she was panting and her legs were tired.

The fox looked around to make sure no one was watching. Then she lifted her nose and said, “Hmph! Those grapes are sour anyway. Who would eat sour grapes like that?”

And she walked off proudly, as if she had never wanted the grapes at all. A little bird sitting on a branch had seen everything, and smiled to herself.

🌟 Moral

It is easy to say bad things about what we cannot get. It is better to admit the truth and try again.

About Aesop's fables

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world. 🦊 Aesop's fables →

🥛 Sheikh Chilli’s Daydream

In a village lived a simple young man called Sheikh Chilli. He worked little and dreamed a lot. One day a milkman said to him, “Carry this pot of milk to the town and I’ll pay you one anna.”

Sheikh Chilli put the pot on his head and set off. As he walked, he began to think: “With one anna I’ll buy hen’s eggs. The eggs will hatch into chicks, the chicks will grow into hens, and then there will be lots of eggs!”

“I’ll sell the eggs and buy a goat, then a cow, then a buffalo. I’ll become rich, build a big house, get married and have children.”

“When the children call me to eat, I will shake my head grandly and say, ‘No, not now!’” And lost in his dream, Sheikh Chilli really did shake his head — hard.

Crash! The pot fell from his head and broke, and all the milk ran into the road. No anna, no eggs, no house. Sheikh Chilli could only stare at the broken pot.

🌟 Moral

Dreaming is good, but first finish the work in your hands with care. Castles in the air don’t fill your stomach.

Questions to ask your child

  1. What job did Sheikh Chilli get?
  2. What did he dream about on the way?
  3. Why did the pot break?
See the answers
  1. To carry a pot of milk to town for one anna.
  2. Eggs, hens, a goat, a cow, a buffalo, a big house and a wedding.
  3. In his dream he shook his head to say “no” — and really shook it.

Words from the story

  • anna — an old Indian coin
  • daydream — dreaming while awake
  • pot — a clay vessel

In everyday life

Dreaming is good, but first finish the work in your hands with care. In Hindi, “Sheikh Chilli’s castles in the air” still means such dreams.

About Sheikh Chilli

Sheikh Chilli is a funny character from North Indian folk tales who daydreams and builds castles in the air. His stories make you laugh and also teach that work gets done by effort, not by dreaming alone.

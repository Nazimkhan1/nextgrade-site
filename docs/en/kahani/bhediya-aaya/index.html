🐺 The Boy Who Cried Wolf

In a hill village, a boy took the village sheep out to graze every day. Sitting alone on the hillside all day, he often got bored.

One day he thought of a prank. He shouted loudly, “Help! Wolf! A wolf is coming!” The villagers came running with sticks — but there was no wolf. The boy held his stomach and laughed.

A few days later he did it again. Again people came running, and again they were fooled. This time they were angry. “Telling lies is not a good thing!” they said.

Then one evening a wolf really did come out of the bushes and moved towards the sheep. The frightened boy shouted, “Wolf! A real wolf! Help!” But the villagers thought he was joking again, and nobody came.

The wolf chased away many of the sheep. The boy went back to the village in tears. An old man gently told him, “Son, when someone lies again and again, nobody believes them even when they tell the truth.”

🌟 Moral

Lies break trust, and broken trust is not easily mended.

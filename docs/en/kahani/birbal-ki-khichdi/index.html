Akbar and Birbal · 3 min

🍲 Birbal’s Khichdi

It was the middle of a bitter winter. Emperor Akbar announced, “Whoever stands in the cold palace pond all night will get a reward.” A poor washerman found the courage and stood shivering in the water the whole night.

In the morning he came for his reward. “How did you last all night in such cold?” asked Akbar. “Your Majesty,” said the washerman, “far away on the palace roof a lamp was burning. I kept looking at it.” At once a courtier said, “So he took warmth from the lamp — he doesn’t deserve the reward!” And Akbar refused to give it.

Birbal did not like this. The next day he didn’t come to court. When Akbar sent for him, the reply came, “Birbal is cooking khichdi; he will come as soon as it is done.” Hours passed, and still Birbal didn’t come.

At last Akbar went to Birbal’s house himself. There was a small fire burning on the ground — and the pot of khichdi was hanging high up on a tall bamboo pole! Akbar laughed. “Birbal, how will the khichdi cook so far from the fire?”

Birbal said politely, “Your Majesty, if the washerman could get warmth from a lamp far away on the roof, then surely this fire can cook my khichdi.” Akbar understood his mistake. That very day he called the washerman and gave him the full reward.

🌟 Moral

Never take away what someone has earned by hard work. Admitting a mistake and putting it right is true greatness.

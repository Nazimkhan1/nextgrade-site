🐜 The Ant and the Dove

On a hot day a thirsty ant went to the riverbank for a drink. As she bent down, her foot slipped and she was swept into the fast water. She was so small that she could not swim to the bank.

A dove sitting in a nearby tree saw this. Quickly she plucked a leaf and dropped it into the water near the ant. The ant climbed onto the leaf, and the leaf floated to the bank.

“You saved my life,” the ant told the dove. “I will never forget it.” The dove smiled — how could such a tiny ant ever help her?

A few days later a hunter came under the same tree. He raised his catapult to aim at the dove. The ant saw him.

The ant ran up the hunter’s leg and bit him hard. “Ouch!” The hunter jumped and missed. Hearing the noise, the dove flew away to safety, and from far away she called out her thanks to the ant.

🌟 Moral

Kindness is never wasted. However small someone is, they can be a big help when the time comes.

About Aesop's fables

Aesop's fables are named after Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece who is believed to have lived about two and a half thousand years ago. These short stories are mostly about animals, and each one ends with a clear lesson. Today they are told to children all over the world. 🦊 Aesop's fables →

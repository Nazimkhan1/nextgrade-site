BMI Full Form

BMI stands for Body Mass Index.

BMI stands for Body Mass Index.
A measure of fitness from weight and height.

In simple words

BMI is a simple calculation that uses weight and height to show whether an adult weight is low, normal or high for their height. The formula is weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in metres. For example, a person who weighs 60 kg and is 1.6 m tall has a BMI of 60 ÷ 2.56, which is about 23.4.

In the standard WHO ranges for adults, a BMI below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is normal, 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or above is obese. Ordinary people come across BMI in health check-ups, at the gym, on school health cards and in insurance forms, and many phone apps calculate it too.

Note: BMI does not measure body fat directly, so a muscular athlete can also show a high BMI. Children and teenagers are assessed with separate age-based charts, and doctors sometimes use slightly lower cut-offs for South Asian people. BMI is only a first signal; take decisions about your health with the advice of a doctor.

JPEG Full Form

JPEG stands for Joint Photographic Experts Group.

JPEG stands for Joint Photographic Experts Group.
The common image format for photos.

In simple words

JPEG takes its name from the Joint Photographic Experts Group, the committee that designed it, and it is the most common format for photographs. It uses lossy compression: it throws away fine details that the eye barely notices, which makes photo files much smaller. The files end in .jpg or .jpeg, and both are the same format.

Most phone cameras save photos as JPEG by default, and WhatsApp, websites and email are full of them. Government and exam forms in India usually ask for a photo and signature in JPG within a fixed size limit, so knowing how to resize and compress a JPEG file is a handy everyday skill.

Common mistake: editing and saving a JPEG again and again lowers its quality each time, because it is compressed afresh. Keep one original copy. JPEG has no transparency, so logos with clear backgrounds belong in PNG. Some newer phones save photos as HEIC; convert them to JPG if a website refuses to accept them.

🧘 Pranayama — breathe along with the screen

Pranayama means breathing in and out the right way. Each practice here has a guide that tells you when to breathe in and when to breathe out — just follow it.

Which pranayama should I do?

👃

Anulom-Vilom

Breathe in through one nostril and out through the other — that is all Anulom-Vilom is. Follow the ball and the left/right nostril cue below.

💨

Kapalbhati

In Kapalbhati the out-breath is forceful and the in-breath happens by itself. The ball shrinks and taps with each stroke — keep to that rhythm.

🐝

Bhramari

In Bhramari you breathe out with a bee-like "hmmm". A soft hum plays here on every out-breath — match your voice to it.

🌬️

Deep breathing (Deergh Shwas)

The easiest practice: breathe in for a count of 4, hold for 2, breathe out slowly for 6. Before studying, during work or before bed — anywhere.

☀️ The 10-minute morning routine

  1. ☀️Surya Namaskar — 3 rounds~4 min
  2. 👃Anulom-Vilom3 min
  3. 🐝Bhramari2 min
  4. 🌬️Deep breathing — finish calm1 min

Do it at the same time every day — every page counts your 🔥 streak.

This is general practice information, not treatment. If you have an illness, take medicines or are pregnant, ask a doctor or a trained yoga teacher first.

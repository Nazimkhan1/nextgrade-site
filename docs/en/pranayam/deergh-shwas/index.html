🌬️ Deep breathing — 4 · 2 · 6

The easiest practice: breathe in for a count of 4, hold for 2, breathe out slowly for 6. Before studying, during work or before bed — anywhere.

How to do it

  1. Sit or lie comfortably, one hand on the belly, one on the chest.
  2. Breathe in through the nose — first the belly rises, then the chest, then a little up to the shoulders.
  3. Hold for 2 (skip the hold if it feels uncomfortable).
  4. Breathe out slowly for 6 through the nose or a slightly open mouth — chest first, then the belly empties.
  5. Keep repeating.
How long: 3–5 minutes is enough; you can do it 2–3 times a day.

What you may feel

  • Slow, deep breathing
  • The body and mind feel looser

Common mistakes

  • Forcing in too much air
  • Rushing the out-breath
⚠️ Precautions
  • If you feel dizzy, drop the hold and breathe normally

Frequently asked questions

How long should I do deep breathing?

3–5 minutes is enough; 2–3 times a day.

What does 4-2-6 mean?

Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 2, breathe out for 6.

Can I do it lying down?

Yes — it is a good thing to do before sleep.

❄️ Where to see snow

Between December and February nights drop below 0°C at these hill places — your best chance to see snow.

Manali

Himachal Pradesh

Snowy peaks along the Beas river, Solang valley, Old Manali and high mountain passes.

Himachal Pradesh

Mall Road, the Ridge, the toy train and cedar forests — the capital of Himachal.

Uttarakhand

Boating on Naini lake, Mall Road and the lakes around — Bhimtal, Sattal.

Shikara rides on Dal lake, houseboats and the Mughal gardens.

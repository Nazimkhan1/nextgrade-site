Tips for travelling in February

  • It is very cold in the hills — carry warm clothes, gloves and a cap; snowy roads sometimes close.
  • Holidays and festivals are crowded — book trains and hotels early.
  • Check the place’s “weather today” a day before you leave.

Before you travel, check roads, permits and temple/park opening times on official websites — they can change every year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best place to visit in February?

By weather, places like Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling are good in February. Use the lists above to pick by what you like — hills, beaches or pilgrimage.

Is it a good idea to go to the hills in February?

The “Hills” list above only has places where February days are comfortable on average and rain is not heavy.

Where does this information come from?

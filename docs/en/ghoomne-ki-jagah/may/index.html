Tips for travelling in May

  • The daytime sun can be strong — carry a cap, a water bottle and sunscreen.
  • Holidays and festivals are crowded — book trains and hotels early.
  • Check the place’s “weather today” a day before you leave.

Before you travel, check roads, permits and temple/park opening times on official websites — they can change every year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best place to visit in May?

By weather, places like Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital are good in May. Use the lists above to pick by what you like — hills, beaches or pilgrimage.

Is it a good idea to go to the hills in May?

The “Hills” list above only has places where May days are comfortable on average and rain is not heavy.

Where does this information come from?

Temperatures and rain are averages of NASA POWER data for 2015–2024; today’s weather comes from MET Norway.

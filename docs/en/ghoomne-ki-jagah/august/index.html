Tips for travelling in August

  • Landslides can happen on hill roads in the rains — check the weather and road news before you set out.
  • Holidays and festivals are crowded — book trains and hotels early.
  • Check the place’s “weather today” a day before you leave.

Before you travel, check roads, permits and temple/park opening times on official websites — they can change every year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best place to visit in August?

By weather, places like Manali, Ooty, Kodaikanal, Munnar are good in August. Use the lists above to pick by what you like — hills, beaches or pilgrimage.

Is it a good idea to go to the hills in August?

The “Hills” list above only has places where August days are comfortable on average and rain is not heavy.

Where does this information come from?

Temperatures and rain are averages of NASA POWER data for 2015–2024; today’s weather comes from MET Norway.

🕌 Jumma Mubarak · जुम्मा मुबारक

Jumma (Friday) is considered the most blessed day of the week in Islam. Mosques hold the Friday prayer at midday, and people wish each other "Jumma Mubarak" with prayers.

About Jumma Mubarak messages

On Friday the midday prayer (Zuhr) is replaced by the Friday congregational prayer, before which the imam gives a sermon (khutbah). Surah Al-Jumu'ah of the Quran mentions this prayer.

On this day it is recommended to take a bath (ghusl), wear clean clothes and perfume, go to the mosque early, send durood and recite Surah Al-Kahf. Many people also give charity (khairat).

Saying "Jumma Mubarak" is a warm tradition that builds brotherhood. Keep your message respectful and simple.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Friday prayer?

On Friday at midday, at the time of Zuhr. The exact time differs from mosque to mosque, so check with your nearest mosque.

Why is Surah Al-Kahf read on Friday?

Hadith mention the virtue of reading Surah Al-Kahf on Friday, so many people recite it on this day.

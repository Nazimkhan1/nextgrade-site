💞 Wedding Anniversary · शादी की सालगिरह

A wedding anniversary celebrates the years spent together. Here are anniversary wishes for your partner, your parents, your brother and sister-in-law, and friends.

Wedding Anniversary wishes

  • Happy Anniversary! May your love grow stronger with every passing year.
  • To a couple who makes love look easy — Happy Anniversary!
  • Wishing you both a lifetime of laughter, trust and togetherness. Happy Anniversary!
  • Happy Anniversary, Mom and Dad. Thank you for showing us what love means.

About Wedding Anniversary messages

The best anniversary message remembers the moments spent together and blesses the years ahead. Write from the heart for your partner, and choose words of respect and blessing for parents and relatives.

Families often hold a small celebration for parents' 25th (silver jubilee) or 50th (golden jubilee) anniversary — a card with old photographs is always loved on such days.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 25th and 50th anniversaries called?

The 25th anniversary is the silver jubilee and the 50th is the golden jubilee.

How do you say Happy Anniversary in Hindi?

"Shaadi ki saalgirah mubarak ho" or "Shaadi ki saalgirah ki hardik shubhkamnayein".

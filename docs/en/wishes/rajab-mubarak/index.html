🌙 Rajab Mubarak · रजब मुबारक

Rajab is the seventh month of the Islamic calendar and one of the four sacred months. When the Rajab moon is sighted, many people wish each other "Rajab Mubarak", because Sha'ban and then Ramadan come after it.

Rajab Mubarak wishes

  • Rajab Mubarak! May Allah bless you and your family with health, peace and barakah in this sacred month.
  • Rajab has begun and Ramadan is near — may Allah let us reach it in good health. Rajab Mubarak!
  • May all your duas be accepted in this blessed month. Rajab Mubarak!
  • Remember us in your duas this Rajab. Rajab Mubarak to you and your family!

Rajab Mubarak wishes in Hindi

  • रजब मुबारक! अल्लाह इस पाक महीने में आपको और आपके घर वालों को सेहत, सुकून और बरकत दे।
  • रजब का चाँद मुबारक हो! अल्लाह हमें रमज़ान तक पहुँचाए और नेक अमल की तौफ़ीक़ दे।
  • हुरमत वाले इस महीने में अल्लाह आपकी हर दुआ क़ुबूल करे। रजब मुबारक!
  • रजब आ गया, रमज़ान क़रीब है — अल्लाह हमारे दिलों को नेकी से भर दे। आमीन। रजब मुबारक!
  • इस मुबारक महीने में हमें भी अपनी दुआओं में याद रखिएगा। रजब मुबारक!

About Rajab Mubarak messages

The Quran (Surah At-Tawbah 9:36) says there are twelve months with Allah, four of them sacred — Muharram, Rajab, Dhu al-Qa'dah and Dhu al-Hijjah. In these months believers are especially taught to avoid wrongdoing and quarrels and to do more good.

Many Muslims observe the 27th night of Rajab as Shab-e-Miraj — the night linked with the Isra and Mi'raj journey of the Prophet ﷺ. Practices differ between regions and schools of thought, so respect each other's views.

In Rajab many people recite the dua "Allahumma barik lana fi Rajab wa Sha'ban wa ballighna Ramadan" — O Allah, bless us in Rajab and Sha'ban and let us reach Ramadan. Scholars say the chain of this narration is weak, but it is still widely recited as a dua.

Frequently asked questions

Which month is Rajab?

Rajab is the seventh month of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar, after Jumada al-Akhirah and before Sha'ban.

Which are the four sacred months?

Muharram, Rajab, Dhu al-Qa'dah and Dhu al-Hijjah.

When will Rajab begin?

When the moon is sighted. In 2026 it is expected around 10-11 December — check the exact date with your mosque.

🌙 Good Night · गुड नाइट

A short good-night message at the end of the day keeps relationships close. Here are simple, heartfelt good-night wishes for family, friends and your special someone.

Good Night wishes

  • Good night! Let go of the day's tiredness and worries and sleep in peace.
  • May the sweetest dreams find you under this starry sky. Good night!
  • Whatever today was, leave it here — tomorrow is a fresh start. Good night!
  • Close your eyes, take a deep breath and drift into a calm sleep. Good night!
  • Rest well — tomorrow's strength comes from tonight's sleep. Good night!

    May your dreams be golden and your sleep deep. Good night!

About Good Night messages

Just as sending a "good morning" has become a habit, saying "good night" keeps people close. After a tiring day it is a sweet message that tells the other person someone is thinking of them.

A good night message works best when it is short and wishes restful sleep and sweet dreams. Before someone's exam, journey or big day, a one-line "everything will be fine tomorrow" means a lot.

A small tip: instead of the same message every day, use the person's name now and then. With the "Make your own wish" button below you can send a link with your name.

Frequently asked questions

When should I send a good night message?

Usually between 9 and 11 pm, when people are getting ready to sleep. Avoid very late messages (after midnight) so nobody's sleep is disturbed.

How do you say good night in Hindi?

"Shubh ratri" (शुभ रात्रि) is the most common. "Meethe sapne" (sweet dreams) is also said with affection.

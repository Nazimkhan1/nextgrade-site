⚖️ How many Masha in 1 Tola?

1 Tola = 12 Masha

The tola is the old Indian unit for gold and silver that jewellers still use. 1 tola = 180 grains = 11.6638 g. Some jewellers loosely call 10 g "one tola" — always ask for the bill in grams.

🇮🇳 Rules for hoisting the Tiranga (home, school, events)

The key rules of the Flag Code of India, 2002 in simple words — read before you put up the flag on 26 January, 15 August or for “Har Ghar Tiranga”.

How the flag is made

  • Length to height ratio is 3:2.
  • Saffron on top, white in the middle, green at the bottom — three equal bands.
  • In the centre of the white band is the navy-blue Ashoka Chakra with 24 spokes.
  • Since the 2021 change, besides hand-spun khadi, machine-made flags of cotton, wool, silk and polyester are allowed.

At home

  • Every citizen may fly the flag with respect on any day and any occasion.
  • Since July 2022, a flag hoisted in the open or on a house may fly day and night.
  • The flag should be in the highest place of honour, saffron band on top.
  • Never fly a torn, dirty or upside-down flag.

At school and events

  • During hoisting, everyone stands to attention facing the flag.
  • The flag is hoisted briskly and lowered slowly and respectfully.
  • The national anthem is sung after the flag is hoisted.
  • On a stage the flag is placed on the speaker’s right; when hung vertically on a wall, the saffron band is on the viewer’s left.

What not to do

  • Do not let the flag touch the ground or water.
  • Do not write or print anything on it; do not use it as decoration cloth, a tablecloth, handkerchief, cushion or as clothing below the waist.
  • Do not use it to wrap or cover anything (state and military funerals are the exception).
  • The flag is flown at half-mast only on government orders, never on your own.

A damaged or old flag

  • Destroy a damaged flag in private and with dignity — for example by burning it respectfully or another dignified way.
  • Do not leave paper flags on the ground after an event; collect them and dispose of them respectfully.

⚖️ Insulting the national flag, the Constitution or the national anthem in public is an offence under the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971, punishable with up to three years in prison, a fine, or both.

FAQ

Can the flag stay up at home at night?

Yes. Since July 2022 a flag hoisted in the open or on a house may fly day and night.

Is a polyester flag allowed?

Yes. Since December 2021, machine-made and polyester flags are allowed.

What is the ratio of the Indian flag?

Length to height 3:2.

How many spokes does the Ashoka Chakra have?

24 spokes, in navy blue.

How do I dispose of an old flag?

In private and with dignity — for example by burning it respectfully; never in the bin.

Based on: Flag Code of India, 2002 (Ministry of Home Affairs) with its 2021 and 2022 amendments. Rules can change — for official events follow MHA instructions.

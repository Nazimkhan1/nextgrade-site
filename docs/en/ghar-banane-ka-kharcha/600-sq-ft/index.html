How it is calculated

  • Cost: basic ₹1,600–1,900, standard ₹1,900–2,400, premium ₹2,500–3,500 per sq ft (typical November 2026 estimate, labour + material; land, interiors and approvals extra). About 15% more in metros, 10% less in small towns.
  • Materials per sq ft of built-up area (common thumb rules): cement 0.4 bag, steel 4 kg, bricks 8, sand 1.8 cft, aggregate 1.35 cft, paint 0.18 L, tiles 1.3 sq ft.
  • 1 gaj (sq yard) = 9 sq ft; 1 sq metre = 10.76 sq ft.

⚠️ This is only an estimate. Soil, design, material prices and labour vary — get a proper estimate from your engineer or contractor.

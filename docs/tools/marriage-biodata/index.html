❓ Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Marriage biodata mein kya-kya likhna chahiye?

Naam, janm tithi, janm samay aur sthan, height, dharm/jaati/gotra, padhai, naukri, parivaar (pita, mata, bhai-behan) aur sampark. Jo jaankari na deni ho, use khaali chhod dein.

Kya meri photo ya details kahin upload hoti hain?

Nahi. Biodata aapke phone ke browser mein hi banta hai; photo aur details kisi server par nahi jaati.

Biodata Hindi mein banega ya English mein?

Dono — aur Bangla mein bhi. Upar "Language" se chunein; labels usi bhasha mein aa jaate hain, aap details kisi bhi bhasha mein likh sakte hain.

Achhe biodata ke liye tips

Saaf, haal ki photo lagayein; sirf sach aur zaroori baatein likhein; phone number wahi dein jis par parivaar baat kar sake. Shaadi ka card bhi chahiye? Hamara Shaadi Card Maker dekhein.
